## 1. Problem Context & 2027 Market Trigger

Founders, corporate innovation leads, and mid-market partners frequently commit tens of thousands of euros and months of engineering or hiring capacity to new product ideas, AI initiatives, or service lines before testing whether real buyer demand, unit economics, and operational feasibility exist.

The 2027 market trigger is capital preservation and evidence-gated execution:

- **Zero Tolerance for Unvalidated Burn:** In 2027's tighter funding and enterprise budget environment, leadership teams want objective go/no-go evidence—competitor pricing, regulatory friction, and operating-model constraints—before greenlighting build budgets.
- **Productizing CBa Consult's Internal Validation Engine:** CBa Consult already proved an internal market-analyst and operating-model validation workflow across this portfolio (`market-analysis-2027.md` and SIDPA Stage 1–3 gates). Packaging that exact methodology as a billable 2-week client sprint creates an immediate, $0 working-capital revenue wedge.

## 2. Solution Architecture & Delivery Workflow

`OP-X-001` is packaged as a rapid **Validation Service (Market Analyst)** delivered through a 2-week core client sprint (within a 4–8 week portfolio rollout and refinement horizon):

1. **Days 1–3 — Idea Framing & Hypothesis Extraction:** Clarify target buyer, problem wedge, revenue model, regulatory boundaries, and the 5–7 make-or-break assumptions requiring empirical verification.
2. **Days 4–7 — Competitive & Market-Signal Scan:** Benchmark direct comparables, alternative spend, pricing bands, and 2027 macro/regulatory tailwinds or blockers.
3. **Days 8–10 — Operating-Model & Sweat-Equity Stress Test:** Model delivery effort, capability gaps, working-capital exposure, unit economics, and negative scope boundaries.
4. **Days 11–14 — Evidence-Backed Go / Pivot / Park Readout:** Deliver an executive validation dossier with scored feasibility, explicit risk register, and recommended next-step pilot design.

## 5. Explicit Negative Scope Boundaries & Stage 1b Gate

To avoid liability and keep the sprint fast and objective, strict scope boundaries apply:

- **Decision Support, Not Guaranteed Commercial Outcomes:** CBa Consult delivers evidence-based feasibility scoring and market signals; commercial execution results remain the client's responsibility.
- **No Primary Statistical Market Surveys or Custom Builds:** Within a 2-week sprint, research relies on secondary market intelligence, comparable benchmarking, and structured stakeholder interviews—not large-scale paid survey panels or MVP coding.
- **Strict Confidentiality & Conflict Check:** Client concepts are evaluated independently without sharing proprietary insights across competing engagements.
- **Stage 1b Gate Status:** Stage 1b ideation framing is complete. Progression requires Director sign-off on the Stage 3 Business Case and standardization of the 2-week readout template.
