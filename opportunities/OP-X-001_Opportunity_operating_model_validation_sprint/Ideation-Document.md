2) ✅ Demand Validation System (Don’t Build Before They Buy)

Step 1: Define the problem → promise → proof

Problem: What hurts right now?

Promise: What outcome in 30–45 days?

Proof: Past results/testimonials (or pilot guarantees)

Step 2: Run micro-tests

Signal tests: 10–15 calls with your ideal audience

Price tests: Quote 2–3 price points; track acceptance

Offer tests: Vary inclusions (coaching vs. done-for-you vs. hybrid)

Step 3: Decide via thresholds

Proceed if ≥30% of conversations lead to paid trials

Pause/pivot if <10% interest or heavy price objections

3) 💎 Lean Offer & Pricing

Offer formula: Outcome + Method + Timeframe + Risk reversal

“We help boutique B2B agencies book 10 qualified demos in 30 days using LinkedIn + email, or we work with you free for an extra two weeks.”

Tiered: Basic (€497) / Pro (€1,200) / Done-for-you (€2,500+)

Milestone-based: Small upfront + success bonus

Subscription: Ongoing support (€250–€500/month)

Value stack: Outcome clarity → Social proof → Scarcity → Guarantee

Finance:

7) 💰 Finance Basics (Working Capital Without Capital)

Cash buffer target: 1–2 months of expenses (start with micro-buffer: €1,000–€3,000)

Payment terms: 50–100% upfront for pilots; milestone-based for larger work

Recurring base: Move pilots to retainer once outcomes are proven

Cost discipline: Keep tools lean; outsource only revenue-linked activities

Simple working capital formula:Working Capital = Current Assets (cash + receivables) − Current Liabilities (payables + short-term debts)

Focus on cash-in-first models (pre-sales, upfront billing) to avoid negative working capital traps.

8) 🧩 ADPA Integration (Adaptive Digital Process Architecture)

Financial Responsibility → Budget tracker + invoicing SOP + upfront payment policy

Operational Responsibility → SOPs for outreach, delivery, reporting; automation (calendar, email sequences)

Strategic Responsibility → Quarterly goals, mappable KPIs, pivot criteria

Personal Responsibility → Burnout guardrails (work hours, recovery routines, “no-meeting” blocks)

ADPA Modules: Idea intake → Offer validation → Sales ops → Delivery ops → Testimonial engine → Referral/JV engine
