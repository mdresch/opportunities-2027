## 2. Business Need & Cost of Inaction

Founders and mid-market leaders routinely burn €20,000–€100,000+ building products, hiring staff, or signing vendor contracts before validating buyer willingness-to-pay, competitive positioning, and operating-model economics. Cost of inaction includes sunk engineering/marketing spend on unviable concepts and delayed pivots.

The assigned ADPA `.docx` corpus (`Growth and possible opportunities 2026.docx`, `opportunities 2026 - no working capital.docx`, `Summary Opportunities 2026 - low working capital.docx`) catalogs 21 zero-cost opportunity archetypes and 3 consolidated strategic paths (AI Automation Agency, Digital Knowledge Merchant, Hyper-Niche Curator) *(Illustrative Pre-Pilot ADPA Source Modeling — Not Realized Client Actuals)*. `OP-X-001` converts CBa Consult's own battle-tested portfolio validation discipline (`market-analysis-2027.md` + SIDPA gates) into a fast, billable 2-week client decision sprint with zero fabricated outcome guarantees.

- **Fixed-Fee Decision Sprint (€800–€3,000):** Billed upfront before Day 1 kickoff so working capital remains $0. Delivers a structured feasibility scorecard, comparable matrix, operating-model stress test, and Go/Pivot/Park recommendation within 10 business days.
- **Compounding Path:** Serves as a low-risk paid entry wedge that naturally feeds validated initiatives into CBa Consult's downstream implementation offers (`OP-DTX-005`, `OP-MKT-001`, `OP-SLS-001`, or `OP-ITG-013`) and builds Case Study proof (`OP-X-004`).
