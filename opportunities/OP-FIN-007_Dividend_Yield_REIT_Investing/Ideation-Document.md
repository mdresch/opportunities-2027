## 1. Problem Context & 2027 Market Trigger

Corporate treasurers, finance directors, and owner-operators holding surplus balance-sheet liquidity face persistent tension between idle low-yield bank deposits, volatile growth equities, and illiquid private commitments. While the original ADPA ideation document (`Dividend Yield & REIT Investing.docx`) framed this as an internal passive-income and treasury-diversification project, CBa Consult's 2027 portfolio repositions the IP as a **structured educational toolkit and analytical screener**.

**2027 Strategic Drivers & Buyer Urgency:**
- **Buyer Persona:** CFOs, Finance Directors, Controllers, and Owner-Operators across Small, Mid, and Enterprise segments.
- **Capital-Allocation Scrutiny:** Elevated 2027 cost discipline and board-level treasury oversight push finance leaders to demand transparent, rules-based frameworks for evaluating cash-generative equities and Real Estate Investment Trusts (REITs).
- **Conflict-Free Alternative:** Finance teams want objective valuation math (payout coverage, FFO/AFFO sustainability, margin of safety) without paying wealth-management percentage-of-AUM fees or relying on speculative retail newsletters.

## 2. Solution Architecture & Delivery Workflow

`OP-FIN-007` packages the **Warren Buffett Value Investing Strategy** toolkit and ADPA source charter into a self-contained **Digital Product (PDF Playbook + Spreadsheet Screener)** produced over a 6–8 week low-to-medium effort sprint:

1. **Fundamental Screening Criteria Module (Weeks 1–2):** Codifies payout-ratio sustainability, free-cash-flow coverage, leverage/interest-coverage caps, REIT FFO (Funds From Operations) metrics, and 10+ sector diversification rules derived from the ADPA source charter.
2. **Valuation & Margin-of-Safety Workbook (Weeks 3–4):** Delivers reusable Excel/spreadsheet DCF, dividend-discount, and yield-on-cost sensitivity templates requiring zero external SaaS subscriptions.
3. **Monitoring & Rebalancing Cadence (Weeks 5–6):** Adapts the ADPA source's quarterly review checklist (concentration limits, dividend-cut early-warning flags, reinvestment tracking) into a repeatable operator guide.
4. **Governance & Disclaimer Wrapper (Weeks 7–8):** Embeds standardized educational-framework disclaimers and clear separation from regulated investment advisory prior to release.

**Core Toolkit Deliverables:**
- Evergreen Dividend & REIT Value-Investing Playbook (PDF)
- Multi-Tab Fundamental Screener & Yield-Coverage Workbook (Excel/Sheets)
- Quarterly Portfolio Risk & Concentration Review Checklist

## 5. Explicit Negative Scope Boundaries & Stage 1b Gate

- **Not Regulated Financial or Investment Advice:** `OP-FIN-007` is strictly an educational analytical framework and spreadsheet toolkit. CBa Consult does **not** provide regulated investment advice, securities recommendations, discretionary portfolio management, tax counsel, or funds custody.
- **No Automated Trading Execution:** Despite the ADPA source's reference to investment-process automation, CBa Consult will not build brokerage APIs, order-routing bots, or live trading scripts; automation is limited to workbook formulas and review checklists.
- **Portfolio Sequencing (`G1b`):** Classified under Core & Specialty Backlog (Q3 2027), secondary to Tier A Q1–Q2 Finance flagships (`OP-FIN-009`, `OP-FIN-012`). Requires explicit legal-disclaimer sign-off and Stage 2 demand validation (`Hypothesis-Register.md`) before any external release.
