## 2. Business Need & Cost of Inaction

Finance leaders and business owners evaluating surplus-cash yield strategies often default either to low-yield cash deposits or to ad-hoc stock-picking without disciplined payout-coverage, REIT FFO (Funds From Operations), and sector-concentration guardrails. Conversely, institutional wealth managers impose recurring percentage-of-AUM fees that are disproportionate for organizations seeking an internal analytical framework.

**Cost of Inaction & ADPA Source Baseline:**
- **For Buyers:** Absent an objective screening model, treasury teams waste hours manually reconciling disparate ratio definitions or overpay for conflicted asset-management commentary.
- **For CBa Consult:** Repackaging existing value-investing templates creates an evergreen digital asset at zero ongoing delivery marginal cost—provided it never displaces Q1–Q2 Tier A `OP-FIN-009` execution.
- **ADPA Modeling Disclaimer:** The assigned ADPA source (`Dividend Yield & REIT Investing.docx`) modeled an internal passive-income portfolio targeting 5–7% annual yield within 12 months across 10+ sectors and 20+ holdings, supported by automated reinvestment and monitoring. *Illustrative Pre-Pilot ADPA Source Modeling — Not Realized Client Actuals:* that 5–7% yield target reflects an illustrative internal treasury benchmark in the source template, **not** a guaranteed investment return or realized CBa Consult product metric.
