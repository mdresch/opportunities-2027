## 1. Problem Context & 2027 Market Trigger

Small and mid-sized organizations migrating legacy email servers, fragmented file shares, or acquired tenants into Microsoft 365 frequently underestimate identity, permission, and change-management complexity. Rushed cutovers trigger unplanned downtime, broken SharePoint/Teams permissions, data-exposure gaps, and poor end-user adoption that turn an IT upgrade into an operational disruption.

**2027 Strategic Drivers & Buyer Urgency:**
- **Buyer Persona:** CIOs, CTOs, IT Directors, and Enterprise Architecture / Cloud Leads in Small & Mid-sized organizations.
- **Compliance & Copilot Readiness:** NIS2/DORA baseline security expectations and 2027 Microsoft Copilot rollouts require clean M365 tenant permissions, identity hygiene, and DLP governance before AI assistants index corporate files.
- **Fixed-Scope Budget Discipline:** SME buyers reject open-ended MSP time-and-materials billing in favor of predictable, risk-managed migration packages.

## 2. Solution Architecture & Delivery Workflow

`OP-ITG-004` packages PMBOK-aligned migration governance into a repeatable **6–10 week Advisory / Starter Kit** designed as an entry wedge into ongoing cloud and AI governance retainers (`OP-ITG-001`, `OP-ITG-002`, `OP-ITG-014`):

1. **Tenant Discovery & Readiness Baseline (Weeks 1–2):** Inventory mailboxes, file shares, identity dependencies, licensing tiers, and security/compliance prerequisites to eliminate cutover surprises.
2. **Migration Blueprint & Risk Register (Weeks 3–4):** Define phased cutover waves, rollback gates, stakeholder communications, and minimum-viable M365 security/DLP guardrails.
3. **Controlled Cutover & Validation (Weeks 5–7):** Oversee staged pilot and production migrations with bounded downtime windows, data-integrity verification, and executive status reporting.
4. **Adoption & Post-Migration Governance Handoff (Weeks 8–10):** Deliver targeted end-user/admin training kits, permission-governance baselines, and a post-migration scorecard surfacing follow-on cloud/AI governance needs.

**Core Starter-Kit Deliverables:**
- M365 Tenant Discovery & Cutover Risk Register
- Phased Migration Runbook, Rollback Plan & Stakeholder Comms Pack
- Post-Migration Permission Hygiene & Adoption Training Kit

## 5. Explicit Negative Scope Boundaries & Stage 1b Gate

- **Not a 24/7 MSP Helpdesk:** `OP-ITG-004` is a fixed-scope migration governance and starter-kit engagement—not ongoing Level-1/Level-2 IT desktop support, hardware procurement, or 24/7 managed NOC/SOC monitoring.
- **No Unbounded Legacy Cleanup:** Multi-terabyte legacy archive restructuring, custom application rewrites, or complex multi-forest Active Directory consolidations require separate scoping outside the baseline Small & Mid package.
- **No License Reselling Liability:** Client contracts directly for Microsoft 365 seat licensing; CBa Consult remains an independent governance partner.
- **Stage 1b (`G1b`) Status:** Ranked in Core & Specialty Backlog (Q2 2027); secondary to Core 5 Tier A outreach unless pulled by inbound SME demand. Requires Stage 2 hypothesis logging before `G4` pilot promotion.
