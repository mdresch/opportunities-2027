## 2. Business Need & Cost of Inaction

SMEs migrating legacy mail and file servers to Microsoft 365 frequently suffer unplanned cutover downtime, exposed SharePoint permissions, and low user adoption when migrations are run without structured stakeholder, security, and risk governance. Meanwhile, small IT teams cannot justify €35k–€150k enterprise SI engagements.

**Cost of Inaction & ADPA Source Baseline:**
- **For Clients:** Unmanaged M365 cutovers disrupt daily operations, create shadow-permission exposures ahead of Copilot/AI deployment, and trigger expensive post-migration rework.
- **For CBa Consult:** A repeatable governance starter kit captures warm SME migration demand cleanly while feeding higher-value cloud and AI governance work.
- **ADPA Modeling Disclaimer:** The assigned ADPA source (`Managed Microsoft 365 Migration Services.docx`) outlines a standardized, low-cost SME migration framework targeting minimal downtime, cost efficiency, security compliance, and upsell into ongoing managed services, without specifying hard dollar ROI/NPV figures. Any projected client downtime savings or upsell conversion targets remain *Illustrative Pre-Pilot ADPA Source Modeling — Not Realized Client Actuals* until validated in a live client cutover.

## 4. Commercial Packaging, $0 Working-Capital Model & Compounding Path

- **Zero-Capital Model:** Client purchases and holds all Microsoft 365 tenant licenses directly; CBa Consult provides PMBOK migration architecture, risk/rollback gates, communication kits, and adoption training using existing templates and [BD-Quick-Reference.md](BD-Quick-Reference.md) assets ($0 working capital).
- **Pricing & Milestone Terms:** **€2,000–€7,500** fixed fee tiered by tenant user count and workload scope (40% discovery/blueprint, 40% cutover completion, 20% post-migration governance & training sign-off).
- **Measurable Outcome KPI:** Zero unplanned business-hours cutover outage beyond the pre-approved maintenance window and 100% completion of post-migration SharePoint/Teams permission baselines.
- **Compounding Path:** Captures foundational tenant hygiene, creating a warm expansion bridge into `OP-ITG-001`/`OP-ITG-002` cloud governance retainers and Tier A `OP-ITG-014` (Knowledge Intelligence & Copilot/RAG readiness).

## 5. Risk Register & Compensating Controls

| Risk | Likelihood / Impact | Compensating Control |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Rushed cutover causes client email/file downtime** | Medium / High | Enforce mandatory pre-cutover readiness checklist, pilot-group validation, and explicit rollback window |
| **Hidden tenant complexity (legacy Exchange/multi-forest)** | High / Medium | Gate fixed-fee SOW behind Week 1–2 Tenant Discovery; trigger formal change request for out-of-band remediation |
| **Post-migration end-user support creep** | Medium / Medium | Cap hypercare window (e.g., 5 business days post-cutover) and exclude ongoing Tier-1 helpdesk |
| **Dilutes Core 5 Tier A enterprise outreach** | Medium / High | Keep `PARKED_IN_INVENTORY` for outbound; activate strictly on warm SME inbound or referral |
