## 1. Problem Context & 2027 Market Trigger

Small and mid-market enterprises face acute 2027 cost-discipline pressure alongside overlapping regulatory mandates (EU AI Act, DORA/NIS2, IFRS 18, and CSRD/ESG). CIOs, CDOs, and Transformation Directors are frequently pitched generic AI software and broad transformation programs without an objective baseline of where manual workflow bottlenecks actually sit or where AI introduction creates operational, compliance, or data-leakage risk.

- **Core Pain:** Disconnected manual handoffs, inconsistent QA/testing cycles, and ad-hoc shadow-AI adoption make it difficult for SME leadership to separate high-payback automation candidates from hype-driven tooling spend.
- **2027 Buying Trigger:** Leadership teams demand fixed-scope, conflict-free diagnostics that quantify measurable hours saved before committing multi-month capex or signing software vendor retainers.

## 2. Solution Architecture & Delivery Workflow

`OP-DTX-001` delivers a 4–6 week fixed-scope diagnostic grounded in the **Digital Transformation** signature toolkit. The engagement decomposes core operational and delivery workflows into a ranked automation-and-AI readiness portfolio:

1. **Week 1 — Workflow & Bottleneck Intake:** Conduct structured interviews and process walkthroughs across 3–5 core workflows (finance ops, sales-to-delivery, service desk, or SDLC) to map manual touchpoints, rework loops, and cycle-time drag.
2. **Week 2–3 — Automation vs. Risk Scoring:** Evaluate each bottleneck on two axes: (a) quantifiable hours saved and error reduction via low-cost automation or AI augmentation, and (b) governance, data-privacy, and EU AI Act risk exposure.
3. **Week 4–5 — Readiness & Architecture Blueprint:** Define target-state workflow patterns using existing client licensing (e.g., M365, ERP, CRM, or lightweight workflow tools) without requiring heavy new platform purchases.
4. **Week 6 — Executive Roadmap & Prioritized Backlog:** Deliver a board-ready diagnostic report ranking Quick Wins (0–90 days), Structural Fixes, and Explicit "Do-Not-Automate" guardrails, feeding directly into downstream implementations (`OP-FIN-004`, `OP-SLS-003`, `OP-ITG-003`).

## 5. Explicit Negative Scope Boundaries & Stage 1b Gate

- **No Hype or Unverified AI Claims:** Recommendations must be anchored in observed workflow steps and conservative, measurable hours-saved estimates rather than generic productivity percentages.
- **No Custom Software Build or SaaS Reselling:** `OP-DTX-001` is strictly a diagnostic and roadmap engagement; production build, integration, or managed services require separate scoped proposals.
- **No Legal Certification:** EU AI Act or GDPR risk flags identify operational exposure for client legal/DPO review and do not constitute formal legal opinions.
