## 2. Business Need & Cost of Inaction

SME leaders face mounting pressure to cut operational drag and adopt AI responsibly in 2027, yet unstructured experimentation wastes budget on unused licenses while leaving core manual bottlenecks untouched. Without a baseline diagnostic, teams either freeze due to EU AI Act / GDPR uncertainty or deploy shadow AI across inconsistent workflows.

- **Cost of Inaction:** Continued manual rework, delayed delivery cycles, and misallocated software spend without measurable hours saved.
- **Source Modeling Benchmark (*Illustrative Pre-Pilot ADPA Source Modeling — Not Realized Client Actuals*):** The underlying ADPA draft (`App - Boost Solutions Delivery with AI.docx`) models 30–40% faster time-to-market and 25% post-deployment defect reduction in AI-augmented delivery lifecycles; CBa Consult treats these strictly as aspirational enterprise reference bounds, grounding SME diagnostics in conservative observed hours saved.

## 4. Commercial Packaging, $0 Working-Capital Model & Compounding Path

- **Packaging:** Fixed-fee 4–6 week diagnostic priced at **€1,000** (single-department SME) to **€3,500** (multi-workflow mid-market), billed 50% on kickoff and 50% on executive roadmap sign-off.
- **$0 Working-Capital Execution:** Delivered entirely via Director sweat-equity using existing CBa Consult workflow-mapping templates and client-owned tools (M365, Jira, ERP logs)—requiring zero third-party SaaS spend or subcontracting.
- **Compounding Path:** Every diagnostic terminates with a prioritized implementation backlog that feeds paid follow-on scopes in Finance (`OP-FIN-004`), Sales (`OP-SLS-003`), and IT Governance (`OP-ITG-003`), plus anonymized before/after hours-saved proof (`OP-X-004`).
