## 2. Business Need & Cost of Inaction

Mid-market and PE-backed enterprises investing in AI without board-level portfolio governance experience rising cloud/license spend, stalled functional pilots, and regulatory exposure ahead of December 2027 EU AI Act milestones.

- **Cost of Inaction:** Siloed tooling decisions, unmeasured pilot sprawl, and misaligned leadership expectations between CFOs (demanding EBIT impact) and IT/Business leads (chasing capabilities).
- **Source Modeling Caveat:** *Illustrative Pre-Pilot ADPA Source Modeling — Not Realized Client Actuals:* `Enterprise AI Strategy & Transformation Partner.docx` projects `10–15%` operational cost savings within 12 months, `15%` process productivity gains, `10%` project cost reductions by Week 12, a `10 FTE` corporate AI department by Week 10, and `80%` senior-management / `60%` employee training completion. These are unverified 2026 template benchmarks—**not** realized CBa Consult client results.
