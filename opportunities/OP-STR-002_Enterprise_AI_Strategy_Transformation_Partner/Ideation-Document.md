## 1. Problem Context & 2027 Market Trigger

CEOs, Boards, and PE Operating Partners in medium and large enterprises are fatigued by disconnected AI proofs of concept that burn budget without shifting unit economics or governance posture:

1. **Fragmented Pilot Sprawl:** Functional teams launch uncoordinated copilots and automation scripts without shared investment thresholds, risk criteria, or board-level KPIs.
2. **Conflict-Free Strategy Deficit:** Large SIs and Big Four practices sell six-figure `"AI Transformation"` roadmaps designed to pull through multi-million-euro implementation teams.
3. **Portfolio Discipline Mandate (2027):** In CBa Consult's own portfolio rule ([Tier_A_Executive_Summary_2027.md](../../Tier_A_Executive_Summary_2027.md)), **generic AI transformation is never pitched cold**—`OP-STR-002` serves as an executive-sponsor roadmap **only** after concrete diagnostic proof or inbound board pull, connecting `OP-CHG-003` (Go/No-Go Gate), `OP-ITG-013`, and `OP-RSK-004`.
