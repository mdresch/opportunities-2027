## 2. Business Need & Cost of Inaction

When B2B marketing and lifecycle emails silently route to spam or bounce due to missing DMARC alignment or unhygienic CRM lists, every euro spent on lead generation, copywriting, and ESP subscriptions underperforms.

- **Cost of Inaction:** Eroded pipeline conversion, silent domain blacklisting risk, and wasted marketing team capacity chasing copy tweaks instead of fixing technical authentication.
- **Fast Q1–Q2 Cash Wedge:** 2–4 week low-effort execution makes `OP-MKT-005` an ideal immediate cash-flow wedge alongside `OP-MKT-001`.

*Note on ADPA Source Evidence:* The source file `Email Deliverability Consultant.docx` cites industry benchmarks (21% of permission-based emails failing inbox placement; 70% of spam complaints from poor targeting; 20–30% revenue-per-email uplift) and project targets (15–20% inbox placement lift, 10–15% open-rate lift, 5–10% CTR lift). All projected uplift targets constitute **Illustrative Pre-Pilot ADPA Source Modeling — Not Realized Client Actuals** and must be measured against each pilot client's baseline ESP telemetry.
