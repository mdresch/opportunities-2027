It monetizes HR governance advisory at €3,000–€10,000 with `` `$0` `` working capital while pulling through core Digital Labor and Policy Corpus offers.

## 2. Business Need & Cost of Inaction

Employers adopting Microsoft Copilot Studio / Teams HR agents without governance face three concrete costs of inaction: (1) inaccurate or conflicting country-level policy answers that create employee-relations grievances, (2) over-privileged agent access to sensitive HR/compensation documents, and (3) wasted ServiceNow/SAP integration spend when bots fail user trust and tickets bounce back to HRBPs.

*Illustrative Pre-Pilot ADPA Source Modeling — Not Realized Client Actuals:* No ADPA `.docx` ROI/NPV workbook is assigned (`0` ADPA files in [Source-Documents.md](Source-Documents.md)). Market demand is anchored in Microsoft's *Frontier HR Leader* CEMEX *Consult HR* and Kantar corpus-readiness narratives; client-specific ticket-deflection and HRBP-hours-saved KPIs will be baselined during Paid Pilot #1 rather than assumed upfront.

## 4. Commercial Packaging, $0 Working-Capital Model & Compounding Path

- **Commercial Structure:** Fixed-scope 6–8 week advisory sprint (**€3,000–€10,000** depending on country/entity count) delivering: (1) Policy Corpus Readiness Sign-Off Criteria, (2) Regional Grounding & Source Matrix, (3) 3-Tier HRBP Escalation Matrix, and (4) `Agent 365` / Permission Governance Pack.
- **`$0` Working-Capital Fit:** Delivered purely with existing advisory frameworks and the client's M365/HR documentation at `` `$0` `` software or contractor outlay.
- **Capacity Guardrail:** Requires `8–10 hrs/wk`, leaving minimal spare bandwidth inside the `10–15 hrs/wk` solo cap; must be scheduled around Tier A commitments.
- **Compounding Path:** Follows the **CEMEX / Kantar sequence**: lead with `OP-ITG-014` (policy corpus cleanup) -> `OP-HR-013` (HR ESS governance pack) -> `OP-ITG-013` (enterprise AI agent governance) and `OP-HR-003` / `OP-HR-009` talent/AI-literacy workstreams.
