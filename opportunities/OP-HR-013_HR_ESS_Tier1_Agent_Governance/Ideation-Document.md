## 1. Problem Context & 2027 Market Trigger

CHROs and HR Shared Services leaders in Medium and Large enterprises face intense pressure in 2027 to deflect routine Tier-1 employee policy inquiries using Microsoft Teams and Copilot Studio agents (exemplified by Microsoft's CEMEX *Consult HR* pattern). However, rolling out an HR self-service agent directly onto uncurated SharePoint folders or fragmented HRIS articles produces conflicting leave/benefits answers across jurisdictions, unauthorized access to sensitive HR guidance, and unclear handoffs when inquiries cross into grievances, disciplinary matters, or managerial discretion.

Most market offerings treat HR self-service agents as an IT chatbot implementation exercise. Buyers lack a business-owned governance and operating-design pack that aligns HR policy grounding, HRBP escalation thresholds, role-based permissions, and enterprise agent registry controls (`Agent 365` / `OP-ITG-013`) before technical deployment scales.

### Buyer & Tier Snapshot

- **Target Buyers:** CHROs, HR Shared Services Directors, and People Ops Leads (Medium & Large enterprises).
- **Portfolio Tier & Toolkit:** Tier C — Supporting · `Human Resources & Talent Management` (€3,000–€10,000 · 6–8 wks).

## 2. Solution Architecture & Delivery Workflow

`OP-HR-013` delivers a conflict-free 6–8 week Advisory & Governance Pack built on CBa Consult's `Human Resources & Talent Management` toolkit and enterprise AI governance patterns:

1. **Kantar-Style Prerequisite Gate (`OP-ITG-014` Alignment):** Verify that underlying HR policy documents are deduplicated, jurisdiction-tagged, and owner-approved before grounding any Tier-1 agent.
2. **Policy Grounding & Regional Coverage Matrix:** Define approved authoritative sources by country/entity, effective dates, and prohibited shadow documents.
3. **HRBP Escalation & Sensitivity Matrix:** Establish deterministic routing rules separating Tier-1 informational queries from sensitive cases requiring human HRBP intervention (complexity, employee relations, accommodations, managerial discretion).
4. **Permissions & Agent 365 Governance Alignment:** Map role-based access, audit logging, and human accountability controls into `OP-ITG-013` enterprise agent governance standards.
5. **HR Operating-Model Handover:** Deliver SLA definitions, fallback scripts, and governance sign-off criteria for client IT/HRIS teams to execute technical deployment safely.

## 5. Explicit Negative Scope Boundaries & Stage 1b Gate

- **Not an IT Chatbot Build or HRIS Integration Project:** CBa Consult does not code Copilot Studio flows, configure ServiceNow/SAP connectors, or take on ongoing Tier-1/Tier-2 HR support operations.
- **Not Employment-Law Counsel:** Client legal/HR compliance approves statutory policy interpretations and works-council/GDPR employee-consultation obligations.
- **Prerequisite Boundary:** Do not allow a client to deploy an ESS agent onto an uncleaned policy corpus; require `OP-ITG-014` corpus readiness first.

### Stage 1b Gate (`G1b`) Checklist

- [ ] Document Kantar-style prerequisite path (policy corpus cleanup prior to ESS agent launch).
- [ ] Finalize 3-dimension HRBP escalation matrix (complexity / sensitivity / managerial discretion).
- [ ] Align Agent 365 permissioning artifacts with `OP-ITG-013`.

### Core Governance Artifacts Included in Pack

- **Policy Corpus Readiness Scorecard (`OP-ITG-014` Handoff):** Pass/fail criteria for policy freshness, jurisdictional tagging, and canonical owner assignment.
- **3-Tier HRBP Escalation Matrix:** Deterministic triggers routing routine policy Q&A to Tier-1 self-service and sensitive, ambiguous, or discretionary cases to human HRBPs.
- **Role & Permission Boundary Spec:** Controls preventing cross-country policy leakage or unauthorized access to executive/confidential HR files.
- **Agent 365 Governance Alignment Sheet:** Maps HR agent ownership, review cadence, and audit logging into `OP-ITG-013`.
