## Positioning

> For **CISOs, defence-alliance programme leads, and enterprise security architects** who must define **global 24/7 monitoring, regional SOC handoff, and continuous observability** without yet standing up a full MSSP, **Global 24/7 SOC Mesh & Continuous Monitoring Architecture Readiness** is an **audit / architecture** offer backed by **DERA, Alliance Resilience, and ADPA Defence SOC ideation** that delivers **a follow-the-sun SOC mesh blueprint, monitoring & escalation requirements, and investment-grade programme outline** with $0 initial working capital.

## What it is

Translate the ADPA **Defence 24-7 SOC** and **DERA** reference material into a client-ready **operating architecture**: which capabilities stay sovereign per region, how NL / Americas / APAC (or client-defined) SOCs hand off, what “continuous monitoring” must prove for regulators and alliance partners, and how incident evidence feeds `OP-RSK-007` clocks—not staffing a 24/7 CBa SOC.

## Boundaries *(non-negotiable)*

- **Not MSSP / not managed SOC:** CBa designs requirements, RACI, runbooks, and architecture; the client or an MSSP operates shift teams and SIEM.
- **Not legal or classified release authority:** alliance and defence distribution rules stay with the client programme office.
- **Complements, does not replace** `OP-ITG-016` (EARN federation / agent-scale architecture) — cross-link for **Global SOC Mesh** patterns only.

## 7 Pivotal Questions (draft)

1. **Buyer & trigger** — CISO, alliance PMO, critical-infrastructure CISO; trigger = multi-region ops, NIS2/DORA incident rigour, or NATO/EU resilience programme needing documented SOC mesh.  
2. **Wedge** — Fragmented regional SOCs, no follow-the-sun model, monitoring gaps between OT/IT and alliance tenants.  
3. **Deliverable & IP** — SOC mesh reference deck, DERA-aligned control map, continuous monitoring requirement set, tabletop handoff script; sources in [Source-Documents.md](Source-Documents.md).  
4. **Feasibility** — 6–10 weeks advisory at 8–12 hrs/wk; workshops + document synthesis.  
5. **Proof** — Signed architecture baseline + one regional handoff tabletop with evidence checklist.  
6. **Risks** — Scope creep into SOC build/MSP; under-specified alliance data-sharing rules.  
7. **Scale path** — `OP-RSK-007` triage pack, `OP-ITG-018` MIP governance retainer, `OP-ITG-002` ICT governance audit.
