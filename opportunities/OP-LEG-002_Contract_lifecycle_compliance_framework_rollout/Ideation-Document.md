## 1. Problem Context & 2027 Market Trigger

Medium and large organizations frequently govern structured database tables rigorously while leaving unstructured content—commercial contracts, vendor agreements, internal policies, and AI-use clauses—scattered across shared drives, email threads, and ad-hoc Confluence or SharePoint folders. In 2027, overlapping **EU AI Act**, **DORA/NIS2**, **CSRD/ESG**, and **GDPR** retention mandates expose General Counsel, DPOs, and Compliance Officers to audit findings, missed renewal/liability clauses, and shadow-AI contract risk.

- **Core Pain:** Business units bypass legal review or store signed agreements outside a designated repository of record, creating fragmented approval trails and unenforced retention/disposal schedules.
- **2027 Buying Trigger:** Legal and compliance leaders need a practical, software-light governance rollout that standardizes contract review/approval workflows and AI-use clauses across existing repositories before regulatory audits bite.

## 2. Solution Architecture & Delivery Workflow

`OP-LEG-002` is an 8–12 week (8–12 hrs/wk) **Audit / Governance Rollout** backed by the **Risk Management** signature toolkit and DMBOK 2.0 Chapter 9 (Document & Content Management) lifecycle discipline:

1. **Week 1–2 — Content Taxonomy & Repository-of-Record Audit:** Inventory existing contract and policy repositories (e.g., SharePoint, Confluence, shared drives) and designate a single repository of record per document class.
2. **Week 3–5 — Lifecycle & Approval Workflow Design:** Standardize end-to-end lifecycle stages (*create, review, publish, archive, dispose*) with risk-tiered legal escalation thresholds and mandatory AI/data-processing clause checks.
3. **Week 6–9 — Business Unit Rollout & Governance Controls:** Configure access-control rules, retention metadata, and approval checklists within the client's existing collaboration stack without mandating expensive enterprise CLM software first.
4. **Week 10–12 — Compliance Handover & Audit Evidence Pack:** Train business-unit contract owners, validate workflow adherence on live agreements, and deliver an audit-ready governance charter.
