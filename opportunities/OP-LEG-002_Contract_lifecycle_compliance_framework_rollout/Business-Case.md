## 2. Business Need & Cost of Inaction

Mid-to-large enterprises enforce strict controls over structured databases while leaving contracts, policies, and AI-use terms unmanaged across informal drives and chat threads. In 2027, **EU AI Act**, **DORA/NIS2**, and **GDPR** accountability demands turn missing contract trails and unreviewed vendor AI clauses into direct regulatory and commercial liability.

- **Cost of Inaction:** Missed auto-renewals, unapproved vendor data/AI processing exposure, and failed compliance audits due to absent repository-of-record discipline.
- **Source Modeling Benchmark (*Illustrative Pre-Pilot ADPA Source Modeling — Not Realized Client Actuals*):** The ADPA source (`Document_Content_Management_Plan_Template_Lifecycle_Spec.docx`) specifies a structural DMBOK 2.0 Chapter 9 template expansion (extending governance across Confluence/SharePoint with 5 lifecycle stages); it provides architectural governance metrics rather than pre-modeled financial NPV, so client ROI will be baselined per engagement via cycle-time and audit-readiness KPIs.

## 4. Commercial Packaging, $0 Working-Capital Model & Compounding Path

- **Packaging:** Milestone-billed 8–12 week engagement: **€2,500–€4,500** (single-BU contract taxonomy + SharePoint/Confluence repository-of-record setup) up to **€5,500–€8,000** (multi-BU rollout, AI-use clause gates, and compliance audit pack).
- **$0 Working-Capital Execution:** Leverages existing ADPA DMBOK 2.0 Ch. 9 lifecycle templates deployed onto the client's existing M365/SharePoint or Confluence stack.
- **Compounding Path:** Bridges unstructured legal/contract governance with structured data governance, retention (`OP-ITG-010`), and AI/vendor risk (`OP-RSK-004` / `OP-RSK-008`).
