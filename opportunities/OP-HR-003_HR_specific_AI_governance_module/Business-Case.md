## 2. Business Need & Cost of Inaction

HR departments increasingly rely on embedded AI across applicant tracking systems, shift planners, and performance tools without formalized human-override rules, bias review cadences, or employee transparency disclosures. Inaction exposes employers to:

- **EU AI Act High-Risk Compliance Exposure:** Recruitment, promotion, task-allocation, and performance-evaluation AI systems trigger high-risk governance duties; lacking documented human oversight creates regulatory and works-council friction.
- **High-Cost Legal Retainer Leakage:** Without an operational toolkit, HR leaders either ignore AI governance entirely or spend disproportionately on hourly external counsel for routine policy and checklist drafting.
- **Employee Trust & Attrition Risk:** Unexplained automated HR decisions undermine candidate experience and internal retention.

## 4. Commercial Packaging, $0 Working-Capital Model & Compounding Path

- **Productized Economics (€500–€2,000):** Because the core intellectual property already exists in `HR Transformation Toolkit version 2027.docx` §8.2 ("Responsible AI in Human Resources"), packaging the PDF/Toolkit requires only low-effort formatting, checklist refinement, and disclaimer review—maintaining $0 cash outlay.
- **Compounding & Bundle Path:** Designed as an upsell or attach module alongside `OP-LEG-001` (general AI-use policy) and `OP-HR-002` (HR maturity assessment), creating a cohesive HR compliance wedge that builds referenceable proof (`OP-X-004`) and opens doors to broader HR transformation retainers.
