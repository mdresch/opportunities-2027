## 1. Problem Context & 2027 Market Trigger

In small B2B service firms and scaling consultancies, founders or early sales leads often close deals in email, proposals, and CRM notes while delivery teams execute from separate task boards and spreadsheets. When a signed deal closes, critical promises—custom scope exclusions, pricing assumptions, stakeholder expectations, and timeline commitments—fall through the cracks, causing rework, margin erosion, and rocky client onboarding.

- **2027 Market Trigger:** In a cost-conscious 2027 environment, founders and RevOps leads resist hiring full-time operations managers (€60k–€90k+ overhead) and instead favor lightweight, fixed-scope diagnostics that stop delivery leakage immediately.
- **Validation Context:** Qualitative pain is strong in founder-led firms, though hard pre-engagement dollar-leakage figures are scarce (`HYP-2027-001` currently in testing; `HYP-2027-005` and `HYP-2027-006` formulated/blocked pending outreach proof).

## 2. Solution Architecture & Delivery Workflow

`OP-OPS-001` is a 3–4 week (3–5 hrs/wk) **Audit / Diagnostic** built on the **Operating Model & Organization Design** signature toolkit:

1. **Week 1 — Closed-Won Deal Shadowing & Leakage Audit:** Review 5–10 recent closed-won deals from quote through Day-30 delivery to pinpoint dropped scope terms, delayed kickoffs, and unbilled effort.
2. **Week 2 — Single-Page Quote-to-Delivery Handoff Map:** Co-design an explicit end-to-end handoff workflow defining trigger events, mandatory deal-desk fields, owner sign-offs, and SLA timers between Sales/Founder and Delivery.
3. **Week 3 — Lightweight Tooling & Intake Templates:** Configure zero-cost checklists and intake templates inside the client's existing CRM/PM stack (no new enterprise PSA purchase required).
4. **Week 4 — Live Deal Dry-Run & Baseline Metrics:** Run the new handoff protocol on one live signed deal, measure kickoff cycle-time improvement, and document proof (`OP-X-004`).

## 5. Explicit Negative Scope Boundaries & Stage 1b Gate

- **No Full ERP/PSA Software Implementation:** `OP-OPS-001` maps the handoff and configures lightweight checklists in existing tools; full CRM migrations or custom PSA builds are excluded.
- **No Cold-Pipeline Sales Coaching:** Scope begins at quote/proposal handoff and ends at Day-30 delivery stabilization; top-of-funnel lead generation is out of scope.
- **Evidence Discipline:** Because pre-engagement dollar-leakage metrics are rarely tracked in Small-tier firms, discovery must capture baseline kickoff delay days and rework hours qualitatively and cleanly.
