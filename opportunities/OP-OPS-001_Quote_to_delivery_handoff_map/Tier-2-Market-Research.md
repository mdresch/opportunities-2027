## 1. The Commercial Wedge: Where Revenue & Margin Leak Between Sales and Delivery
In B2B professional services and mid-market SaaS/project businesses (25–500 staff), deals close in CRM (HubSpot/Salesforce/Dynamics) and are thrown over the wall to Delivery/Onboarding via email or slide decks:
- **Unpriced Scope Creep:** Verbal sales promises never make it into the SOW or delivery kickoff, eroding gross margin before Day 30.
- **90-Day Onboarding Friction (`OP-CX-001`):** Clients repeat discovery questions to delivery teams, delaying time-to-first-value and triggering early churn.
- **Billing & Revenue Recognition Breaks:** SOW milestones, rate cards, and change orders do not match ERP/invoicing structures.
