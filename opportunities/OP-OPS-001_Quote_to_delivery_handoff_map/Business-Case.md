## 2. Business Need & Cost of Inaction

Founder-led Small B2B service firms lose margin and client trust in the gap between closed-won signature and project kickoff. Unrecorded scope caveats, missing delivery prerequisites, and slow handoffs force teams into unbilled rework while delaying time-to-value. Yet Small-tier buyers cannot justify a €60k–€90k+ full-time Operations Manager hire in 2027.

- **Cost of Inaction:** Persistent delivery rework, founder bottlenecking on post-sale firefighting, and preventable Day-30 client churn.
- **Evidence Basis:** *No ADPA `.docx` ROI modeling is assigned to `OP-OPS-001`.* Qualitative pain is confirmed in [Tier-2-Market-Research.md](Tier-2-Market-Research.md), while hard pre-engagement dollar-leakage figures remain scarce (`HYP-2027-001` currently under active testing in [Wave-1-Outreach-Evidence-Log.md](Wave-1-Outreach-Evidence-Log.md)).

## 3. Strategic Options Analysis (BABOK v3 Aligned)

| Option | Description | Commercial Viability | Strategic Alignment |

| **Option B: Fixed-Scope Handoff Map Sprint (Recommended)** | 3–4 wk lightweight diagnostic (€500–€1,500) producing handoff map, checklists, and live dry-run | Low effort (3–5 hrs/wk); $0 capex; pairs naturally with `OP-CX-001` | Ideal low-friction Tier C cash/proof engine |
| **Option C: Full RevOps / PSA Systems Overhaul** | Re-architect CRM/PSA stack end-to-end | Priced out of Small-tier budget; high implementation drag | Violates lightweight fixed-scope diagnostic thesis |

## 4. Commercial Packaging, $0 Working-Capital Model & Compounding Path

- **Packaging:** Fixed-fee **€500–€1,500** 3–4 week sprint (100% upfront or 50/50 split), capped at 12–20 total Director hours to preserve effective hourly yield.
- **$0 Working-Capital Execution:** Executed through [Pilot-Charter.md](Pilot-Charter.md) using existing CBa Consult process-mapping templates inside the client's existing CRM, Notion, Asana, or Jira setup.
- **Compounding Path:** Bundles seamlessly with `OP-CX-001` (Quote-to-Onboarding) to lift deal size while generating fast, publishable before/after kickoff-cycle proof (`OP-X-004`).
