## 2. Business Need & Cost of Inaction

The source ADPA document (`Asset Flipper (Arbitrage).docx`) describes a local refurbished-goods arbitrage venture via platforms like Marktplaats aimed at capturing resale spread while extending product lifecycles. In that `.docx` digest, target metrics include achieving a 20% profit margin on resold items and building a repeat customer base of 500+ active buyers within 12 months (*Illustrative Pre-Pilot ADPA Source Modeling — Not Realized Client Actuals*). For CBa Consult in 2027, the **cost of inaction is zero**—and the opportunity cost of acting would be severe, as physical refurbishment trading diverts Director hours away from high-margin Tier A B2B AI/context governance engagements.
