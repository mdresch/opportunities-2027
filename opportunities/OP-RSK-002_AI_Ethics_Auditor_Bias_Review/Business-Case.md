## 2. Business Need & Cost of Inaction

High-stakes AI decisions in recruitment, credit underwriting, and customer tiering carry acute regulatory, legal, and brand penalties when disparate impact or proxy bias goes unchecked:

- **Regulatory & Litigation Exposure:** Under EU AI Act Annex III (Dec 2027), undocumented bias in HR or financial-scoring models exposes firms to enforcement fines, mandatory model suspension, and discrimination claims.
- **Blind Audit Readiness:** Internal teams lack independent test documentation required by risk committees, external auditors, or enterprise B2B procurement reviews.
- **Source Modeling Caveat:** *Illustrative Pre-Pilot ADPA Source Modeling — Not Realized Client Actuals:* `AI Ethics Auditor Bias Review Service.docx` targets a `30%` reduction in model bias metrics within 12 months, `95%` client satisfaction, `100%` regulatory compliance, and `3+` expert approvals. Those targets reflect 2026 draft aspirations—**not** realized client audit outcomes.
