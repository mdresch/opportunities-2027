## 7 Pivotal Questions

1. **Buyer & 2027 Trigger** — CIO, CTO, and Head of Digital Transformation. **Microsoft Ignite 2026** introduced the *Microsoft IQ* context layer, *Copilot Workspaces*, *Azure Catalyst*, and flexible *Copilot Studio* spending guardrails—just as Forrester warns **usage-based AI pricing** will force enterprises to cut ~1/3 of unmeasured AI initiatives in 2027.
2. **The Wedge (Pain)** — Consumption-based Copilot Studio and agent deployments create unpredictable monthly bills ("bill shock") while pulling from messy, ungoverned context—risking the >40% agentic AI cancellation rate Gartner predicts for 2027.
3. **Deliverable & Existing IP** — Bridges CBa Consult's **`Power Apps Center of Excellence.docx`** (`OP-ITG-007`), **`Power Platform Solutions.docx`** (`OP-DTX-003`), and **ADPA/ECS context governance (`OP-ITG-014`)** into a practical **Microsoft IQ + Copilot Studio FinOps Playbook**.
4. **Sweat-Equity Feasibility** — 6–8 weeks at 6–10 hrs/week (Medium effort), \$0 cash outlay; pilots cleanly on 1–2 business-unit Copilot Studio use cases before org-wide enablement.
5. **Outcome & Proof** — Hard consumption/throttling caps live in Power Platform Admin Center, curated *Work IQ* data sources connected without oversharing, and measurable hours-saved vs. token-cost ROI per agent.
6. **Boundaries & Risk** — Consumption-based AI pricing surprises if spending caps aren't enforced early; keep scope on **governance, context wiring, and FinOps guardrails** rather than open-ended custom software builds.
7. **Compounding / Scale Path** — Connects `OP-ITG-007` (Power Apps CoE) and `OP-DTX-003` (Power Platform Solutions) upward into Tier A **`OP-ITG-014`** (Knowledge Intelligence & Context Governance) and **`OP-ITG-013`** (Enterprise AI Agent Governance).
