## 2. Business Need & Cost of Inaction

Small & Mid B2B firms frequently spend heavily on outbound or paid campaigns while sending prospects to poorly positioned landing pages with weak search visibility and unclear value propositions. Without persona-aligned copy, inbound conversion stalls and sales cycles lengthen.

*Illustrative Pre-Pilot ADPA Source Modeling — Not Realized Client Actuals:* The assigned ADPA file (`SEO-Focused Copywriting (Contract).docx`) cites industry benchmarks (71% of B2B buyers starting with generic search; 60% citing SEO as top lead channel) alongside aspirational pre-pilot targets (top-10 keyword ranking within 6 months, +20% inbound leads, 4% engagement rate, <60% bounce rate, 12 articles/quarter, and 30% faster content production). These figures represent *Illustrative Pre-Pilot ADPA Source Modeling — Not Realized Client Actuals* and cannot be contracted as guaranteed outcomes.

## 4. Commercial Packaging, $0 Working-Capital Model & Compounding Path

- **Commercial Scope Cap:** Package as a fixed-deliverable sprint (e.g., **€800–€1,500** for 1 flagship landing page + 2 SEO briefs, or **€2,000–€3,000** for 3 core web pages + keyword/persona map) over 4–8 weeks with max 2 revision rounds.
- **`$0` Working-Capital Model:** Executed using existing CBa messaging frameworks and standard office/keyword research tools without paid freelance subcontractors or SaaS retainers.
- **Success KPI Alignment:** Track pre/post CTR, bounce rate, and conversion-goal baseline rather than unguaranteed SERP rank promises.
- **Compounding Path:** Serves as a tangible quick-win artifact generating early cash flow, **`OP-X-004`** case-study proof, and a direct upsell bridge into **`OP-MKT-004` (CRO)**.
