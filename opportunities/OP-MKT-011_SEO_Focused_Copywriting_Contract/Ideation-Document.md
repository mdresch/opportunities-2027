## 1. Problem Context & 2027 Market Trigger

Founders, CMOs, and B2B Marketing Leads at Small & Mid firms face a familiar 2027 tension: organic search remains a primary B2B discovery channel, yet generic AI-generated blog posts fail to rank or convert niche buyers, while full-service marketing retainers carry high fixed overhead. Buyers need persona-driven, keyword-targeted web pages, landing pages, and thought-leadership briefs that combine subject-matter credibility with conversion discipline under a predictable, low-risk fixed contract.

For CBa Consult, `OP-MKT-011` is classified as **Tier C — Supporting**: a lightweight 4–8 week fixed-fee wedge (€800–€3,000) usable for fast cash flow, advisory relationship building, or post-repositioning content refresh alongside Conversion Rate Optimization (`OP-MKT-004`).

### Buyer & Tier Snapshot

- **Target Buyers:** Founders, CMOs, and B2B Marketing Leads (Small & Mid segment).
- **Portfolio Tier & Toolkit:** Tier C — Supporting · `Sales, Marketing & Communication Strategy` (€800–€3,000 · 4–8 wks).

## 2. Solution Architecture & Delivery Workflow

Built on CBa Consult's `Sales, Marketing & Communication Strategy` toolkit, delivery runs as a lean 5-step sprint with zero external contractor spend:

1. **Persona & Search-Intent Mapping:** Align 1–2 priority ICP personas with commercial-intent B2B keyword clusters and buyer objections.
2. **Standardized Content & Performance Briefs:** Lock H1–H3 structure, proof points, internal links, and target conversion CTAs prior to drafting.
3. **Conversion-Oriented Copywriting:** Draft core landing pages and/or targeted B2B articles combining clear positioning with SEO structure and editorial review.
4. **Structured Review & Revision Cap:** Execute up to two consolidated stakeholder feedback rounds to prevent scope creep and preserve effective hourly yield.
5. **Handover & Measurement Baseline:** Deliver ready-to-publish copy plus a simple tracking sheet (impressions, CTR, bounce rate, conversion events) to feed `OP-MKT-004` (CRO).
