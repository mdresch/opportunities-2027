# Ideation Document — `OP-SLS-002`: Sales Operations Framework

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Governance & SSOT Callout:** This Stage 1b Ideation Document adheres to [Tailored Hybrid SIDPA Governance](../../docs/governance/Tailored-Hybrid-SIDPA-Governance.md) and aligns with [inventory-of-ideas.md](../../inventory-of-ideas.md) and [Ideation-Spark.md](Ideation-Spark.md). No client pilot actuals or forecast accuracy gains are claimed prior to Stage 4 execution.

## 1. Problem Context & 2027 Market Trigger

Medium and large B2B sales organizations scaling across regions or product lines frequently suffer from inconsistent territory rules, politicized quota allocation, polluted CRM pipelines, and unreliable revenue forecasts. In 2027, CROs, Founders, and Sales/RevOps Leads require disciplined commercial governance to protect margin and forecast predictability without committing to multi-quarter RevOps consultancy transformations.

- **Target Buyer:** Founders, CROs, and Sales/RevOps Leads (**Medium & Large** tier).
- **Core Wedge Pain:** Divergent stage definitions across teams, stale CRM opportunities, and uncalibrated territory/quota models that undermine board-level revenue forecasting.
- **2027 Market Trigger:** Heightened board scrutiny on pipeline conversion, CAC payback, and clean revenue governance across distributed sales teams.

## 2. Solution Architecture & Delivery Workflow

`OP-SLS-002` is an **Audit / Governance Rollout** built on the **Sales, Marketing & Communication Strategy** signature toolkit, scoped for **8–12 weeks** (8–12 hrs/wk, High effort) with **$0 initial working capital**.

1. **Weeks 1–3 — CRM & Pipeline Audit:** Assess current territory assignments, quota methodology, stage-exit criteria, and CRM data hygiene across regions/teams.
2. **Weeks 4–6 — Governance & Territory Design:** Establish standardized territory/quota rules, canonical pipeline stages, and a repeatable CRM data-quality governance checklist.
3. **Weeks 7–9 — Forecasting Cadence Rollout:** Implement weekly forecast inspection rituals, commit-category definitions, and executive variance dashboards.
4. **Weeks 10–12 — Enablement & Handover:** Train regional sales managers on the new governance charter and document before/after forecast hygiene baselines.

## 5. Explicit Negative Scope Boundaries & Stage 1b Gate

Because `OP-SLS-002` carries a High sweat-equity effort profile (8–12 wks @ 8–12 hrs/wk), scope discipline is mandatory:

- **No Full CRM Re-Platforming:** Excludes migrating from Salesforce to HubSpot (or vice versa) or writing custom Apex/API integrations.
- **No Compensation Legal Drafting:** Provides quota/territory logic and governance principles, not legally binding commission plan contracts.
- **No Outsourced SDR / Sales Execution:** CBa Consult installs the governance framework rather than running live outbound sales cadences.
