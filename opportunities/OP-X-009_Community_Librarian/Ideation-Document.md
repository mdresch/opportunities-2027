## 1. Problem Context & 2027 Market Trigger

Niche B2B communities, founder networks, and practitioner groups hosted on Discord, Slack, or Circle frequently suffer from the "graveyard channel" syndrome: initial sign-ups spike, then threads become noisy, valuable institutional answers disappear in chat scrollback, and founders burn out trying to moderate discussions while struggling to monetize engagement.

**2027 Strategic Drivers & Buyer Urgency:**
- **Buyer Persona:** Founders, Managing Partners, and Mid-Market Ecosystem Leaders running niche practitioner communities (Small segment).
- **Signal Over AI Noise:** As public social feeds saturate with synthetic content in 2027, high-trust **curated micro-communities and paid practitioner clinics** gain renewed commercial value.
- **Sustainable Curation Model:** Community owners need structured knowledge libraries and predictable, time-boxed advisory office hours rather than uncompensated 24/7 chat policing.

## 2. Solution Architecture & Delivery Workflow

`OP-X-009` refines `Community Librarian.docx` into a capital-light **6–8 week Group Clinic & Community Curation Offer** aligned to CBa Consult's Management Consulting toolkit and `OP-X-003` group-clinic funnel:

1. **Community Audit & Taxonomy Setup (Weeks 1–2):** Restructure channels (Discord/Slack/Circle), establish code-of-conduct & moderation rules, and design a searchable **Knowledge Library** indexing recurring answers and templates.
2. **Engagement & Office-Hours Cadence (Weeks 3–4):** Launch structured weekly prompts, peer-review rituals, and fixed-time bi-weekly expert office hours that replace ad-hoc reactive moderation.
3. **Monetization & Tier Architecture (Weeks 5–6):** Configure membership tiers, partner/sponsor guidelines, and clear upgrade bridges from group membership into paid `OP-X-003` clinics or bespoke advisory diagnostics.
4. **Retention Scorecard & Handoff (Weeks 7–8):** Establish active-member, question-resolution, and retention KPIs with lightweight automation templates so community owners sustain operations without full-time overhead.

**Core Sprint Deliverables:**
- Channel Taxonomy, Moderation Charter & Indexed Knowledge Library
- Bi-Weekly Office-Hours & Engagement Ritual Playbook
- Membership Tier Architecture & `OP-X-003` Clinic Conversion Bridge

## 5. Explicit Negative Scope Boundaries & Stage 1b Gate

- **No 24/7 Unbounded Moderation Liability:** Under CBa Consult's **10–15 hrs/week** portfolio capacity cap, `OP-X-009` cannot commit to round-the-clock live chat policing; moderation is bounded to scheduled curation windows, community guidelines, and automated platform filters.
- **No Custom Bot/Platform Software Engineering:** Use native Discord/Slack/Circle tooling; do not build bespoke community web apps or custom AI moderation bots inside `oppurtunities-2027`.
- **No Revenue-Target Guarantees:** Client owns member acquisition and pricing; CBa Consult guarantees structured library setup, governance, and scheduled office hours.
- **Stage 1b (`G1b`) Positioning:** Tier C Supporting offer (Q2 2027); remains parked behind Tier A Core 5 execution unless deployed selectively as a low-effort lead-nurture clinic for CBa Consult's target CFO/CIO ecosystem.
