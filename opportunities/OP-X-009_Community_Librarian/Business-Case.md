## 2. Business Need & Cost of Inaction

Niche professional communities on Discord, Slack, or Circle frequently lose retention within 90 days because institutional answers vanish in chat streams and founders lack time to curate content or structure paid office hours. Without an organized knowledge library and repeatable engagement cadence, community-led growth stalls.

**Cost of Inaction & ADPA Source Baseline:**
- **For Clients:** Uncurated channels turn into noisy chatrooms that churn paying members and fail to convert participants into higher-value advisory engagements.
- **For CBa Consult:** Repackaging `OP-X-009` as a bounded setup & office-hours sprint creates a clean ecosystem feeder for `OP-X-003` clinics without falling into low-leverage daily chat moderation.
- **ADPA Modeling Disclaimer:** The assigned ADPA source (`Community Librarian.docx`) modeled an outsourced community-management business targeting 3–5 pilot communities with 100% pilot retention, 80% satisfaction, 20% quarterly active-member growth, $5,000/month recurring income per community, and 90% client retention. *Illustrative Pre-Pilot ADPA Source Modeling — Not Realized Client Actuals:* those recurring-revenue and growth figures are legacy speculative targets in the source `.docx` and are **not** realized CBa Consult actuals or guaranteed client returns.

## 4. Commercial Packaging, $0 Working-Capital Model & Compounding Path

- **Zero-Capital Model:** Client covers their native Discord/Slack/Circle subscription; CBa Consult contributes Management Consulting taxonomy frameworks, library templates, and structured facilitation ($0 cash outlay).
- **Pricing Anchor:** **€500–€2,500** fixed fee for a 6–8 week Community Knowledge-Library & Office-Hours Setup Sprint (50% kickoff / 50% taxonomy, library, and monetization playbook handoff).
- **Measurable Outcome KPI:** Complete channel taxonomy & indexed FAQ/template library live, plus >=2 structured office-hours sessions run with baseline member satisfaction survey.
- **Compounding Path:** Converts recurring community members into higher-margin **`OP-X-003`** group advisory clinics and builds ecosystem case-study proof (`OP-X-004`) without consuming full-time moderation bandwidth.
