## 2. Business Need & Cost of Inaction

- **Buyer Pain:** External auditors deploying agentic platforms (such as KPMG Clara intelligence / KCi in 2026–2027) run full-population testing and AI-drafted workpapers, exposing outdated manual close narratives during live fieldwork.
- **Cost of Inaction:** Unprepared finance teams suffer last-minute audit queries, control-deficiency findings on AI/automated journals, prolonged close-to-signoff cycles, and costly emergency remediation billed at external-auditor escalation rates.

## 4. Commercial Packaging, $0 Working-Capital Model & Compounding Path

- **Zero Working-Capital Mechanics:** Delivered entirely via principal finance/controls expertise and existing Financial Modeling, Planning & Analysis templates—€0 upfront software or license cost.
- **Tiered Packaging:** Standard Diagnostic (€2,500–€4,500 for single-entity close & payroll scope, 3 weeks) and Extended Multi-Cycle Readiness (€5,000–€7,500 including population-testing lineage & remediation roadmap, 5 weeks).
- **Compounding Path:** Designed as a Tier B pull wedge that directly feeds remediation work into `OP-FIN-012`, bundles with `OP-RSK-004` ("Assurance for AI Bridge"), and creates annual pre-audit recurring demand.
