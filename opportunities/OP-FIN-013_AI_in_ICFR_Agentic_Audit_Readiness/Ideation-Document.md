# Ideation Document — `OP-FIN-013`: AI-in-ICFR & Agentic Audit Readiness

## 1. Problem Context & 2027 Market Trigger

- **Target Buyer:** CFOs, Corporate Controllers, and Chief Audit Executives (CAEs) in Small & Mid private companies whose external auditors are deploying agentic audit platforms.
- **2027 Market Trigger:** Major audit networks are rolling out agentic audit tooling (such as KPMG Clara intelligence / KCi in 2026–2027) capable of full-population testing and AI-drafted audit workpapers.
- **The Structural Friction:** While external auditors compress audit cycles using autonomous analytics and agents, client Internal Control over Financial Reporting (ICFR) narratives still assume manual close, sampling-based testing, and human-only journal approvals.
- **Independence Gap:** Where AI or workflow automation touches close, payroll, revenue recognition, and journal entries, human-vs-agent control ownership blurs. Conflict-sensitive entities cannot rely on the same audit firm for both independent pre-audit remediation and statutory audit sign-off.

## 2. Solution Architecture & Delivery Workflow

Built on CBa Consult's **Financial Modeling, Planning & Analysis** Signature Toolkit, `OP-FIN-013` is delivered as a 3–5 week (5–10 hrs/wk) fixed-scope pre-assurance diagnostic:

1. **Week 1 — AI & Automation Touchpoint Mapping:** Inventory every AI, RPA, script, or automated rule touching financial close (`OP-FIN-009` linkage), payroll/Cost-of-Sales (`OP-FIN-010`), billing, reconciliations, and accounting estimates.
2. **Week 2 — Control Narrative Modernization:** Rewrite ICFR control narratives to explicitly define human-in-the-loop (HITL) review points, exception thresholds, and agent-assisted processing boundaries in auditor-facing language.
3. **Week 3 — Population-Testing Readiness:** Assess data lineage, completeness/accuracy (IPE) controls, and exception routing where agents pre-process transactions prior to full-population external audit ingestion.
4. **Weeks 4–5 — Remediation Register & Executive Briefing:** Deliver a prioritized gap list feeding `OP-FIN-012` and `OP-RSK-004`, plus a CFO/CAE management briefing detailing next-cycle audit expectations and evidence-retention standards.
