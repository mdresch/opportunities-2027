## Positioning

> For **CFOs, controllers, and chief audit executives** whose external auditors are adopting **agentic audit tools** (full populations, AI-drafted workpapers) while **client control narratives still assume manual close**, **AI-in-ICFR & Agentic Audit Readiness** is a **pre-assurance diagnostic** that maps **where AI touches the finance cycle** and delivers **audit-ready control language and evidence trails**—without CBa Consult performing the external audit.

## The Problem (Before Solution)

- Auditors compress cycles with **agents and analytics**; clients discover gaps **during** fieldwork.
- AI/automation on **close, payroll, revenue, journals** blurs **human vs agent-assisted** control ownership.
- Same firm may sell audit and advisory — **independent prep** has value for conflict-sensitive entities.

## What the Client Is Entitled To (Deliverables)

**Duration:** 3–5 weeks · **Fee band:** €2,500–€7,500

1. **AI/automation touchpoint map** on finance cycle: close (`OP-FIN-009`), payroll/CoS (`OP-FIN-010`), billing, reconciliations, estimates.
2. **Updated control narratives** for human-in-the-loop / agent-assisted processing (auditor-facing wording).
3. **Population-testing readiness** — lineage, completeness, exception routing where agents pre-process transactions.
4. **Gap list vs agentic audit** — prioritised remediation register (feeds `OP-FIN-012`, `OP-RSK-004`).
5. **CFO/CAE management briefing** — what changes next audit cycle; what evidence to retain.
