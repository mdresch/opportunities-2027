## 1. Problem Context & 2027 Market Trigger

Enterprise and mid-market B2B organizations frequently lose momentum during the first 90 days after contract signature. Closed-won deals stall in delivery because sales-to-delivery handoffs lack objective client-readiness scoring, stakeholder expectations diverge, and onboarding teams spend weeks manually reviewing scattered project initiation artifacts.

**2027 Strategic Drivers & Buyer Urgency:**
- **Buyer Persona:** COOs, VPs of Customer Success, and Enterprise Delivery Leaders at Medium & Large B2B organizations.
- **NRR Over New-Logo Volume:** Gartner frames 2027 customer onboarding as a primary Net Revenue Retention (NRR) growth engine, shifting executive KPIs from speed-to-close to **Day-1-to-First-Value (TTFV)** and 90-day retention.
- **Over-Automation Backlash:** Forrester's 2027 CX predictions warn that low-empathy, fully automated onboarding flows have triggered rising early churn (~20% CX staffing rebound), creating urgent demand for structured **human-plus-AI governance checkpoints**.

## 2. Solution Architecture & Delivery Workflow

`OP-CX-002` distills the comprehensive 35-document UniCorn COAS PMBOK/BABOK/DMBOK blueprint into a lean, modular **Client Onboarding Assessment & Governance System** delivered via a 6–8 week fixed-scope sprint ([Delivery-Playbook-and-SOW.md](Delivery-Playbook-and-SOW.md)):

1. **Cohort Audit & Maturity Baseline (Weeks 1–2):** Score current onboarding operations against the 25-control [COAS-Onboarding-Maturity-Scorecard.md](COAS-Onboarding-Maturity-Scorecard.md) and audit 5–10 recent client onboardings for TTFV delays, handoff gaps, and escalation triggers.
2. **Pre-Kickoff Readiness & Persona Gate (Weeks 3–4):** Establish a mandatory Sales-to-Delivery acceptance gate, Client Readiness Scorecard (Technical, Data, Organizational), and Stakeholder Persona Matrix derived from the COAS `User_Personas` and `Stakeholder_Register` IP.
3. **TTFV Playbook & Human+AI Cadence (Weeks 5–6):** Deploy a 30/60/90-day Mutual Success Plan template, Day-14/45/90 pulse checkpoints, and balanced human-led vs. AI-assisted document-triage workflows.
4. **Handoff & Optional ADPA Automation Path (Weeks 7–8):** Transfer the governance cadence and scorecards to the client CS/PMO lead, with optional future graduation into an automated ADPA document-benchmarking module (`OP-ITG-019`) once Track A advisory value is proven.

**Core Pilot Deliverables:**
- COAS Maturity Baseline & P0 Friction Report
- Pre-Kickoff Client Readiness & Persona Gate Pack
- Tiered 30/60/90-Day Mutual Success Plan & Human+AI Playbook
