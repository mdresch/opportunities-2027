# Tier-2 Market Research & Competitive Validation — `OP-CX-002`: ADPA Client Onboarding Assessment System (UniCorn COAS)

## 1. Market Demand & 2027 Strategic Triggers

In 2027, B2B SaaS, IT services, and regulated mid-market enterprises face a structural shift in growth economics:
1. **Net Revenue Retention (NRR) Over New-Logo Volume:** Gartner frames **2027 customer onboarding as a primary NRR growth engine**, shifting executive KPIs from *speed-to-close* to **Day-1-to-First-Value** and 90-day retention.
2. **The Over-Automation Backlash:** Forrester's 2027 CX predictions warn that over-automated, low-empathy digital onboarding has driven rising early churn (~20% CX staffing rebound), creating urgent demand for structured **human-plus-AI onboarding checkpoints**.
3. **Broken Sales-to-Delivery Handoffs:** Deals closed in CRM frequently stall in the first 90 days because client technical readiness, stakeholder alignment, and success criteria were never scored before kickoff.

## 2. Proprietary IP Moat (Already Built — Zero Working Capital)

`OP-CX-002` distills CBa Consult's existing **33-document UniCorn COAS PMBOK/BABOK & Persona Suite** into a lean, mid-market-ready **Onboarding Maturity Diagnostic & Governance Layer** — eliminating heavy enterprise PMBOK ceremony while retaining full scoring rigor.

- **Direct Entry Funnel:** Rides directly behind Wave 1 Priority #1 (**`OP-OPS-001` Quote-to-Delivery Handoff** + **`OP-CX-001` 90-Day Onboarding Friction Audit**). Clients who prove handoff friction in a 3–4 week wedge (`€1,500–€3,500`) graduate cleanly into `OP-CX-002` (`€2,500–€8,000`).
