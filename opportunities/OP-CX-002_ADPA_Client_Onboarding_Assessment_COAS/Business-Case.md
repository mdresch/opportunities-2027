## 2. Business Need & Cost of Inaction

Manual, subjective onboarding reviews create multi-week bottlenecks between contract close and first value realization. When sales-to-delivery handoffs lack readiness gates, client implementations stall in the first 90 days, inflating delivery escalations and eroding Net Revenue Retention (NRR).

**Cost of Inaction & ADPA Source Baseline:**
- **For Clients:** Unchecked handoff gaps delay Time-to-First-Value (TTFV), burn CS capacity on avoidable escalations, and depress expansion NRR.
- **For CBa Consult:** Leaving the 35-document UniCorn COAS suite archived wastes mature IP, while prematurely coding a SaaS portal would breach the $0 working-capital and 10–15 hrs/week limits.
- **ADPA Modeling Disclaimer:** The assigned ADPA business case (`Business_Case_Template_9d0de704.docx`, Oct 2025) models an automated "Project Readiness as a Service" platform projecting a 5-year ROI of 312.5%, an NPV (@ 8%) of $2,851,674, and "minutes vs. weeks" assessment turnaround. *Illustrative Pre-Pilot ADPA Source Modeling — Not Realized Client Actuals:* those enterprise software projections assume multi-year platform commercialization and are **not** used as CBa Consult Pilot #1 revenue commitments.
