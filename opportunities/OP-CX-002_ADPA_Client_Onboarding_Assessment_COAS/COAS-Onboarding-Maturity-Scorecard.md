# COAS Client Onboarding Maturity Scorecard (`OP-CX-002`)

Score each of the **25 diagnostic controls** (`1` to `5`) using verifiable operational evidence (CRM handoff records, kickoff decks, project trackers, CSAT/NPS surveys, 90-day churn logs):

| Score | Maturity Level | Evidence Standard |
| :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | **Ad Hoc / Heroic** | Every client onboarding reinvented from scratch; sales promises lost in email; frequent Day-30 escalations. |
| **2** | **Checklist-Driven** | Generic task checklist exists, but no client readiness scoring, persona tailoring, or formal handoff gates. |
| **3** | **Standardized Playbook** | Defined Sales→Delivery handoff gate, clear kickoff roles, and baseline Time-to-First-Value tracking. |
| **4** | **Measured & Predictive** | Pre-kickoff Client Readiness Scorecards, automated risk triggers, balanced human+AI touchpoints, NRR attribution. |
| **5** | **Optimized NRR Engine** | Continuous COAS telemetry (ADPA-backed), proactive expansion readiness, <5% preventable 90-day churn. |

## Top-10 P0 Client Onboarding Red-Flag Triage
1. **No formal Sales→Delivery handoff gate** — Delivery learns about deals after signature.
2. **Unassessed client data/IT readiness** causing multi-week stalls after kickoff.
3. **No named client Executive Sponsor** engaged past contract signature.
4. **Clients repeating discovery answers** to 3 different internal teams.
5. **No measurable definition of "First Value"** agreed in Week 1.
6. **Over-automated ticket-only onboarding** driving silent disengagement in Days 1–30.
7. **Silent scope creep** during onboarding with zero change-control tracking.
8. **Zero visibility into onboarding hours/margin** per deal.
9. **No Day-14 / Day-45 pulse check** — churn risks discovered only at renewal.
10. **No transition sign-off** — accounts linger in "permanent onboarding" past Day 90.
