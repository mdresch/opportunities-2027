## Positioning

> For **CISOs, Chief Risk Officers, CIOs, and Legal/Procurement Leads** at EU financial entities and NIS2-covered enterprises who struggle with **static vendor spreadsheets, unmapped 4th-party/cloud concentration risks, and missing DORA Article 28+ Register of Information (RoI) & exit plans**, **DORA / NIS2 Third-Party ICT Risk & RoI Governance** is a **fixed-scope Pre-Assurance & Governance Diagnostic** backed by **CBa Consult's ICT Governance & Sovereignty frameworks** that delivers **an inspection-ready Register of Information, 4th-party concentration map, contractual audit-rights gap list, and actionable cloud exit blueprints** with \$0 initial working capital.

## The Problem (Before Solution)

Early 2025–2026 supervisory inspections by the ESAs (EBA, EIOPA, ESMA), DNB, and national NIS2 authorities consistently flag **Third-Party ICT Risk** as the **#1 compliance breakdown**:
- **Static Onboarding-Only Vendor Lists:** Organizations check vendors once at procurement and never maintain a live, regulator-submittable **Register of Information (RoI)** covering critical or important functions (CIFs).
- **Blindness to 4th-Party & Hyperscaler Concentration:** Teams cannot see when 15 different SaaS and AI vendors all depend on the same non-EEA cloud or LLM sub-processor — creating systemic concentration risk.
- **Paper-Only Exit Strategies:** Contracts lack DORA-mandated audit/access clauses and teams have zero tested exit plans for critical SaaS/cloud providers.

## What It Is

A fixed-scope, **conflict-free** 4–8 week assessment and governance pack that turns scattered vendor spreadsheets into an inspection-ready DORA Art. 28+ / NIS2 supply-chain control system:
1. **DORA-Compliant Register of Information (RoI) Architecture:** Map critical/important functions to ICT & AI suppliers, sub-contractors (4th parties), and data-residency locations.
2. **Concentration & Contractual Gap Assessment:** Identify single-point-of-failure cloud/AI dependencies and missing DORA/NIS2 contractual clauses (audit rights, SLA termination triggers, incident cooperation).
3. **Actionable Exit-Strategy & Board Oversight Pack:** Practical exit/portability blueprints for top 3–5 critical ICT providers plus a board-level oversight summary addressing NIS2/DORA management-body accountability.
