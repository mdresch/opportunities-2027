## 1. Problem Context & 2027 Market Trigger

Early 2025–2026 supervisory inspections by the European Supervisory Authorities (EBA, EIOPA, ESMA), De Nederlandsche Bank (DNB), and national NIS2 authorities consistently flag **Third-Party ICT Risk** as the **#1 compliance breakdown** across regulated EU financial entities and NIS2-covered enterprises (18 critical sectors). Management bodies now face explicit accountability for three recurring gaps:

1. **Static Onboarding-Only Vendor Lists:** Procurement checks suppliers once at contract signing and lacks a live, regulator-submittable **DORA Article 28+ Register of Information (RoI)** linking vendors to Critical or Important Functions (CIFs).
2. **4th-Party & Hyperscaler Concentration Blindness:** Firms cannot see when 15 distinct SaaS and AI vendors rely on the same non-EEA cloud or LLM sub-processor.
3. **Paper-Only Exit Strategies:** Vendor contracts omit mandatory DORA audit/access/termination clauses, and teams hold zero tested exit or portability blueprints for critical providers.

## 2. Solution Architecture & Delivery Workflow

`OP-RSK-006` is a conflict-free 4–8 week diagnostic delivered on existing client spreadsheets, SharePoint, or GRC tools at `` `$0` `` new software outlay:

1. **Critical/Important Function (CIF) & RoI Baseline:** Map CIFs to ICT/AI suppliers, 4th-party sub-processors, contractual hierarchy, and data-residency locations in an ESA-aligned DORA Art. 28+ / NIS2 Register of Information structure.
2. **Concentration & Contractual Gap Assessment:** Identify single-point-of-failure cloud/AI sub-processor dependencies and flag missing DORA/NIS2 contractual provisions (audit rights, SLA termination triggers, incident-cooperation duties).
3. **Top-5 Critical Vendor Exit Blueprints:** Author practical exit, data-portability, and transition-runbook blueprints for the client's 3–5 highest-criticality ICT providers.
4. **Management-Body Oversight Pack:** Synthesize residual concentration risk and remediation priorities for Board / Risk Committee sign-off.
