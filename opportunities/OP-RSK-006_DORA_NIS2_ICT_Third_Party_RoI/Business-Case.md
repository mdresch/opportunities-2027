## 2. Business Need & Cost of Inaction

Under active ESA/DNB DORA reviews and national NIS2 enforcement, failing third-party ICT governance exposes financial institutions and essential/important entities to formal supervisory remediation orders, administrative fines, forced service transitions, and personal management-body accountability. Mid-sized regulated firms cannot justify €75k+ Big Four engagements or €50k+/yr TPRM SaaS suites just to pass RoI and exit-plan scrutiny, leaving a clear budget-friendly advisory gap.
