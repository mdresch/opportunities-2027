## 2. Business Need & Cost of Inaction

Enterprise boards in 2027 face mounting scrutiny over AI capital efficiency: organizations spend heavily on LLM APIs, copilots, and knowledge programs without knowing what durable Intelligence Capital they actually own, where it resides, who maintains it, or whether it can be trusted under audit. Without an executive valuation and provenance lens:

- **Blunt Cost-Cutting Risk:** CFOs cut AI seats indiscriminately while high-value SOPs, ontologies, and agent workflows remain undocumented or lost during restructuring and M&A.
- **Unmonetized Proprietary IP:** For CBa Consult, leaving the already-authored 13-chapter SIDPA manuscript unpublished wastes a high-differentiation executive door-opener while competitors dominate board-level AI FinOps narratives.

*Illustrative Pre-Pilot ADPA Source Modeling — Not Realized Client Actuals:* Supporting ADPA IP documentation (`ADPA Patent and IP Opportunities NL.docx` and `ADPA Patent and IP Opportunities.docx`) benchmarks automated 14-entity PMBOK baseline extraction at 5–10 seconds versus 8 hours of manual baseline authoring (an illustrative 99.98% time-reduction figure processing 844 entities in internal test cases). These figures represent internal prototype benchmarks and illustrative IP value narratives—not realized client billing or audited financial actuals.
