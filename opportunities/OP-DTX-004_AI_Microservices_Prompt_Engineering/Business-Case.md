## 2. Business Need & Cost of Inaction

Small & Mid enterprises face widening productivity gaps as competitors embed practical AI automations into customer service, support, and back-office workflows. Without structured prompt engineering and bounded API/no-code integration, SMEs incur recurring manual labor overhead, inconsistent service quality, and rising shadow-AI exposure.

- **Cost of Inaction:** Delayed operational efficiency, fragmented employee experimentation without quality controls, and missed Q1–Q2 2027 early-entry advisory positioning for CBa Consult.
- **Quantitative Evidence Baseline:** *Illustrative Pre-Pilot ADPA Source Modeling — Not Realized Client Actuals:* The ADPA source digest outlines productivity gains across healthcare, finance, retail, and logistics use cases, but no realized client pilot actuals exist yet. Tangible before/after cycle-time metrics must be captured during the first paid pilot for `OP-X-004`.

## 4. Commercial Packaging, $0 Working-Capital Model & Compounding Path

`OP-DTX-004` operates strictly on CBa Consult sweat equity ($0 initial working capital) by leveraging existing prompt engineering, API/no-code integration, and UX capabilities within the Digital Transformation toolkit. Client-side LLM API or platform subscriptions remain billed directly to the client tenant.

- **Packaging Structure:** Fixed-scope 4–6 week sprint delivering 1–3 production-ready AI micro-workflows, prompt version documentation, and handover runbooks within the €500–€2,500 band.
- **Compounding Path:** Reuse modular vertical prompt packs across adjacent niches, bundle curated packs from `OP-ING-003` and `OP-X-005`, document case-study proof in `OP-X-004`, and upsell successful clients into broader Tier A/B Digital Transformation retainers.
