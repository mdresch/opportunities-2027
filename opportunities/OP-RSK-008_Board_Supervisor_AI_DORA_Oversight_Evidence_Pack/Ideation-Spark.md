## Positioning

> For **board secretaries, audit committee chairs, Chief Risk Officers, and supervisory dialogue owners** (especially **AFM/DNB-regulated** firms) who must show that **AI and DORA are overseen operationally**—not only on paper—**Board, Audit Committee & Supervisor AI / DORA Oversight Evidence Pack** is a **fixed-scope governance pack** that delivers **board-ready oversight artefacts** with **$0 new GRC software**, positioning CBa Consult as **conflict-free pre-assurance** before Big Four Trusted AI or PwC Assurance for AI.

## The Problem (Before Solution)

- **Supervisory intensity:** AFM 2026 prioritises **responsible AI** (inventory, model risk, data quality, incident reporting) and **DORA in practice**.
- **Board liability gap:** Policies exist; **quarterly evidence** of review, literacy, and escalation does not.
- **Big Four mismatch:** Deloitte IDT / EY FSO depth is often **oversized or independence-conflicted** for mid-market prep.

## What the Client Is Entitled To (Deliverables)

**Duration:** 2–3 weeks · **Fee band:** €2,000–€5,000

1. **Oversight calendar** — quarterly board/audit committee agenda items: AI register changes, incidents, DORA tests, material third-party ICT changes.
2. **Training & AI literacy evidence structure** — role-based literacy map (EU AI Act); attendance/completion tracking template (not mass content production).
3. **AI register snapshot** — aligned with `OP-RSK-004`: systems, risk tier, owner, human-oversight hook.
4. **DORA incident & resilience hooks** — how oversight connects to reporting clocks and ICT third parties (bridges `OP-RSK-006` / `OP-RSK-007`).
5. **Escalation RACI** — thresholds for AI/ICT risk to board notification (not live SOC operations).
6. **Optional annex:** **Supervisor-ready index** (AFM themes: model risk, data quality, outsourcing) for management briefing.
