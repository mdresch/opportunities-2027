## 2. Business Need & Cost of Inaction

Under the AFM 2026–2027 supervisory agenda and DORA enforcement, Boards and Audit Committees at regulated financial and mid-market enterprises must demonstrate operational oversight of AI systems and ICT resilience—not merely static policy documents. When supervisors or external AI assurance teams request quarterly oversight minutes, literacy logs, and escalation trails, fragmented documentation creates immediate governance friction.

- **Cost of Inaction:** Adverse supervisory dialogue findings, delayed external assurance readiness (e.g., Big Four Trusted AI / PwC Assurance for AI), and avoidable Audit Committee remediation cycles.
- **Evidence Basis:** *No ADPA `.docx` ROI modeling is assigned to `OP-RSK-008`.* Commercial rationale is derived from competitive gap analysis ([Source-Documents.md](Source-Documents.md)) and AFM 2026 supervisory priorities without fabricated quantitative client savings.
