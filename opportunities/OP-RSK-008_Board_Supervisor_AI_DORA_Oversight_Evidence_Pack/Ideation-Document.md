## 1. Problem Context & 2027 Market Trigger

Financial institutions and regulated mid-to-large enterprises (especially AFM/DNB-supervised entities in the Netherlands) face intense supervisory scrutiny in 2026–2027. The AFM 2026 Agenda explicitly prioritizes **responsible AI in practice** (inventory, model risk, data quality, incident reporting) alongside operational **DORA** enforcement. While most firms have drafted high-level AI or ICT risk policies, Boards and Audit Committees lack **quarterly operational evidence** proving active oversight, AI literacy, and escalation execution.

- **Core Pain:** Policies sit on SharePoint while board packs omit structured AI register deltas, DORA resilience test outcomes, and role-based AI literacy records—creating personal and institutional governance exposure during supervisory dialogues.
- **2027 Buying Trigger:** CROs, Audit Committee Chairs, and Board Secretaries seek a fast, conflict-free pre-assurance pack before external auditors (e.g., Big Four Trusted AI or PwC Assurance for AI) or regulators examine their governance trail.

## 2. Solution Architecture & Delivery Workflow

`OP-RSK-008` is a 2–3 week (5–8 hrs/wk) fixed-scope **Audit / Diagnostic & Governance Pack** built on the **Risk Management** signature toolkit and Strategic Asset 4 (*Evidence & Governance*):

1. **Week 1 — Governance & Register Alignment:** Map existing AI systems, DORA ICT third-party/resilience registers, and current board reporting cadences; align AI register snapshot fields with `OP-RSK-004`.
2. **Week 2 — Core Oversight Pack Assembly:** Produce the four core board-ready artifacts with **$0 new GRC software**:
   - **Quarterly Oversight Calendar** covering AI register changes, incidents, DORA tests, and material ICT third-party shifts.
   - **Role-Based AI Literacy Evidence Structure** (EU AI Act alignment) with attendance and completion tracking templates.
   - **AI Register Snapshot & DORA Hooks** linking systems, risk tiers, owners, and human-oversight checkpoints.
   - **Escalation RACI & Optional AFM-Style Supervisor Index** indexing policies, minutes, and test logs for rapid supervisory retrieval.
3. **Week 3 — Audit Committee Dry-Run & Handoff:** Walk the Board Secretary, CRO, and Audit Committee Chair through the Q+1 pack execution rhythm and prep handoff readiness for external assurance.
