## 2. Business Need & Cost of Inaction

Terrestrial AI compute growth is straining power grids and sharpening sovereign data-residency debates, prompting long-horizon aerospace concepts around solar-powered orbital data centers (`Global Data Centers in Space.docx`). However, commercial feasibility for a boutique advisory firm in 2027 is non-existent: orbital compute remains capital-intensive R&D for aerospace primes and sovereign consortiums post-2030.

- **Cost of Inaction (to CBa Consult):** **Zero commercial penalty.** Keeping `OP-ITG-009` parked protects scarce Director capacity for immediate revenue-generating Tier A/B engagements.
- **Source Modeling Benchmark (*Illustrative Pre-Pilot ADPA Source Modeling — Not Realized Client Actuals*):** The ADPA draft (`Global Data Centers in Space.docx`) outlines aspirational objectives such as 100% solar-powered orbital compute and reduced global transmission latency; these are conceptual aerospace R&D themes, not viable 2027 consulting business-case economics.
