## 1. Problem Context & 2027 Market Trigger

Terrestrial hyperscale data centers face mounting constraints around grid power capacity, water cooling, land permitting, and cross-border jurisdictional sovereignty. In parallel, aerospace and satellite operators (such as the SES concept explored in the ADPA source draft) are investigating solar-powered orbital compute nodes for low-latency global relays and sovereign off-planet storage.

- **2027 Market Reality Check:** While orbital compute is an intriguing 2030+ frontier concept, there is **zero viable 2027 commercial consulting buyer** for a $0-capital solo/boutique advisory pilot. Launch economics, radiation hardening, and orbital maintenance remain multi-billion-euro aerospace R&D challenges.
- **Portfolio Role:** Retained strictly as a **Tier D Thought-Leadership / Foresight Asset** (`Parked`) to spark executive conversations around long-horizon sovereign infrastructure (`OP-ITG-015`), never as a 2027 delivery pilot.

## 2. Solution Architecture & Delivery Workflow

In line with its `Parked` classification, `OP-ITG-009` has **no client-delivery workflow** in 2027. If activated solely as a zero-spend knowledge asset, execution is limited to editorial synthesis:

1. **Source Distillation:** Extract sovereign-compute, solar-energy sustainability, and orbital latency themes from `Global Data Centers in Space.docx`.
2. **Foresight Framing:** Contrast speculative orbital infrastructure with near-term 2027 sovereign cloud and terrestrial energy-governance realities (`OP-ITG-015`).
3. **Editorial Publication (Optional):** Publish a concise thought-leadership brief or LinkedIn discussion piece (`OP-MKT-006`) only when spare non-billable capacity exists outside Tier A/B/C delivery.
