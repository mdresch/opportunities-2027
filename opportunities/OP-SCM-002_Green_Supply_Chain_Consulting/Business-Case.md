## 2. Business Need & Cost of Inaction

Medium and large organizations face tightening 2027 customer sustainability mandates, CSRD Scope 3 disclosure expectations, and rising logistics/packaging inefficiencies. Procurement and supply-chain leaders who fail to establish standardized vendor emissions baselines and circularity scorecards risk losing enterprise tender eligibility and missing direct freight/waste cost savings.

For CBa Consult, `OP-SCM-002` captures high-value mid-market operational advisory without requiring software capex. *Illustrative Pre-Pilot ADPA Source Modeling — Not Realized Client Actuals*: the source ADPA `.docx` targets measurable client carbon reductions within 12–18 months of engagement; quantitative financial ROI and emissions savings are illustrative pre-pilot goals pending live client baseline calibration.
