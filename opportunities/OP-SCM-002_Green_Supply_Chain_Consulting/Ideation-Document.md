## 1. Problem Context & 2027 Market Trigger

Medium and large enterprises face converging 2027 pressure from EU CSRD/Scope 3 disclosure mandates, customer procurement scorecards, and volatile logistics/energy costs. For most product and distribution companies, 60–80%+ of total emissions and circularity impact sits upstream in purchased goods, vendor operations, and freight logistics—areas where procurement and supply chain teams lack standardized supplier-data workflows and practical decarbonization levers.

COOs, CPOs, and Supply Chain Directors need conflict-free, execution-oriented advisory that embeds carbon tracking, circularity criteria, and vendor audit routines directly into existing procurement and logistics workflows without Big Four advisory fees or multi-year software lock-in.

## 2. Solution Architecture & Delivery Workflow

`OP-SCM-002` is structured as an 8–12 week Audit / Diagnostic and roadmap engagement backed by CBa Consult's Supply Chain Strategy toolkit:

1. **Supply Chain Emissions & Spend Hot-Spot Mapping:** Baseline Scope 1–3 procurement and logistics categories using spend-based and activity-based proxies to isolate top-20% emission and cost drivers.
2. **Supplier Readiness & Circularity Diagnostic:** Conduct structured sustainability assessments across Tier-1 strategic vendors covering data availability, packaging/waste circularity, and logistics mode efficiency.
3. **Procurement & Logistics Governance Integration:** Embed ESG weighting into vendor scorecards, RFP evaluation templates, and carrier-selection SOPs.
4. **Decarbonization & Cost-Efficiency Roadmap:** Deliver a prioritized 12–18 month reduction roadmap linking directly into enterprise ESG compliance (`OP-SUS-002`) and SMB sustainability (`OP-SUS-001`).

## 5. Explicit Negative Scope Boundaries & Stage 1b Gate

To avoid unfeasible data-collection commitments and assurance liability, `OP-SCM-002` sets explicit boundaries:

- **No Statutory CSRD Assurance / Financial Audit Sign-Off:** CBa Consult delivers operational diagnostics and management reporting; formal third-party limited/reasonable assurance remains with accredited auditors.
- **No Custom Carbon-Accounting Software Build:** Custom SaaS platform engineering is excluded; deliverables leverage client BI tools or established templates.
- **No Guaranteed Tier-2/3 Primary Data Collection:** Where upstream sub-tier suppliers lack primary telemetry, standardized secondary emission factors and spend-based proxies are explicitly documented.
- **No Physical Fleet / Warehouse Capital Capex Execution:** Equipment procurement or facility construction management is outside advisory scope.
