## 2. Business Need & Cost of Inaction

Once a mid-market acquisition closes, value erosion accelerates rapidly if payroll/escrow integration, HR alignment, IT retention/recovery, ICT controls, and operational SOPs are managed in silos. Without a single integration governance office, synergy realization slips by quarters, key talent exits during ambiguity, and post-close audit readiness deteriorates.

- **Cost of Inaction:** Delayed deal synergies, post-close payroll/escrow disputes, unmanaged IT/cyber inheritance risk, and multi-entity stakeholder paralysis.
- **Expected Pre-Pilot Value (Unvalidated Hypothesis):** Functioning cross-entity Integration Management Office with unified ownership across five workstreams, capturing the highest single-engagement revenue tier (€6,000–€18,000+) in CBa's Tier A portfolio.

## 4. Commercial Packaging, $0 Working-Capital Model & Compounding Path

- **Packaging:** Fixed-scope 8–12 week Integration Governance Office retainer (`10–15 hrs/wk`) structured around Day-1/30/60/90 gates, weekly steering cadence, and 5-workstream sign-off registers.
- **Channel Discipline:** Pre-positioned during `OP-STR-004` pre-deal assessments as the natural post-close implementation governance continuation.
- **$0 Working-Capital Model:** Delivered via CBa Director sweat-equity and pre-built CBa toolkits—zero external subcontracting payroll float or software procurement.
- **Compounding Path:** Represents the **apex of CBa's M&A/PMI revenue ladder**, synthesizing `OP-FIN-010`, `OP-HR-002`, `OP-ITG-010`, `OP-ITG-002`, and `OP-OPS-007` as the direct compounding continuation of `OP-STR-004`.
