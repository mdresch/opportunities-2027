Communications Plan – ADPA (Advanced Documents Processing and Analytics) Owner: Project Manager (PM) – Menno Drescher | Time zone: UTC Version: 1.0 Draft for Review

Objectives

Ensure the right stakeholders receive the right information at the right cadence via agreed channels.

Drive timely decisions, surface and resolve risks/issues, and foster adoption.

Provide traceability through centralized artifacts (Confluence/SharePoint) and dashboards.

Stakeholder Communications Matrix

Executive Sponsor & Steering Committee (SteerCo)

Information needs: Value realization, scope/schedule/budget status, key risks/issues/decisions, change requests.

Channel(s): Meetings, email, Confluence/SharePoint (decision and status pages), dashboards.

Frequency/cadence: Biweekly SteerCo; ad hoc for urgent escalations; monthly benefits status.

Sender/owner: PM (Menno Drescher) with Product Owner and Tech Lead as contributors.

Format/artifacts: SteerCo deck, one-page status (RAG, KPIs), decision log, RAID log excerpts.

Feedback mechanism: Meeting Q&A; Confluence comments on decision items; email replies to PM.

Product Owner / Business Lead

Information needs: Backlog health, scope trade-offs, UAT progress, benefits, adoption feedback.

Channel(s): Meetings, email, Confluence/SharePoint (backlog and plans), dashboards.

Frequency/cadence: Weekly sync; ad hoc grooming; sign-off checkpoints per milestone.

Sender/owner: PM; Product Owner co-owns backlog communications.

Format/artifacts: Backlog views, status mail, feature burndown, UAT defect reports.

Feedback mechanism: Confluence comments on user stories; change requests via form/page; email to PM/PO.

PMO / Governance

Information needs: Compliance to methodology, stage gates, financials, change control.

Channel(s): Email, meetings, Confluence/SharePoint (stage gate evidence), dashboards.

Frequency/cadence: Monthly governance report; per gate review.

Sender/owner: PM.

Format/artifacts: Stage gate checklist, change log, financial summary.

Feedback mechanism: Review notes in SharePoint; email queries to PM.

Core Delivery Team (Engineering, QA, BA, Architect)

Information needs: Sprint goals, dependencies, risks/issues, release plan, quality/throughput metrics.

Channel(s): Meetings, Confluence/SharePoint (tech docs), dashboards, email.

Frequency/cadence: Weekly project sync; technical design reviews as needed; release readiness reviews per release.

Sender/owner: PM (coordination), Tech Lead (technical content).

Format/artifacts: Weekly notes/actions, design docs, runbooks, release checklist.

Feedback mechanism: Meeting discussion; Confluence comments; action items tracked on Confluence/SharePoint.

InfoSec / Compliance / Legal

Information needs: Security/privacy posture, risk assessments, audit evidence, exceptions, data residency.

Channel(s): Email, meetings, SharePoint (evidence), Confluence (policies and decisions).

Frequency/cadence: Monthly risk/compliance touchpoint; ad hoc for assessments.

Sender/owner: PM with Security Lead.

Format/artifacts: PIA/DPIA documents, pen test reports, access reviews, exception register.

Feedback mechanism: Review notes in SharePoint; email threads for clarifications.

Data Platform / IT Operations / Cloud Platform

Information needs: Environment readiness, performance, monitoring, incident trends, change windows.

Channel(s): Meetings, SharePoint (runbooks), dashboards, email.

Frequency/cadence: Weekly ops readiness; release window briefings per deployment.

Sender/owner: PM; Ops/Platform leads for operational updates.

Format/artifacts: Ops status, capacity and SLO dashboards, change calendar, incident summaries.

Feedback mechanism: Ops review meetings; runbook change requests via SharePoint.

Business Users / Pilot Champions

Information needs: Feature availability, UAT plans, known issues, training materials, release impacts.

Channel(s): Email, Confluence/SharePoint (guides), meetings (showcases), dashboards.

Frequency/cadence: UAT showcases weekly during UAT phases; release briefings per deployment.

Sender/owner: PM and Product Owner.

Format/artifacts: UAT plan, quick reference guides, release notes, known-issues page.

Feedback mechanism: UAT feedback form/page; Confluence comments; email to PM/PO.

Vendors / Partners

Information needs: Integration timelines, SLAs, incidents, roadmap changes.

Channel(s): Email, meetings, SharePoint (contracts/SLAs), dashboards as applicable.

Frequency/cadence: Monthly vendor review; incident/post-incident reviews as needed.

Sender/owner: PM with Vendor Manager/Tech Lead.

Format/artifacts: Action logs, SLA scorecards, incident reports.

Feedback mechanism: Meeting minutes acknowledgments via email; change requests documented on SharePoint.

Support / Service Desk (post go-live)

Information needs: Runbooks, on-call matrix, release notes, known errors, KPIs.

Channel(s): SharePoint (runbooks/KB), email, meetings (handover), dashboards.

Frequency/cadence: Handover sessions pre go-live; weekly during hypercare; per release thereafter.

Sender/owner: PM with Ops Lead.

Format/artifacts: Support model doc, KB articles, release notes, incident playbooks.

Feedback mechanism: KB comments/requests on SharePoint; email to Ops Lead/PM.

Key Ceremonies

Weekly Project Sync (meetings)

Audience: Core delivery team; invited: Product Owner, Ops, Security as needed.

Cadence: Weekly, 45–60 min, UTC.

Agenda: Status (RAG), blockers/risks, dependency review, upcoming releases, actions.

Inputs/outputs: Dashboard snapshot; updated action log; meeting notes on Confluence/SharePoint.

Biweekly SteerCo (meetings)

Audience: Executive Sponsor, Product Owner, PM, Tech Lead, PMO.

Cadence: Every two weeks, 60 min, UTC.

Agenda: KPIs/benefits, scope/schedule/budget, risk/issue escalations, decisions/approvals.

Artifacts: SteerCo pack, decision log, updated RAID.

UAT Showcases (meetings)

Audience: Business users, SMEs, Product Owner, QA, PM.

Cadence: Weekly during UAT cycles, 45 min.

Purpose: Demonstrate increments, validate acceptance criteria, capture feedback and sign-offs.

Artifacts: UAT scorecard, demo notes, acceptance/sign-off record on SharePoint.

Release Notes (email + Confluence/SharePoint)

Cadence: Per deployment/release.

Content: What changed, why, risks, impact, downtime windows, rollback, verification, contacts.

Distribution: Stakeholder distribution lists; posted on project hub; linked from dashboards.

Standard Reports and Artifacts

Weekly status email: RAG status, highlights, risks/issues, decisions needed, next steps.

Dashboards (e.g., Tableau): Document throughput, accuracy by doc type, exception rates, SLA adherence, spend vs. budget; updated daily or per batch.

RAID log: Risks, assumptions, issues, dependencies maintained on Confluence/SharePoint; reviewed weekly.

Decision log: Owner, date, context, decision, impacts; linked to meeting minutes.

Change log: Requests, impacts, approvals; aligned with governance.

Channels and Repositories

Email: Official broadcasts and decisions; use descriptive subject tags (e.g., [ADPA][Status][YYYY-MM-DD]).

Confluence/SharePoint: Single source of truth for plans, minutes, logs, runbooks, and release notes; page owners maintain freshness.

Dashboards: Linked from the project hub; snapshot included in SteerCo packs and weekly sync notes.

Meetings: Calendar invites include agenda, goals, and links to artifacts; minutes posted within 24 hours.

Escalation Paths

Delivery issues/blockers

L1 (within 1 business day): PM (Menno Drescher) engages owner; track in RAID; discuss in Weekly Project Sync.

L2 (within 2 business days): Product Owner and Tech Lead; schedule targeted working session; adjust scope/plan as needed.

L3 (next SteerCo or ad hoc urgent session): Executive Sponsor/SteerCo for decisions on budget/scope/timeline.

Security/Privacy incidents

Immediate: Notify Security Lead and PM; convene incident review; follow incident response process.

Reporting: Summary posted to SharePoint; updates provided via email to SteerCo.

Production incidents (post go-live)

Immediate: Ops/Service Desk engages runbooks; PM notified.

Communication: Incident minutes on SharePoint; post-incident review scheduled; release of corrective actions.

Roles and Ownership

Overall comms owner: PM (Menno Drescher).

Content owners: Product Owner (business changes), Tech Lead (technical updates), Ops Lead (operational updates), Security Lead (security/compliance updates).

Page owners: Named per Confluence/SharePoint page; owners ensure updates before ceremonies.

Feedback and Continuous Improvement

Mechanisms: Confluence comments, UAT feedback page, short pulse surveys after showcases/releases, retrospective notes.

Review: Quarterly review of this communications plan; propose updates at SteerCo for approval.
