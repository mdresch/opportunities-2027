## 1. Problem Context & 2027 Market Trigger

Medium and large enterprises frequently commission Finance, HR, Legal, or IT governance audits only to see newly recommended operating frameworks, compliance checklists, and AI-enabled tools stall within 90 days of handover. Without structured stakeholder cadence, role-specific enablement, and centralized decision traceability, operational teams revert to legacy spreadsheets and informal workarounds.

In 2027, board-level cost discipline alongside mandatory regulatory milestones (IFRS 18, EU AI Act, CSRD/ESG, and DORA/NIS2) leaves transformation PMOs, CHROs, and Program Directors unable to justify audit spend that fails to translate into sustained day-to-day adoption.

- **Target Buyer Segment:** CHROs, Transformation PMOs, and Program Directors in Medium & Large organizations.
- **Core Wedge Pain:** Audit recommendations fail to stick because teams lack stakeholder communications cadence, UAT feedback loops, and adoption KPI tracking.
- **Positioning Anchor:** A fixed-scope Track A rollout kit backed by the **Program, Project & Change Management** toolkit with `$0` initial CBa working capital.

## 2. Solution Architecture & Delivery Workflow

`OP-CHG-001` packages CBa Consult's **Program, Project & Change Management** toolkit and ADPA communication governance structures into a repeatable **6–8 week** Track A rollout playbook:

| Phase | Timing | Core Activities | Concrete Client Deliverables |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Stakeholder & Adoption Baseline** | Weeks 1–2 | Map impacted groups (SteerCo, Business Leads, PMO, Delivery, InfoSec/Legal, IT Ops); define baseline adoption KPIs and resistance points. | Stakeholder Communications Matrix & Adoption KPI Baseline |
| **2. Cadence & Artifact Architecture** | Weeks 3–4 | Configure biweekly SteerCo, weekly delivery sync, and monthly PMO/risk touchpoints using centralized Confluence/SharePoint decision and RAID logs. | Tailored Rollout Governance Plan & Confluence/SharePoint Templates |
| **3. Enablement & AI-Ready Rollout** | Weeks 5–6 | Deliver role-based micro-guides, AI-assisted change communications, and UAT/feedback loops tied to active audit remediation items. | Role-Based Training Kit & Feedback/Exception Register |
| **4. Handover & Stickiness Audit** | Weeks 7–8 | Conduct 30-day usage telemetry review, close open escalation loops, and hand over ownership to internal PMO/HR sponsors. | 30-Day Adoption Scorecard & Internal PMO Runbook |

### Core Operational Capabilities Extracted from ADPA

- **Six-Tier Stakeholder Routing:** Differentiates information needs, channels, cadence, and feedback paths across SteerCo, Product Owners/Business Leads, PMO, Core Delivery, InfoSec/Compliance/Legal, and IT/Cloud Platform Ops.
- **Centralized Traceability:** Anchors every rollout decision, change request, DPIA/exception, and RAG status inside the client's existing Confluence or SharePoint workspaces.
- **AI-Augmented Comms Guardrails:** Uses `Communication Management Plan in Age of AI.docx` patterns for faster stakeholder brief drafting with mandatory PM review before distribution.

- **In Scope:** Stakeholder communications matrix, governance cadence design, Confluence/SharePoint artifact templates, role-based adoption guides, and 30-day KPI tracking.
- **Explicitly Out of Scope:** Custom enterprise LMS software development, multi-year cultural transformation retainers, HR restructuring/headcount decisions, and third-party SaaS licensing procurement.
- **Required Delivery Skills:** Change management, stakeholder communications, and training design (Director-led, zero external contractors).
