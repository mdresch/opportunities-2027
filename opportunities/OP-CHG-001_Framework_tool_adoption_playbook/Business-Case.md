## 1. Executive Commercial Summary & Bolt-On Attach Thesis

Across CBa Consult's 2027 portfolio, high-value Finance, HR, Legal, Operations, and AI Governance diagnostics consistently unlock a second, higher-retention commercial question immediately upon readout: *"How do we make our teams actually follow this framework across entities without slipping back into spreadsheets and shadow habits?"*

**`OP-CHG-001`** monetizes that post-audit execution gap without expanding delivery headcount or adding software spend:
- **Primary Revenue Role (Attach Engine):** Sold as a structured **+25% to +40% implementation bolt-on** attached to Medium & Large diagnostic engagements (`OP-FIN-002`, `OP-FIN-009`, `OP-HR-002`, `OP-HR-015`, `OP-LEG-002`, `OP-OPS-002`, `OP-ITG-013`/`014`).
- **Standalone Acquisition Role:** Offered directly to CHROs and Transformation PMOs stalled mid-rollout on a stalled ERP, Continuous Close, HR, or AI-tool adoption initiative.
- **Compounding Retainer Bridge:** Establishes CBa Consult inside SteerCo and weekly BAU governance ceremonies, creating a natural progression into **Multi-Pillar Strategy (`OP-STR-002`)** or **Managed Intelligence Provider (`OP-ITG-018` MIP)** recurring retainers.

## 4. Client ROI & Mandatory Adoption KPIs *(Anti-"Nice-to-Have" Safeguard)*

To defeat CFO/procurement pushback that change management is discretionary, **every `OP-CHG-001` engagement contractually anchors to observable adoption metrics**:

| KPI Category | Metric Definition | Baseline Benchmark (Typical Pre-Rollout) | Target Threshold (`Day 30 / 60 / 90`) | Audit / P&L Impact |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Enablement Reach** | `%` of target operational roles completing role-based `QRG` & UAT showcase walkthrough | Ad hoc (`<35%`) | **`≥85%` by Day 30** | Prevents frontline ignorance of new rules |
| **Behavioral Adherence** | `%` of live transactions / workflows complying with new framework controls (e.g., `<5d` exception aging, mandatory AI review tags) | Fragmented (`40–60%`) | **`≥80%` by Day 60 · `≥90%` by Day 90** | Protects parent audit ROI & regulatory readiness |
| **Escalation Velocity** | Mean resolution time on `L1`/`L2` adoption blockers logged in RAID | Untracked (`>10 days`) | **`L1 ≤1 day` · `L2 ≤2 days`** | Stops silent workarounds before habits calcify |
| **Hypercare Deflection** | Weekly volume of repetitive "how-to / routing" Service Desk tickets post go-live | Spike & plateau | **`≥50%` reduction Day 14 → Day 60** | Frees Ops/IT support capacity post-handover |

## 5. Commercial Governance & Rules of Engagement (`Rules-of-Engagement.md`)

All `OP-CHG-001` sales and delivery motions strictly enforce CBa Consult's binding governance rules:

1. **No Shadow Delivery (`Rules-of-Engagement.md §1` & `§5`):**
   - Zero workshops, stakeholder interviews, internal Confluence/SharePoint structuring, or tenant access occurs prior to **Gate `G1` (Written PO / Signed SOW)** and **Gate `G2` (DPA / Security clearance if touching employee-identifiable data)**.
   - Credit cards, P-cards, and "start now, expense later" requests are declined (`Commercial-Engagement-Invoicing-and-Procurement-Policy.md`).
2. **Multiparty Client Authorization (`Rules-of-Engagement.md §2`):**
   - Line-manager or HR-champion enthusiasm alone is insufficient. Every SOW requires written alignment across:
     1. **Client Sponsor** (Transformation Lead / HR / Functional Owner)
     2. **Executive Sponsor / Budget Owner** (CHRO, CFO, COO, or CIO)
     3. **Finance / Procurement** (PO issued against the correct contracting legal entity)
     4. **CBa Consult Director** (scope, fixed fee, and independence clearance logged)
3. **Independence & Advisory Boundary (`Rules-of-Engagement.md §4` & `§8`):**
   - CBa Consult operates strictly as **fixed-scope advisory and enablement** (not RIM-style interim line placement into client employment authority, and not external statutory auditing).
4. **Standard SOW Clauses Required (`Rules-of-Engagement.md §8` Gap-Closure Standard):**
   - Explicit deliverables list (`M1–M5`) and target adoption KPIs (`§4` above).
   - Named Client Executive Sponsor, RACI for internal Content/Page Owners, and Biweekly SteerCo cadence.
   - Written Change Request control for scope additions beyond mapped stakeholder groups.
   - Confidentiality / NDA / DPA clause, Deliverable Handover & Termination terms, **optional 30-day Q&A aftercare window**, and **PI insurance certificate availability on request**.

## 6. Risk Register & Mitigations

| ID | Risk Description | Likelihood / Impact | Concrete Mitigation Built into `OP-CHG-001` |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **R1** | Procurement strips change management out as "nice to have" during SOW negotiation | **High / High** | Frame as **Audit-ROI & Remediation Assurance (`§4`)** rather than generic workshops; offer **Option A (`€1,500–€2,500`)** low-friction attach directly inside parent audit SOWs. |
| **R2** | Client lacks designated internal Content/Page Owners, pushing BAU admin onto CBa Consult | **Medium / High** | Require named internal `PO`, `Tech`, `Ops`, and `Security` Content Owners (`Ideation-Document.md §3.2`) as a **pre-kickoff SOW dependency**. |
| **R3** | Scope creep across unplanned business units, languages, or custom video/LMS production | **Medium / Medium** | Fix distribution strictly to client's native Confluence/SharePoint, email & BI tools; lock stakeholder group count (`≤9`) in SOW; enforce Change Control (`§5.4`). |
| **R4** | "Zero adoption telemetry" — client cannot export dashboard/ticketing metrics to verify KPIs | **Medium / Medium** | Include manual 2-minute UAT/pulse survey fallback (`§3.2`) when automated BI/Service Desk logs are unavailable during initial 30 days. |
| **R5** | Competing executive priorities stall SteerCo decisions beyond L2 escalation | **Low / High** | Hardwired **`L1 (1d) → L2 (2d) → L3 SteerCo`** escalation SLA with visible Decision Log accountability. |
