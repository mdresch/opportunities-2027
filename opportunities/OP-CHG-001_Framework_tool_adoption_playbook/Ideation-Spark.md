## Positioning

> For **CHROs, Transformation PMOs, Functional Directors (CFO/COO/CIO), and Program Leads** in Medium & Large enterprises who face **"binder-on-the-shelf" post-audit failure** when newly designed Finance, HR, Sales, Operations, or AI governance frameworks are ignored by daily practitioners, **`OP-CHG-001`: Framework & Tool Adoption Playbook** is a **fixed-scope Advisory / Starter Kit** backed by CBa Consult's **Program, Project & Change Management** toolkit and production **ADPA Communications & Enablement IP** that converts static audit recommendations into **measurable 30/60/90-day behavioral adoption and audit-remediation velocity** with **€0 initial working capital**.

---

## What It Is

A modular, KPI-anchored **change-management, stakeholder-communications, champion-enablement, and hypercare rollout kit** deployed immediately alongside or following diagnostic audits (`OP-FIN-002`/`009`, `OP-HR-002`/`015`, `OP-LEG-002`, `OP-OPS-002`, `OP-ITG-013`/`014`, `OP-STR-002`) so new processes, guardrails, and AI-enabled tools stick in Business-As-Usual (`BAU`) operations.

## 7 Standard Pivotal Questions ([ideation-template.md §6b](../../ideation-template.md#6b-standard-opportunity-brief-7-pivotal-questions))

1. **Buyer & 2027 Trigger**  
   **Economic / Executive Buyer:** CHRO, Transformation PMO Lead, or Functional Program Sponsor (CFO/COO/CIO co-sponsoring post-audit execution).  
   **2027 Trigger:** Regulatory deadlines (**IFRS 18** mandatory `1 Jan 2027`, **EU AI Act**, **DORA/NIS2**, **CSRD**) and tighter CFO/PMO cost discipline mean boards no longer fund diagnostic reports whose controls fail in operational adoption—while AI-tool rollouts suffer from the widening *individual vs. organizational impact gap* (employees use tools ad hoc without standardized workflow adoption).

2. **The Wedge (Pain Eliminated)**  
   Eliminates **"post-audit adoption decay"**: the 60–70% drop-off between leadership sign-off of a new Finance close rule, HR performance/AI policy, SOP, or governance framework and consistent frontline execution across distributed business units and shared services. Anchored directly to **rework reduction, faster audit-finding closure, and realized ROI on the parent transformation**.

3. **Deliverable & Verified Existing IP (`≥70%` Coverage Target)**  
   **Client Deliverables:** 5-module Adoption Playbook tailored to the target framework (Stakeholder & Impact Matrix, Multi-Channel Communications & Ceremony Engine, Champion Network & Role-Based Quick-Reference Enablement, 30/60/90-Day Adoption KPI Scorecard, and 30-Day Hypercare Handover Kit).  
   **Verified Internal IP:**
   - [Ideation-Document.md](Ideation-Document.md) (structured from `ADPA - Communication Management Plan.docx` — 9 stakeholder groups, 4 governance ceremonies, RAID/Decision/Change logs, 3-tier escalation paths, UAT showcase cadence, and distribution/feedback architecture).
   - Supporting ADPA asset: `Communication Management Plan in Age of AI.docx` (AI-era stakeholder messaging & change cadence).
   - Local CBa BD practice folders indexed in [BD-Quick-Reference.md](BD-Quick-Reference.md) (`CBA - Change Management`, `Building an adoption and change management practice`).

4. **Sweat-Equity Feasibility (`€0` Cash / Capacity Fit)**  
   **Timeline & Effort:** **6–8 weeks** fixed-scope delivery at **6–8 hrs/week** (`~36–52` total Director hours), staying comfortably inside the **10–15 hrs/week** portfolio capacity cap.  
   **Capital Outlay:** **€0 initial working capital** (`€1,500–€5,000` fixed-fee pricing band; `€1,500–€2,500` Kickstart Bolt-On / `€3,500–€5,000` Full Adoption Playbook).

5. **Outcome & Proof KPI**  
   Ties change management directly to measurable adoption metrics (never sold as "soft workshops"):
   - **≥85%** target-role completion of micro-enablement & UAT showcase check-ins within Day 30.
   - **≥80%** process/tool compliance rate on 60-day post-launch pulse & exception telemetry (matching audit control KPIs such as `<5d` exception aging or AI-policy adherence).
   - **≥50%** reduction in "how-to / routing" Service Desk tickets between Week 2 hypercare and Day 60 BAU handover.
   - Feeds referenceable proof into `OP-X-004` case studies and converts single-audit buyers into recurring retainers (`OP-ITG-018` / `OP-STR-002`).

6. **Scope Boundaries & Primary Risk (`Rules-of-Engagement.md`)**  
   **Explicitly Out of Scope (Negative Scope Boundary):**
   - **Not** building custom LMS software, SCORM video production studios, or HRIS/ERP software configuration.
   - **Not** interim line management or shadow staffing (adheres strictly to `Rules-of-Engagement.md §8` — fixed-scope advisory with multiparty Client Sponsor + Executive Sponsor + Finance/Procurement PO).
   - **Not** generic "AI prompt training" divorced from client governance and measurable adoption KPIs (`OP-HR-015` guardrails).  
   **#1 Commercial Risk:** Positioned as an optional "nice-to-have" add-on that procurement cuts. *Mitigation:* Bundle by default as the Phase-2 implementation bridge in every audit proposal and tie pricing to at least two hard audit-remediation KPIs.

7. **Compounding & Cross-Pillar Scale Path**  
   Functions as CBa Consult's **universal attach SKU** across Medium & Large engagements:
   - **Finance Audits & Continuous Close:** `OP-FIN-002`, `OP-FIN-009` (rolling out `23rd→3rd` close habits across GL, Payroll, Project Accounting, and FP&A).
   - **HR & Manager AI Enablement:** `OP-HR-002`, `OP-HR-015` (embedding performance/coaching AI guardrails cleanly into manager routines).
   - **Legal, Ops & Enterprise AI Governance:** `OP-LEG-002`, `OP-OPS-002`, `OP-ITG-013`, `OP-ITG-014`, `OP-STR-002` (bridging diagnostic frameworks into multi-entity rollout and `OP-ITG-018` MIP retainers).
