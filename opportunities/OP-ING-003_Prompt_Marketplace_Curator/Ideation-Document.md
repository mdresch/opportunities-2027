## 1. Problem Context & 2027 Market Trigger

As generative AI becomes embedded in everyday knowledge work, founders, solo operators, and niche professionals waste substantial time drafting and debugging inconsistent prompts. While generic prompt lists flood the internet, domain-specific, QA-tested prompt workflows tailored to specialized professions (such as healthcare, finance, marketing, and legal services operations) remain scarce.

- **2027 Market Trigger:** Cost-conscious Small-tier buyers seek fixed-scope, low-ticket digital assets (€300–€1,500 equivalent value or productized packs) that immediately compress task time and avoid hiring overhead.
- **Core Buyer Pain:** Solo operators and niche teams lack reliable, context-aware prompt collections and structured workflow templates that produce consistent, accurate outputs out of the box.
- **Target Buyer Profile:** Founders, Creators, and Solo Operators in the Small segment needing lightweight productivity acceleration with zero platform lock-in.

## 2. Solution Architecture & Delivery Workflow

`OP-ING-003` is a Digital Product (PDF / Toolkit) initiative delivered on a 4–6 week build horizon (Low effort, $0 initial working capital) backed by the Business Plan & Entrepreneurship toolkit.

1. **Niche Selection & Use-Case Mapping (Week 1):** Select 1–2 high-value vertical professions (e.g., finance/legal ops or boutique advisory) where generic prompts fail due to domain terminology and structure requirements.
2. **Prompt Engineering & Workflow Curation (Weeks 2–3):** Author, chain, and parameterize domain-specific prompt packs with explicit input schemas, edge-case constraints, and output formatting standards.
3. **Structured QA Testing (Week 4):** Benchmark prompt packs across common model versions to verify repeatability, clarity, and low hallucination rates; document usage guidance.
4. **Packaging & Cross-Portfolio Bundling (Weeks 5–6):** Publish as clean PDF/Toolkit digital product assets and package modularly so packs can bundle into `OP-DTX-004` microservices or `OP-X-005` starter packs.

## 5. Explicit Negative Scope Boundaries & Stage 1b Gate

Because generic prompt marketplaces are rapidly commoditizing, `OP-ING-003` enforces tight boundaries to avoid building an undifferentiated SaaS platform prematurely:

- **No Custom Marketplace Software Build at MVP:** Initial commercialization uses lightweight digital product distribution (PDF/Toolkit packs) rather than building a custom multi-sided web marketplace from scratch.
- **No Generic Consumer Prompts:** All curated assets must target verifiable professional workflows with QA test evidence; generic lifestyle or broad creative writing packs are out of scope.
- **No Ongoing Custom API Maintenance:** Buyers receive static, documented prompt templates and usage guides; bespoke API wiring belongs to `OP-DTX-004`.
- **Stage 1b Gate Readiness:** Core ideation and ADPA source alignment are complete. Director sign-off is required to validate the initial target niche and approve lightweight pilot distribution.
