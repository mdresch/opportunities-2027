## 2. Business Need & Cost of Inaction

Founders, solo operators, and niche practitioners in 2027 rely heavily on generative AI to offset small team sizes, yet generic prompt libraries deliver inconsistent results in specialized domains (such as healthcare, finance, marketing, and legal operations). Buyers want affordable, fixed-scope digital toolkits that shorten time-to-value without recurring software fees or consulting retainers.

- **Cost of Inaction:** CBa Consult misses a low-effort Q1–Q2 2027 cash-flow entry product and loses a reusable prompt asset library that feeds higher-ticket microservice engagements.
- **Quantitative Evidence Baseline:** *Illustrative Pre-Pilot ADPA Source Modeling — Not Realized Client Actuals:* The ADPA source document models subscription, one-time purchase, and licensing revenue paths conceptually, with no realized sales actuals yet. First-release conversion and buyer feedback must be captured in `OP-X-004`.

## 4. Commercial Packaging, $0 Working-Capital Model & Compounding Path

Built on the Business Plan & Entrepreneurship Signature Toolkit, `OP-ING-003` requires only 4–6 weeks of Low sweat-equity curation and QA testing ($0 initial working capital). Distribution uses existing lightweight digital checkout or direct toolkit packaging rather than bespoke platform infrastructure.

- **Packaging Structure:** Domain-specific PDF / Toolkit prompt packs with tested input/output schemas, edge-case guardrails, and QA verification notes positioned in the €300–€1,500 value/licensing tier.
- **Compounding Path:** Serves as a low-friction top-of-funnel proof generator (`OP-X-004`), bundles directly into `OP-X-005` starter packs, and upgrades buyers needing workflow integration into `OP-DTX-004`.
