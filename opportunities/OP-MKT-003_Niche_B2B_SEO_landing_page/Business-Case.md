## 2. Business Need & Cost of Inaction

Small B2B firms entering 2027 cannot afford six-month agency retainers or a €60k+ in-house content hire, yet poorly articulated landing pages waste every outbound email and referral visit. Leaving hero messaging and search intent unaddressed depresses demo requests and lengthens sales cycles. Because `OP-MKT-003` is a Spark-derived Tier C adaptation of 2026 `OP-IE-001` & `OP-IE-002` with 0 assigned ADPA `.docx` files and no Stage 2 pilot actuals yet, no speculative conversion-lift percentages are claimed; commercial viability rests on immediate fixed-fee margin and low delivery overhead.

## 4. Commercial Packaging, $0 Working-Capital Model & Compounding Path

- **Standardized SKU Tiers:** Offer either a fixed "Landing Page Micro-Copy Sprint" (€500–€1,200, 2–3 weeks) or a "4-Article Niche B2B SEO Sprint" (€800–€2,000/month), invoiced 100% upfront for sprints under €1,000 or 50/50 for larger packs.
- **$0 Working-Capital Execution:** Relies solely on existing LLM subscriptions and CBa editorial checklists—zero ad spend or external software purchases.
- **Compounding Path:** Package reusable intake briefs and wireframes into a "Landing Page Copy Kit," delegate first-draft generation while retaining senior positioning QA, and funnel satisfied founders into broader Pillar 4/5 or clinic offers.
