## 1. Problem Context & 2027 Market Trigger

Niche B2B firms, consultancies, and specialized SaaS teams routinely underperform on inbound conversion because their landing pages lead with generic feature jargon rather than sharp buyer-pain positioning, clear CTAs, and high-intent search structure. In 2027's cost-conscious climate, small B2B founders and marketing leads cannot justify €3,000–€6,000/month retainers at traditional agencies or the fixed overhead of a full-time content hire. Simultaneously, unedited generic AI copy has flooded search results, penalizing brands that fail to combine AI drafting speed with domain-accurate human editing and UX micro-copy craft.

## 2. Solution Architecture & Delivery Workflow

Built on CBa Consult's **Sales, Marketing & Communication Strategy** signature toolkit, `OP-MKT-003` operates as a fast-turnaround, fixed-scope Freelance Content Service (4–8 weeks, Low–Med effort, $0 initial working capital):

1. **Offer & ICP Diagnostic (Week 1):** Audit existing landing page conversion friction, buyer triggers, and keyword intent gaps in a 60-minute structured intake.
2. **Conversion Wireframe & Hero Micro-Copy (Weeks 2–3):** Draft and human-edit core landing page copy (value-proposition hero, objection-handling blocks, social proof framing, and high-clarity CTAs).
3. **Targeted B2B SEO Sprint (Weeks 4–6):** Produce up to 4 niche, search-intent-aligned B2B articles per month using AI-assisted structuring paired with rigorous human editorial QA, factual verification, and internal linking.
4. **Handoff & Copy Kit Packaging (Weeks 7–8):** Deliver clean Markdown/CMS-ready assets plus a reusable "Landing Page Copy Kit" checklist so the client can maintain voice consistency.
