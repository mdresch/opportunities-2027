## 2. Business Need & Cost of Inaction

Mid-market and large organizations lose institutional memory every time a compliance, SOX, HR, or RevOps project closes with static Word SOPs. Without formal template binding and an explicit operational acceptance gate, hard-won control language cannot be reused across business units, Operations rejects unclear ownership, and known deferred gaps vanish between document versions—recreating audit findings and rework in subsequent quarters. Because the assigned ADPA `.docx` design documents focus on architecture rather than financial modeling (and no Stage 2 pilot metrics exist yet), no speculative monetary ROI or NPV figures are fabricated; business value is anchored in avoided rework and auditable handover control.

## 4. Commercial Packaging, $0 Working-Capital Model & Compounding Path

- **Fixed-Scope Engagement:** Priced at €1,500–€5,000 for binding 1–3 priority project SOP streams (e.g., Finance close, SOX offboarding, or Quote-to-Cash RevOps) to client-context templates and executing the formal Ops Handover Gate; billed 50% upfront / 50% at Ops acceptance.
- **$0 Working-Capital Leverage:** Core design (`Project-to-Operations SOP Handover Framework.docx`, `SOP Handover Document.docx`, and lifecycle spec) is already authored in ADPA—requiring minimal pre-pilot buildout.
- **Compounding Path:** Every client deployment enriches CBa Consult's parameterized SOP template library, lowers marginal delivery hours on future engagements, and pairs naturally with `OP-FIN-002` or compliance remediation work.
