## 1. Problem Context & 2027 Market Trigger

Remediation and transformation projects routinely produce high-value operational knowledge — control language, RACI ownership, jurisdiction-specific rules (such as Dutch *Ziektewet nawerking* offboarding checks), and hard-won edge-case corrections. Yet once a project closes, that knowledge typically stays trapped inside static, single-client Word documents. Three failures follow: knowledge cannot be reused across clients or adjacent processes; Operations lacks a formal acceptance gate to assume ongoing ownership; and deliberately deferred backlog items are lost between versions. In 2027, mid-market and large organizations tightening RevOps, Finance, HR, and ICT compliance need a repeatable bridge from project delivery to steady-state operations.

## 2. Solution Architecture & Delivery Workflow

Built on CBa Consult's **Operating Model & Organization Design** toolkit and the ADPA handover specification, `OP-OPS-007` delivers a 6–8 week Template / Tool Service ($0 initial working capital):

1. **SOP & Control Inventory Audit (Weeks 1–2):** Extract existing project-produced SOPs across Finance, HR, ICT, and Operations; identify static client-hardcoded fields versus reusable core control logic.
2. **Client-Context ADPA Binding Schema (Weeks 3–4):** Parameterize SOP templates so client-specific variables (entity, jurisdiction, RACI roles, ERP triggers) dynamically bind at generation time instead of manual copy-paste editing.
3. **Formal Handover Document & Acceptance Gate (Weeks 5–6):** Implement the standardized Handover Document structure capturing sign-off criteria, SLA owners, exception paths, and an explicit carried-forward deferred-item backlog.
4. **Operational Adoption & Pilot Walkthrough (Weeks 7–8):** Run joint Project-to-Ops acceptance workshops to lock in operational ownership and lifecycle versioning rules.
