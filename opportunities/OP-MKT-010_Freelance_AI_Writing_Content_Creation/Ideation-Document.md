# Ideation Document — `OP-MKT-010`: Freelance AI Writing & Content Creation

> **Governance SSOT:** Governed by [Tailored-Hybrid-SIDPA-Governance.md](../../docs/governance/Tailored-Hybrid-SIDPA-Governance.md), tracked in [inventory-of-ideas.md](../../inventory-of-ideas.md), and positioned as a **Tier C — Supporting** cash-flow wedge alongside [Tier_A_Executive_Summary_2027.md](../../Tier_A_Executive_Summary_2027.md).

## 1. Problem Context & 2027 Market Trigger

B2B founders, CMOs, and marketing leads at small-to-mid firms need credible thought-leadership blogs, executive newsletters, and technical whitepapers to sustain pipeline visibility. Purely generic AI copy destroys brand trust, while traditional B2B agencies charge steep monthly retainers and lack deep finance, governance, or enterprise architecture literacy.

- **Target Buyer:** Founders, CMOs, and B2B Marketing Leads (Small tier), especially in technical, fintech, sustainability, AI, or governance-adjacent verticals.
- **The Wedge (Pain):** Marketing teams struggle to publish consistent, domain-accurate long-form content without pulling senior executives away from core delivery.
- **2027 Trigger:** Cost-conscious 2027 marketing budgets favor fixed-scope, fast-turnaround content sprints over full-time content hires or bloated agency retainers—provided AI speed is paired with rigorous human domain editing.

## 2. Solution Architecture & Delivery Workflow

Delivered as a 4–6 week fixed-scope sprint (`Low` effort) leveraging the **Sales, Marketing & Communication Strategy** toolkit:

1. **Week 1 — Editorial Positioning & Voice Calibration:**
   - Audit existing B2B collateral, buyer personas, SEO topic clusters, and technical proof points.
   - Conduct one structured 60-minute SME extraction interview and lock editorial fact-checking guardrails.
2. **Weeks 2–4 — Human-Edited Production Sprint:**
   - Draft and rigorously human-edit a bundled deliverable set (e.g., 1 anchor whitepaper + 2–3 technical blog/newsletter pieces).
   - Verify domain accuracy, regulatory terminology, and differentiated executive voice (zero generic AI clichés).
3. **Weeks 5–6 — Distribution Checklist & Retainer Option:**
   - Deliver publication-ready Markdown/Docx assets, LinkedIn/newsletter excerpts, and CTA variants.
   - Establish engagement metrics and evaluate optional consolidation into broader marketing support (`OP-MKT-003` / `OP-MKT-006`).

## 5. Explicit Negative Scope Boundaries & Stage 1b Gate

- **No Commodity SEO Spam or Unedited AI Output:** CBa Consult never delivers unverified, generic AI-generated fluff; every artifact requires senior human domain editing and source verification.
- **No Paid Media Buying or Full-Stack Web Dev:** Ad spend management, CRM engineering, and website rebuilds are excluded unless separately scoped.
- **No Subcontractor Pool Dependency at Launch:** Despite the original ADPA concept mentioning a 20-writer network, CBa 2027 solo-track execution avoids managing freelance payroll until repeatable demand is proven.
