## 1. Problem Context & 2027 Market Trigger

Founders, strategy leads, and mid-market partners routinely delay product launches, market-entry decisions, and competitive repositioning because traditional market research agencies take weeks and charge high retainers, while raw self-serve LLM queries hallucinate statistics, fabricate competitor claims, and lack structured commercial synthesis.

- **Target Buyers:** Founders, Partners, Product Leads, and Mid-Market Executives across All size tiers.
- **2027 Market Trigger:** Accelerated AI-driven market shifts require rapid competitor and demand-signal intelligence under tight 2027 budgets that reject slow agency retainers and full-time market analyst hires.
- **Core Pain (The Wedge):** Decision-makers need fast, verified market, competitor, and demand-signal briefs delivered on demand without spending days prompting LLMs or waiting weeks for traditional research firms.

## 2. Solution Architecture & Delivery Workflow

Built on CBa Consult's **Data Analytics Strategy** Signature Toolkit and structured human-in-the-loop verification workflows, `OP-X-007` delivers a **2–4 week** concierge validation sprint (or rapid single-brief turnaround):

1. **Phase 1 — Decision Question & Scope Scoping (Days 1–3):** Lock exact commercial questions (target segment, competitor set, pricing benchmarks, demand signals) and citation standards.
2. **Phase 2 — AI-Accelerated Multi-Source Collection (Days 4–8):** Execute structured synthesis across public filings, pricing pages, industry reports, review/job-signal data, and trade publications.
3. **Phase 3 — Human Citation Audit & Triangulation (Days 9–14):** Independently verify every primary statistic, competitor claim, and URL source to eliminate AI hallucination and stale-data errors.
4. **Phase 4 — Executive Brief & Action Readout (Days 15–20):** Deliver a clean, decision-ready Market & Competitive Intelligence Brief with explicit confidence ratings, strategic implications, and next-step options.

### Core Deliverables & Verification Standards

- **Verified Market & Competitor Brief:** Structured 5–10 page executive dossier comparing positioning, pricing anchors, and demand triggers.
- **Source Provenance & Confidence Log:** 100% URL-checked citation ledger separating verified facts from analyst synthesis.
- **Decision Options Summary:** Actionable GTM or positioning recommendations ready for leadership sign-off.

## 5. Explicit Negative Scope Boundaries & Stage 1b Gate

To avoid hallucination liability and unscalable custom research engineering under a `$0` working-capital rule, `OP-X-007` enforces strict exclusions:

- **Out of Scope:** Custom web-scraping infrastructure engineering, proprietary panel/survey fieldwork requiring paid respondent recruitment, regulated financial/investment advice, and continuous SaaS dashboard hosting.
- **Verification Mandate:** Every quantitative claim in a client deliverable must link to a primary, human-checked source; unverified AI outputs are strictly prohibited.
- **Accountable Ownership:** CBa Consult executes source triangulation, verification logging, and strategic synthesis; the client owns downstream commercial or GTM execution decisions.
- **Stage 1b Review Outcome:** Framed as a Tier C validation service; leveraged internally for CBa Tier A prospect research while parked for external sales.
