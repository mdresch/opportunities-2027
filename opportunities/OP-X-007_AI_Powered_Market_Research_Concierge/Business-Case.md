## 2. Business Need & Cost of Inaction

Strategic decisions made on stale assumptions or unverified AI summaries carry heavy commercial downside:

- **Cost of Inaction:** Weeks lost waiting on traditional research vendors, misguided GTM bets built on unverified LLM hallucinations, or €15k+ overspend on bloated agency studies.
- **Commercial Value Proposition:** Combining AI-assisted synthesis with rigorous human source verification delivers decision-grade competitor and demand-signal briefs in days/weeks at fixed €500–€2,500 pricing.
- **Model Provenance Notice:** Operational benchmarks cited in `Opportunities to Publish/AI-Powered Market Research Concierge.docx` (such as 70% manual effort reduction, 95% predictive accuracy, 100+ concurrent requests/month, and 30% cost reduction) represent *Illustrative Pre-Pilot ADPA Source Modeling — Not Realized Client Actuals* and do not reflect verified concierge unit economics.

## 4. Commercial Packaging, $0 Working-Capital Model & Compounding Path

- **Package Structure:** Fixed-scope Concierge Brief (€500–€2,500 scaled by competitor count and depth) delivered with a verified source appendix and executive readout deck.
- **Working-Capital Discipline:** `\$0` new software or syndicated database subscriptions; leverages existing AI research workflows, public/open filings, and CBa Consult's **Data Analytics Strategy** templates.
- **Compounding & Scale Path:**
  1. **Quarterly Sector Brief Retainer:** Convert single-brief buyers into recurring monthly or quarterly competitor-monitoring retainers.
  2. **Tier A Door-Opener:** Offer tailored executive market/AI-readiness briefs as low-friction discovery wedges into Strategy (`OP-STR-004`) or MIP (`OP-ITG-018`) engagements.
