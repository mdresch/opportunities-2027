## 2. Business Need & Cost of Inaction

Organizations deploying inspection drones, remote telemetry sensors, and edge gateways across ports, utilities, logistics yards, or campuses often assume cellular and GPS links are continuously reliable. Unmapped RF dependencies, unencrypted edge gateways, and absent GPS-denied fallback protocols turn localized interference or misconfiguration into operational downtime and NIS2 continuity gaps.

A rapid, hardware-free posture diagnostic exposes these single points of failure before an operational outage or regulatory audit forces emergency capital expenditure on oversized counter-UAS or OT replacement projects.

## 4. Commercial Packaging, $0 Working-Capital Model & Compounding Path

- **Packaging & Pricing Anchor:** Fixed-scope 4–6 week Drone & IoT RF Posture Diagnostic priced in the €1,500–€5,000 band, delivering an Asset & Spectrum Dependency Map, Edge Posture Scorecard, and Prioritized Monitoring Checklist.
- **Zero Working-Capital Mechanics:** Assessment runs strictly on client-supplied architecture diagrams, device inventories, gateway configs, and operational telemetry logs—requiring \$0 specialized RF hardware or field-equipment capex from CBa Consult.
- **Unit-Economics Guardrail:** Scope pilot delivery to a single physical site/campus and one primary drone/IoT estate so 4–8 hrs/week effort remains cleanly profitable inside the €1,500–€5,000 bracket.
- **Compounding Path:** Designed as a specialist wedge into broader enterprise engagements: `OP-RSK-001` (Risk Management governance), `OP-ITG-001`/`OP-ITG-002` (ICT Governance), and `OP-ITG-016` (EARN operational resilience architecture).
