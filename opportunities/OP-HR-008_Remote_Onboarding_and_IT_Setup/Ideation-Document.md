## 1. Problem Context & 2027 Market Trigger

Small and mid-market organizations operating hybrid or remote teams routinely lose 5–7 business days of new-hire and contractor productivity because HR onboarding checklists and IT device/account provisioning run in disconnected silos. Manual ticket handoffs produce delayed laptops, missing M365/SaaS permissions, inconsistent security baselines, and poor Day-1 employee experience.

- **Target Buyers:** CHROs, People Ops Leads, HR Transformation Leads, and IT Operations Managers (Small & Mid segment).
- **2027 Market Trigger:** Distributed hiring of staff and freelance specialists, NIS2/ISO 27001 access-hygiene scrutiny on joiner-mover-leaver workflows, and budget pressure to eliminate manual IT/HR coordination drag without buying heavy enterprise ITSM suites.
- **Core Pain (The Wedge):** Slow, error-prone manual handoffs between HR offer acceptance and IT account/hardware readiness.

## 2. Solution Architecture & Delivery Workflow

Built on CBa Consult's **Human Resources & Talent Management** Signature Toolkit combined with M365/IAM operational baselines, `OP-HR-008` delivers a turnkey remote onboarding and IT provisioning blueprint in **4–6 weeks**:

1. **Phase 1 — Joiner Workflow & Bottleneck Audit (Week 1):** Map current HR-to-IT handoffs, role-based access profiles, hardware lead times, and security compliance gaps.
2. **Phase 2 — Unified HR/IT Onboarding Playbook & SOPs (Weeks 2–3):** Design standardized pre-boarding checklists, role-based software/access bundles, and automated trigger workflows connecting HR intake forms to IT provisioning queues.
3. **Phase 3 — M365 / IAM Configuration & Policy Alignment (Weeks 4–5):** Configure standard identity templates, MFA/conditional-access baselines, remote device setup guides, and offboarding symmetry checks on client-owned tooling.
4. **Phase 4 — Pilot Cohort Validation & KPI Handover (Week 6):** Test the workflow with live new-hire/contractor cohorts, measure turnaround against the 24–48 hour target window, and hand over runbooks to HR and IT owners.

### Core Deliverables & Target Outcomes

- **Role-Based Access & Provisioning Matrix:** Standardized software, M365 group, and security baseline bundles for core roles and contractors.
- **Automated HR-to-IT Pre-Boarding Workflow:** Triggered checklist eliminating manual email/ticket chases prior to start date.
- **Joiner/Leaver SLA Scorecard:** Tracks Day-1 readiness rate, 24–48 hour provisioning SLA adherence, and offboarding revocation hygiene.

## 5. Explicit Negative Scope Boundaries & Stage 1b Gate

To uphold `$0` working-capital discipline and avoid becoming a hardware reseller or L1 helpdesk, `OP-HR-008` sets clear boundaries:

- **Out of Scope:** Hardware procurement/warehousing, physical laptop imaging/shipping logistics, 24/7 L1 IT helpdesk operations, and bespoke HRIS software development.
- **Client Prerequisites:** Active M365/identity tenant admin access during setup and joint nomination of one HR lead plus one IT lead.
- **Accountable Ownership:** CBa Consult designs the unified SOPs, role access matrices, and automated intake triggers; client HR and IT retain operational execution post-handover.
- **Stage 1b Review Outcome:** Ready to deploy as a cross-pillar bridge connecting `OP-HR-001` and `OP-ITG-004` upon Stage 2 buyer validation.
