## 2. Business Need & Cost of Inaction

Manual, fragmented remote onboarding imposes immediate productivity and security costs on Small & Mid employers:

- **Cost of Inaction:** 5–7 business days of idle payroll per new hire or contractor, recurring ad-hoc helpdesk interruptions, inconsistent security access configurations, and early hire frustration.
- **Commercial Value Proposition:** Standardizing role-based access profiles and HR-to-IT pre-boarding workflows compresses setup turnaround toward 24–48 hours while tightening joiner/leaver compliance.
- **Model Provenance Notice:** Performance targets cited in `Remote Onboarding and IT Setup Service.docx` (such as 90% onboarding within 48 hours and 80% reduction in manual IT interventions) represent *Illustrative Pre-Pilot ADPA Source Modeling — Not Realized Client Actuals* and must be benchmarked against live client ticket logs during Pilot execution.

## 4. Commercial Packaging, $0 Working-Capital Model & Compounding Path

- **Package Structure:** Fixed-scope 4–6 week Starter Kit (€800–€3,000 scaled by role count and M365/SaaS integration depth) delivering an end-to-end Joiner SOP, role-based access matrix, automated intake-to-ticket workflow, and offboarding checklist.
- **Working-Capital Discipline:** `\$0` inventory, hardware, or third-party licensing outlay; all automations run natively inside the client's existing M365, Google Workspace, or HRIS environment.
- **Compounding & Cross-Sell Path:**
  1. **Cross-Pillar Bridge:** Links `OP-HR-001` (People Ops & HR Transformation) directly to `OP-ITG-004` (M365 Governance & Copilot Readiness).
  2. **Identity Hygiene Upsell:** Exposes orphaned contractor/leaver accounts during audit, opening natural follow-on work for Entra Agent/Identity governance (`OP-ITG-013` / `OP-ITG-018`).
