## 2. Business Need & Cost of Inaction

Accelerators, industry associations, and entrepreneurship programs struggle to provide consistent, practical operating-model toolkits across cohorts, often relying on fragmented volunteer mentors. A co-branded, ready-to-deploy Operating-Model Starter Pack gives partner institutions structured curriculum and templates at a fraction of internal authoring cost.

No ADPA `.docx` modeling is assigned to `OP-X-005` ([Source-Documents.md](Source-Documents.md)); no realized partner revenue, cohort adoption, or ROI actuals exist yet. The €2,000–€8,000/partner target in [inventory-of-ideas.md](../../inventory-of-ideas.md) is an illustrative licensing benchmark. Building this pack in Q3–Q4 2027 carries minimal opportunity cost today while offering high distribution leverage once CBa Consult's individual pillar templates are field-proven in Q1–Q2.

## 4. Commercial Packaging, $0 Working-Capital Model & Compounding Path

- **Annual Partner License (€2,000–€8,000 / partner):** Co-branded Notion/Google template vault, 4–6 workshop slide decks, facilitator guides, and cohort onboarding checklists.
- **Optional Add-On — Live Design Clinics (`OP-X-003`):** Paid cohort office hours or founder diagnostics layered atop the base license.
- **$0 Working-Capital Discipline:** Assembled exclusively from validated internal CBa Consult templates and hosted inside existing Notion/Drive partner environments—zero custom LMS capex.
- **Compounding Path:** Turns each partner organization into a recurring B2B2B channel exposing dozens of founders/SMEs to CBa Consult's broader Tier A/B/C portfolio and `OP-X-004` proof ecosystem.
