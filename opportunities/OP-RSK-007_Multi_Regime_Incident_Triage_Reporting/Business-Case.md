## 2. Business Need & Cost of Inaction

Under 2026–2027 enforcement sweeps across DORA, NIS2, GDPR, the EU AI Act, and SEC Reg S-P, supervisory bodies increasingly treat missed early-warning windows (4h DORA / 24h NIS2 / 72h GDPR) and unrehearsed paper playbooks as governance failures warranting direct fines and executive scrutiny:

- **Siloed Intake Cost:** Separate Security, Privacy, and Legal forms delay incident classification past the 4-hour DORA initial notice window.
- **Inspection Exposure:** Paper policies without dated tabletop proof fail supervisory readiness audits.
- **Software-Free Urgency:** Buyers need a rapid operational bridge running on existing tools rather than a six-month GRC integration.

*Illustrative Pre-Pilot Sizing Estimates — Not Realized Client Actuals:* Per [Source-Documents.md](Source-Documents.md), zero ADPA `.docx` ROI/NPV financial models are assigned to `OP-RSK-007`. ## 4. Commercial Packaging, $0 Working-Capital Model & Compounding Path

`OP-RSK-007` runs on **\$0 initial working capital** as a solo-deliverable 3–4 week sprint using CBa Consult's existing Risk Management templates and the client's existing ITSM/Teams/SharePoint tooling. Pricing at a low-friction fixed-fee entry point minimizes procurement delays for CISOs, DPOs, and General Counsel ahead of Q2 2027 supervisory cycles:

- **Fast-Close Wedge:** 5–8 hrs/wk delivery slots seamlessly alongside Tier A retainers without bandwidth disruption.
- **Direct Upstream & Downstream Pull:** Tabletop dry-runs reliably surface third-party ICT gaps (**`OP-RSK-006`** DORA/NIS2 RoI), AI incident-logging gaps (**`OP-RSK-004`** "SOC 2 for AI" & **`OP-ITG-013`**), and evidence-assurance gaps (**`OP-RSK-005`**).
- **Retainer & Proof Engine:** Converts one-off sprints into recurring **`OP-ITG-018`** (MIP governance) retainers and anonymized **`OP-X-004`** case studies.
