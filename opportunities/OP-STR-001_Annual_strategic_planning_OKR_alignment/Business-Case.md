## 2. Business Need & Cost of Inaction

Mid-market CEOs and PE Operating Partners lose quarters of execution velocity when annual plans stay disconnected from measurable OKRs and resource trade-offs:

- **Cost of Inaction:** Conflicting departmental priorities, zombie projects consuming budget without ROI, missed board milestones, and leadership team misalignment.
- **Commercial Value Proposition:** A fixed-fee 2-week facilitated reset (4-week total engagement) converts founder/board strategy into quantified quarterly OKRs, explicit stop-doing decisions, and resource-allocated owner scorecards.

## 4. Commercial Packaging, $0 Working-Capital Model & Compounding Path

- **Package Structure:** Fixed-scope Strategy & OKR Reset (€1,000–€4,000 based on leadership team size and workshop count) delivered in 4 weeks with zero software dependency.
- **Working-Capital Discipline:** `\$0` external spend or tooling cost; uses CBa Consult's **Corporate/Business Strategy & Strategic Planning** facilitation kit and client-owned spreadsheets/decks.
- **Compounding & Cross-Sell Path:**
  1. **Quarterly Strategy-Review Retainer:** Convert annual reset clients into a recurring 1-day/quarter QBR facilitation & OKR recalibration retainer.
  2. **Tier A Pipeline Feeder:** Strategic resets routinely surface AI adoption, Continuous Close finance (`OP-FIN-009`), or governance (`OP-ITG-018`) priorities that feed Tier A retainers.
