Supporting artifacts: [Ideation-Spark.md](Ideation-Spark.md) and [Source-Documents.md](Source-Documents.md).

## 1. Problem Context & 2027 Market Trigger

Mid-market and large organizations face escalating 2027 compliance reporting cycles across CSRD, GRI, TCFD, and customer ESG questionnaires. Yet sustainability data inside most firms remains trapped in fragmented annual spreadsheets across finance, HR, facilities, and procurement—resulting in manual reconciliation errors, late submissions, and weak audit trails.

CFOs, Chief Sustainability Officers, and ESG/CSRD Leads want transparent, repeatable ESG data pipelines and reporting dashboards without committing to six-figure enterprise ESG software licenses or heavy custom IT integration projects.

## 2. Solution Architecture & Delivery Workflow

To honor CBa Consult's $0 initial working-capital model, `OP-SUS-005` adapts the ADPA "cloud-based SDaaS platform" vision into a **productized ESG data-pipeline & BI dashboard service** deployed inside the client's existing analytics stack (e.g., Power BI / M365 / modern data warehouse) over 8–10 weeks:

1. **ESG Metric & Disclosure Mapping:** Align client data sources to target reporting schemas (CSRD/ESRS indicators, GRI, TCFD, or SDG metrics) with clear metric definitions and data owners.
2. **Automated Data-Ingestion Blueprint & Templates:** Build standardized collection templates and repeatable ETL/Power Query pipelines aggregating energy, waste, workforce, and supplier metrics.
3. **Audit-Ready BI Dashboard Suite:** Deploy interactive executive and compliance dashboards with lineage notes, period-over-period variance tracking, and exportable disclosure packs.
4. **Recurring Data-Ops & Refresh Cadence:** Transition into a lightweight quarterly/monthly data-stewardship subscription for indicator updates, supplier-data normalization, and regulatory schema maintenance.

## 5. Explicit Negative Scope Boundaries & Stage 1b Gate

To avoid capital-intensive SaaS platform development and statutory audit liability, `OP-SUS-005` enforces strict boundaries:

- **No Proprietary Multi-Tenant Cloud SaaS Hosting:** CBa Consult does not build or host a custom multi-tenant SaaS application; pipelines run on the client's existing BI/cloud environment.
- **No Statutory Audit / Assurance Opinion:** Dashboards provide audit-ready lineage and evidence packs, while accredited external auditors retain statutory sign-off.
- **No Unbounded Custom Supplier Portals:** Supplier intake relies on standardized templates/forms rather than bespoke vendor portal software engineering.
- **No Guaranteed Real-Time IoT Sensor Integration:** Direct industrial telemetry/SCADA integrations require separate OT/engineering scoping.
