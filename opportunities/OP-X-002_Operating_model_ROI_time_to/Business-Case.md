## 2. Business Need & Cost of Inaction

Buyers evaluating operational or AI governance engagements in 2027 frequently delay decisions because internal champions lack credible, standardized ROI and payback math for CFO review. Without a shared time-to-value calculator, CBa Consult and its partner cohorts must rebuild bespoke business-case spreadsheets for every pitch. Because [Source-Documents.md](Source-Documents.md) assigns zero ADPA `.docx` digests to `OP-X-002`, no pre-pilot conversion lift or licensing revenue totals are modeled here; any ROI figures generated by the tool itself must be explicitly labeled as scenario assumptions rather than guaranteed outcomes.
