# Ideation Document — `OP-X-002`: Operating-Model ROI & Time-to-Value Calculator

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Governance & SSOT Callout:** This Stage 1b Ideation Document is governed by [Tailored Hybrid SIDPA Governance](../../docs/governance/Tailored-Hybrid-SIDPA-Governance.md) and linked to [inventory-of-ideas.md](../../inventory-of-ideas.md) and [Ideation-Spark.md](Ideation-Spark.md). No realized calculator benchmark data or partner licensing revenue is claimed prior to Stage 4 approval.

## 1. Problem Context & 2027 Market Trigger

Founders, partner ecosystems, and mid-market leaders evaluating operational improvements across finance, HR, IT governance, or sales frequently stall because they cannot quantify expected ROI, hours saved, and payback periods cleanly before committing budget. In 2027, buyers demand transparent, auditable business-case math upfront before approving even modest advisory wedges.

- **Target Buyer:** Founders, Partners, and Mid-Market Leaders (**All** tiers; serves both direct clients and CBa Consult ecosystem partners).
- **Core Wedge Pain:** Lack of a standardized, credible, cross-functional model to estimate hours saved, risk reduction, and payback across CBa Consult's 16 functional areas.
- **2027 Market Trigger:** CFO/procurement gatekeeping requires clear pre-engagement payback justification, while CBa Consult benefits from a reusable discovery asset that accelerates Tier A/B/C deal conversion.

## 2. Solution Architecture & Delivery Workflow

`OP-X-002` is a **Tool / Toolkit (Market Analyst)** offering built on the **Financial Modeling, Planning & Analysis** signature toolkit, adapted from 2026 `OP-MA-002`, with an **8-week** build horizon (Medium effort, **$0 initial working capital**):

1. **Weeks 1–2 — Parameter Schema Design:** Define standardized input/output drivers (baseline hours, loaded labor rates, error/rework cost, ramp time) across all 16 functional pillars.
2. **Weeks 3–4 — Calculator Engine Build:** Construct a transparent spreadsheet workbook and optional lightweight static web calculator with locked assumption guardrails and sensitivity ranges.
3. **Weeks 5–6 — Cross-Pillar Calibration:** Validate assumption ranges against Tier A/B diagnostic patterns and bundle hooks for `OP-OPS-003` and partner cohorts.
4. **Weeks 7–8 — UX Polish & Documentation:** Add plain-language disclaimers, printable executive summary output, and partner walkthrough guide.

## 5. Explicit Negative Scope Boundaries & Stage 1b Gate

To avoid turning a lightweight enabling toolkit into a full SaaS product build, `OP-X-002` enforces strict boundaries:

- **No Multi-Tenant SaaS Platform:** No user accounts, cloud databases, subscription billing engines, or enterprise SOC2 SaaS hosting.
- **No Guaranteed Financial Returns:** Calculator outputs must carry explicit labels distinguishing modeled assumptions from realized client actuals.
- **No Live ERP Data Connectors:** Inputs are manually entered or pasted from standard exports rather than fetched via custom API integrations.
- **Stage 1b Gate Readiness:** Capability framing is complete; assumption-accuracy controls and partner/bundle monetization are evaluated in [Business-Case.md](Business-Case.md).
