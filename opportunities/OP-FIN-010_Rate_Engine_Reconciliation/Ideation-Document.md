## Positioning

> For **CFOs, Controllers, and Practice Leads** at small-and-mid professional-services firms who struggle with **a 7-layer statutory-to-contract rate waterfall that never reconciles cleanly into Revenue and Cost of Sales**, **Statutory-to-Contract Rate Engine & Revenue/CoS Reconciliation** is an **Audit / Diagnostic** backed by **a 2005–2006 unified-payroll baseline methodology initiated by Menno Drescher** that delivers **real-time hourly & all-in cost-rate truth against billable hours, sales rates, Revenue and CoS** with $0 working capital.

## What it is

Reconcile the 7-layer policy waterfall (Global → Country Tax/Social Security → Sector → CLA/CAO → Entity → Division/Dept → Employment Contract) plus CoS benefit/asset items (laptop, study, company car, flex-benefits schema & individual cost exceptions) into a **real-time Standard All-In Hourly CoS Rate anchored to `1,500` annual billable hours/year** (`~72.1%` net billable utilization on a `2,080`-hr `52 × 40h` FTE year — i.e., `125.0` hrs/calendar month, or `115.38` hrs per 4-week / `144.23` hrs per 5-week `4-4-5` period) vs. sales rates, Revenue, and Cost of Sales (CoS), with Autonomous Accounting Readiness controls and `€0.00` monthly absorption-variance tie-out (`FIN-CC-10` in [RGP-23rd-to-3rd-Playbook-Internal.md](../OP-FIN-009_Continuous_Close/RGP-23rd-to-3rd-Playbook-Internal.md)).

## 7 Pivotal Questions

1. **Buyer & 2027 Trigger** — CFO / Controller / Practice Leads. The Dutch Wtp (Wet toekomst pensioenen) transition deadline (Jan 2028) forces exactly this kind of statutory-to-contract reconciliation work onto finance teams' 2027 roadmaps.
2. **The Wedge (Pain)** — Multi-country payroll/tax/CLA/flex-benefit rules create a 7-layer waterfall that most firms never reconcile down to a true all-in cost rate — so billable-hour pricing and margin reporting run on stale or wrong assumptions.
3. **Deliverable & Existing IP** — Builds on Menno's own pioneering move (initiated 1 Jan 2006) to bring company-car inclusion into payroll to secure social-security premiums toward a unified payroll baseline — proprietary provenance (🟢🟢 in the inventory), closing the gap between HR/statutory payroll reality and commercial Revenue/CoS reporting. Connects `OP-FIN-009` (23rd→3rd+ agentic close) with `OP-OPS-002` (Quote-to-Cash) into an end-to-end P&L margin-truth engine.
4. **Sweat-Equity Feasibility** — Tighter than most Tier A items: 8–10 weeks at 8–12 hrs/wk, High effort. Realistic for one person only once `OP-FIN-009` is already piloted and the close cadence is clean.
5. **Outcome & Proof** — A reconciled, real-time all-in cost rate per employee/role that ties cleanly to billable rates, Revenue, and CoS — with GAAP/IFRS Revenue kept separated from operational WIP until after the post-3rd close.
6. **Boundaries & Risk** — Cross-border tax/social-security, CLA, and flex-benefit updates require versioned effective-dating or the engine silently drifts out of compliance; this is not a one-time build, it needs a maintenance cadence.
7. **Compounding / Scale Path** — Converges directly with `OP-STR-004`/`OP-STR-005` (M&A Readiness & PMI Governance Office), where payroll/pension/escrow harmonization is a named pre-deal and post-close workstream.
