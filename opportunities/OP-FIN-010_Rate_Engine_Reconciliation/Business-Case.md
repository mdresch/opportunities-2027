## 2. Business Need & Cost of Inaction

Professional-services CFOs face margin leakage whenever statutory payroll, company cars, flex-benefits, CLA/CAO adjustments, and country social-security rules sit in disconnected HR files while Practice Leads price engagements off legacy cost-rate tables. Inaction leads to underpriced fixed-fee bids, unexplained monthly CoS absorption variances, and messy pre-close accruals that delay management reporting.
