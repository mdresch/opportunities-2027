SECTION 9: ARCHITECTURAL DECISION LOGS

9.1 [FIELD 33] Formal Decision, Alternative Analysis & Rationale Log

Decision: Resequence the Kairos Bay Ideation-phase WBS and schedule so that Persona & Experience Requirements Definition, High-Level Service Design, and Commercial Terms Definition (WBS 1.1–1.3) precede Partner Evaluation & Selection (WBS 2.1), reversing the prior baseline in which Partner Selection Finalization was the first-scheduled, gating activity.

Alternative considered and rejected — "Partner-First" (prior baseline): Select a partner first (scored on authenticity-vs-scale trade-offs alone), then develop the Service Design Blueprint and Commercial Framework within that partner's existing operating constraints. Rejected because: it risks anchoring the blueprint to whichever partner is chosen first, rather than choosing a partner because it best serves an independently-defined blueprint — directly reintroducing the R-01 risk this amendment exists to close, and structurally biasing the outcome toward whichever candidate has the more persuasive relationship-building presentation rather than the better specification fit.

Alternative considered and rejected — "Fully parallel" development: Run Partner Evaluation & Selection concurrently with Blueprint and Commercial Framework definition, on the theory that this saves calendar time. Rejected because: without a finalized blueprint to score against, "parallel" partner evaluation would in practice default to scoring partners on generic brand strength (Mediterranean provenance, Bonvoy scale) rather than blueprint fit, collapsing back into the rejected Partner-First model under a different label.

Chosen rationale: The Project Owner's explicit direction — that partnership is an enabler of the defined integrated brand and service experience, not the primary value-delivery objective — is architecturally implemented by making WBS 1.x a hard predecessor to WBS 2.1 in the schedule baseline (Field 10), by demoting the "Optimal Partner Selection" success criterion from Objective 1 to Objective 3 (Field 07), and by reframing R-01 around premature partner selection rather than "wrong" partner selection (Field 29). This creates one auditable requirements chain of custody (Field 26) from persona to partner, and accepts the modest critical-path lengthening identified in R-03 as the necessary cost of that ordering.
