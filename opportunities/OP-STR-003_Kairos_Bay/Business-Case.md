## 2. Business Need & Cost of Inaction

Hospitality and corporate-retreat ventures routinely destroy differentiation by negotiating hotel-chain partnerships *before* codifying their guest service blueprint and commercial terms—allowing legacy operator standards to constrain the product. Meanwhile, consulting clients launching asset-light brand concepts need a disciplined governance framework that validates value delivery ahead of channel lock-in.

**Cost of Inaction & ADPA Source Baseline:**
- **For Clients:** Partner-first dealmaking leads to unfavorable revenue splits, diluted customer experience, and costly contract renegotiations.
- **For CBa Consult:** Repurposing the `Kairos Bay` re-sequencing logic as a reusable venture-blueprinting sprint monetizes Strategy IP without diverting capital into hospitality operations.
- **ADPA Modeling Disclaimer:** The 5 assigned ADPA documents in `Opportunities Business/Kairos Bay/` establish an amended v2 ideation baseline (`2026-07-08`, 0.70 completeness threshold) without fixed ROI/NPV spreadsheets. Any illustrative hospitality RevPAR, conference-package pricing, or partner revenue-share projections remain *Illustrative Pre-Pilot ADPA Source Modeling — Not Realized Client Actuals* until `WBS 1.3` commercial modeling is validated.

## 4. Commercial Packaging, $0 Working-Capital Model & Compounding Path

- **Zero-Capital Model:** Strictly asset-light—zero property acquisition, zero lease liabilities, and zero pre-funded event inventory. All work uses existing CBa Consult Strategy & Business Plan templates.
- **Commercial Packaging:** **€1,500–€5,000** fixed-scope **Blueprint-Before-Partner Strategy Sprint** covering Guest/Customer Persona Baseline (`WBS 1.1`), High-Level Service Blueprint (`WBS 1.2`), Commercial Operating Terms (`WBS 1.3`), and Partner Selection Scorecard (`WBS 2.x`), invoiced 50% at kickoff and 50% upon blueprint baseline approval.
