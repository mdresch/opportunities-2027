## 2. Business Need & Cost of Inaction

Mid-market publishers, specialist consultancies, and domain-rich enterprises hold curated knowledge libraries that AI builders increasingly need for domain fine-tuning and RAG evaluation benchmarks. Without structured provenance, PII/GDPR scrubbing, and standardized licensing schemas, asset owners either leave high-value IP unmonetized or risk unmanaged scraping without attribution or royalties.

*Illustrative Pre-Pilot ADPA Source Modeling — Not Realized Client Actuals*: the underlying ADPA `.docx` outlines an AI dataset licensing framework emphasizing GDPR/CCPA compliance and recurring royalties, without realized client financial actuals; quantitative unit economics remain subject to Stage 2/3 pilot validation.

## 4. Commercial Packaging, $0 Working-Capital Model & Compounding Path

`OP-ING-002` avoids software platform capex by delivering a fixed-scope packaging, governance, and licensing readiness kit using client-owned archives:

- **Starter Packaging Sprint (6–8 wks):** Rights/provenance checklist, PII redaction SOP, JSONL/RAG-eval structuring guide, and standardized licensing term-sheet templates.
- **Compounding Path:** Repeatable across niche B2B publishers and industry associations, with optional royalty-structuring advisory or downstream AI governance referrals.
