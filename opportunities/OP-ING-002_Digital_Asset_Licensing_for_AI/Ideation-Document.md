# Ideation Document — `OP-ING-002`: Digital Asset Licensing for AI Training

> **Governance & SSOT Callout:** This Stage 1b Ideation Document is governed by the [Tailored Hybrid SIDPA Governance](../../docs/governance/Tailored-Hybrid-SIDPA-Governance.md) framework and anchored to the [Inventory of Ideas](../../inventory-of-ideas.md) and [Tier A Executive Summary](../../Tier_A_Executive_Summary_2027.md). Supporting artifacts: [Ideation-Spark.md](Ideation-Spark.md) and [Source-Documents.md](Source-Documents.md).

## 1. Problem Context & 2027 Market Trigger

AI model builders and enterprise RAG teams increasingly struggle with plateauing returns from scraped public web corpora, alongside rising legal and regulatory scrutiny under the EU AI Act, GDPR, and copyright enforcement actions. Meanwhile, niche publishers, domain experts, and mid-market enterprises sit on valuable proprietary archives—technical documentation, structured case libraries, industry taxonomies, and specialized media—that remain unmonetized.

In 2027, Founders, Innovation Leads, and Product Owners need practical, defensible ways to package, govern, and license domain-specific datasets for AI fine-tuning and RAG evaluation without building a bespoke legal or data-engineering department from scratch.

## 2. Solution Architecture & Delivery Workflow

`OP-ING-002` translates the ADPA concept into a lean, service-plus-toolkit advisory product rather than a capital-heavy two-sided marketplace:

1. **Asset Inventory & Rights Provenance Audit:** Catalog the client's proprietary content or data libraries and verify chain-of-title, third-party IP clearances, and PII/GDPR redaction requirements.
2. **Dataset Structuring & RAG Evaluation Packaging:** Clean, chunk, annotate, and format high-signal assets into standardized machine-readable bundles (JSONL/Parquet + metadata schemas + evaluation benchmarks).
3. **Commercial Licensing & Governance Kit:** Prepare standardized non-exclusive licensing term sheets, attribution/usage guardrails, and EU AI Act training-data transparency summaries (subject to client legal review).
4. **Buyer Readiness & Distribution Enablement:** Package data cards and sample evaluation subsets so the asset owner can license directly to enterprise AI teams or curated data brokers.

## 5. Explicit Negative Scope Boundaries & Stage 1b Gate

To uphold CBa Consult's $0 working-capital constraint and avoid platform-build traps, `OP-ING-002` enforces strict boundaries:

- **No Custom Two-Sided Marketplace Platform:** Building and operating a standalone SaaS marketplace or payment escrow platform is explicitly excluded from Stage 1–4 scope.
- **No Unlicensed Scraping or Third-Party Data Aggregation:** Only assets with clear client ownership or explicit redistribution rights are eligible.
- **No Binding Legal Representation:** Licensing templates and compliance checklists do not constitute formal legal opinions; client IP counsel retains final contract sign-off.
- **No Regulated Clinical/Medical Data Brokering:** High-liability datasets (e.g., identifiable patient medical imaging) are excluded until dedicated regulatory partners are contracted.
