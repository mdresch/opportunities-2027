## 2. Business Need & Cost of Inaction

Medium and large enterprises suffer persistent margin leakage from manual handoffs, duplicate data entry, and disconnected Quote-to-Cash / Sales-to-Operations workflows. Under 2027 cost-discipline pressures, CROs and RevOps leaders must unlock throughput and eliminate operational drag without launching slow, expensive ERP overhauls.

- **Cost of Inaction:** Ongoing cycle-time delays, elevated error rates, staff burnout on manual workarounds, and stalled revenue realization.
- **Quantitative Evidence Baseline:** *Illustrative Pre-Pilot ADPA Source Modeling — Not Realized Client Actuals:* The ADPA ideation digest cites an illustrative industry claim that enterprises lose 20–30% of annual revenue to suboptimal processes and operational drag; this is macro context rather than realized CBa Consult client actuals. Pilot #1 must establish verifiable before/after cycle-time and defect baselines.

## 4. Commercial Packaging, $0 Working-Capital Model & Compounding Path

`OP-OPS-004` executes on $0 initial working capital by applying CBa Consult's Lean 6 Sigma Signature Toolkit and process-mining / automation-design skills over an 8–12 week program. Any third-party automation or iPaaS licenses remain on client infrastructure.

- **Packaging Structure:** Fixed-scope 8–12 week transformation diagnostic (€3,000–€10,000 band) delivering value-stream maps, quantified bottleneck baselines, Lean waste-elimination rules, and a prioritized automation architecture blueprint.
- **Compounding Path:** Serves as the structured implementation-design phase pairing directly with `OP-OPS-002` and `OP-DTX-001`, while capturing hard before/after operational metrics for `OP-X-004`.
