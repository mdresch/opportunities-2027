## 1. Problem Context & 2027 Market Trigger

Medium and large organizations frequently lose substantial operational capacity to fragmented handoffs, manual workarounds, and disconnected Sales-to-Operations / Quote-to-Cash workflows. As highlighted in the ADPA source framing, manual bottlenecks create hidden operational drag, inconsistent customer experiences, and frustrated staff.

- **2027 Market Trigger:** Heightened 2027 margin pressure forces CROs and Sales/RevOps Leads in Medium & Large enterprises to eliminate manual overhead and accelerate revenue-cycle execution without committing to multi-year Big Four transformation programs.
- **Core Buyer Pain:** Core cross-functional workflows suffer from unmeasured cycle-time bottlenecks, duplicate data entry, and legacy system workarounds that block scalable growth.
- **Target Buyer Profile:** CROs and Sales/RevOps Leads at Medium & Large organizations seeking a structured, measurable transformation program.

## 2. Solution Architecture & Delivery Workflow

`OP-OPS-004` pairs Lean 6 Sigma diagnostic rigor with targeted workflow automation over an 8–12 week Audit / Diagnostic and transformation design program (High effort, $0 initial working capital).

1. **Value-Stream & Process Mining Discovery (Weeks 1–3):** Map end-to-end Quote-to-Cash / Sales-to-Operations workflows, quantify cycle-time variance, and locate manual bottlenecks.
2. **Root-Cause & Lean Elimination Design (Weeks 4–6):** Apply Lean 6 Sigma waste elimination to simplify handoffs and standardize business rules prior to automating broken steps.
3. **Automation Architecture & Pilot Blueprint (Weeks 7–9):** Design pragmatic workflow automations respecting legacy system constraints, defining KPI baselines and ROI/effort prioritization.
4. **Change Readiness & Implementation Handoff (Weeks 10–12):** Deliver executive transformation roadmap, stakeholder adoption plan, and implementation backlog (pairing cleanly with `OP-OPS-002` and `OP-DTX-001`).

## 5. Explicit Negative Scope Boundaries & Stage 1b Gate

Because stakeholder resistance and legacy system constraints pose high delivery risk, `OP-OPS-004` maintains strict scope discipline:

- **No Core ERP Replacement:** Work focuses on process redesign and lightweight workflow automation around existing systems rather than full ERP/CRM replatforming.
- **No Unbounded Custom Software Engineering:** Deliverables cover process architecture, automation specs, and bounded pilot configurations; enterprise-scale software builds require separate scoping.
- **No Guaranteed Headcount Reductions:** Efficiency gains are framed around capacity reclamation, cycle-time compression, and error reduction, leaving organizational staffing decisions with client leadership.
- **Stage 1b Gate Readiness:** ADPA ideation digest and Lean 6 Sigma capability alignment are complete. Requires Director adjudication on the Business Case and Stage 2 hypothesis testing.
