## 2. Business Need & Cost of Inaction

Small and mid-market firms promoting technical specialists into management roles in 2027 face high manager burnout, inconsistent delegation, and costly regrettable attrition when leadership upskilling is deferred. Traditional 1:1 executive coaching is budget-prohibitive beyond senior executives, while passive self-paced video libraries yield poor workplace application. Note on legacy source figures: the assigned ADPA `.docx` business-case digest models a multi-instructor consumer platform targeting 1,000 active learners and "$1.2M in annual revenue by Year 3" (*Illustrative Pre-Pilot ADPA Source Modeling — Not Realized Client Actuals*). Those legacy marketplace projections do not apply to CBa Consult's B2B advisory model and are replaced here by a lean €1,000–€4,000 per-cohort pilot framing.

## 4. Commercial Packaging, $0 Working-Capital Model & Compounding Path

- **Cohort-Based Packaging:** Fixed fee of €1,500–€4,000 per B2B cohort (6–12 participants over 6–12 weeks), delivered over client-licensed Zoom/Teams with downloadable workbook PDFs and zero custom LMS infrastructure.
- **$0 Working-Capital Mechanics:** Upfront cohort invoice prior to Week 1 kickoff; curriculum and PMBOK governance artifacts are adapted directly from the existing 38-document ADPA suite.
- **Compounding Path:** Replicate the validated cohort curriculum across multiple HR clients with minimal marginal prep, capture pre/post manager-readiness proof (`OP-X-004`), and cross-sell HR operating-model or AI-enablement diagnostics.
