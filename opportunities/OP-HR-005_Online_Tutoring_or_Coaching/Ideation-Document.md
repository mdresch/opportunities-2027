1. Executive Summary

(250 words)

This Ideation Template outlines the strategic development of an Online Tutoring or Coaching Platform, designed to deliver high-value, niche educational services through digital channels. Leveraging the PMBOK 7 framework, this initiative aligns with the growing demand for flexible, on-demand learning in specialized subjects, from academic tutoring to professional skill development. The platform will operate via Zoom, Udemy, and private coaching sessions, targeting learners seeking personalized, expert-led instruction.

The project addresses key market gaps, including:

Accessibility: Breaking geographical barriers for learners and instructors.

Niche Expertise: Offering specialized subjects not widely available in traditional education.

Scalability: Enabling instructors to monetize their skills while maintaining low operational overhead.

Core Objectives include:

Launching a minimum viable product (MVP) within 6 months, featuring 50+ instructors across 10+ subjects.

Achieving 1,000 active learners within the first year, with a 90% satisfaction rate.

Establishing a sustainable revenue model through subscription tiers, pay-per-session, and course sales.

Strategic Value:

Future-Proofing: Capitalizes on the $10B+ global e-learning market, projected to grow at 14% CAGR through 2027.

Differentiation: Combines live interaction (Zoom) with self-paced learning (Udemy) for a hybrid experience.

Social Impact: Democratizes education for underserved communities and professionals seeking upskilling.

Next Steps:

Validate demand through market research and pilot programs.

Secure partnerships with instructors, platforms (e.g., Udemy), and payment processors.

Develop a go-to-market strategy targeting learners and instructors via digital marketing.

Submit Request: Stakeholder submits a Change Request Form (CRF) via Jira.

Initial Review: Project Manager assesses urgency and completeness.

Impact Analysis: CCB evaluates scope, cost, and timeline impact.

Approval/Rejection: CCB votes (majority required for approval).

Implementation: Approved changes are prioritized in the backlog.

Communication: Stakeholders are notified of the decision.

Documentation: Changes are logged in the Change Log (Confluence).

Requestor: Name, role, contact.

Description: Detailed change description.
