## 1. Problem Context & 2027 Market Trigger

Small and mid-market organizations face persistent leadership and capability gaps as AI adoption, hybrid work, and cost discipline reshape manager expectations in 2027. CHROs, People Ops leads, and HR Transformation managers cannot justify executive coaching retainers priced at €400–€800/hour across broader manager cohorts, yet generic self-paced e-learning platforms suffer low completion and weak workplace transfer. The legacy ADPA *Online Tutoring or Coaching* suite (38 project artifacts) originally framed a broad multi-instructor consumer marketplace via Zoom and Udemy; for CBa Consult in 2027, that raw concept must be reframed into a focused B2B advisory and cohort-based leadership coaching program backed by the **Leadership Development** signature toolkit.

## 2. Solution Architecture & Delivery Workflow

Rather than building a two-sided consumer tutoring marketplace, CBa Consult delivers a structured 6–12 week B2B cohort coaching and capability program with $0 initial working capital:

1. **Capability Diagnostic & Persona Alignment (Weeks 1–2):** Map target participant cohorts (first-time managers, project leads, or functional specialists) against client competency gaps using existing ADPA persona and curriculum templates.
2. **Hybrid Curriculum Packaging (Weeks 3–4):** Combine live facilitated group sessions (Zoom/Teams) with structured micro-learning modules, reflection workbooks, and practical workplace assignments.
3. **Cohort Delivery & Facilitated Practice (Weeks 5–10):** Run weekly or bi-weekly 60–90 minute live coaching clinics with peer accountability and manager check-ins, keeping solo-advisor time leveraged across 6–12 participants per cohort.
4. **Transfer Evaluation & Playbook Handover (Weeks 11–12):** Deliver before/after skill-confidence assessment, attendance/completion metrics, and reusable internal facilitation guides for client HR continuity.

## 5. Explicit Negative Scope Boundaries & Stage 1b Gate

- **Explicitly Out of Scope:** Building custom LMS/marketplace software, recruiting 50+ third-party instructors, B2C academic K-12/university tutoring, clinical psychological counseling, and accredited degree/certification issuance.
- **Key Boundary Guardrail:** Do not pursue open-ended 1:1 coaching retainers; every engagement must use reusable cohort templates so preparation scales across clients.
- **Stage 1b Gate Decision:** Ready for Stage 2 B2B buyer validation and Stage 3 cohort pricing adjudication; aligned with [Tier_A_Executive_Summary_2027.md](../../Tier_A_Executive_Summary_2027.md) portfolio guardrails against non-core platform builds.
