## 2. Business Need & Cost of Inaction

Early-stage B2B founders and lean marketing teams struggle to break through paid-ad saturation without spending €4k–€12k/month on traditional PR retainers. Niche podcasts and B2B micro-influencers (1k–100k followers) offer high-trust distribution, yet founders lack curated target lists and structured outreach workflows.

**Cost of Inaction & ADPA Source Baseline:**
- **For Clients:** Relying solely on paid search/social drives rising customer-acquisition cost (CAC), while unguided VA cold pitches damage brand credibility.
- **For CBa Consult:** A bounded 4–6 week Launch Micro-PR Sprint offers low-risk, $0-capital cash flow and proof generation only when spare capacity exists.
- **ADPA Modeling Disclaimer:** The assigned ADPA source (`3 Influencer Outreach Micro-PR Service.docx`) originally modeled a full matchmaking platform targeting 500 micro-campaigns, $250,000 Year-1 revenue via 15–20% commissions, a 10,000+ creator database, 70% setup-time reduction, and 90% NPS. *Illustrative Pre-Pilot ADPA Source Modeling — Not Realized Client Actuals:* those SaaS marketplace targets are legacy speculative assumptions from the source document and are **excluded** from CBa Consult's lean 2027 operating model.

## 4. Commercial Packaging, $0 Working-Capital Model & Compounding Path

- **Zero-Capital Model:** Executed using existing CBa Consult outreach templates, spreadsheet trackers, and standard email/LinkedIn workflows ($0 software build; any paid creator sponsorships are contracted directly by the client).
- **Pricing Anchor:** **€500–€2,500** fixed fee per 4–6 week Launch Micro-PR Sprint (50% upfront upon media-list kickoff / 50% at campaign debrief and UTM attribution handoff).
- **Measurable Outcome KPI:** Delivery of 25–40 vetted niche targets, documented multi-touch outreach completion, and tracked referral traffic / qualified conversations via UTMs.
- **Compounding Path:** Productizes into a repeatable sprint kit and builds early case-study proof (`OP-X-004`) for small-tier marketing clients without pulling capacity away from Core 5 Tier A engagements.
