## 2. Business Need & Cost of Inaction

Small-tier founders and emerging People Ops leads frequently make costly hiring, onboarding, and performance-management errors because bespoke HR consulting is priced out of reach. Without structured peer and advisor feedback, early-stage teams suffer avoidable turnover and inconsistent manager rituals. Note that [Source-Documents.md](Source-Documents.md) assigns zero ADPA `.docx` digests to `OP-HR-004`; accordingly, no quantitative pre-pilot ROI or retention-lift claims are modeled prior to Q2 2027 cohort execution.

## 4. Commercial Packaging, $0 Working-Capital Model & Compounding Path

- **Session Pricing (€300–€1,500 / session):** Sold either per-seat for multi-company peer cohorts (€300–€500/seat) or as a dedicated leadership-team clinic (€1,000–€1,500/session).
- **$0 Working-Capital Model:** Pre-paid registration prior to each 4-week cycle; uses standard video conferencing and existing CBa Consult Leadership Development templates with zero venue or software spend.
- **Compounding Path:** Systematically funnels qualified clinic attendees into fixed-scope `OP-HR-001` HR starter kits while generating anonymized case-study proof (`OP-X-004`).
