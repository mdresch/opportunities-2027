No external pilot sessions or client revenues are recorded prior to Stage 4 gate clearance.

## 1. Problem Context & 2027 Market Trigger

Founders, CHROs, and People Ops leads in **Small**-tier organizations frequently encounter acute hiring, onboarding, and performance-management bottlenecks without the budget for a full-time HR Director or bespoke consultancy. In 2027, heightened cost discipline and growing regulatory expectations around structured people processes make lightweight, conflict-free peer and advisor forums highly attractive compared to expensive open-ended retainers.

- **Target Buyer:** Founders, CHROs, People Ops, and HR Transformation Leads (Small segment).
- **Core Wedge Pain:** Founders improvising job scorecards, onboarding checklists, and performance conversations in isolation, leading to costly mis-hires and inconsistent retention.
- **2027 Market Trigger:** Demand for fast, low-commitment (€300–€1,500 / session) practical problem-solving clinics that deliver immediate action plans and de-risk follow-on HR investments.

## 2. Solution Architecture & Delivery Workflow

`OP-HR-004` is a **Group Clinic** anchored in the **Leadership Development** signature toolkit, adapted from the 2026 `OP-ME-002` Group Clinic pattern and structured on a **4-week cycle** with **Low** effort and **$0 initial working capital**.

1. **Week 1 — Cohort Intake & Bottleneck Triage:** Collect structured pre-work briefs from 4–8 participating founders/HR leads covering one concrete hiring, onboarding, or performance bottleneck per seat.
2. **Week 2 — Pre-Clinic Template Mapping:** Match submitted cases against CBa Consult's leadership and people-ops frameworks to prepare targeted diagnostic prompts.
3. **Week 3 — Live 90-Minute Facilitated Clinic:** Run a structured hot-seat peer/advisor session where each participant leaves with a concrete 30-day action artifact.
4. **Week 4 — Follow-Up & Starter-Kit Funnel:** Distribute distilled clinic takeaways and invite qualified participants into fixed-scope `OP-HR-001` HR starter-kit engagements.

## 5. Explicit Negative Scope Boundaries & Stage 1b Gate

To maintain clinic quality and avoid legal or operational overreach, `OP-HR-004` enforces clear boundaries:

- **No Employment Law Counsel:** Clinic facilitation covers operational people-ops and leadership practices; statutory labor law, termination disputes, and works-council compliance are excluded.
- **No Retained Executive Search:** Does not include active candidate sourcing, screening, or placement fees.
- **No Custom HRIS Implementation:** Excludes configuring payroll, ATS, or HRIS software environments during clinic cycles.
- **Stage 1b Gate Readiness:** Framing is complete; scheduling and cohort segmentation risks (ensuring value across diverse company stages) are carried into [Business-Case.md](Business-Case.md) for Q2 2027 Director adjudication.
