## 2. Business Need & Cost of Inaction

Multi-entity mid-market and large organizations lose substantial finance capacity each month reconciling inconsistent close checklists, ad-hoc manual journals, and incompatible business-unit forecast workbooks. In 2027, failing to standardize close and FP&A workflows exposes CFOs to delayed management reporting, elevated audit-prep costs under IFRS 18 and CSRD/ESG assurance linkages, and weak cash-flow visibility during tighter capital cycles. Because no ADPA `.docx` financial model is assigned to `OP-FIN-002` (and zero Stage 2 pilot actuals exist yet), specific client ROI or close-day reduction percentages are deliberately omitted until a live baseline is measured during pilot delivery.

## 4. Commercial Packaging, $0 Working-Capital Model & Compounding Path

- **Fixed-Scope Packaging:** Tiered fixed fee based on business-unit count (e.g., €2,500–€4,500 for 2–3 entities; €5,000–€8,000 for 4+ entities), billed 50% upfront on kickoff and 50% on Golden-Standard Blueprint delivery, maintaining $0 working-capital exposure.
- **Zero-Software Overhead:** Built entirely on client-owned Excel/ERP exports and CBa Consult's reusable diagnostic scorecard templates.
- **Compounding Path:** Each engagement yields a reusable cross-industry close-maturity benchmark, generates referenceable case-study proof (`OP-X-004`), and serves as a natural wedge into recurring FP&A governance or Tier A/B transformation retainers.
