## 2. Business Need & Cost of Inaction

Under DORA, NIS2, EU tech-sovereignty mandates, and CLOUD Act extraterritorial jurisdiction risks, boards of regulated European enterprises, defense contractors, and healthcare institutions face severe legal, operational, and reputational exposure if a single hyperscaler, KMS/HSM operator, privileged admin, or foreign subpoena can unilaterally reconstruct sensitive data. Standard cloud compliance audits only verify physical storage region ("where data sits"), leaving reconstruction vulnerability unmeasured. Because `OP-ITG-015` has 0 assigned ADPA `.docx` files and is pre-Stage 2, no fabricated financial loss or breach-probability ROI percentages are asserted here; value is grounded in board-level risk reduction and regulatory defensibility.
