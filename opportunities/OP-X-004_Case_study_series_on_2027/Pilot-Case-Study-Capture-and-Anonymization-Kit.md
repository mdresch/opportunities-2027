# Day-1 Pilot Case-Study Capture & 3-Tier Anonymization Kit (`OP-X-004 × OP-X-006`)

> **Why Day 1, Not Readout Day:** Asking a CFO or CISO for public case-study approval at final readout fails because commercial leverage is gone and audit/cyber findings feel sensitive. This kit locks baseline capture and privacy boundaries **at SOW / Pilot Charter signature**, pairs consent with `OP-X-006` Early-Adopter Co-Design incentives, and restricts each pilot's total documentation overhead to **≤4.0 solo hours**.

---

## 1. The 3-Tier Client Anonymization & Consent Protocol

Every Wave 1–2 Pilot Charter selects one tier upfront. Higher tiers carry a pre-agreed `OP-X-006` co-design benefit while preserving a hard client redaction veto:

| Consent Tier | What May Be Published | Client Privacy Guarantee | Recommended `OP-X-006` Co-Design Trade |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Tier 1 — Blind Telemetry Only** | Industry sector band, headcount band (e.g., *150–400 employees*), functional wedge ID, and anonymized KPI delta inside aggregate benchmark tables | Zero company name, zero city/country, zero narrative context, zero quotes | Standard pilot fee (minimum baseline permission so no pilot ends unmeasured) |
| **Tier 2 — Sanitized Archetype Case Brief** *(Recommended Default)* | Full **6-Block Operating-Model Brief** (`Context → Week-1 Baseline → Intervention → Verified Delta → Disclosed Pitfalls → Reusable Playbook`), role-attributed quote (*"Corporate Controller, European B2B Services Firm"*) | Mandatory **10-Point De-Identification Checklist** + strict **5-business-day written review/redaction veto** before publication | **10–15% Early-Adopter Co-Design Pilot Credit** (or complimentary executive readout / next-step scoping workshop) |
| **Tier 3 — Named Reference / Partner Case Study** | Client organization name, logo (if supplied), named executive sponsor quote, plus up to **2 × 15-min private peer reference calls/quarter** for qualified Tier A / `OP-X-011` prospects | Explicit written approval of final HTML/PDF text and logo placement; revocable on 30 days' notice for peer calls | **15% Early-Adopter Co-Design Credit + priority early access** to updated 2027 templates/playbooks |

## 2. Ready-to-Insert SOW / Pilot Charter Consent Clause (`OP-X-004 × OP-X-006`)

*Paste the block below verbatim into client scoping proposals or Wave 1–3 Pilot Charters:*

> **Clause X — Operating-Model Baseline Telemetry & Case-Study Co-Design (`OP-X-004` / `OP-X-006`):**  
> During Week 1 and Final Readout, CBa Consult and Client shall jointly record operational before/after KPI metrics relevant to this Pilot (for example: close cycle business days, clearing variance, handoff rework loops, or governed control score). Client selects its publication permission tier below at kickoff:
> - `[ ]` **Tier 1 (Blind Telemetry Only):** CBa Consult may include aggregated, non-attributable KPI deltas and anonymized industry/size bands in industry benchmarks.
> - `[ ]` **Tier 2 (Sanitized Operating-Model Brief — Co-Design Credit Applied):** CBa Consult may publish a sanitized 6-block operating-model brief describing the anonymized archetype, baseline, intervention, verified delta, and lessons learned, provided CBa Consult completes the 10-Point De-Identification Checklist and grants Client **five (5) business days** for written review, redaction, or veto prior to any external publication.
> - `[ ]` **Tier 3 (Named Reference — Co-Design Credit Applied):** Client grants Tier 2 rights plus approval of exact attribution wording, organization name, and optional private executive peer references (capped at two 15-minute calls per quarter) upon final written sign-off of the text.
>
> **Strict Boundaries:** Under no tier may CBa Consult publish personally identifiable information (PII), material non-public financial information (MNPI), security vulnerability details, vendor tenant IDs, or statutory auditor names. Nothing in any brief constitutes a statutory audit attestation.

## 3. 10-Point De-Identification & Sanitization Checklist

Complete every check before sending a Tier 2 draft to the client (target time: `≤45 mins`):

- [ ] **1. Entity & Brand Stripping:** Replace legal name, brand, product names, and subsidiaries with a neutral archetype (e.g., *"Mid-Market B2B SaaS & Services Provider, EU, ~180 FTE"*).
- [ ] **2. Geography Generalization:** Broaden city/street to region/jurisdiction only (e.g., *"Benelux"* or *"DACH"*) unless Tier 3 named.
- [ ] **3. Financial Banding:** Express revenue, ARR, and balance-sheet figures in wide bands (`€25M–€75M`), never exact statutory figures.
- [ ] **4. Stack Abstracted Cleanly:** Keep architectural pattern (*"mid-market cloud ERP + external payroll bureau"* or *"Microsoft 365 + Copilot Studio"*) while stripping tenant IDs, workspace URLs, and internal tool codenames.
- [ ] **5. Audit & Legal Firewall:** Remove external audit firm names, partner names, active litigation, or open Management Letter phrasing verbatim; summarize only structural control classes (e.g., *cut-off accrual timing*, *SoD conflict*).
- [ ] **6. Security & Cyber Redaction (`OP-ITG-013/014`):** Never print unpatched CVEs, service principal IDs, secret names, SharePoint site URLs, or unmasked employee counts by department if uniquely identifying.
- [ ] **7. Date Normalization:** Convert calendar dates to **Quarter + Relative Week** (*"Q1 2027 · Week 1 vs. Week 4"*).
- [ ] **8. Quote De-Identification:** Attribute quotes solely by role archetype (*"CFO"*, *"CISO"*, *"COO"*), stripped of idiosyncratic phrasing.
- [ ] **9. Anti-Selection-Bias Verification:** Verify Block 5 lists at least **1 real pitfall, precondition, or scope limitation** encountered during execution.
- [ ] **10. Client 5-Day Written Sign-Off:** Archive written client approval (or redaction notes) in the pilot workspace before switching brief from `Draft` to `Published`.
