## 1. Problem-Before-Solution: The 2027 Proof & Consent Gap

Across CBa Consult's 136-opportunity portfolio, `OP-X-004` is cited over 150 times as the mechanism that turns early SME/mid-market wedges into Q3–Q4 enterprise flagships (`OP-FIN-010`, `OP-ITG-015`, `OP-ITG-016`, `OP-STR-004`/`005`), recurring retainers (`OP-ITG-018` MIP), and channel partner sales (`OP-X-011`). Without operationalized discipline, however, consulting case studies fail in three predictable ways:

1. **Missing Week-1 Baselines:** Advisory sprints start fixing problems immediately without locking baseline timestamps, cutoff variances, rework counts, or shadow-agent counts—leaving no verifiable before/after delta at readout.
2. **Readout-Stage Legal/CISO Vetoes:** Asking for public endorsement *at final executive readout* (when pricing leverage is gone and audit/cyber findings are fresh) pushes Legal, Audit, and CISO stakeholders to default to *"no"*.
3. **Selection-Bias Skepticism:** Buyers in 2027 discount glossy testimonial PDFs that quote happy sponsors while hiding failure modes, ERP/M365 preconditions, and true internal client time spent.

**CBa Consult's Structural Answer:** Embed a lightweight **Day-1 Capture & 3-Tier Anonymization Kit** into every pilot SOW/Charter from kickoff (`OP-X-004 × OP-X-006`) and enforce a uniform **6-Block Anti-Selection-Bias Schema** backed by an honest Two-Class Evidence standard.
