## 1. Problem Context & 2027 Market Trigger

- **Target Buyer:** CFOs, Chief Sustainability Officers (CSOs), ESG/CSRD Leads, and Municipal/Real-Estate Sustainability Directors in Medium & Large organizations.
- **2027 Market Trigger:** CSRD biodiversity and ecosystem disclosures (ESRS E4), EU climate-adaptation pressures, urban heat-island mitigation mandates, and municipal SDG 11/13/15 commitments force asset owners and municipalities to move beyond carbon-only spreadsheets toward measurable nature-based solutions (NBS).
- **Core Wedge:** Real-estate portfolios and municipalities struggle to connect urban green infrastructure, biodiversity monitoring, and ecological restoration into a coherent governance, KPI, and multi-stakeholder delivery plan without getting trapped in expensive, disconnected engineering studies.

## 2. Solution Architecture & Delivery Workflow

Built on CBa Consult's **Management Consulting** Signature Toolkit and aligned with partner engineering/ecology specialists, the 8–12 week (High effort) advisory starter kit follows four phases:

1. **Weeks 1–3 — Baseline Asset & Stakeholder Alignment:** Map target urban/real-estate sites, municipal or corporate CSRD/SDG priorities, and public/private stakeholder governance requirements.
2. **Weeks 4–6 — Nature-Based Solutions Portfolio Framing:** Structure priority interventions across urban green infrastructure, ecological restoration, and climate-resilience co-benefits.
3. **Weeks 7–9 — Biodiversity Monitoring & ESG Metric Architecture:** Define verifiable biodiversity/ESG KPIs, baseline monitoring cadences, and audit-ready reporting links for CSRD/ESRS E4 or municipal impact reporting.
4. **Weeks 10–12 — Partner Delivery & Funding Roadmap:** Deliver an implementation charter and tender/partner readiness pack enabling execution with specialized engineering and ecology firms.

## 5. Explicit Negative Scope Boundaries & Stage 1b Gate

- **In Scope:** Urban green infrastructure strategy, biodiversity/ESG metric framing, restoration program governance, stakeholder alignment, and partner-tender readiness.
- **Explicit Negative Scope (Out of Scope):** CBa Consult does **not** perform licensed civil/structural engineering, execute physical construction/landscaping works, conduct certified biological field-lab testing, or submit statutory environmental permits without qualified ecology partners.
- **Stage 1b Gate Criteria:** Establish pre-vetted Memorandum-of-Understanding (MoU) relationships with specialist ecology/engineering partners and focus initial outreach on commercial real-estate portfolios alongside municipalities to buffer long public-sector procurement cycles.
