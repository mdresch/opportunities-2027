## 2. Business Need & Cost of Inaction

Enterprise AI investments stall at the frontline manager layer: Work Trend Index (WTI) benchmarks highlight a **67% organizational vs. 32% individual AI impact gap**. During performance reviews and coaching cycles, time-poor managers either avoid AI completely or misuse consumer chatbots on sensitive employee records, creating EU AI Act and GDPR exposure.

- **Cost of Inaction:** Wasted enterprise AI seat spend, inconsistent manager coaching quality, and heightened legal/works-council risk from un-governed AI usage in employment decisions.
- **Evidence Basis:** *No ADPA `.docx` ROI modeling is assigned to `OP-HR-015`.* Value framing rests on published WTI adoption benchmarks and Microsoft *Frontier HR Leader* Use Case 03; client-specific time-saved metrics will be baselined during first pilot delivery.
