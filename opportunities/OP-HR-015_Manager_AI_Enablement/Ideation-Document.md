## 1. Problem Context & 2027 Market Trigger

Work Trend Index (WTI) data highlights a persistent 67% organizational vs. 32% individual AI impact gap: enterprises buy AI licenses, yet frontline people managers either avoid AI during performance cycles out of compliance fear or paste sensitive employee feedback into unapproved tools. CHROs and L&D leaders need practical enablement that closes this adoption gap without violating employment law or EU AI Act high-risk HR boundaries.

- **Core Pain:** Generic "prompt engineering" workshops fail because managers lack time and clear guardrails on what can be delegated to AI (evidence synthesis, coaching prep) versus what must remain strictly human-owned (ratings, promotion, disciplinary, and pay decisions).
- **2027 Trigger:** EU AI Act obligations regarding AI systems used in employment/worker management require documented human oversight, clear decision boundaries, and manager literacy prior to performance review cycles.

## 2. Solution Architecture & Delivery Workflow

`OP-HR-015` is a 4–6 week (6–8 hrs/wk) **Advisory / Enablement** package grounded in the **Human Resources & Talent Management** signature toolkit and Microsoft *Frontier HR Leader* Use Case 03:

1. **Week 1 — Human Decision Boundary Charter:** Align with HR, Legal, and Works Council/compliance stakeholders on the `OP-HR-003` boundary model—explicitly separating permitted AI synthesis from prohibited automated rating or employment decisions.
2. **Week 2–3 — Manager Playbook & Synthesis Templates:** Build role-specific workflows showing managers how to synthesize 360 feedback, project notes, and goal progress into structured coaching prep while verifying source accuracy.
3. **Week 4–5 — Micro-Learning & Live Calibration Clinics:** Deliver focused micro-learning modules (`OP-HR-007`) and facilitated coaching-prep clinics anchored to real upcoming review cycles—never abstract prompt tricks.
4. **Week 6 — Escalation Protocol & Adoption Baseline:** Establish Legal/HR escalation triggers for edge cases (PIP, accommodations, bias flags) and hand over governance-aligned adoption metrics (`OP-CHG-001`).
