## 3. Structured 25-Minute Discovery Call Script

### Part 1 — Context & Baseline Close Timing *(Minutes 0–10 · Tests `HYP-2027-002` Pain)*
*Rule: Ask open, factual questions first — do not pitch the bridge before hearing their actual calendar.*

1. **Payroll vs. GL Cutoff:** *"Walk me through your monthly calendar — when does payroll lock (23rd, 25th, last day), and when does HRIS actually tie out to your GL payroll clearing accounts?"*
2. **Post-3rd Close Lag:** *"How many business days after the 3rd does it take today before you have attested, locked company-wide numbers — and what are the top two bottlenecks holding up Days 1–5?"*
3. **Provisional Flash & IFRS 18 (¶117 MPMs):** *"Between the 25th and close sign-off, do executives or investors see 'flash' EBITDA/operating numbers — and have you mapped how IFRS 18's new Operating Profit subtotal and audited MPM rules affect those packs for 2027?"*

### Part 2 — Testing the `$0-Software` Value Proposition *(Minutes 10–16 · Tests `HYP-2027-002` Viability)*
4. **Software Appetite Check (Non-Leading):** *"Have you looked at close-automation tools like BlackLine, Workiva, or FCCS — or are you trying to fix this inside your current ERP?"*
   - *(Listen carefully: if they say "We only want a bundled ERP/FCCS SI partner," record that honestly toward `HYP-2027-002` falsification!)*
5. **The Scoped Bridge Test:** *"If we could get your 23rd-payroll-to-close bridge down to ≤3 business days post-3rd with €0.00 payroll clearing variance — using **\$0 new software**, just 6 structured ERP/SoD guardrails over a fixed **6–8 week pilot (€1,500–€5,000)** — is that something you'd want a 1-page scoped proposal on for Q1–Q2?"*

### Part 3 — Paired Audit & Side-Letter Check *(Minutes 16–22 · Optional Tests for `HYP-2027-010` & `HYP-2027-011`)*
*Use these 3 questions whenever time permits (especially during Q1–Q2 audit season):*
6. **Management Letter Findings (`HYP-2027-010`):** *"In your external auditor's last Management Letter (Accountantsverslag), did any points repeat from the prior year — like IT access reviews, Segregation of Duties, undocumented review thresholds (MRCs), or late audit adjustments?"*
7. **Side-Letter & Rep-Letter Exposure (`HYP-2027-011`):** *"Before your CFO/CEO signs the annual Management Representation Letter (ISA 580 / AS 2805) confirming all oral or written side agreements are disclosed, how do you verify across Sales, Legal, and Lenders that no side letters were missed — centralized registry, or email/spreadsheet chase?"*
8. **Conflict-Free Wedge Check:** *(If #6 or #7 hits a nerve)* *"Since your external auditor isn't allowed to design your remediation controls or side-letter registry under independence rules, would a fixed **€1,500–€5,000 Findings-to-Root-Cause & Side-Letter Readiness Diagnostic** be a higher priority for you right now than close speed?"*
