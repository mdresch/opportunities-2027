## Positioning

> For **CFOs and Corporate Controllers** at small-and-mid companies who struggle with **a dead zone between the 23rd-payroll close and the monthly close after the 3rd**, **Continuous Close & Real-Time Reconciliation Bridge** is an **Audit / Diagnostic** backed by **a 2007–2008 RGP-originated baseline P&L & Balance Sheet specification and payroll-closure methodology** that delivers **a repeatable, agentic 23rd-to-3rd close bridge** with $0 working capital.

## What it is

Bridge the 23rd-payroll close (when real-time payroll data becomes available) to the monthly close after the 3rd (when company-wide reports publish) via Agentic Close, automated pre-close payroll/GL accruals, daily matching, Financial Truth Management, and Autonomous Accounting Readiness during the 23rd–3rd window.

## 7 Pivotal Questions

1. **Buyer & 2027 Trigger** — CFO / Corporate Controller. IFRS 18 effective Jan 2027 tightens P&L presentation discipline right as Gartner's FCCS and BlackLine's Agentic Financial Operations trend lines make "continuous close" a board-level topic — without the enterprise price tag.
2. **The Wedge (Pain)** — Real-time payroll data lands on the 23rd but company-wide financial reporting doesn't close until after the 3rd of the following month; that gap hides accrual errors and delays Financial Truth until it's too late to act on.
3. **Deliverable & Existing IP** — A repeatable "23rd-to-3rd continuous & agentic-close" playbook, built directly on Menno's own 2007–2008 RGP (Resources Global Professionals) in-house baseline P&L & Balance Sheet specification and payroll-closure methodology — IP that predates, and arguably informed the market need later productized by BlackLine. This is proprietary provenance, not a reconstructed best-practice survey (🟢🟢 in the inventory). It also absorbs `OP-FIN-001` (monthly close checklist), `OP-FIN-002` (multi-unit close audit), and `OP-FIN-008` (employee payments/client funds).
4. **Sweat-Equity Feasibility** — Yes: 6–8 weeks at 6–10 hrs/wk, Medium effort, solo-deliverable for Pilot #1.
5. **Outcome & Proof** — Target: post-3rd close reduced to ≤2–3 business days with clean payroll-to-GL cutoff rules and no new software spend — this is the Case Study #1 candidate (`OP-X-004`) referenced in the Go-To-Market sequence.
6. **Boundaries & Risk** — Explicitly do NOT pitch expensive enterprise FCCS replacements to budget-constrained mid-market firms (market data: <10% of finance-ops budget goes to modernization at 1/3 of mid-market firms). Requires clean payroll-to-GL cut-off rules before the bridge can run reliably.
7. **Compounding / Scale Path** — Feeds directly into `OP-FIN-010` (Rate Engine) for full P&L margin truth, and into `OP-STR-004`/`005` (M&A/PMI) where payroll/financial harmonization is a pre-deal and post-close requirement.
