## 5. Call script

### Opener (2 min)

Thanks for making time. This isn't a software demo — I want to understand how your team runs **payroll lock through attested publish after the 3rd**, and whether a **fixed-scope, no-new-license** pilot would be worth designing. I'll ask three questions, then you can tell me if a one-pager and diagnostic are worth sending.

### Discovery questions (from [Wave-1-Discovery-Evidence-Log.md](Wave-1-Discovery-Evidence-Log.md))

1. **Payroll lock:** Where does payroll lock relative to your month-end close — 23rd, last day, or something else by entity?
2. **Publish cadence:** How many **business days after the 3rd** do attested company-wide numbers publish today (board / lenders)?
3. **Pilot fit:** If we could tighten that using **no new close software**, would a **6–8 week €1.5–5k** fixed pilot be on your radar for Q1–Q2 2027?

### Objection handlers

| Objection | Response |
| --- | --- |
| **"We can't spend on software."** | That's the wedge — the pilot assumes **€0 new FCCS/BlackLine**. We map gates, Reason Codes, payroll clearing, and KPI tiers on **your ERP and BI**. Software stays off the table unless you choose it later. |
| **"We already have BlackLine."** | Great — we don't replace it. The bridge is **calendar, accruals, SoD, and provisional vs T2 KPIs** around the 23rd–3rd window, often where BlackLine doesn't define *payroll lock → HRIS sync → attested publish*. Pilot can align with your existing tasks. |
| **"We're slammed at month-end — bad time."** | Understood. We can book **day 12–20 of the month** for discovery only, or **week 2 of M+1** for a 30-minute retro. Pilot work is paced **6–8 weeks at 6–10 hrs/wk** on your side — mostly workshop + one live close dry-run. |

### Close (1 min)

I'll log this in my discovery tally. If two of the three answers point to pain after the 3rd, I'll send the **diagnostic questionnaire** and we can decide on a scoped pilot proposal. No obligation.
