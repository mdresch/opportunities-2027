## 2. Business Need & Cost of Inaction

Small founders and managing partners routinely underprice engagements, accept unbounded project scope, or misjudge operational delivery risks—yet avoid €10,000+ advisory retainers when testing early adjustments. Without a low-friction diagnostic forum, buyers either delay decisions or repeat costly trial-and-error cycles. For CBa Consult, relying solely on unpaid 1:1 discovery calls consumes scarce Director hours without qualifying commitment. Because `OP-X-003` has 0 assigned ADPA `.docx` files and is pre-Stage 2, no fabricated attendee ROI or conversion percentages are assumed; commercial viability rests on low-effort group economics and pipeline pull-through.

| **Option B: Monthly Paid Group Clinic** | Run structured 4–6 seat monthly clinic (€300–€1,500/session) | Low effort (4-wk cycle), $0 working capital | Strong: monetizes diagnostics & feeds starter kits | **Select** |
| **Option C: Large Broadcast Webinar** | Host 50+ person generic slides webinar | Moderate prep; low intimacy and weak diagnostic authority | Weak: crowded format in 2027 | Reject |

## 4. Commercial Packaging, $0 Working-Capital Model & Compounding Path

- **Unit Economics & Pricing:** Target 4–6 vetted seats per monthly cycle (e.g., €250–€350/seat or €1,500 sponsored single-company mini-clinic), delivering €300–€1,500+ per 90–120 minute session with 100% advance registration payment.
- **$0 Working-Capital Execution:** Hosted on existing video-conferencing and Markdown/PDF templates—zero venue, catering, or paid-ad spend required.
- **Compounding Path:** Standardize the monthly clinic cadence into a repeatable funnel that converts operational bottlenecks directly into pillar starter kits (`OP-MKT-003`, `OP-SLS-003`, `OP-OPS-007`) and captures anonymized before/after cases (`OP-X-004`).
