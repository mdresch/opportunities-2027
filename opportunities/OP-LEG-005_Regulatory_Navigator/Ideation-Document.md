## 1. Problem Context & 2027 Market Trigger

Small and medium-sized sustainable and AI-driven ventures across Europe face overlapping regulatory obligations under the EU AI Act (entered into force August 2024 with phased enforcement through 2026–2027), GDPR, and CSRD/sustainability reporting rules. Unlike large enterprises with dedicated regulatory affairs teams, Small & Mid ventures frequently lack affordable, plain-language guidance to translate legal texts into operational controls.

- **2027 Market Trigger:** Phased EU AI Act compliance milestones, continuous GDPR enforcement, and supply-chain CSRD/ESG questionnaires force SMEs to demonstrate operational readiness without absorbing Big Four or partner-level law firm hourly rates.
- **Core Buyer Pain:** General Counsel, DPOs, and Compliance Officers in lean organizations face fragmented, jargon-heavy guidance and struggle to prioritize compliance actions across AI, privacy, and sustainability.
- **Target Buyer Profile:** General Counsel, DPOs, and Compliance Officers at Small & Mid sustainable or AI-enabled ventures.

## 2. Solution Architecture & Delivery Workflow

`OP-LEG-005` delivers a structured Advisory / Starter Kit over a 6–8 week engagement (Medium effort, $0 initial working capital) built on CBa Consult's Risk Management Signature Toolkit.

1. **Regulatory Footprint Scoping (Weeks 1–2):** Map the venture's AI use cases, personal data flows, and sustainability reporting obligations against EU AI Act, GDPR, and CSRD applicability thresholds.
2. **Plain-Language Playbook Synthesis (Weeks 3–4):** Translate applicable requirements into role-based operational checklists, control owners, and evidence artifacts using CBa Consult's Risk Management templates.
3. **Gap Triage & Compliance Roadmap (Weeks 5–6):** Prioritize remediation actions by risk exposure and effort, producing a phased compliance roadmap and board-ready summary.
4. **Enablement Workshop & Handoff (Weeks 7–8):** Walk internal stakeholders through playbook usage, establish update triggers for regulatory changes, and identify follow-on deep dives (`OP-LEG-003` or `OP-SUS-001`).

## 5. Explicit Negative Scope Boundaries & Stage 1b Gate

To uphold regulatory accuracy and prevent unauthorized practice of law, `OP-LEG-005` enforces explicit non-legal boundaries:

- **No Formal Legal Advice or Representation:** Deliverables constitute operational compliance mapping and readiness guidance, not formal legal opinions or courtroom/regulator representation; licensed legal counsel must review binding legal interpretations.
- **No Statutory Audit or Assurance Sign-Off:** Playbooks prepare internal evidence and controls but do not serve as third-party regulatory certification.
- **Point-in-Time Regulatory Baseline:** Roadmaps reflect regulatory guidance current at delivery; continuous legislative monitoring requires an ongoing update retainer.
- **Stage 1b Gate Readiness:** Source provenance and non-legal scope guardrails are established. Ready for Director adjudication on the companion Business Case.
