## 2. Business Need & Cost of Inaction

Sustainable and AI-driven Small & Mid ventures operate under intersecting EU regulatory pressures—specifically the EU AI Act, GDPR, and CSRD/ESG supply-chain expectations. Lean compliance teams and General Counsel struggle to translate dense legal mandates into day-to-day operational controls without incurring prohibitive Big Four or external law-firm hourly billing.

- **Cost of Inaction:** SMEs face avoidable compliance gaps, delayed enterprise customer onboarding due to missing governance evidence, and reactive legal remediation costs.
- **Quantitative Evidence Baseline:** *Illustrative Pre-Pilot ADPA Source Modeling — Not Realized Client Actuals:* The ADPA source document outlines qualitative incident-reduction and literacy goals, with no realized client ROI/NPV actuals recorded yet. Pilot engagements must capture baseline-to-post-sprint readiness improvement for `OP-X-004`.

## 4. Commercial Packaging, $0 Working-Capital Model & Compounding Path

`OP-LEG-005` requires $0 initial working capital by reusing CBa Consult's Risk Management Signature Toolkit templates and regulatory synthesis expertise across a 6–8 week Medium-effort engagement.

- **Packaging Structure:** Fixed-scope Advisory / Starter Kit (€1,000–€4,000 band) delivering a scoped applicability matrix (EU AI Act, GDPR, CSRD), plain-language compliance playbooks, control-owner assignments, and a prioritized remediation roadmap.
