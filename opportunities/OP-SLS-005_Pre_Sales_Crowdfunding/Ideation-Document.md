## 1. Problem Context & 2027 Market Trigger

Small businesses, founders, and B2B service/product creators frequently invest months of build time and working capital into new courses, toolkits, or physical/digital products before confirming whether paying customers actually want them. When launch-day demand fails to materialize, founders are left with sunk build costs and unsold inventory.

In 2027's capital-constrained environment, Founders, CROs, and Sales/RevOps Leads need a disciplined, low-risk way to validate product-market fit, collect real pre-orders or founding-member deposits, and incorporate early buyer feedback **before** committing full production capacity.

- **Target Buyer Segment:** Founders, CROs, and Sales/RevOps Leads in Small organizations.
- **Core Wedge Pain:** Unvalidated build-first product launches draining scarce cash and team capacity.
- **Positioning Anchor:** Fixed-scope 6–8 week validation kit backed by the **Sales, Marketing & Communication Strategy** toolkit with `$0` initial CBa working capital.

## 5. Explicit Negative Scope Boundaries & Stage 1b Gate

- **In Scope:** Offer positioning, pre-sales threshold design, landing/campaign copy, checkout/waitlist workflow guidance, and post-campaign go/no-go readout.
- **Explicitly Out of Scope:** Advancing paid ad-spend capital, managing physical manufacturing/logistics fulfillment, legal securities/equity crowdfunding compliance, or guaranteeing campaign funding totals.
- **Required Delivery Skills:** Offer positioning, pre-sales funnel design, and campaign operations (Director-led, zero external ad-agency spend).
- **Client Prerequisites:** Existing warm prospect list or partner outreach channel and client-owned payment/crowdfunding account.
- **Target Outcome & Proof (`OP-X-004`):** Validated early revenue commitments + quantitative demand proof before full-scale build spend.
- **Compounding / Scale Path:** Serves as CBa Consult's zero-capital validation engine for launching new client offers cleanly.
- **Primary Boundary Risk:** Fulfillment risk if pre-sales outpace actual client delivery capacity—mitigated via hard founding-cohort caps.
- **Lifecycle & Gate Status:** `Status = Idea` | `Active Pilots = 0`. Promotion to Stage 2 (`Hypothesis-Register.md`) and Stage 4 (`G4`) requires locking a B2B/creator-safe pre-sales SOW shell with explicit refund/fulfillment-cap clauses.
