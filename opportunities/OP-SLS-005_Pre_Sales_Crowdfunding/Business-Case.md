## 2. Business Need & Cost of Inaction

Small-tier founders and product teams routinely sink months of engineering or content creation into unproven offers, burning scarce cash before securing a single committed buyer.

- **Cost of Inaction:** High build-first failure rates, unsold inventory/unused courses, and missed early-adopter feedback that could sharpen positioning prior to production.
- **Zero-Capital Validation:** Turning pre-sales into a gated 6–8 week sprint funds production from real customer commitments rather than debt or speculative build hours.

*Note on ADPA Source Evidence:* The source file `Pre-Sales & Crowdfunding.docx` cites illustrative campaign targets (30% backer conversion rate, 120% funding goal achievement, 4.5/5 early-adopter rating, and 20% development-cost savings). All such targets represent **Illustrative Pre-Pilot ADPA Source Modeling — Not Realized Client Actuals** and require pilot benchmarking across actual client campaigns.

## 4. Commercial Packaging, $0 Working-Capital Model & Compounding Path

- **Working-Capital Discipline:** `$0` upfront cash outlay. Campaigns run on client-owned landing pages, Stripe/Gumroad/crowdfunding accounts, and existing CRM/email lists; client funds any optional ad spend directly.
- **Commercial Packaging & Milestones:** Priced within **€1,000–€4,000** as a fixed-fee 6–8 week **Pre-Sales Validation Sprint**, billed 50% kickoff and 50% at live campaign launch/readout.
- **Success Proof Metric (`OP-X-004`):** Qualified pre-orders/deposits secured vs. go/no-go threshold, plus validated buyer feedback score.
- **Compounding Path:** Functions both as a client-facing Sales pillar offer and as CBa Consult's internal **zero-capital validation engine** for testing new offers alongside `OP-X-006` co-design pilots.
