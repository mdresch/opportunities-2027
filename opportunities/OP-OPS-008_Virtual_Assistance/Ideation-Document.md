## 1. Problem Context & 2027 Market Trigger

Solo founders, boutique service firms, and lean Sales/RevOps leads lose 10–15 hours per week to fragmented administrative tasks—calendar coordination, inbox triage, CRM hygiene, meeting follow-ups, and basic vendor admin. In 2027's cost-conscious climate, Small-tier buyers cannot justify a full-time operations hire (€45,000+ loaded cost), yet traditional ad-hoc virtual assistants often require heavy daily micromanagement and lack structured process documentation.

At the same time, buyers are wary of paying for expensive enterprise workflow software just to organize routine back-office operations. There is a clear Q1 2027 cash-flow wedge for a standardized, cloud-based executive and operational virtual assistance starter kit built on free or low-cost productivity stacks with AI-assisted SOPs.

## 2. Solution Architecture & Delivery Workflow

Anchored in CBa Consult's **Management Consulting** Signature Toolkit and the ADPA source (`Virtual Assistance.docx`), `OP-OPS-008` combines standardized cloud tooling with repeatable operational workflows:

1. **Free/Low-Cost Productivity Stack Setup:** Standardized configuration of Google Workspace / Microsoft 365, Notion/Trello task boards, Calendly/Cal.com scheduling, and lightweight automation (Zapier/Make free tiers or native rules).
2. **Core Ops Playbook & SOP Library:** Pre-built checklists for inbox zero triage, executive calendar protection, CRM data hygiene, invoice/receipt routing, and weekly KPI summaries.
3. **AI-Assisted Execution Workflows:** Standardized prompts and templates for meeting-note synthesis, follow-up drafting, and recurring reporting—maximizing throughput without sacrificing human review.
4. **Delivery Cadence:** Rapid 4-week setup and stabilization sprint (Low effort, $0 working capital) transitioning cleanly into a fractional ops-support retainer or delegated junior-VA operating model.

## 5. Explicit Negative Scope Boundaries & Stage 1b Gate

To avoid trapping senior Director capacity in low-margin time-for-money admin, `OP-OPS-008` enforces strict guardrails:
- **No indefinite Director-level manual admin:** Director involvement is strictly capped to the 4-week stack/SOP setup and QA; recurring execution must be automated or delegated to vetted fractional VA partners.
- **No licensed legal, accounting, or regulated HR execution:** Scope covers administrative coordination and workflow hygiene only.
- **No paid enterprise software mandates:** Architecture prioritizes free-tier or existing client productivity tools to preserve zero-capital economics.
