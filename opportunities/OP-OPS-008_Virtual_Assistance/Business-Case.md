## 2. Business Need & Cost of Inaction

Small-business founders and lean RevOps leads lose substantial executive hours to unstandardized calendar, inbox, CRM, and administrative routines, yet cannot justify a €45,000+ full-time operations hire. Traditional freelance VAs lack structured SOPs and automation, recreating management overhead for the buyer.

In the ADPA source (`Virtual Assistance.docx`), pre-pilot modeling projects 30% lower operational costs versus traditional VA services and a 40% reduction in task completion time through automation (*Illustrative Pre-Pilot ADPA Source Modeling — Not Realized Client Actuals*). No verified CBa client time-savings or margin actuals exist yet. For CBa Consult, the primary commercial risk is not inaction—it is misallocating senior Director hours into low-leverage recurring admin unless the offer is strictly packaged as a system-setup kit or delegated fractional retainer.

## 4. Commercial Packaging, $0 Working-Capital Model & Compounding Path

- **Core Offer — 4-Week Cloud Ops & VA Starter Kit (€500–€2,000):** Standardized free/low-cost productivity stack configuration, core admin SOP playbook, AI-assisted meeting/inbox templates, and handover training.
- **Optional Retainer Extension:** Ongoing fractional ops-support executed by vetted subcontractor VA under CBa SOPs, preserving healthy gross margin without Director bandwidth drain.
- **$0 Working-Capital Discipline:** Leverages client's existing Google/Microsoft workspace and free-tier automation tools; zero upfront payroll or software spend.
- **Compounding Path:** Generates early Q1 cash flow and `OP-X-004` efficiency proof while qualifying growing founders for broader Quote-to-Cash or Finance (`OP-FIN-001`) engagements.
