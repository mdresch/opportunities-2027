## 1. Problem Context & 2027 Market Trigger

Small and mid-sized B2B and SaaS businesses spend heavily on paid search, LinkedIn outreach, and content marketing, yet frequently leak 60–85% of qualified landing-page traffic due to unclear value propositions, friction-heavy forms, slow page hierarchy, and untested calls-to-action. In 2027, rising customer acquisition costs (CAC) and tighter marketing budgets mean Founders and CMOs cannot simply "buy more clicks" to hit pipeline targets.

Instead, marketing leaders want rapid, measurable conversion lifts from existing traffic without increasing ad spend or hiring a full-time CRO specialist. Most SMBs lack both the statistical testing discipline and the UX analytics setup to run structured experiments, creating an immediate wedge for a fixed-scope CRO Audit & Test Sprint.

## 2. Solution Architecture & Delivery Workflow

Backed by CBa Consult's **Sales, Marketing & Communication Strategy** Signature Toolkit and the ADPA source (`Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO) Specialist.docx`), `OP-MKT-004` delivers a repeatable, data-driven CRO sprint:

1. **Funnel & Friction Audit (Week 1):** Heuristic teardown of top 1–3 landing pages, analytics/event-tracking verification (GA4, heatmaps, session recordings), and baseline conversion benchmarking.
2. **Hypothesis Backlog & Experiment Design (Week 2):** Prioritized ICE/PIE test matrix targeting headline clarity, social proof placement, form-field reduction, and CTA framing.
3. **Rapid Experiment Execution & QA (Weeks 3–4):** Deployment of A/B or sequential split tests using client-owned low-cost/free testing stacks, with traffic-sufficiency checks to avoid false positives.
4. **Readout & Playbook Handover (Weeks 5–6):** Statistical readout, implemented winning variants, and a reusable internal experimentation SOP—creating direct case-study proof (`OP-X-004`) and an upsell path to ongoing growth retainers.

## 5. Explicit Negative Scope Boundaries & Stage 1b Gate

To maintain fixed-scope delivery and statistical integrity, `OP-MKT-004` enforces clear boundaries:
- **No paid media buying or ad-account management:** Scope is strictly on-page conversion optimization, not running Google/Meta/LinkedIn ad campaigns.
- **No full-site rebuilds or custom CMS engineering:** Experiments use lightweight page-builder edits or client-approved A/B overlays.
- **Minimum traffic qualification gate:** Clients with insufficient monthly sessions for valid A/B testing receive a qualitative UX/messaging teardown and pre-launch checklist rather than statistically invalid split tests.
