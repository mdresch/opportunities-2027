## 2. Business Need & Cost of Inaction

SMBs and B2B SaaS firms regularly experience elevated customer acquisition costs because paid and organic traffic lands on unoptimized pages with unclear positioning and leaky lead-capture forms. Buying more traffic without fixing landing-page conversion compounds wasted marketing spend.

The ADPA source (`Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO) Specialist.docx`) outlines qualitative benefits across lead generation, CAC reduction, and ROI improvement (*Illustrative Pre-Pilot ADPA Source Modeling — Not Realized Client Actuals*). No realized CBa client conversion lifts or NPV metrics exist yet. Delaying `OP-MKT-004` means foregoing a rapid 4–6 week fixed-fee sprint capable of producing quantifiable before/after metrics for CBa Consult's Q2 `OP-X-004` proof library.

## 4. Commercial Packaging, $0 Working-Capital Model & Compounding Path

- **Tier 1 — CRO Friction Audit & Wireframe Teardown (€800–€1,200, 2 weeks):** For lower-traffic SMBs; delivers analytics health check, heuristic teardown, and rewritten high-converting copy/wireframe.
- **Tier 2 — Full CRO Audit & Test Sprint (€1,800–€3,000, 4–6 weeks):** Adds experiment prioritization, A/B test setup on client tools, and statistical readout.
- **$0 Working-Capital Discipline:** Executes entirely inside client analytics/CMS/testing accounts using CBa internal frameworks—zero software overhead.
- **Compounding Path:** Measurable conversion lift feeds directly into `OP-X-004` case studies and opens quarterly GTM/RevOps optimization retainers.
