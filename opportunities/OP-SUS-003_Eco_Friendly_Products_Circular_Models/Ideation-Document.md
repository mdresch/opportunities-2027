## 1. Problem Context & 2027 Market Trigger

Small and mid-market product businesses face growing commercial and regulatory pressure to transition away from linear "take-make-waste" product and packaging models. Under 2027 **CSRD/ESG** reporting expectations, extended producer responsibility (EPR) fees, and B2B procurement sustainability scorecards, CFOs, Chief Sustainability Officers, and ESG Leads must demonstrate credible circularity—refurbishment, take-back loops, and low-waste packaging—without triggering unaffordable physical manufacturing capex.

- **Core Pain:** Sustainability initiatives often stall as disconnected green-marketing pledges or overly capital-intensive R&D proposals that fail CFO unit-economics scrutiny.
- **2027 Buying Trigger:** Leadership needs pragmatic business-model and operating-model design that proves margin viability, waste reduction, and supply-chain transparency before committing physical tooling capex.

## 2. Solution Architecture & Delivery Workflow

`OP-SUS-003` is a 6–8 week fixed-scope **Advisory / Venture** engagement backed by the **Management Consulting** signature toolkit:

1. **Week 1–2 — Product & Packaging Baseline Audit:** Map top-revenue SKUs, packaging waste drivers, return/refurbishment leakage, and current ESG/CSRD data gaps.
2. **Week 3–4 — Circular Business Model Options:** Evaluate viable low-capex circular pathways (e.g., modular refurbishment, B2B take-back/reconditioning, recycled/compostable packaging shifts) against unit economics and margin impact.
3. **Week 5–6 — Operating Model & Supply-Chain Alignment:** Define reverse-logistics touchpoints, supplier transparency requirements, and governance KPIs (paired seamlessly with `OP-SCM-002` Green Supply Chain).
4. **Week 7–8 — CFO-Ready Transition Roadmap:** Deliver a phased 12–24 month rollout blueprint separating zero-capex packaging/process wins from gated capital investments.

## 5. Explicit Negative Scope Boundaries & Stage 1b Gate

- **No Physical Manufacturing Capex or Inventory Financing:** CBa Consult delivers business-model, operating-model, and financial-viability advisory; clients fund any physical tooling, materials testing, or inventory shifts.
- **No Accredited Lab Testing or Formal ESG Assurance:** Lifecycle impact estimates rely on client operational data and published benchmarks, not laboratory material certification or statutory CSRD audit sign-off.
- **Target Metrics Discipline:** Aspirational source-document targets (such as 80% recycled materials or 50% packaging waste reduction) are treated as candidate client KPIs to be calibrated per SKU, never guaranteed pre-diagnostic outcomes.
