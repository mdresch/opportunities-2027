## 2. Business Need & Cost of Inaction

Small and mid-market manufacturers and product brands face rising extended producer responsibility (EPR) costs, CSRD/ESG buyer questionnaires, and margin pressure on virgin packaging and linear product waste. Without a financially grounded circular business model, sustainability initiatives either remain unexecuted CSR pledges or trigger unvetted physical capex proposals.

- **Cost of Inaction:** Rising packaging/EPR fees, lost B2B tender competitiveness on sustainability scorecards, and missed margin recovery from refurbishment or closed-loop material reuse.
- **Source Modeling Benchmark (*Illustrative Pre-Pilot ADPA Source Modeling — Not Realized Client Actuals*):** The ADPA source (`Eco-Friendly Products & Circular Models.docx`) sets illustrative target objectives of 80% recycled/biodegradable product materials, 50% packaging waste reduction, and a 24-month operational transition; CBa Consult frames these strictly as pre-pilot reference benchmarks to be validated per client SKU economics.

## 4. Commercial Packaging, $0 Working-Capital Model & Compounding Path

- **Packaging:** Fixed-scope 6–8 week advisory priced at **€1,000–€2,000** (packaging waste & circular SKU diagnostic) up to **€3,500** (full circular operating model + CFO transition roadmap).
- **$0 Working-Capital Execution:** Delivered via Director management-consulting workshops and financial/operational modeling spreadsheets—client retains 100% ownership and funding responsibility for physical product or supplier changes.
- **Compounding Path:** Naturally pairs with `OP-SCM-002` (Green Supply Chain) for upstream supplier transparency and generates SME sustainability proof (`OP-X-004`).
