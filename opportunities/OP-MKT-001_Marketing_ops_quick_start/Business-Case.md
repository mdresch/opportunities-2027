## 2. Business Need & Cost of Inaction

Small B2B organizations routinely spend time and discretionary budget on outbound campaigns, LinkedIn content, and events without foundational ICP positioning or UTM/CRM attribution. The cost of inaction includes:

- **Unmeasured Marketing Burn:** Leadership cannot tie outreach activity to qualified pipeline, triggering premature cuts to effective channels or continued spending on dead ones.
- **SaaS Tool Bloat & Lost Leads:** Overlapping email, form, and spreadsheet tools cause slow follow-up and dropped inbound inquiries.
- **Hiring Friction:** Founders delay fixing foundational ops until they can afford a full-time Marketing Ops hire, forfeiting months of clean pipeline data.

## 4. Commercial Packaging, $0 Working-Capital Model & Compounding Path

- **Fixed-Scope Sprint (€500–€2,000):** Delivered across 4–6 weeks at 4–6 hrs/week on upfront/milestone billing, ensuring $0 working capital and high effective hourly yield. Client retains ownership of all CRM/email tool accounts.
- **Compounding Path:** Standardizes into a repeatable "Day-1 Marketing Ops" sprint, pairs naturally with `OP-SLS-001` (Sales process & CRM setup) to form a Small-tier RevOps starter bundle, and generates rapid Case Study proof (`OP-X-004`).
