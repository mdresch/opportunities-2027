## 2. Business Need & Cost of Inaction

- **Buyer Pain:** Early-stage founders and corporate innovation leads routinely burn €15,000–€50,000+ building websites, software, or inventory before testing whether buyers will pay for the core value proposition.
- **Cost of Inaction:** Proceeding without structured 30-day validation locks up scarce working capital in unvalidated offers, prolongs non-viable initiatives due to sunk-cost bias, and delays cash-generative pivots.
- **Pre-Pilot Modeling Note:** *Illustrative Pre-Pilot ADPA Source Modeling — Not Realized Client Actuals:* The ADPA source documents (`Opportunities zero working capital.docx` and `Strategic Framework for Zero Capital Business Opportunities.docx`) establish qualitative sweat-equity capital generation and reinvestment principles; no realized client ROI/NPV metrics are claimed pre-pilot.

## 4. Commercial Packaging, $0 Working-Capital Model & Compounding Path

- **Zero Working-Capital Mechanics:** Reuses CBa Consult's existing 2026/2027 feasibility and sweat-equity toolkit—zero paid ad testing, zero custom software overhead, 100% upfront or 50/50 milestone billing.
- **Packaging Tiers:** Founder Quick-Check (€800–€1,250 for single-offer framing & scorecard) and Full 30-Day Launch Validation Sprint (€1,750–€2,500 including live buyer discovery synthesis and 90-day execution plan).
- **Compounding Path:** Successful validated launches generate `OP-X-004` case-study proof and naturally convert clients into downstream CBa Consult growth, finance, or compliance starter packs.
