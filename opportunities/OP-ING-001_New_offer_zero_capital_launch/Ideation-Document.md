# Ideation Document — `OP-ING-001`: New-Offer / Zero-Capital Launch Validation Sprint

> **Governance & SSOT Callout:** This Stage 1b document synthesizes [Ideation-Spark.md](Ideation-Spark.md) and [Source-Documents.md](Source-Documents.md) under [Tailored-Hybrid-SIDPA-Governance.md](../../docs/governance/Tailored-Hybrid-SIDPA-Governance.md). ## 1. Problem Context & 2027 Market Trigger

- **Target Buyer:** Founders, Innovation Leads, and Product Owners in Small enterprises or spin-out teams testing a new service or product line before committing scarce capital.
- **2027 Market Trigger:** Tight venture/credit liquidity and heightened 2027 cost discipline penalize premature build-outs. Founders cannot afford 6-month product builds or agency retainers only to discover weak willingness-to-pay.
- **Core Wedge:** Translate CBa Consult's internal "sweat-equity, zero working capital" feasibility discipline into a structured, fixed-scope 30-day validation sprint that proves demand, unit economics, and regulatory/operational viability before capital lock-in.

## 2. Solution Architecture & Delivery Workflow

Built on the **Business Plan & Entrepreneurship** Signature Toolkit and ADPA's zero-working-capital framework, the 4-week (Low–Med effort) sprint follows four weekly checkpoints:

1. **Week 1 — Offer Framing & Constraints Audit:** Define the target buyer, sharp wedge pain, zero-capital resource inventory (time, skills, existing tools), and explicit risk/compliance boundaries.
2. **Week 2 — Rapid Market & Demand Testing:** Run structured buyer discovery, pre-sell/LOI experiments, and competitive positioning checks without paid ad spend or custom software engineering.
3. **Week 3 — Feasibility & Unit Economics Scoring:** Apply CBa Consult's feasibility scorecard (sweat-equity hours, gross margin, payback speed, and compounding reinvestment potential).
4. **Week 4 — Go / Pivot / Kill Gate Package:** Deliver an executive validation dossier, initial cash-generation playbook, and 90-day execution roadmap—enabling immediate launch or clean termination at minimal sunk cost.

## 5. Explicit Negative Scope Boundaries & Stage 1b Gate

- **In Scope:** Offer design, 30-day structured demand testing, feasibility scoring, zero-capital launch blueprint, and Go/Pivot/Kill gate recommendation.
- **Explicit Negative Scope (Out of Scope):** CBa Consult does **not** raise external venture capital or act as a broker-dealer, write production software/SaaS code during the validation sprint, guarantee commercial revenue outcomes, or fund client working capital.
- **Stage 1b Gate Criteria:** Package the internal 2026/2027 zero-capital feasibility scorecard into a client-ready 30-day playbook and mitigate client impatience with discovery via mandatory Week 2 market-contact milestones.
