## 1. Problem Context & 2027 Market Trigger

Consumer and employee wellness programs suffer from fragmentation across wearables, habit trackers, nutrition apps, and human coaching. Pure-play apps deliver generic automated nudges with high churn, while bespoke human wellness coaching carries high hourly costs that restrict access. The legacy ADPA concept (`AI Health & Wellness Advisors.docx`) proposes combining certified human coaching with AI-assisted habit and wellness tracking for personalized client journeys under a tiered subscription model.

For CBa Consult in 2027, however, this opportunity sits outside core B2B enterprise demand drivers (financial truth, AI/ICT governance, DORA/NIS2 compliance, and sovereign information). Regulatory scrutiny around automated health recommendations, GDPR Article 9 special-category health data, and the EU AI Act / Medical Device Regulation (MDR) boundaries raises compliance overhead relative to CBa Consult's solo-capacity (`10–15 hrs/wk`) and `` `$0` `` working-capital operating model.

## 2. Solution Architecture & Delivery Workflow

If ever reactivated via explicit B2B client pull, delivery would follow a lightweight, non-clinical coaching and CX journey framework rather than building proprietary software:

1. **Intake & Baseline Habit Framing:** Structured non-clinical lifestyle and goal intake using existing survey/CX journey templates (`Customer Experience Strategy & Design Thinking` toolkit), excluding medical diagnosis or clinical triage.
2. **AI-Assisted Tracking & Synthesis:** Integration with client-owned wearables or habit logs to generate pre-session coaching summaries and adherence patterns under human-in-the-loop review.
3. **Human Coaching Cadence:** Fixed-scope coaching check-ins focused on behavioral accountability, goal adaptation, and retention metrics.
4. **Community & Subscription Layer:** Optional peer-accountability or B2B employer-sponsored wellness cohort packaging if validated by a corporate sponsor.
5. **Governance & Liability Guardrails:** Explicit user consent, non-medical disclaimers, and human-coaching sign-off checkpoints before any AI summary reaches end users.
