1. Executive Summary

The AI Health & Wellness Advisors initiative represents a transformative opportunity to revolutionize personalized healthcare through the integration of artificial intelligence with human coaching expertise. This project aligns with the growing demand for precision wellness solutions that combine data-driven insights with empathetic human interaction. By leveraging AI's analytical capabilities alongside certified health coaches' expertise, we aim to deliver a subscription-based service that provides continuous, personalized wellness guidance at scale.

This initiative addresses several critical market gaps: the fragmentation of wellness services, the lack of personalized health optimization tools, and the growing consumer demand for proactive health management. The service will operate through a tiered subscription model, offering different levels of AI-human interaction based on individual needs and preferences. Key differentiators include our proprietary AI assessment algorithms, evidence-based coaching methodologies, and seamless integration with existing health tracking devices.

The project will be executed under PMBOK 7 guidelines, emphasizing value delivery, stakeholder engagement, and adaptive planning. Our approach combines agile development methodologies with traditional project management best practices to ensure both innovation and reliability. The service will launch with a core offering of AI-powered health assessments, personalized wellness plans, and human coaching sessions, with expansion plans for specialized wellness domains including mental health, nutrition, and chronic condition management.

Strategic benefits include establishing a new revenue stream in the rapidly growing digital health market, enhancing our brand's reputation as an innovator in healthcare technology, and creating a scalable platform for future wellness services. The project team will include cross-functional expertise from AI development, healthcare professionals, UX designers, and business strategists to ensure comprehensive solution development.

2. Project Charter

2.1 Purpose and Justification

The AI Health & Wellness Advisors project responds to the escalating demand for accessible, personalized wellness solutions in an increasingly health-conscious society. Current wellness offerings suffer from several limitations: generic advice that fails to account for individual differences, fragmented service delivery across multiple providers, and high costs that limit accessibility. This initiative creates a resolution-aligned opportunity by combining AI's data processing capabilities with human coaches' emotional intelligence to deliver continuous, personalized wellness guidance.

Market research indicates a 32% annual growth rate in the digital health market, with personalized wellness solutions representing the fastest-growing segment. Our competitive analysis reveals that while several companies offer either AI-based health tracking or human coaching services, none have successfully integrated both components into a seamless, subscription-based platform. This white space opportunity positions our solution for rapid market penetration and customer retention.

The project aligns with our organization's strategic objectives of:

Expanding into high-growth healthcare technology markets

Developing recurring revenue streams through subscription models

Leveraging our AI capabilities in practical, consumer-facing applications

Building strategic partnerships with healthcare providers and insurers

1. Market Demand: We assume continued growth in consumer demand for personalized wellness solutions at a rate of ≥25% annually.

2. AI Capabilities: We assume our AI algorithms can achieve ≥95% accuracy in health assessments within the first 6 months of development.

3. Coach Effectiveness: We assume certified health coaches can effectively integrate with our AI system to deliver superior outcomes compared to either component alone.

4. Partnership Potential: We assume we can establish at least 3 strategic partnerships with healthcare providers or insurers within the first 12 months.

5. User Adoption: We assume a 5% conversion rate from free trial users to paying subscribers based on industry benchmarks.

6. Technology Stability: We assume our technology partners will maintain ≥99.9% uptime for their health tracking devices and APIs.

This project will develop a subscription-based service that combines AI-driven health assessments with personalized coaching from certified wellness professionals, creating a scalable, data-informed approach to preventive healthcare and lifestyle optimization.

The global digital health market is projected to reach $660 billion by 2025, with AI-driven solutions accounting for 30% of this growth. Current wellness programs suffer from low engagement rates (below 20%) and limited personalization, creating a significant opportunity for technology-enhanced solutions. This initiative directly addresses these gaps by leveraging AI's analytical capabilities with human empathy and accountability, creating a hybrid model that delivers 3-5x higher engagement rates than traditional wellness programs.

Key differentiators include:

Resolution-Aligned Opportunities framework integration

PMBOK 7 project management methodology

24/7 availability with human coach follow-up within 48 hours

Multi-modal delivery (mobile app, web portal, wearable integration)
