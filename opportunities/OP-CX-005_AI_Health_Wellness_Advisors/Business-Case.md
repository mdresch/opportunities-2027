## 2. Business Need & Cost of Inaction

From a client-market lens, organizations and coaching practices experimenting with AI wellness assistants face fragmented user journeys and high drop-off when automated tracking lacks human empathy. However, from CBa Consult's portfolio-allocation lens, the **opportunity cost of action** far exceeds any cost of inaction: allocating 6–8 weeks of Medium effort to a €500–€2,500 Small-tier wellness offer directly displaces high-margin Tier A Flagships (`OP-FIN-009`, `OP-FIN-010`, `OP-ITG-013`, `OP-ITG-015`).

*Illustrative Pre-Pilot ADPA Source Modeling — Not Realized Client Actuals:* The legacy ADPA source (`AI Health & Wellness Advisors.docx`) frames a tiered subscription coaching business citing a 32% digital-health market growth rate and broad multi-domain expansion (mental health, nutrition, chronic conditions). Those figures reflect unverified pre-pilot ADPA ideation assumptions rather than realized CBa Consult economics or validated willingness-to-pay.

## 4. Commercial Packaging, $0 Working-Capital Model & Compounding Path

Under the `` `$0` `` working-capital rule, CBa Consult will not fund software development, wearable API licensing, clinical liability insurance, or paid B2C acquisition.

- **Conditional Pull Packaging (Option B Only):** Fixed-fee CX Service Design & AI Guardrails workshop (€1,500–€2,500) using existing design-thinking templates, with client ownership of all wellness practitioners, platforms, and legal disclaimers.
- **Compounding Assessment:** Synergy with CBa's 5 Strategic Assets (Financial Truth, Sovereign Information, Digital Labor, Evidence & Governance) remains minimal; referenceable proof (`OP-X-004`) is earned with higher leverage inside Tier A/B engagements.
