## 1. Problem Context & 2027 Market Trigger

Hospitality operators, destination marketing groups, and corporate offsite organizers seek authentic, small-scale local experiences rather than generic packaged tours. While post-pandemic experiential demand remains active, curating hyper-local itineraries requires recurring vendor coordination, seasonal scheduling, and consumer marketplace distribution (e.g., Eventbrite) that fall outside CBa Consult's 2027 enterprise advisory and regulatory compliance core.

- **Target Buyer (Original Concept):** CCOs, Head of Customer Success, and Operations Leads in hospitality/tourism or corporate event teams (Small tier).
- **The Wedge (Pain):** Lack of structured, repeatable local partner bundles and curated itineraries without carrying full-time hospitality concierge payroll.
- **Root Trigger:** Need for differentiated, community-driven local itineraries and vendor bundles that convert passive visitors or corporate teams into engaged participants.
- **2027 Portfolio Fit:** Low strategic synergy with CBa Consult's 5 Strategic Assets and 2027 regulatory triggers (IFRS 18, EU AI Act, DORA/NIS2, CSRD), resulting in `Parked` classification.

## 2. Solution Architecture & Delivery Workflow

If ever activated strictly on inbound client pull, delivery follows a lightweight 4–6 week design sprint (`Low` effort) grounded in the **Customer Experience Strategy & Design Thinking** toolkit:

1. **Weeks 1–2 — Discovery & Niche Mapping:**
   - Audit destination cultural assets, seasonal demand windows, and target traveler or corporate-offsite personas.
   - Map local guide, artisan, and venue supply along with capacity and margin constraints.
2. **Weeks 3–4 — Experience Design & Vendor Onboarding:**
   - Co-create structured itinerary templates, guest journey touchpoints, and quality/safety checklists.
   - Standardize partner SLA expectations, cancellation windows, and non-exclusive rev-share templates.
3. **Weeks 5–6 — Channel Packaging & Pilot Validation:**
   - Configure booking page templates (e.g., Eventbrite or client corporate portal) and post-experience CSAT feedback loops.
   - Hand over partner onboarding playbooks and pricing bundles to the client's operations owner.
