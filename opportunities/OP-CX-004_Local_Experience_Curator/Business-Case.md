## 2. Business Need & Cost of Inaction

In the hospitality and corporate offsite segment, fragmented local vendor sourcing causes inconsistent guest experiences and low repeat engagement. From CBa Consult's portfolio standpoint, however, the **cost of pursuing `OP-CX-004` actively in 2027** outweighs the cost of inaction: every hour spent building local tourism partnerships displaces high-margin CFO, AI Governance, and PMI advisory capacity.

- **Portfolio Opportunity Cost:** Under a `10–15 hrs/wk` solo-Director delivery ceiling, allocating 4–6 weeks to local venue curation directly cannibalizes GTM Priority #1–#3 Tier A pilots.
- *Illustrative Pre-Pilot ADPA Source Modeling — Not Realized Client Actuals:* The underlying `2 Local Experience Curator.docx` draft modeled launch targets of 10 curated experiences in 3 months, 15 local vendor agreements, 500 bookings within 6 months via Eventbrite, 20% month-over-month booking growth, and 90%+ post-experience satisfaction. These figures represent *Illustrative Pre-Pilot ADPA Source Modeling — Not Realized Client Actuals* and have not been validated with paying CBa clients.
