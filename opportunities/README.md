## Why "Spark"

`Spark` is a workspace-stage marker, separate from the `Status` column in [inventory-of-ideas.md](../inventory-of-ideas.md) (which stays `Idea` until a real pilot starts). It means: this flagship now has its own folder, its Tier 1 macro signal check is done, and a first Opportunity Brief draft exists — but Tier 2 deeper research and the Tier 3 pre-pilot gate are still open. See the glossary in [ideation-template.md](../ideation-template.md#9-glossary).
