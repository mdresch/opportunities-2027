## 2. Business Need & Cost of Inaction

Enterprises deploying generative AI alongside SOX 404, DORA, NIS2, and EU AI Act controls increasingly face **evidence contamination**: AI-generated summaries are stored alongside human attestations without provenance tags, WORM immutability, or chain-of-custody logs. When regulators or external auditors challenge a control, teams cannot reconstruct the exact evidentiary basis of past decisions.

- **Cost of Inaction:** Escalated audit-prep hours, regulatory enforcement exposure under EU AI Act Annex III (Dec 2027), and weakened defensibility in tax, litigation, or M&A diligence disputes.
- **Expected Pre-Pilot Value (Unvalidated Hypothesis):** Auditable separation of verified human evidence from AI-generated artifacts, lowering audit-prep friction and cross-framework rework.
