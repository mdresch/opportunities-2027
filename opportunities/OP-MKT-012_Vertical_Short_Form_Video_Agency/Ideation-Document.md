## 1. Problem Context & 2027 Market Trigger

B2B founders, CMOs, and marketing leads regularly invest significant budget into webinars, podcasts, executive keynotes, and thought-leadership interviews, yet those long-form assets decay rapidly after a single live broadcast. Meanwhile, LinkedIn, YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, and TikTok reward frequent, vertical, hook-driven video clips—a format most Small and Mid-tier B2B teams lack the time, narrative editing judgment, or consistent workflow to produce in-house.

Grounded in `Vertical Short Form Video Agency.docx` (also referenced under 2026 `OP-IE-008`) and CBa Consult's *Sales, Marketing & Communication Strategy* toolkit, `OP-MKT-012` provides a structured repurposing service:

- **Zero-Shoot Leverage:** Converts recordings the client already owns into multi-channel executive distribution assets.
- **Capacity Guardrail:** Under CBa Consult's 2027 solo-principal discipline, the model avoids manual editing bottlenecks by retaining **narrative hook strategy and standardized tier packaging**, outsourcing routine cutting/captioning as volume scales.

## 2. Solution Architecture & Delivery Workflow

Delivered as a standardized **6–8 week** Minimum Viable Service (MVS) pilot sprint converting existing client recordings into multi-platform vertical assets:

1. **Content Audit & Hook Extraction (Week 1):** Review client webinars, podcasts, or founder interviews; verify source content licensing/rights; and extract high-retention B2B narrative hooks aligned to buyer pain points.
2. **Narrative Script & Platform Spec (Week 2):** Map selected moments to 30–60 second vertical storyboards tailored for LinkedIn video, YouTube Shorts, and Reels/TikTok pacing.
3. **AI-Assisted Clip Production & QA (Weeks 3–6):** Produce branded vertical clips using AI-assisted transcription, framing, and dynamic captions (with routine editing routed to vetted freelance capacity as volume grows).
4. **Distribution Cadence & Engagement Review (Weeks 7–8):** Publish on an agreed calendar, track view-through/engagement metrics, and lock repeatable monthly tier templates.

**Core Pilot Deliverables:**
- **Hook & Narrative Audit Sheet:** Timestamped selection of high-authority B2B soundbites from existing long-form assets.
- **Vertical Clip Batch (Fixed Quota):** Branded, captioned 9:16 exports formatted for LinkedIn, Shorts, and Reels.
- **Engagement & Distribution Scorecard:** 8-week reach and retention readout guiding monthly tier renewal.

## 5. Explicit Negative Scope Boundaries & Stage 1b Gate

- **No On-Site Film Production or Studio Shoots:** Scope is strictly repurposing existing client-provided long-form video/audio recordings.
- **No Solo-Principal Manual Editing Trap:** Director retains strategy, hook selection, and QA only; standardized tier scaling requires outsourcing raw clip editing to keep Tier A advisory capacity protected.
- **Explicit Source Rights Clearance:** Client must warrant ownership and third-party speaker/music licensing for all submitted source webinars and podcasts.
