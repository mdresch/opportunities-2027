1. Executive Summary

The Vertical Short-Form Video Agency is a forward-thinking service model designed to help businesses transform their long-form content into engaging, platform-optimized short-form videos for platforms like Instagram Reels, TikTok, and YouTube Shorts. In an era where attention spans are shrinking and video content dominates digital engagement, businesses struggle to repurpose their existing content efficiently while maintaining brand consistency and audience relevance. This agency addresses that gap by offering a scalable, end-to-end solution that leverages expertise in content creation, storytelling, and platform-specific optimization.

This initiative aligns with the PMBOK 7 principles of delivering value, fostering stakeholder engagement, and adapting to dynamic environments. The agency will operate as a Service Hustle, providing a low-overhead, high-margin model that capitalizes on the growing demand for short-form video content. By focusing on future-proofing, the agency will incorporate emerging trends such as AI-driven editing, interactive content, and data-driven personalization to stay ahead of the curve.

The primary objectives of this project include establishing a minimum viable service offering (MVS) within six months, achieving a client retention rate of 80%, and generating $50,000 in revenue within the first year. Success will be measured through key performance indicators (KPIs) such as client acquisition cost (CAC), video engagement rates, and customer satisfaction scores. This document outlines the strategic approach, key components, implementation plan, and metrics for success, providing a clear roadmap for launching and scaling the agency.

2. Project Charter

2.1 Purpose

The Vertical Short-Form Video Agency is designed to address the growing need for businesses to adapt their content strategies to the short-form video format. With platforms like TikTok and Instagram Reels driving unprecedented levels of engagement, businesses that fail to leverage these formats risk losing visibility, audience connection, and competitive advantage. This agency will bridge the gap by offering a turnkey solution that transforms long-form content—such as webinars, podcasts, blog posts, and whitepapers—into high-quality, platform-optimized short-form videos.

The purpose of this project is to:

Democratize short-form video production for businesses that lack in-house expertise or resources.

Increase content ROI by repurposing existing assets into high-engagement formats.

Future-proof businesses by helping them adapt to evolving digital consumption trends.

Create a scalable, repeatable service model that can be expanded to include additional content formats and platforms.

2.3 Requirements

2.3.1 Business Requirements

1. Service Delivery Framework: Develop a standardized process for transforming long-form content into short-form videos, including content analysis, scriptwriting, editing, and platform optimization.

2. Client Onboarding: Create a seamless onboarding process that includes a content audit, goal-setting session, and service agreement.

3. Technology Stack: Implement tools for video editing (e.g., Adobe Premiere Pro, CapCut), project management (e.g., Trello, Asana), and client communication (e.g., Slack, Zoom).

4. Pricing Model: Establish a transparent pricing structure based on video length, complexity, and platform requirements (e.g., tiered pricing for 15-second, 30-second, and 60-second videos).

5. Marketing Strategy: Develop a go-to-market strategy that includes a website, social media presence, case studies, and targeted outreach to potential clients.

2.3.2 Stakeholder Requirements

1. Clients: Expect high-quality, engaging videos that align with their brand voice and drive measurable engagement.

2. Content Creators: Require clear guidelines, creative freedom, and access to tools and resources to produce high-quality videos efficiently.

3. Project Managers: Need visibility into project timelines, client feedback, and resource allocation to ensure smooth service delivery.

4. Investors/Partners: Seek transparency into financial performance, growth metrics, and strategic direction.

4. Team Capabilities: The team will have the skills and resources to produce high-quality videos that meet client expectations.

5. Client Collaboration: Clients will provide timely feedback and necessary assets (e.g., brand guidelines, raw footage) to facilitate the repurposing process.

6. Technology Reliability: Video editing tools and social media platforms will remain stable and accessible throughout the project lifecycle.

3.2 Scope Management Plan

3.2.1 Scope Statement

The Vertical Short-Form Video Agency will deliver the following services:

1. Content Analysis: Review long-form content (e.g., webinars, podcasts, blog posts) to identify key messages and repurposing opportunities.

2. Scriptwriting: Develop concise, engaging scripts tailored to short-form video platforms.

3. Video Editing: Transform scripts into high-quality videos using professional editing tools and techniques.

4. Platform Optimization: Optimize videos for specific platforms (e.g., TikTok, Instagram Reels) by incorporating trending sounds, hashtags, and captions.

5. Performance Analytics: Provide clients with engagement metrics and insights to measure the success of their videos.

6. Client Feedback Loop: Incorporate client feedback to refine scripts, editing styles, and overall service delivery.

3.2.3 Exclusions

The following items are out of scope for this project:

1. Original Content Creation: The agency will not create original long-form content for clients. It will only repurpose existing content.

2. Paid Advertising: The agency will not manage paid advertising campaigns for clients (e.g., TikTok Ads, Instagram Ads).

3. Influencer Partnerships: The agency will not facilitate influencer collaborations or sponsorships.

4. Live Video Production: The agency will not produce live-streaming content or real-time video events.

5. Non-Video Content: The agency will not repurpose content into non-video formats (e.g., infographics, blog posts).

2. Problem Statement

2.1 Current State and Enterprise Limitations

The current landscape for businesses seeking to leverage short-form video content is fraught with inefficiencies and systemic limitations. Despite the exponential growth of platforms like TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts—where short-form videos now account for over 50% of total digital engagement—many businesses lack the internal capabilities to repurpose their existing long-form content effectively. The primary challenges include:

1. Manual and Time-Consuming Processes: Businesses often rely on in-house teams or freelancers to manually edit and optimize long-form content for short-form platforms. This process is not only labor-intensive but also prone to inconsistencies in quality, branding, and messaging. For example, a 60-minute webinar may require 10-15 hours of editing to produce a single 60-second Reel, with no guarantee of audience engagement.

2. Lack of Platform-Specific Expertise: Each short-form video platform has unique algorithms, audience preferences, and content formats. Businesses struggle to tailor their content to these nuances, resulting in lower engagement rates and reduced visibility. For instance, TikTok favors raw, authentic content, while Instagram Reels prioritizes polished, visually appealing videos. Without specialized knowledge, businesses risk producing content that fails to resonate with their target audience.

3. Inconsistent Branding and Messaging: Repurposing long-form content into short-form videos often leads to fragmented branding and diluted messaging. Businesses may inadvertently create content that misaligns with their core values or fails to convey their intended message, leading to brand erosion and reduced customer trust.

4. High Opportunity Costs: The time and resources spent on manual content repurposing could be better allocated to core business activities, such as product development, customer acquisition, or strategic planning. The opportunity cost of not leveraging short-form video effectively is substantial, with businesses missing out on potential revenue growth and audience expansion.

5. Scalability Challenges: As businesses grow, their content needs evolve. However, manual processes are inherently unscalable, leading to bottlenecks and inefficiencies. For example, a business with 10 long-form videos may struggle to repurpose them into 50 short-form videos without a scalable solution, limiting their ability to capitalize on new opportunities.

These limitations are exacerbated by the rapidly changing digital landscape, where trends and algorithms evolve at an unprecedented pace. Businesses that fail to adapt risk falling behind competitors who leverage short-form video to enhance engagement, drive traffic, and increase conversions.

2.2 Business Impact (Cost of Inaction)

The cost of inaction in addressing these challenges is both quantifiable and strategic. Failing to implement a scalable, efficient solution for repurposing long-form content into short-form videos results in the following annual impacts:

1. Lost Revenue Opportunities: Businesses that do not leverage short-form video effectively miss out on $250,000 annually in potential revenue growth. This estimate is based on industry benchmarks, where businesses with active short-form video strategies experience 30-50% higher engagement rates and 20-30% increases in conversion rates compared to those without such strategies.

2. Increased Operational Costs: Manual content repurposing processes incur $150,000 annually in labor costs, including salaries for in-house teams or fees for freelance editors. These costs are further compounded by inefficiencies, such as rework due to inconsistent quality or misaligned branding.

3. Reduced Competitive Advantage: Businesses that lag in adopting short-form video strategies risk losing market share to competitors who effectively engage audiences through these platforms. The competitive disadvantage is estimated at $100,000 annually, based on lost customer acquisition and retention opportunities.

4. Brand Erosion and Customer Attrition: Inconsistent or low-quality short-form video content can damage a business’s brand reputation, leading to customer attrition rates of 15-20%. The financial impact of this attrition is estimated at $200,000 annually, factoring in the cost of acquiring new customers to replace lost ones.

5. Missed Platform-Specific Opportunities: Each short-form video platform offers unique monetization opportunities, such as TikTok’s Creator Fund or Instagram’s Reels Play Bonus. Businesses that do not optimize their content for these platforms miss out on $50,000 annually in potential earnings from platform-specific incentives.

In total, the annual cost of inaction is estimated at $750,000, encompassing lost revenue, increased operational costs, competitive disadvantages, brand erosion, and missed platform opportunities. This figure underscores the urgent need for a scalable, efficient solution to address the current limitations and unlock the full potential of short-form video content.

3.1 Option 1: Status Quo (Do Nothing)

Description

Maintain the current approach of relying on in-house teams or freelancers to manually repurpose long-form content into short-form videos. This option involves no upfront investment in new tools, processes, or resources, and businesses continue to operate with their existing capabilities.

The status quo assumes that businesses will allocate 10-15 hours per week to content repurposing, with in-house teams or freelancers handling the editing, optimization, and distribution of short-form videos. This approach is characterized by minimal standardization, with each piece of content being treated as a standalone project. Businesses may also continue to use generic editing tools (e.g., Adobe Premiere Pro, iMovie) and basic analytics platforms (e.g., native platform insights) to guide their efforts.

Pros/Cons

Pros:

No Upfront Investment: Businesses avoid the costs associated with new tools, training, or hiring specialized staff.

Familiarity: Teams continue to work with processes and tools they are already comfortable with, reducing the learning curve.

Flexibility: Businesses can adjust their content strategies on an ad-hoc basis without being locked into a specific workflow or platform.

Cons:

High Operational Costs: Manual processes incur $150,000 annually in labor costs, including salaries, freelance fees, and rework due to inconsistencies.

Inconsistent Quality: Lack of standardization leads to variability in video quality, branding, and messaging, which can erode customer trust and engagement.

Scalability Limitations: Manual processes are unscalable, making it difficult for businesses to handle increased content demands as they grow.

Missed Revenue Opportunities: Businesses forgo $250,000 annually in potential revenue growth due to lower engagement rates and conversion rates compared to competitors with optimized short-form video strategies.

6.2 Next Steps

Upon approval of this business case, the following actions will be initiated:

1. Project Charter Initiation: Develop and finalize the Project Charter to formally authorize the project and define its scope, objectives, and stakeholders.

2. Team Assembly: Recruit and onboard the project team, including content creators, freelance editors, project managers, and marketing personnel.

3. Tool Procurement: Finalize contracts with technology providers and procure the necessary tools and infrastructure.

4. Kickoff Meeting: Conduct a project kickoff meeting to align the team on goals, timelines, and responsibilities.

5. Pilot Program Launch: Begin the pilot program to test the agency’s processes, tools, and service offerings with a select group of clients.

6. Performance Monitoring: Establish performance tracking mechanisms to monitor KPIs and ensure alignment with the success criteria outlined in Section 5.3.

7. Stakeholder Communication: Develop a communication plan to keep stakeholders informed of progress, challenges, and milestones throughout the project lifecycle.
