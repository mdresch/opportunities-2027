## 2. Business Need & Cost of Inaction

B2B companies routinely spend thousands of euros producing webinars, podcasts, and event panels that generate value for only a single week before sitting idle in video archives:

- **Asset Decay:** Without a repeatable short-form distribution engine, marketing teams lose organic LinkedIn and YouTube Shorts reach to faster-moving competitors.
- **Unproven Content ROI:** Teams struggle to justify long-form event budgets when executive insights never reach buying committees in snackable formats.
- **Portfolio Role:** Packaging a lightweight repurposing sprint offers CBa Consult an accessible Tier C cash-flow stream—provided execution avoids trapping senior Director hours in manual editing.

*Illustrative Pre-Pilot ADPA Source Modeling — Not Realized Client Actuals:* The ADPA source document (`Vertical Short Form Video Agency.docx`) outlines an MVS launch within six months targeting 5+ pilot clients (with 4+/5 feedback), an 80% client retention rate, and $50,000 in first-year revenue. Those targets reflect *Illustrative Pre-Pilot ADPA Source Modeling — Not Realized Client Actuals* from the 2026 `OP-IE-008` concept and have not yet been validated with live 2027 B2B unit-margin data.

## 3. Strategic Options Analysis (BABOK v3 Aligned)

| Option | Description | Effort & Cash Outlay | Commercial & Risk Profile | Verdict |

| **Option A: Strategy-Led Tiered MVS + Outsourced Editing** | CBa owns hook selection & B2B positioning; vetted freelance editor executes AI-assisted cuts per fixed tier | 6–8 wks / \$0 upfront (funded by client retainer) | Preserves Director margin and Tier A focus; scalable across Small & Mid clients | **Recommended** |
| **Option B: Solo-Executed Boutique Editing** | Director performs all clip cutting, captioning, and exports manually | High Director hrs / \$0 | Time-intensive bottleneck; poor hourly yield vs. Tier A governance advisory | **Rejected** |
| **Option C: Full-Service Video Production Agency** | End-to-end shooting, scripting, and paid social ad management | High overhead / Capex | Violates \$0 working-capital rule and drifts outside CBa Consult core positioning | **Rejected** |

**Options Conclusion:** Option A is the only commercially viable path that protects solo-principal bandwidth while delivering repeatable B2B video output.

## 4. Commercial Packaging, $0 Working-Capital Model & Compounding Path

`OP-MKT-012` maintains **\$0 initial working capital** by operating strictly on pre-paid pilot sprints or monthly fixed-clip tiers using existing AI-assisted editing tools and client-supplied long-form recordings:

- **Variable-Cost Scaling:** Freelance editing capacity (beyond Pilot 1 validation) is procured strictly on a variable per-clip basis funded out of collected client fees.
- **Standardized Product Tiers:** Package into Bronze/Silver/Gold monthly clip quotas with strict revision caps to prevent scope creep.
- **Ecosystem Compounding:** Pair executive thought-leadership clips with **`OP-MKT-006`** (*LinkedIn Executive Thought Leadership*) and capture verified reach/lead metrics as **`OP-X-004`** case studies.
