## 1. Problem Context & 2027 Market Trigger

Mid-market manufacturing, retail, and technical field-service organizations face rising costs for physical product demos, complex equipment onboarding, and distributed technician training. While spatial computing and WebAR/mobile AR hardware have matured incrementally, most Small and Mid-tier organizations lack in-house spatial UX design capabilities and cannot justify bespoke six-figure 3D agency retainers. As a result, teams either rely on static 2D PDFs and slide decks or experiment with fragmented, non-reusable AR prototypes that fail to integrate into training or customer experience workflows.

`OP-ITG-008` reframes the legacy ADPA ideation concept (`Spatial AR Content Design.docx`) through CBa Consult's *Customer Experience Strategy & Design Thinking* Signature Toolkit:

- **Template-First Design:** Rather than custom headset engineering, CBa Consult designs modular, reusable **spatial/AR experience blueprints, interactive showcase templates, and training storyboards**.
- **Accessible Delivery Surface:** Anchored on browser-based WebAR and mobile 3D viewers so clients avoid specialized headset fleet CapEx.

## 2. Solution Architecture & Delivery Workflow

Delivered as a fixed-scope **6–8 week** Digital Product (PDF / Toolkit) and advisory design blueprint:

1. **Use-Case & Device Reality Check (Week 1):** Evaluate client training or product-showcase bottlenecks and lock delivery to accessible targets (mobile WebAR / browser-based 3D viewers rather than niche enterprise headsets).
2. **Spatial UX & Storyboard Blueprint (Weeks 2–3):** Map customer or trainee interaction flows, spatial anchors, and measurable learning/conversion checkpoints using Design Thinking canvases.
3. **Modular Content Template Pack (Weeks 4–6):** Produce reusable interaction templates, asset specifications (3D/USDZ/glTF handoff specs), and prompt/content guidelines so internal or freelance creators can populate scenes repeatedly at lower unit cost.
4. **Pilot Walkthrough & Scale Guidelines (Weeks 7–8):** Validate one prototype showcase or onboarding module with target users and deliver a governance/reuse guide for retail or technical training expansion.

**Primary Packaged Deliverables:**
- **Spatial UX Blueprint & Storyboard Deck:** End-to-end user journey for one retail showcase or technician onboarding flow.
- **Modular WebAR Content Template Kit (PDF + Specs):** Reusable scene patterns and glTF/USDZ handoff specs.
- **Production Governance Checklist:** Guidelines separating UX strategy from third-party 3D asset production.
