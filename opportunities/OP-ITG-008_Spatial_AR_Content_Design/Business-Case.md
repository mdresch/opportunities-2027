## 2. Business Need & Cost of Inaction

Small and mid-tier organizations selling physical products or onboarding field/operations staff struggle to communicate complex spatial workflows through flat PDFs and slide decks. Yet jumping directly into custom XR agency builds exposes buyers to high upfront costs and brittle, single-use applications:

- **Buyer Value Gap:** A structured blueprint and modular template kit lets buyers test spatial/WebAR experiences safely without vendor lock-in.
- **Portfolio Sequencing Discipline:** From CBa Consult's standpoint, rushing into spatial R&D in H1 2027 would carry high opportunity cost against urgent Tier A regulatory and AI governance demand—making Q3 2027 pull-based sequencing essential.

*Illustrative Pre-Pilot ADPA Source Modeling — Not Realized Client Actuals:* The ADPA source document (`Spatial AR Content Design.docx`) projects target outcomes of 30% faster onboarding, 40% higher knowledge retention, 50% customer engagement uplift, 20% conversion uplift, and 25% production-cost savings via modular templates, alongside third-party Gartner 2026 experimentation / 35% CAGR citations. All target KPIs from `Spatial AR Content Design.docx` are *Illustrative Pre-Pilot ADPA Source Modeling — Not Realized Client Actuals* and require live pilot verification.

## 4. Commercial Packaging, $0 Working-Capital Model & Compounding Path

`OP-ITG-008` is structured to enforce **\$0 initial working capital**: CBa Consult does not pre-purchase XR hardware, enterprise AR licenses, or 3D asset packs. Any Q3 2027 pilot uses browser-based WebAR viewers or client-owned visualization tools, while CBa Consult sells a fixed-scope **Spatial Experience Blueprint & Modular Template Pack**:

- **Fixed-Scope Digital Product:** PDF architecture guide, reusable interaction templates, and vendor-handoff glTF/USDZ specifications.
- **Zero Engineering Payroll:** Heavy CAD-to-3D conversion (when needed) is contracted directly on the client's budget.
- **Compounding Path:** Turn bespoke showcase or onboarding blueprints into repeatable industry template packs for retail merchandising and industrial training, feeding validated outcome metrics into **`OP-X-004`**.
