## 2. Business Need & Cost of Inaction

Enterprises deploying copilots and autonomous agents in 2027 face a structural operating-model gap:

- **Uncaptured Productivity (Value Realization Gap):** Time saved by individual employees fragments into slack capacity rather than measurable throughput or cost avoidance because team spans, handoffs, and approval rights stay unchanged.
- **Accountability & EU AI Act Exposure:** When autonomous agents trigger HR, procurement, or customer-facing actions without named human supervisors and action-approval gates, organizations risk Annex III non-compliance (Dec 2, 2027) and works-council pushback.
- **Cultural Friction:** Unclear human-vs-agent task boundaries breed shadow-AI avoidance or defensive resistance across middle management.

*Note: `OP-HR-009` has no ADPA `.docx` financial forecast; the `€3,000–€10,000` commercial anchor is an unvalidated Tier B benchmark pending Stage 2 discovery.*
