## 1. Problem Context & 2027 Market Trigger

Medium and large organizations in 2027 are deploying copilots and autonomous AI agents across HR, operations, and shared services, yet most manage them as isolated IT licenses rather than a **blended workforce**. Three verified triggers from [Ideation-Spark.md](Ideation-Spark.md) drive CHRO and COO urgency:

1. **Agents as Managed Digital Labor:** 2027 industry benchmarks (Arion Research *Agentic AI Trends 2027*, Gartner CHRO Priorities) treat AI agents as a managed workforce tier requiring role definitions, span-of-control design, and capacity metrics.
2. **The EBIT Value Realization Gap:** Organizations capture localized copilot time savings that fail to reach the P&L because jobs, supervision layers, and approval rights were never redesigned.
3. **December 2, 2027 EU AI Act Annex III Enforcement:** Employment and HR AI systems face strict high-risk human-oversight and accountability mandates, creating legal and works-council friction when human-in-the-loop authority is undefined.
