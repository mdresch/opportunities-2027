## Positioning

> For **CHROs, COOs, and Chief AI Officers** in medium and large organizations who struggle with **managing a blended workforce of humans, copilots, and autonomous agents without clear accountability or productivity metrics**, **Human + Agent Workforce Operating Model** is an **Advisory / Operating Model** backed by **`HR Transformation Toolkit version 2027.docx` (§8.2 & §10.8–10.10)** that delivers **a governed human-plus-agent role, oversight, and productivity operating model** with \$0 working capital.

## What it is

Design the operating model for a blended workforce (humans, copilots, autonomous agents): define which jobs become human-supervised, which tasks agents own, action-approval gates, and hybrid productivity metrics.

## 7 Pivotal Questions

1. **Buyer & 2027 Trigger** — CHRO, COO, and Chief AI Officer. Arion Research's *Agentic AI Trends 2027* and Gartner's 2027 CHRO Priorities increasingly treat AI agents as a **managed workforce** rather than software, while **December 2, 2027** EU AI Act Annex III enforcement requires explicit human oversight for employment/HR AI.
2. **The Wedge (Pain)** — Organizations see localized copilot time savings but suffer an EBIT "value realization gap" (McKinsey) because jobs, supervision layers, and approval rights were never redesigned for digital labor.
3. **Deliverable & Existing IP** — Extends CBa Consult's **`HR Transformation Toolkit version 2027.docx`** (§8.2 *Responsible AI in Human Resources* and §10.8–10.10 *6-dimension maturity model, including AI/Digital Labor*) into a concrete **Human + Agent Role & Supervision Blueprint**.
4. **Sweat-Equity Feasibility** — 6–10 weeks at 8–12 hrs/week (High effort), \$0 cash outlay; fastest path is attaching it as a follow-on module to `OP-HR-002` (HR Maturity Assessment) or `OP-ITG-013` (Enterprise AI Agent Governance).
5. **Outcome & Proof** — Clear task-ownership matrix (human-owned vs. human-supervised vs. agent-owned), named human-accountability gates, and blended capacity/productivity KPIs tied to P&L rather than vanity token usage.
6. **Boundaries & Risk** — Cultural resistance, works-council / CLA consultation requirements in Europe, and unclear human-in-the-loop liability; explicitly *not* legal labor-counsel advice.
7. **Compounding / Scale Path** — Natural expansion of `OP-HR-002` & `OP-HR-003`; bridges CHRO budgets directly into CIO/CISO `OP-ITG-013` (Agent Governance) and `OP-ITG-016` (*"COBIT for Agents"*).
