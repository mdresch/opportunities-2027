## 2. Business Need & Cost of Inaction

Even after the EU CSRD omnibus narrowed mandatory statutory reporting cohorts, commercial pressure on Small & Mid companies from banks, private investors, and enterprise supply-chain customers continues to intensify. CFOs and founders need a credible, assurance-ready voluntary reporting baseline without paying enterprise CSRD transformation fees or locking into an accounting firm that bundles readiness with future assurance.

- **Cost of Inaction:** SMEs respond piecemeal to recurring bank and customer ESG questionnaires, risk losing preferred-supplier or financing terms, and waste internal finance hours rebuilding unverified KPI spreadsheets.
- **Quantitative Evidence Baseline:** *Illustrative Pre-Pilot ADPA Source Modeling — Not Realized Client Actuals:* No ADPA `.docx` financial model or realized client engagement metrics exist yet (portfolio-derived from competitive intelligence). Pilot #1 metrics must be logged directly after the first paid engagement.

## 4. Commercial Packaging, $0 Working-Capital Model & Compounding Path

`OP-SUS-006` operates on $0 initial working capital via 3–5 weeks of focused Management Consulting diagnostic effort (5–8 hrs/wk). Deliverables rely on structured scoping, control-gap mapping, and roadmap templates without requiring client software purchases.

- **Packaging Structure:** Fixed-scope €1,500–€4,000 diagnostic yielding: (1) CSRD vs. EU VS vs. VSME scope decision, (2) light materiality map, (3) data & control gap analysis, (4) assurance-readiness checklist, and (5) one-season reporting roadmap.
- **Compounding Path:** Bundles naturally as a **VS Reporting Starter** alongside `OP-SUS-001`, links to `OP-LEG-005`, `OP-SCM-002`, and `OP-FIN-009` (IFRS 18 MPM cross-links), and creates an upsell path to `OP-SUS-002` when deeper materiality is required.
