## 1. Problem Context & 2027 Market Trigger

Following the EU CSRD omnibus adjustments, many Small & Mid enterprises fell outside mandatory statutory CSRD reporting thresholds. However, actual commercial demand for structured ESG data from banks, private investors, and enterprise supply-chain customers did not shrink. SMEs increasingly receive supplier and lender questionnaires demanding credible, assurance-ready sustainability metrics.

- **2027 Market Trigger:** Mid-tier accounting networks (e.g., Grant Thornton, Baker Tilly) actively market EU Voluntary Standard (VS) readiness—frequently positioning the same firm as both readiness advisor and future limited-assurance provider ahead of the July 2027 market wave.
- **Core Buyer Pain:** CFOs and founders outside mandatory CSRD lack a proportionate, conflict-free diagnostic to bridge legacy VSME expectations and EU VS reporting without building costly enterprise CSRD machinery.
- **Target Buyer Profile:** CFOs and founders at Small & Mid companies facing bank, investor, or enterprise customer ESG data requests.

## 2. Solution Architecture & Delivery Workflow

`OP-SUS-006` delivers a fixed-scope 3–5 week Audit / Diagnostic (5–8 hours/week, Medium effort, €1,500–€4,000 fee band, $0 working capital) under the Management Consulting Signature Toolkit.

1. **Scope Decision (Week 1):** Clarify CSRD exemption status and map mandatory-adjacent expectations across EU VS versus legacy VSME frameworks to lock voluntary reporting boundaries.
2. **Light Materiality & Topic Map (Week 2):** Produce a proportionate material-topic map focused strictly on stakeholder-requested disclosures (with clean upsell path to `OP-SUS-002` if deeper double materiality is needed).
3. **Data & Control Gap Analysis (Week 3):** Identify data collectors, process owners, and evidentiary gaps for requested ESG KPIs.
4. **Assurance Readiness Checklist & One-Season Roadmap (Weeks 4–5):** Deliver an independent assurance-readiness checklist (aligned with limited-assurance expectations) and a one-season execution roadmap with owners, timelines, and finance cross-links (`OP-FIN-009` / IFRS 18 MPM where relevant).

## 5. Explicit Negative Scope Boundaries & Stage 1b Gate

To avoid regulatory liability and preserve clear independence from statutory assurance providers, `OP-SUS-006` enforces strict exclusions:

- **No Sustainability Assurance Sign-Off:** CBa Consult performs readiness diagnostics, gap analysis, and control framing only—we never sign limited or reasonable sustainability assurance opinions.
- **No Full Enterprise ESRS/CSRD Implementation:** Scope is strictly proportionate to voluntary EU VS / VSME reporting; full mandatory CSRD transformations require separate scoping (`OP-SUS-002`).
- **No Legal Compliance Certification:** Deliverables support CFO and founder governance without replacing statutory legal or audit counsel.
