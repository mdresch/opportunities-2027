## Positioning

> For **CFOs and founders who fell out of mandatory CSRD scope** but still receive **bank, investor, and customer ESG questionnaires**, **EU Voluntary Standard (VS) Reporting Readiness** is a **proportionate diagnostic** that scopes **VS vs legacy VSME**, maps **material topics**, and produces an **assurance-readiness checklist**—without CBa Consult signing sustainability assurance opinions.

## The Problem (Before Solution)

- **CSRD omnibus** shrinks mandatory cohort; **demand for ESG data did not shrink**.
- Mid-tiers (Grant Thornton, Baker Tilly) push **assurance-ready** reporting—often **same firm as future assurer**.
- SMEs need **credible voluntary reporting** without full enterprise CSRD machinery.

## What the Client Is Entitled To (Deliverables)

**Duration:** 3–5 weeks · **Fee band:** €1,500–€4,000

1. **Scope decision** — CSRD vs **EU VS** vs legacy VSME expectations; what client chooses to report voluntarily.
2. **Light materiality / topic map** — proportionate to voluntary reporting (upsell to `OP-SUS-002` if needed).
3. **Data & control gap analysis** — collectors, owners, evidence for KPIs stakeholders request.
4. **Assurance readiness checklist** — what limited/reasonable assurance path will request (market tone from Jul 2027 wave).
5. **One-season reporting roadmap** — owners, timeline, finance cross-links (`OP-FIN-009`, IFRS 18 MPM where relevant).

## What CBa Consult Does — and Does Not

| CBa does | CBa does not |
| --- | --- |
| Diagnostic, gap analysis, control framing for ESG data | Sign assurance on sustainability reports |
| Prepare client for **`OP-SUS-001`** toolkit execution | Full CSRD programme (`OP-SUS-002`) unless scoped |
| Stay **independence-friendly** vs future auditor | Provide legal CSRD opinions |

## Why CBa Consult Should Offer This

- **Conflict-free pre-assurance** parallel to AI (`OP-RSK-004`).
- Captures **post-omnibus** demand without pretending every SME is CSRD.
- **`OP-SCM-002`** supply-chain pressure creates inbound.
- Template-heavy, fits solo delivery.
