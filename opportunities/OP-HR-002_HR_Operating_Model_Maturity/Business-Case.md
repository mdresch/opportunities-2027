## 2. Business Need & Cost of Inaction

Medium and large organizations frequently manage HR processes unevenly across business units without an empirical, comparable maturity baseline. CHROs and COOs face 2027 compliance pressures (including ADP's 2027 compliance cycle) alongside board expectations for skills-based talent models and AI/digital labor integration, yet lack objective diagnostic data to sequence investments.

- **Cost of Inaction:** HR transformation capital is misallocated based on anecdote, cross-BU governance gaps persist, and enterprises default to conflicted HRIS vendors or high-overhead Big Four assessments.
- **Quantitative Evidence Baseline:** *Illustrative Pre-Pilot ADPA Source Modeling — Not Realized Client Actuals:* No standalone ADPA `.docx` ROI/NPV model or realized client pilot metrics are attached yet; however, the underlying 6-dimension, 5-level maturity framework and 28-KPI scorecard are already fully specified in `HR Transformation Toolkit version 2027.docx` (§10.8–10.10).

## 4. Commercial Packaging, $0 Working-Capital Model & Compounding Path

`OP-HR-002` achieves $0 initial working capital because the core IP—6 dimensions (service delivery, data/analytics, skills/talent, tech, governance/risk, AI/digital labor), 5 maturity levels, and 28 KPIs—already exists in `HR Transformation Toolkit version 2027.docx`. Delivery relies purely on senior advisory sweat equity over 8–12 weeks.

- **Packaging Structure:** Fixed-scope Tier A executive diagnostic yielding a scored 6-dimension maturity profile, completed 28-KPI scorecard, cross-BU gap readout, and prioritized remediation roadmap.
- **Compounding Path:** Each engagement builds Strategic Asset 4 (Evidence & Governance) benchmark data, generates Tier A case-study proof, and creates natural follow-on retainers for HR data governance, skills architecture, or AI/digital labor implementation.
