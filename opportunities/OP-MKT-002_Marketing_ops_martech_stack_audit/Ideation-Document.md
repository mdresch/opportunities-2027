## 1. Problem Context & 2027 Market Trigger

Mid-market and large B2B organizations accumulate overlapping SaaS subscriptions across marketing automation, ABM, analytics, webinar tooling, and attribution platforms. In 2027's disciplined budget environment, Founders, CMOs, and B2B Marketing Leads face intense scrutiny from Finance to eliminate redundant licenses, standardize campaign reporting, and fix broken CRM-to-martech data flows without hiring an expensive full-time Marketing Ops architect.

- **Target Buyer:** Founders, CMOs, and B2B Marketing Leads (**Medium & Large** tier).
- **Core Wedge Pain:** Duplicate martech spend, disconnected campaign attribution, and inconsistent UTM/lifecycle definitions across regional or product marketing teams.
- **2027 Market Trigger:** Renewal-cycle budget freezes and CFO demand for provable software rationalization and clean pipeline reporting.

## 2. Solution Architecture & Delivery Workflow

`OP-MKT-002` is a fixed-scope **Audit / Diagnostic** grounded in the **Sales, Marketing & Communication Strategy** signature toolkit, delivered over **6–10 weeks** (6–10 hrs/wk) with **$0 initial working capital**.

1. **Weeks 1–2 — Stack & Contract Inventory:** Catalog all active martech tools, license tiers, renewal dates, and integration touchpoints across marketing and sales teams.
2. **Weeks 3–4 — Redundancy & Utilization Scoring:** Benchmark feature overlap, seat utilization, and data-sync latency using a standardized martech redundancy scorecard.
3. **Weeks 5–7 — Attribution & Reporting Standardization:** Define canonical campaign taxonomy, UTM standards, and CRM-to-martech data integration rules.
4. **Weeks 8–10 — Rationalization Roadmap & Readout:** Deliver a prioritized decommission/consolidation plan, vendor negotiation brief, and target campaign reporting blueprint.

## 5. Explicit Negative Scope Boundaries & Stage 1b Gate

To prevent scope creep into agency production or custom middleware engineering, `OP-MKT-002` enforces strict boundaries:

- **No Creative Campaign Execution:** Excludes ad buying, copywriting, landing page design, or ongoing email campaign operations.
- **No Custom ETL / API Coding:** Delivers integration architecture specifications and field-mapping rules, not custom software development.
- **No Vendor Procurement Mediation Liability:** CBa Consult provides negotiation briefs and cost-cut options; contract terminations remain client-executed.
