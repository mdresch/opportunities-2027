## 2. Business Need & Cost of Inaction

Small firms operating without standardized onboarding checklists, a consolidated employee handbook, and a consistent performance-review cadence experience slower new-hire ramp-up, inconsistent manager decisions, and elevated compliance friction as headcount crosses 10–50 employees. Hiring a full-time HR leader is financially premature, while delaying structure creates compounding people-ops debt.

For CBa Consult, `OP-HR-001` provides a fast-cycle Tier C cash-flow wedge requiring zero external software or ad spend. *Illustrative Pre-Pilot ADPA Source Modeling — Not Realized Client Actuals*: because this opportunity is Spark-derived with zero assigned ADPA `.docx` files, no ADPA ROI/NPV model exists; financial targets rest strictly on sweat-equity hours and pilot fee validation.

## 4. Commercial Packaging, $0 Working-Capital Model & Compounding Path

Built entirely from CBa Consult's Human Resources & Talent Management toolkit, `OP-HR-001` requires $0 working capital and 5–8 hours/week of Director/consultant time over 4–6 weeks.

- **Core Deliverable Bundle:** Scaled-down 5-level HR maturity self-assessment, tailored employee handbook framework, onboarding checklist, and lightweight performance-review cadence.
- **Compounding Path:** Uses rapid delivery to capture initial case-study proof (`OP-X-004`) and upsell expanding clients into full HR maturity transformations (`OP-HR-002`) or M365 onboarding automation (`OP-DTX-003`).
