## 2. Business Need & Cost of Inaction

Growing mid-market and enterprise teams that outgrow founder-led coordination lose weeks on stalled approvals, duplicated work, and blurred accountability across Finance, HR, IT, and Operations.

- **Cost of Inaction:** Executive escalation fatigue, high decision latency on cross-functional initiatives, and ambiguous regulatory accountability across 2027 mandates.
- **High-Margin Advisory Wedge:** Neutral Director-led facilitation competes favorably against €60k+ Big Four org-design retainers while pairing naturally with HR and Ops audits.

## 4. Commercial Packaging, $0 Working-Capital Model & Compounding Path

- **Working-Capital Discipline:** `$0` upfront cash outlay. Delivered via structured executive interviews, neutral workshops, and standardized RACI/decision-rights templates in client-owned workspaces.
- **Commercial Packaging & Milestones:** Priced within **€2,500–€7,500** depending on organization size and workflow count (5–8 core handoffs), billed 40% kickoff, 30% draft RACI readout, and 30% final Operating Model Playbook.
- **Success Proof Metric (`OP-X-004`):** Reduction in decision-escalation loops and SLA handoff delays across targeted cross-functional workflows.
- **Compounding Path:** Pairs directly with `OP-HR-002` (HR audit) and `OP-OPS-002` (Operations audit), and creates downstream demand for `OP-CHG-001` adoption rollouts.
