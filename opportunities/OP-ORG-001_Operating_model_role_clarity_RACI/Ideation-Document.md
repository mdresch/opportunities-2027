## 1. Problem Context & 2027 Market Trigger

Medium and large organizations that have scaled beyond founder-led or informal departmental coordination routinely suffer from overlapping decision rights, stalled cross-functional handoffs, and shadow escalation loops. When Finance, HR, Legal, and Operations leaders cannot pinpoint who is Accountable versus Consulted on core workflows, transformation initiatives stall and middle management burns capacity in status meetings.

In 2027, cost discipline, leaner spans of control, and cross-functional regulatory mandates (IFRS 18, EU AI Act, CSRD/ESG, DORA/NIS2) force CEOs, CHROs, and Operating-Model Sponsors to demand explicit governance accountability without commissioning a six-figure Big Four org-design engagement.

- **Target Buyer Segment:** CEOs, CHROs, and Operating-Model Sponsors in Medium & Large organizations.
- **Core Wedge Pain:** Blurred decision rights and slow cross-functional handoffs in teams outgrowing founder-led coordination.
- **Positioning Anchor:** Neutral Track A diagnostic backed by the **Operating Model & Organization Design** toolkit with `$0` initial CBa working capital.

## 2. Solution Architecture & Delivery Workflow

`OP-ORG-001` packages CBa Consult's **Operating Model & Organization Design** toolkit into a neutral, structured **6–10 week** Track A **Audit / Diagnostic**:

| Phase | Timing | Core Activities | Concrete Client Deliverables |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Decision-Right & Handoff Discovery** | Weeks 1–3 | Conduct structured executive/lead interviews and map 5–8 high-friction cross-functional workflows to isolate decision bottlenecks. | Executive Interview Synthesis & Handoff Friction Map |
| **2. Target Operating Model & RACI Design** | Weeks 4–6 | Draft clear decision-rights charters, role boundaries, and RACI matrices across leadership, PMO, and functional teams. | Role-Clarity Charter & Cross-Functional RACI Matrix |
| **3. Conflict Resolution & Alignment Workshops** | Weeks 7–8 | Facilitate neutral sponsor workshops to adjudicate contested ownership, escalation SLAs, and governance touchpoints. | Adjudicated Decision-Rights Log & Escalation Protocol |
| **4. Handover & Ops/HR Integration** | Weeks 9–10 | Package role definitions and RACI artifacts for HR/Ops rollout, feeding cleanly into `OP-HR-002` and `OP-OPS-002`. | Operating Model Playbook & 90-Day Adoption Checklist |

### Core Design Principles

- **Workflow-Anchored RACI:** Maps decision rights to real operational handoffs (close, hiring, procurement, release) rather than abstract org-chart boxes.
- **Single-Point Accountability:** Eliminates multi-owner "shared A" deadlocks by enforcing one Accountable executive/lead per gate.
- **Neutral Mediation:** Provides an external conflict-free arbiter so internal political tensions do not derail role clarity.
