## 1. Problem Context & 2027 Market Trigger

Across both small service firms and complex mid-to-large enterprises, revenue leakage and delivery friction concentrate at the exact same seam: the sales-to-operations handoff where a signed commitment must become delivered, accepted, and invoiced work. Missed scope caveats, manual CRM-to-ERP re-keying, unbilled change orders, and delayed invoicing inflate Days Sales Outstanding (DSO) and erode delivery margin.

In 2027, COOs, CROs, and CFOs face intense working-capital discipline. Industry benchmarks cited in the portfolio spark (ISG 2027 RevOps projections and SyncGTM adoption data) show RevOps has become a mainstream priority, shifting buyer demand from conceptual awareness toward repeatable, evidence-backed diagnostics that pinpoint operational leakage rapidly.

## 2. Solution Architecture & Delivery Workflow

`OP-OPS-003` productizes CBa Consult's Quote-to-Cash methodology into a tiered, scalable diagnostic toolkit built directly on field-proven delivery from `OP-OPS-001` (handoff map for small firms) and `OP-OPS-002` (cross-functional audit for larger firms):

1. **Modular Diagnostic Intake & Data Pack:** Structured questionnaire and lightweight export checklist covering CRM deal stages, SOW/quote approvals, delivery kickoff, time/milestone capture, and invoice issuance.
2. **Tiered Process & Bottleneck Scoring:** Apply a single standardized Lean Six Sigma / RevOps scoring rubric—scoped down to a rapid handoff map for SMEs or scaled up to multi-department SLA and ERP-handoff forensics for larger enterprises.
3. **Quantified Leakage & Root-Cause Matrix:** Deliver a visual Quote-to-Cash friction heat map quantifying cycle-time delays, rework loops, and unbilled scope leakage.
4. **Prioritized 90-Day Remediation Blueprint:** Output an executive-ready action backlog separating zero-cost process fixes from targeted system automation.

## 5. Explicit Negative Scope Boundaries & Stage 1b Gate

`OP-OPS-003` carries a mandatory portfolio sequencing gate and strict scope limits:

- **Strict Pre-Build Sequencing Gate:** Do **not** build or commercially launch the productized toolkit before `OP-OPS-001` and `OP-OPS-002` complete live client pilots and validate real-world friction patterns.
- **No Custom Software / ERP Development:** ERP re-platforming, custom middleware coding, or billing-engine builds are excluded from the diagnostic product.
- **No Statutory Financial Audit:** Diagnostic findings identify operational revenue leakage and cycle-time waste, not statutory GAAP/IFRS audit opinions.
- **No Unbounded Implementation Retainer:** Execution of recommended CRM/ERP changes requires a separate scoped delivery SOW.

**Stage 1b Gate Readiness:** Framing is complete, with explicit dependency gating (`OP-OPS-001` / `OP-OPS-002` pilots first). Held at Director sign-off until prerequisite pilot evidence is harvested.
