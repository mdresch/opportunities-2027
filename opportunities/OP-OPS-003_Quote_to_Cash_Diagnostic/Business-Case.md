## 2. Business Need & Cost of Inaction

Revenue commitments routinely erode between deal signature and invoice collection due to unstandardized handoffs, unbilled scope changes, and manual CRM-to-finance re-entry. Market signals captured in the portfolio spark (ISG 2027 projecting RevOps embedded in strategic planning at over 50% of firms; SyncGTM reporting 78% RevOps adoption) confirm strong buyer readiness for structured Quote-to-Cash rigor.

For CBa Consult, delivering Quote-to-Cash diagnostics purely as bespoke consulting limits scalability. Codifying field findings into a single tiered productized diagnostic enables repeatable licensing and higher effective hourly realization. *Illustrative Pre-Pilot ADPA Source Modeling — Not Realized Client Actuals*: this is a Tier A Spark opportunity with no ADPA `.docx` assigned; commercial pricing and ROI benchmarks will be calibrated directly from `OP-OPS-001` and `OP-OPS-002` pilot outcomes.

## 4. Commercial Packaging, $0 Working-Capital Model & Compounding Path

`OP-OPS-003` is funded entirely through sweat equity harvested from live `OP-OPS-001` and `OP-OPS-002` engagements ($0 upfront working capital):

- **Two-Tier Modular Packaging:** Lite Handoff-Map Edition (for Small firms, modeled on `OP-OPS-001`) and Full Cross-Functional Audit Edition (for Mid/Large firms, modeled on `OP-OPS-002`).
- **Compounding Path:** Diagnostic kit serves both as a standalone digital/advisory product and as a low-friction diagnostic wedge into broader Operating Model & Organization Design retainers.
