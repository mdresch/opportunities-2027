`OP-SUS-001` provides Small & Mid suppliers with a modular €800–€3,000 ESG/CSRD data-collection and supplier-questionnaire starter kit at `` `$0` `` working capital. Positioned for Q3 2027, it protects early-year Tier A bandwidth while creating a practical supply-chain compliance add-on alongside `OP-SCM-001` and `OP-LEG-001`.

## 2. Business Need & Cost of Inaction

SMEs supplying large EU enterprises risk losing preferred-vendor status or delaying enterprise contract renewals when they cannot answer Scope 3 and ESG procurement questionnaires accurately. Conversely, over-buying enterprise ESG software or unstructured consultant hours wastes scarce SME margin, especially while EU CSRD / Omnibus regulatory thresholds continue to evolve.

## 4. Commercial Packaging, $0 Working-Capital Model & Compounding Path

- **Two-Tier Packaging:** (1) **Self-Serve Digital Starter Kit (€800–€1,200)** — PDF playbook, modular ESG data-collection workbook, and supplier-response templates; (2) **Guided Readiness Sprint (€1,800–€3,000)** — toolkit plus 2–3 structured owner-mapping workshops over 6 weeks.
- **`$0` Working-Capital Model:** Delivered entirely via reusable Markdown/PDF/Excel IP with zero third-party carbon-data APIs or SaaS overhead.
- **Pilot Proof Metric:** Reduction in hours required for the client's Finance/Ops team to complete an enterprise customer's annual ESG supplier questionnaire.
- **Compounding Path:** Designed to bundle cleanly with **`OP-SCM-001` (Supply Chain Continuity)** and **`OP-LEG-001` (Compliance)**, and can serve as an entry wedge for **`OP-X-004`** case studies or broader governance retainers.

## 5. Risk Register & Compensating Controls

| Risk ID | Risk Description | Likelihood / Impact | Compensating Control |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `R-01` | **Regulatory Timeline & Threshold Shifts (EU Omnibus):** Statutory CSRD deadlines/scope change mid-2027. | High / Medium | Anchor toolkit on enterprise **supply-chain buyer demand** (VSME-aligned) and keep regulatory modules decoupled. |
| `R-02` | **Unprofitable Custom Hours in €800–€3k Band:** Bespoke ESG coaching erodes solo effective rate. | Medium / High | Enforce productized-kit delivery (max 2–3 fixed workshops in guided tier) and amortize build across `OP-SCM-001` bundles. |
| `R-03` | **Assurance / Greenwashing Liability:** Client presents self-reported SME estimates as audited ESG claims. | Medium / High | Embed explicit non-assurance disclaimers and source-evidence logs distinguishing estimates from audited actuals. |
| `R-04` | **Low Standalone Pull vs. Tier A:** SMEs delay ESG spending unless procurement forces a deadline. | Medium / Medium | Target outreach exclusively at SMEs with active Tier-1 buyer questionnaires or bundle into `OP-SCM-001`. |
