## 2. Business Need & Cost of Inaction

Solo founders and micro-agencies routinely delay formal cash-flow modeling until a liquidity crunch occurs, resulting in surprise VAT/tax shortfalls, delayed invoicing, and reactive cost cuts. Without a lightweight close discipline, small operators over-rely on raw bank balances that ignore committed payables and receivables aging.

No ADPA `.docx` modeling is assigned to `OP-FIN-003` ([Source-Documents.md](Source-Documents.md)); accordingly, no quantitative client ROI/NPV actuals are claimed here. The €300–€1,500 target range in [inventory-of-ideas.md](../../inventory-of-ideas.md) represents a modest initial digital-product cash-flow target intended to validate willingness to pay and seed upsells into higher-ticket `OP-FIN-001` done-with-you setups. Cost of inaction for CBa Consult is low, though delaying leaves an easy evergreen lead-magnet and entry-tier monetization asset unused.

## 4. Commercial Packaging, $0 Working-Capital Model & Compounding Path

- **Initial Wedge Offer (€49–€149 self-serve pack):** Notion Monthly Close Control Board + Google Sheets/Excel 13-week Cash-Runway Model + walkthrough SOP, targeting €300–€1,500 in initial pilot sales via organic LinkedIn teardowns and direct founder outreach.
- **$0 Working-Capital Discipline:** Built 100% on existing Notion/Sheets accounts and zero-upfront checkout platforms (revenue-share transaction fee only; zero paid ads).
- **Compounding Path:** Every template buyer receives an embedded call-to-action for a fixed-fee "done-with-you" setup sprint (`OP-FIN-001`), turning low-ticket digital buyers into €1,500+ advisory engagements and documented `OP-X-004` case studies.
