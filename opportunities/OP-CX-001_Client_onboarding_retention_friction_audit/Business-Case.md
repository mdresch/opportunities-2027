## 2. Problem Statement & Cost of Inaction *(Problem-Before-Solution)*

Per [market-analysis-2027.md](../../market-analysis-2027.md#L174), Gartner identifies 2027 onboarding as a strategic Net Revenue Retention (NRR) growth engine (shifting metrics from *speed-to-close* to **Time-to-First-Value**), while Forrester warns that over-automated, low-empathy onboarding is actively eroding early customer retention. Yet Small and Mid-Market B2B teams consistently lose margin and retention in the first 90 days after a contract is signed:

1. **Broken Sales-to-Delivery / CS Handoffs:** Promises made during sales cycles fail to reach Delivery and Customer Success cleanly, forcing clients to repeat discovery questions and creating immediate buyer's remorse.
2. **Ad-Hoc Readiness & Missing Intake Gates:** Projects kick off with incomplete technical, legal, or data prerequisites, stalling Time-to-First-Value by weeks while Delivery absorbs unbilled rework.
3. **Over-Automated, Low-Trust Checkpoints:** Generic automated onboarding emails replace high-trust human alignment at pivotal Day 1, Day 14, and Day 30 governance gates.
4. **Delayed Churn Visibility:** Without leading operational telemetry in Days 1–30 (kickoff latency, rework loops, unresolved escalations), churn risk stays hidden until Month 9–12 renewal.

### Operational Cost of Inaction

- **Direct Margin Leakage:** Senior CS leads and delivery managers burn recurring non-billable hours chasing missing scoping artifacts, re-explaining assumptions, and resolving avoidable early escalations.
- **Delayed Cash & Value Recognition:** Slow intake-to-kickoff delays milestone sign-offs, invoicing triggers, and Day-90 expansion readiness.
- **Renewal Drag:** First-90-day friction depresses product/service adoption before the customer ever reaches steady-state ROI, compounding Year-1 contraction and logo churn.

## 4. Scope, Delivery Architecture & Core Deliverables (`Track A`)

Rather than deploying software, `OP-CX-001` applies four lean diagnostic phases built on the **Customer Experience Strategy & Design Thinking** Signature Toolkit and tailored **UniCorn COAS (1–5 Maturity Rubric)** criteria:

| Phase / Week | Focus | Core Activity | Concrete Deliverable |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Week 1** | **Observe & Baseline** | Trace **3–5 recent closed-won deals** from signature through Day 90 (`Quote → Contract → Handover → Kickoff → First Value → Invoice`); lock **Week-1 baseline & `OP-X-004` Consent Tier** | Cohort Trace Matrix & Baseline Telemetry Log |
| **Week 2** | **Pinpoint Friction & Score** | Map rework loops, stalled intake prerequisites, low-trust automation drops, and support escalation bottlenecks; score onboarding maturity (1–5) | Day 1–90 Friction & Rework Register + Readiness Score |
| **Week 3** | **Design & Govern** | Co-design a lean **90-Day Onboarding & Time-to-Value Playbook** with RACI ownership, go/no-go intake checklist, and balanced human touchpoints | 90-Day Onboarding Playbook, RACI & Handoff Exception Checklist |
| **Week 4** *(+ Wks 5–6 if standalone)* | **Pilot & Certify** | Test live on **1–2 incoming client deals**; measure Time-to-Value & billing readiness; draft sanitized **Case Study #1 (`OP-X-004`)** | Certified Pilot Scorecard + Expansion Roadmap (`OP-OPS-003` / `OP-CX-002`) |

### Distilled 5-Level Onboarding Maturity Rubric *(Adapted from `ADPA - Onboarding.docx`)*

1. **Level 1 — Ad-hoc (`<60%`):** Informal verbal handoffs; recurring missing client inputs; unpredictable kickoff timing.
2. **Level 2 — Developing (`60–74%`):** Basic onboarding checklist exists, but ownership breaks across Sales, CS, Delivery, and Finance.
3. **Level 3 — Defined (`75–84%`):** Standardized Sales-to-CS intake gate, clear RACI, and tracked Day 1 / Day 30 / Day 90 milestones.
4. **Level 4 — Managed (`85–94%`):** Pre-kickoff readiness verified consistently; Time-to-First-Value and rework exceptions reviewed monthly.
5. **Level 5 — Optimized (`95%+`):** Continuous Day 1–90 telemetry feeds retention playbooks and expansion triggers (`OP-CX-002` readiness).
