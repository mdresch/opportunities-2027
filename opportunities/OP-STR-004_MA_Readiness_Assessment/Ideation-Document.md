## 1. Problem Context & 2027 Market Trigger

Private Equity deal teams, Corporate Development leads, and mid-market acquirers entering 2027 transactions routinely conduct thorough financial and legal due diligence—yet suffer costly post-close surprises across operational seams that traditional DD skips. Unchecked data-readiness gaps, Dutch 30%-ruling and key-talent retention exposure, incompatible payroll/HR structures, overlapping operating models, vendor contract traps, tech-stack duplication, and undocumented SOP knowledge-transfer risks routinely erode deal thesis IRR within the first 100 days.

`OP-STR-004` is a Tier A Flagship (Q3–Q4 2027 scale-out; Strategy Pillar / **Mergers & Acquisitions** Signature Toolkit) engineered to converge four existing CBa Consult capabilities—`OP-FIN-010`, `OP-HR-002`, `OP-ITG-010`, and `OP-OPS-007`—into one confidential, rapid-turnaround pre-deal and carve-out diagnostic feeding directly into `OP-STR-005` (PMI Governance Office).

Three deal-making pressures drive buyer urgency across mid-market M&A in 2027:

- **Carve-Out & Bolt-On Complexity:** Smaller platform add-ons rarely have clean standalone IT, payroll, or SOP documentation for Day-1 separation.
- **Talent & Cross-Border Tax Sensitivity:** Mispriced retention risks or Dutch 30%-ruling compliance gaps trigger key-staff attrition right after signing.
- **Compressed VDR Timelines:** PE operating partners need one integrated operational risk view without coordinating four separate boutique vendors.

## 2. Solution Architecture & Delivery Workflow

Executed across 6–10 weeks (or compressed under NDA deal timelines) by synthesizing scored modules from four converging CBa capabilities:

1. **Weeks 1–2 — Confidential Data-Room Intake & Risk Scoping:** Align with the PE/Corp Dev deal thesis under strict NDA; ingest virtual data room (VDR) extracts across payroll/rate models, HRIS, IT infrastructure, key contracts, and core SOPs.
2. **Weeks 3–6 — Four-Pillar Converged Diagnostic:** Score (a) financial/payroll & rate-engine integrity (`OP-FIN-010`), (b) HR harmonization & 30%-ruling/retention exposure (`OP-HR-002`), (c) tech overlap & Split-Clock data/retention recovery (`OP-ITG-010`), and (d) operational SOP handover readiness (`OP-OPS-007`).
3. **Weeks 7–10 — Red-Flag Synthesis & Day-1 PMI Handoff:** Deliver an executive M&A Readiness Scorecard quantifying Day-1 operational blockers, price-chip/TSA negotiation inputs, and a ready-to-launch `OP-STR-005` PMI Governance Office charter.

Core tangible deliverables produced across the diagnostic:

- **Converged Four-Pillar Readiness Scorecard:** Weighted risk ratings across Data/Tech, Finance/Payroll, HR/Retention, and SOP Handover.
- **TSA & SPA Operational Input Brief:** Concrete Transition Service Agreement schedules and pre-close remediation conditions.
- **Day-1 `OP-STR-005` PMI Launch Charter:** Seamless post-close transition roadmap for the first 100 days.
