## 2. Business Need & Cost of Inaction

Mid-market acquisitions and carve-outs routinely lose value between signing and Day-100 when unexamined payroll/30%-ruling exposure, incompatible HR/IT systems, missing Transition Service Agreement (TSA) boundaries, and undocumented SOPs stall integration. Discovering these gaps post-close forces expensive emergency contractors and delays synergy realization.

By surfacing these operational risks while SPA warranties, purchase price adjustments, and TSA schedules are still negotiable, deal teams protect IRR and move seamlessly into post-close execution.

## 4. Commercial Packaging, $0 Working-Capital Model & Compounding Path

- **Packaging & Pricing Anchor:** Fixed-scope 6–10 week M&A & Carve-Out Readiness Diagnostic (€5,000–€15,000+ band, with compressed 3–4 week sprint option for live VDR windows), priced via fixed milestone retainers.
- **Zero Working-Capital Mechanics:** Operates entirely inside the buyer's existing Virtual Data Room (VDR) under NDA using CBa's converged M&A, HR, Finance, IT, and SOP checklists—requiring \$0 software outlay or external contractor capital.
- **Unit-Economics Guardrail:** Standardized scoring templates across the four converging pillars prevent bespoke analysis bloat during compressed VDR windows, protecting margin at 8–12 hrs/week.
- **Compounding Path:** Creates CBa Consult's flagship M&A revenue ladder—pre-close readiness (`OP-STR-004`) naturally transitions on closing day into `OP-STR-005` (PMI Governance Office), pulling through downstream remediation across Finance, HR, IT, and Operations.
