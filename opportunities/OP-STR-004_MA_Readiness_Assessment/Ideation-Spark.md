## Positioning

> For **Private Equity firms, Corporate Development teams, and Mid-Market Acquirers** who struggle with **no single pre-deal diagnostic covering data, retention, payroll/HR, operating-model, contract, tech, and knowledge-transfer risk at once**, **M&A Readiness Assessment** is an **Audit / Diagnostic** that converges **`OP-FIN-010`, `OP-HR-002`, `OP-ITG-010`, and `OP-OPS-007`** to deliver **a single pre-deal readiness diagnostic feeding directly into post-close PMI** with $0 working capital.

## What it is

Pre-deal & carve-out readiness diagnostic evaluating data readiness, retention/30%-ruling exposure, payroll & HR harmonization, operating-model overlap, contract exposure, tech overlap, and knowledge-transfer risk.

## 7 Pivotal Questions

1. **Buyer & 2027 Trigger** — Private Equity, Corporate Development, Mid-Market Acquisitions. No single external regulatory deadline drives this one — the trigger is deal flow itself, and the inventory names it as converging four already-scoped capabilities into one buyer-ready offer.
2. **The Wedge (Pain)** — Pre-deal diligence usually checks financials and legal, but rarely checks data readiness, retention/30%-ruling exposure, payroll/HR harmonization feasibility, operating-model overlap, contract exposure, tech overlap, and knowledge-transfer risk together — gaps in any of these surface expensively post-close.
3. **Deliverable & Existing IP** — Not a from-scratch build: converges `OP-FIN-010` (rate engine/payroll), `OP-HR-002` (HR maturity), `OP-ITG-010` (retention/recovery), and `OP-OPS-007` (SOP handover) into a single diagnostic — maps directly to the Mergers & Acquisitions Signature Toolkit.
4. **Sweat-Equity Feasibility** — 6–10 weeks at 8–12 hrs/wk, High effort — feasible once at least a couple of the four converging capabilities (likely `OP-FIN-010` and `OP-HR-002`) have been piloted independently first.
5. **Outcome & Proof** — A delivered pre-deal readiness report a PE firm or Corp Dev team can act on before signing — directly feeding `OP-STR-005`'s post-close governance office as proof of continuity.
6. **Boundaries & Risk** — Deal-timeline confidentiality and fast turnaround pressure are the operating constraints here, not the methodology — this has to run fast and quietly, under NDA, with no room for a slow discovery phase.
7. **Compounding / Scale Path** — Feeds directly into `OP-STR-005` (PMI Governance Office) as the natural pre-close → post-close pipeline; the two together are the M&A/PMI revenue ladder for the whole portfolio.
