## 2. Business Need & Cost of Inaction

Sellers on niche and regional marketplaces (Bol.com, Marktplaats, Etsy, SaaS app directories) lose high-intent organic buyers due to poorly structured titles, missing search attributes, and weak conversion copy. Because enterprise marketplace suites focus almost exclusively on Amazon, smaller sellers lack affordable, structured optimization.

The ADPA source (`Niche Marketplace Listing Optimizer.docx`) describes an iterative, NLP-assisted optimization model to raise search visibility, CTR, and conversion (*Illustrative Pre-Pilot ADPA Source Modeling — Not Realized Client Actuals*). No realized CBa client CTR deltas or financial ROI actuals exist yet. Converting this concept into a lean, fixed-price audit-and-rewrite sprint avoids software build risk while testing commercial demand quickly in Q2 2027.

## 4. Commercial Packaging, $0 Working-Capital Model & Compounding Path

- **Fixed-Price Starter Package (€400–€900, 2 weeks):** Audit + full keyword/copy rewrite + visual brief for 3–5 hero listings on one target marketplace.
- **Full Optimization & Test Sprint (€1,000–€1,800, 4 weeks):** Covers up to 10 listings plus 14-day pre/post impression, CTR, and conversion iteration cycle.
- **$0 Working-Capital Discipline:** Uses existing LLM analysis workflows, spreadsheets, and client marketplace seller dashboards—zero paid software subscriptions.
- **Compounding Path:** Strong CTR/sales deltas feed `OP-X-004` proof assets and cross-sell into broader landing-page CRO (`OP-MKT-004`) or sales-enablement retainers.
