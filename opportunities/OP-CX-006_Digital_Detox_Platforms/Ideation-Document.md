## 1. Problem Context & 2027 Market Trigger

Excessive screen time, constant notification switching, and post-pandemic digital dependency create measurable friction in knowledge-worker focus, stress levels, and team collaboration. While consumer wellness and regulatory conversations (such as EU Digital Services Act attention-economy debates and WHO digital wellbeing guidance) keep low-screen-time habits visible, enterprise budgets in 2027 are tightly anchored to hard ROI, statutory deadlines (IFRS 18, EU AI Act, DORA/NIS2, CSRD), and core operational efficiency rather than discretionary lifestyle initiatives.

The legacy ADPA ideation file (`Digital Detox.docx`) originally envisioned a broad consumer and organizational software platform targeting 50,000 monthly active users (MAUs), behavioral tracking, gamified challenges, and wellness-app API integrations:

- **Target Buyer Segment:** CCOs, Heads of Customer Success, and Operations Leads (Small tier) under discretionary wellbeing budgets.
- **2027 Portfolio Mismatch:** Building and marketing a standalone B2C/B2B2C software platform is off-thesis, capital-dilutive, and exposed to budget freezes.
- **Reactivation Condition:** Any future reactivation requires narrowing `OP-CX-006` into a zero-capital, fixed-scope B2B employee-wellbeing workshop add-on paired with `OP-HR-007`.

## 2. Solution Architecture & Delivery Workflow

If ever un-parked upon explicit client demand, `OP-CX-006` would be stripped of custom app engineering and delivered purely as a structured Digital Product (PDF / Toolkit) and facilitated workshop module over 4–6 weeks:

1. **Baseline Attention Audit (Week 1):** Lightweight intake survey measuring team meeting load, notification density, after-hours messaging norms, and focus-block fragmentation using existing M365 or Google Workspace telemetry exports.
2. **Intentional Workflow Charter (Weeks 2–3):** Design low-screen-time operating norms (async-first protocols, protected focus blocks, device-free meeting cadences) tailored using the *Customer Experience Strategy & Design Thinking* Signature Toolkit.
3. **Behavioral Habit Toolkit (Weeks 4–5):** Deliver PDF playbooks, manager checklists, and team detox challenge templates requiring zero proprietary software deployment or third-party app integrations.
4. **30-Day Adoption Check & Handoff (Week 6):** Measure self-reported focus recovery and meeting-hour reduction, then hand off ongoing maintenance to internal HR/Operations leads or fold into `OP-HR-007`.

**Core Packaged Deliverables (Conditional Only):**
- **Team Digital-Hygiene Diagnostic Summary:** 1-page baseline of meeting load and notification hotspots.
- **Async & Focus-Block Playbook (PDF):** Practical manager and team rules for intentional low-screen-time collaboration.
- **Facilitated 90-Minute Workshop Kit:** Repeatable session agenda attachable to `OP-HR-007`.
