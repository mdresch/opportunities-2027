## 2. Business Need & Cost of Inaction

Knowledge teams lose productive capacity to fragmented attention, excessive synchronous meetings, and always-on messaging norms. However, from CBa Consult's 2027 portfolio perspective, the **cost of pursuing** `OP-CX-006` proactively exceeds the cost of inaction:

- **Discretionary Spend Exposure:** Buyer spend for standalone digital detox programs is highly discretionary and routinely cut first during cost-discipline cycles.
- **Commoditized Alternatives:** Low-cost consumer apps and native OS focus modes make standalone differentiation difficult without deep enterprise HR embedding.
- **Low Strategic Synergy:** Standalone wellbeing toolkits sit outside CBa Consult's five core strategic assets (Financial Truth, Organizational Intelligence, AI Governance, Evidence & Governance, and Sovereign Advisory).

*Illustrative Pre-Pilot ADPA Source Modeling — Not Realized Client Actuals:* The legacy ADPA source (`Digital Detox.docx`) modeled an 18-month platform rollout targeting 50,000 monthly active users (MAUs), 30% three-month retention, and 10 brand/employer partnerships between Q3 2026 and Q1 2028. Those projections assume consumer SaaS product engineering, paid user acquisition, and partner sales overhead that violate CBa Consult's \$0 working-capital operating model and are therefore formally retired from 2027 planning.

## 4. Commercial Packaging, $0 Working-Capital Model & Compounding Path

Under the `PARKED_IN_INVENTORY` posture, CBa Consult incurs **\$0 working capital** and **zero speculative build hours**. Should an existing HR or Operations client explicitly request digital-wellbeing support during an active engagement, the deliverable would be packaged strictly as a pre-paid, fixed-fee micro-engagement (€1,500–€3,000 advisory/toolkit package) leveraging existing PDF templates and M365/Google Workspace audit checklists rather than software licensing:

- **Zero Upfront Tooling Cost:** Delivered exclusively with standard PDF playbooks and client-owned collaboration telemetry.
- **On-Demand Only:** No pre-built portal, app store maintenance, or community moderation overhead.
- **Compounding Path:** Horizontal attachment to **`OP-HR-007`** (employee-wellbeing workshop add-on) or communication-hygiene norms inside **`OP-CHG-001`**, capturing verified before/after focus metrics for `OP-X-004` without diverting core 2027 pipeline focus.
