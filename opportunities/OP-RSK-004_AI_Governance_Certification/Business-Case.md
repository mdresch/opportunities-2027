## 2. Business Need & Cost of Inaction

Enterprises deploying AI across customer, HR, or risk workflows without mapped governance controls face dual exposure in 2027: statutory enforcement ahead of the December 2, 2027 EU AI Act Annex III deadline (penalties up to €35M or 7% of global turnover) and stalled enterprise procurement from B2B buyers increasingly demanding ISO/IEC 42001 or NIST AI RMF assurance. Attempting separate compliance projects per framework multiplies internal audit fatigue and external legal spend.

Consolidating inventory, Annex III risk classification, and AIMS documentation into one 6–10 week readiness sprint shields client leadership from last-minute Q4 2027 compliance scrambles and establishes repeatable evidence governance before formal registrar Stage 1 audits.
