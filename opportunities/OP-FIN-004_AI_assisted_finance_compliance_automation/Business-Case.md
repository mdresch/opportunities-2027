## 2. Business Need & Cost of Inaction

Small finance teams operating under lean headcount face recurring close bottlenecks, missed statutory/internal deadlines, and fragmented audit evidence.

- **Cost of Inaction:** Hidden controller overtime each month-end, late compliance filings, and brittle manual tracking that breaks whenever a key finance team member is absent.
- **Enterprise Tool Gap:** Buying BlackLine/FloQast-class enterprise close suites is financially unjustifiable at the Small tier, while unguarded autonomous AI promises have eroded CFO trust.

## 4. Commercial Packaging, $0 Working-Capital Model & Compounding Path

- **Working-Capital Discipline:** `$0` upfront cash outlay. Built inside the client's existing Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, or no-code automation subscriptions using CBa Consult prompt and close-checklist templates.
- **Commercial Packaging & Milestones:** Priced within **€500–€2,000** as a fixed-fee add-on module attached to `OP-FIN-001` (100% upfront or 50% kickoff / 50% close-cycle dry run).
- **Success Proof Metric (`OP-X-004`):** Reduction in overdue close tasks and controller reminder-chasing hours verified across one full month-end cycle.
- **Compounding Path:** Designed as a repeatable add-on module for fractional finance clients, expanding one-off `OP-FIN-001` engagements into ongoing compliance-ops oversight.
