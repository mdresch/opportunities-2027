## 2. Business Need & Cost of Inaction

Regulated enterprises deploying high-impact AI models face acute regulatory and audit exposure ahead of the **December 2, 2027 EU AI Act Annex III deadline**. Without standardized model cards, data sheets, and human-readable decision traceability, organizations risk failed internal audits, delayed product approvals, and costly remediation cycles—while Big Four firms face independence restrictions when advising on systems they later audit.

**Cost of Inaction & ADPA Source Baseline:**
- **For Clients:** Unexplained "black box" AI systems block internal-audit sign-off, expose firms to GDPR Art. 22 and EU AI Act penalties, and stall enterprise adoption.
- **For CBa Consult:** Positions `OP-RSK-003` as a high-trust specialist extension to Tier A `OP-RSK-004` without triggering Big Four auditor-independence conflicts.
- **ADPA Modeling Disclaimer:** The assigned ADPA source (`AI Explainability & Audit Specialist.docx`) frames rollout milestones including validating an explainability framework across 3 pilot teams, deploying an artifact generator to 5+ teams, and reaching 80% SDLC compliance-workflow adoption. *Illustrative Pre-Pilot ADPA Source Modeling — Not Realized Client Actuals:* these adoption targets represent illustrative internal project milestones from the source file, **not** realized client outcomes.
