## 1. Executive Commercial Summary & Quarterly Retainer Flywheel

`OP-CHG-002` gives CBa Consult a **fast-close, repeatable executive access offer** priced at **`€500–€2,000 / event`** with **`€0` capital expenditure**. Because every client runs on its existing Microsoft Teams, Zoom, or Slido infrastructure (explicitly retiring the legacy custom-platform capex concept noted in [Ideation-Document.md §3.3](Ideation-Document.md#33-adpa-source-reconciliation-note-custom-platform-retirement)), engagement economics deliver ~100% cash gross margin while placing the CBa Director directly alongside the **CEO, CHRO, and Transformation PMO** every quarter.

**Strategic Portfolio Value:**
1. **Predictable Quarterly Retainer Conversion:** Four quarterly town halls convert a `€1,250–€1,800` trial event into a **`€5,000–€7,200 / yr` recurring annual cadence**.
2. **Pull-Through Engine for `OP-CHG-001` & Diagnostic Audits:** Town hall Q&A telemetry exposes frontline operational friction (Finance close delays, HR/AI confusion, process gaps) that feeds directly into paid **`OP-CHG-001` (Adoption Playbook)**, **`OP-HR-015`**, and **`OP-FIN-009`** follow-on work.

## 4. Client ROI & Measurable Event Impact

Compared to **physical regional all-hands** (`€15k–€60k+` venue/travel/productivity loss) or **AV production agencies** (`€8k–€35k/event`), a **`€1,250–€2,000`** CBa orchestration engagement delivers immediate hard and behavioral returns:

1. **Direct Logistical Cost Avoidance (`≥30%` savings target):** Eliminates multi-site travel, catering, and external studio fees while preserving executive presence across hybrid teams.
2. **Quantified Employee Reach & Participation:** Targets **`≥90%` live + 48-hr replay reach** and **`≥70%` live poll/Q&A engagement**, preventing costly rework and rumors after major policy or finance announcements.
3. **Elimination of Post-Town-Hall Black Holes:** Guarantees **`100%` of unanswered/deferred employee questions receive a named owner & published response within 5 business days (`T+5d`)**.

## 5. Commercial Governance & Rules of Engagement (`Rules-of-Engagement.md`)

All `OP-CHG-002` facilitation engagements strictly enforce CBa Consult's commercial and ethical guardrails:

1. **No Unapproved Rendering / Shadow Facilitation (`Rules-of-Engagement.md §1` & `§5`):**
   - Zero access to executive draft announcements, employee Q&A logs, or client Teams/Zoom tenant links occurs prior to **Gate `G1` (Written PO / Signed SOW)** and **Gate `G2` (NDA / Confidentiality & DPA clearance covering C-Suite strategy and employee PII in chat/Q&A)**.
   - No credit-card checkouts or informal manager side-arrangements (`Commercial-Engagement-Invoicing-and-Procurement-Policy.md`).
2. **Multiparty Approval Gate (`Rules-of-Engagement.md §2`):**
   - Requires written alignment across **Client Sponsor** (CHRO / Internal Comms Lead), **Executive Sponsor** (CEO / Chief of Staff / Transformation Sponsor), **Finance / Procurement** (PO on contracting legal entity; bundle single-event retainers onto an annual PO where possible to avoid repeated procurement cycles), and **CBa Consult**.
3. **Clear IT / AV Technical Boundary:**
   - **Client IT / AV Ops** remains solely responsible for network stability, SSO authentication, tenant recording policies, and streaming platform uptime; CBa Consult owns *facilitation, pacing, dry-run checklist execution, Q&A curation, and follow-up governance*.
4. **Standard SOW Clauses Required (`Rules-of-Engagement.md §8`):**
   - Fixed deliverables (`P1–P4`), target KPIs (`§4`), and **explicit Client Speaker & IT Dry-Run SLA** (`T-2d` mandatory attendance).
   - **Rescheduling / Cancellation Notice Window:** Clear fee terms if executive sponsors postpone `<72` hours before scheduled live broadcast.
   - Strict **NDA / Non-Disclosure** protecting unannounced financials, M&A, or workforce restructurings reviewed during `T-14d` prep, plus **PI insurance certificate availability on request**.

## 6. Risk Register & Mitigations

| ID | Risk Description | Likelihood / Impact | Built-In Mitigation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **R1** | C-Suite speakers skip rehearsal or override the `15–20 min` narrative cap, squeezing Q&A | **High / High** | Bake **mandatory `T-2d` Dry-Run** and fixed time-boxed run-of-show cues directly into SOW; Director holds visible timer discipline live. |
| **R2** | Sensitive or hostile Q&A questions create live executive friction or reputational awkwardness | **Medium / High** | Pre-collect Q&A `T-7d` to brief executives on top 5 hard themes beforehand; use impartial thematic grouping so tough concerns are addressed candidly without personal attacks. |
| **R3** | Live Teams/Zoom audio, screen-share, or Slido failure during broadcast | **Low / High** | Client IT Ops co-hosts live; `T-2d` technical dry-run tests backup co-host, secondary audio bridge, and local slide deck failover. |
| **R4** | Low attendance or passive silent audience across regional business units | **Medium / Medium** | Launch `T-7d` calendar hold + pre-poll prompt; embed 2–3 mandatory live polls during the first 15 minutes to activate remote attendees early. |
| **R5** | Client procurement friction on small standalone POs (`€500–€1,500`) | **Medium / Medium** | Pitch as a **4-Event Annual Retainer (`€4,500–€7,200` single PO)** or attach directly as a milestone line item inside `OP-CHG-001` / Tier A transformation SOWs. |
