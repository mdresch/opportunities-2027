## 2. Business Need & Cost of Inaction

Distributed enterprises running transformation or cost-discipline programs suffer measurable alignment loss when quarterly leadership communications fail to engage hybrid staff:

- **Cost of Inaction:** Unstructured town halls incur unnecessary travel/venue spend, low remote attendance, repeated manager clarification overhead, and rumor-driven productivity drag during organizational transitions.
- **Commercial Value Proposition:** A fixed-scope orchestration service converts fragmented executive updates into measurable participation, structured Q&A resolution, and documented follow-up accountability without software procurement.
- **Model Provenance Notice:** Any engagement-level attendance lift, travel-avoidance savings, or ROI/NPV projections referenced from `Opportunities to Publish/Town Hall Meetings Orchestration.docx` constitute *Illustrative Pre-Pilot ADPA Source Modeling — Not Realized Client Actuals*. Realized metrics must be captured from an actual client baseline during Pilot execution.

## 4. Commercial Packaging, $0 Working-Capital Model & Compounding Path

- **Package Structure:** Fixed-fee per-event orchestration (€500–€2,000 depending on audience size, rehearsal complexity, and post-event follow-up tracking) delivered entirely on the client's existing Teams/Zoom/webinar infrastructure.
- **Working-Capital Discipline:** `\$0` upfront hardware, software, or contractor spend; 100% sweat-equity delivery utilizing CBa Consult's existing **Program, Project & Change Management** templates.
- **Compounding & Cross-Sell Path:** Initial single-event delivery builds C-suite trust and feeds:
  1. A recurring **quarterly town-hall facilitation retainer** (4 events/year predictable revenue).
  2. Warm expansion into broader change-management, transformation PMO, or Tier A governance retainers (`OP-ITG-018`) once executive sponsorship is established.
