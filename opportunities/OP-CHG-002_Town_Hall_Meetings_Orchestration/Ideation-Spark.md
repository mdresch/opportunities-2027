## Positioning

> For **CHROs, Internal Communications Directors, CEO Chiefs of Staff & Transformation PMOs** in Medium & Large enterprises whose all-hands updates suffer from low remote turnout, one-way slide monologues, and unaddressed employee questions, **`OP-CHG-002`: Town Hall Meetings Orchestration** is a **fixed-scope Executive Facilitation & Orchestration service** backed by CBa Consult's **Program, Project & Change Management** toolkit that runs cleanly on the client's existing Teams/Zoom/Slido stack to deliver **structured agendas, moderated live Q&A, and 5-day post-event action closure** with **€0 initial working capital**.

---

## What It Is

End-to-end design, pre-event presenter rehearsal, live impartial moderation/Q&A orchestration, and post-event follow-through tracking (`T-14d` to `T+5d`) that turns passive hybrid company town halls into measurable alignment and engagement ceremonies.

## 7 Standard Pivotal Questions ([ideation-template.md §6b](../../ideation-template.md#6b-standard-opportunity-brief-7-pivotal-questions))

1. **Buyer & 2027 Trigger**  
   **Economic / Executive Buyer:** CHRO, VP Internal Communications, CEO Chief of Staff, or Transformation PMO Director (`Medium & Large` hybrid organizations).  
   **2027 Trigger:** Restructuring, AI-workforce transitions, multi-site Finance/ERP changes, and tighter travel/event budgets require leadership to drive strategy clarity across dispersed teams without flying staff to quarterly physical all-hands venues.

2. **The Wedge (Pain Eliminated)**  
   Eliminates **"executive theater" and post-meeting silence** — where leaders talk at 500+ distributed employees for 45 minutes using dense slide decks, skip sensitive pre-submitted questions, run out of time for Q&A, and leave action items unassigned after the call ends.

3. **Deliverable & Verified Existing IP (`≥70%` Coverage Target)**  
   **Client Deliverables:** 4-Phase Town Hall Runbook (`T-14d` narrative & agenda, presenter coaching + dry-run checklist, live moderated Q&A/polling flow, and `T+5d` Q&A & Action Closure Digest).  
   **Verified Internal IP:**
   - [Ideation-Document.md](Ideation-Document.md) (KPI architecture, 5 core engagement/reliability metrics, stakeholder role matrix, technical/operational checklists, and change/risk controls extracted from `Opportunities to Publish/Town Hall Meetings Orchestration.docx` — reconciled for COTS delivery).
   - Supporting local BD assets indexed in [BD-Quick-Reference.md](BD-Quick-Reference.md) (`CBA - Change Management`, `General/Meetings`).

4. **Sweat-Equity Feasibility (`€0` Cash / Capacity Fit)**  
   **Timeline & Effort:** **2–4 weeks** per event at **Low effort (`~3–5 hrs/wk`, `8–18 hrs` total per town hall)**, easily fitting alongside Tier A advisory work within the **10–15 hrs/week** portfolio cap.  
   **Capital Outlay:** **€0 initial working capital** (`€500–€2,000 / event` fixed fee). Operates natively inside the client's licensed **Microsoft Teams Town Hall / Zoom Events / Slack / Slido** tools.

5. **Outcome & Proof KPI**  
   Measures every town hall using concrete operational targets adapted from the ADPA source charter:
   - **≥95%** technical execution smoothness (zero dead-air/presenter handoff failures after mandatory `T-2d` dry-run).
   - **≥90%** target workforce attendance / 48-hr replay reach (`+20%` lift in remote-employee participation).
   - **≥70%** active attendee interaction via structured Q&A upvoting and live polls.
   - **100%** of unanswered or deferred live Q&A items published with an accountable owner and response within **5 business days (`T+5d`)**.

6. **Scope Boundaries & Primary Risk (`Rules-of-Engagement.md`)**  
   **Explicitly Out of Scope (Negative Scope Boundary):**
   - **Not** bespoke A/V software engineering or building custom streaming platforms (explicitly retiring the legacy custom-platform assumption from the raw Word draft).
   - **Not** physical stage lighting/broadcast crew rental or corporate PR/speechwriting retainers.
   - **Not** shadow delivery without multiparty approval and C-Suite briefing NDA coverage (`Rules-of-Engagement.md`).  
   **#1 Commercial Risk:** Success depends heavily on C-Suite sponsorship and Internal Comms access (`inventory-of-ideas.md`). *Mitigation:* Lock Executive Sponsor + Internal Comms Co-Owner + IT AV Lead in kickoff RACI prior to scheduling.

7. **Compounding & Scale Path**  
   Designed as a **low-friction recurring cadence & pipeline wedge**:
   - Converts single-event pilots into **Recurring Quarterly Town Hall Retainers** (`4 events/yr`).
   - Serves as the launch/alignment kickoff ceremony attaching **`OP-CHG-001` (Framework & Tool Adoption Playbook)**, **`OP-HR-015` (Manager AI Enablement)**, and enterprise audit rollouts (`OP-FIN-009`, `OP-ITG-013`).
