## 1. Problem Context & 2027 Market Trigger

Advisory firms and B2B founders frequently suffer feast-or-famine project revenue: clients complete a one-off diagnostic or purchase a governance template pack, then disengage until a regulatory or operational crisis forces another reactive project. Meanwhile, mid-market leaders in 2027 need continuous updates as EU AI Act standards, IFRS 18 reporting rules, and operational toolkits evolve—without paying full-time consulting retainer rates.

`OP-X-010` adapts CBa Consult's ADPA **Community-as-a-Service** concept (`Subscription & Membership Services.docx`) into a practical, zero-capex recurring membership program backed by the **Customer Experience Strategy & Design Thinking** Signature Toolkit. Rather than building a custom creator-economy SaaS platform from scratch, it converts past diagnostic, template, and micro-service buyers into recurring monthly/annual members receiving live template updates, cross-pillar playbooks, and structured monthly Q&A clinics.

Key commercial drivers for launching this cross-pillar continuity layer in Q2–Q3 2027 include:

- **Post-Project Retention Gap:** Preserving active touchpoints with alumni who finished a 4–8 week sprint (`OP-FIN-*`, `OP-RSK-*`, `OP-MKT-*`) without needing a full-scale retainer.
- **Continuous 2027 Regulatory Drift:** Giving buyers living updates to checklists and governance clauses as EU AI Act, DORA, and IFRS 18 guidance matures.
- **Compounding IP Leverage:** Turning sanitized client artifacts produced during consulting sprints into scalable, multi-subscriber membership drops.

## 2. Solution Architecture & Delivery Workflow

Built in 6–8 weeks at Medium effort using off-the-shelf community and billing tooling:

1. **Weeks 1–2 — Tier Architecture & Value Cadence Design:** Apply Design Thinking member-journey mapping to define 2–3 clear B2B membership tiers (e.g., Template Library Access, Monthly Executive Q&A Clinics, Priority Advisory Credits) and lock a sustainable monthly update cadence.
2. **Weeks 3–4 — Low-Code Hub & Payment Setup:** Configure gated content hosting, Stripe recurring billing, automated onboarding sequences, and member retention triggers on low-cost off-the-shelf platforms (zero bespoke software development).
3. **Weeks 5–6+ — Founding Cohort Launch & Retention Telemetry:** Migrate warm alumni from prior `OP-*` sprints into a founding cohort, track 90-day retention and NPS, and refine content drops based on member Q&A demand.

Core tangible deliverables produced across the setup:

- **Tiered Membership Charter & Pricing Matrix:** Clear packaging separating template access, group Q&A, and paid advisory upgrades.
- **Low-Code Gated Hub & Billing Pipeline:** Configured off-the-shelf portal, Stripe subscription tiers, and automated onboarding emails.
- **Quarterly Content & Q&A Operating Calendar:** Repeatable editorial cadence fed directly by active consulting IP updates.

## 5. Explicit Negative Scope Boundaries & Stage 1b Gate

- **No bespoke multi-creator SaaS marketplace engineering:** CBa Consult will not build or maintain custom community software, AI recommendation engines, or multi-currency creator-payout infrastructure during 2027.
- **No unscalable bespoke 1-on-1 consulting inside base membership fees:** Direct client-specific delivery remains billed via dedicated `OP-*` sprints or retainers; membership covers shared templates and group Q&A clinics.
- **Sequenced behind initial `OP-*` delivery volume:** A membership hub compounds only once enough one-off template/audit buyers exist to seed a viable founding cohort.
