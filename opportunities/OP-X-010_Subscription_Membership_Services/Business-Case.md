## 2. Business Need & Cost of Inaction

Without a structured post-project continuity tier, every completed diagnostic or starter kit ends the commercial relationship. Re-acquiring former buyers for new regulatory deadlines or adjacent toolkits carries high sales friction, leaving recurring advisory demand uncaptured between major projects.

Offering a lightweight recurring tier keeps alumni engaged with living template updates and monthly Q&A clinics so CBa Consult remains the default advisor when larger Tier A transformation or governance budgets open up.

> *Illustrative Pre-Pilot ADPA Source Modeling — Not Realized Client Actuals:* The legacy ADPA source (`Subscription & Membership Services.docx`) models a multi-creator SaaS platform targeting 100 creators, 5,000 paying members, USD 250K–500K ARR within 18 months, 70%+ gross margins, and 80% six-month retention. Those figures belong to an unbuilt creator-marketplace concept and are strictly *Illustrative Pre-Pilot ADPA Source Modeling — Not Realized Client Actuals*. CBa Consult's 2027 business case is scoped instead as a lean B2B alumni/template membership hub launched with \$0 working capital once initial client volume is established.

## 4. Commercial Packaging, $0 Working-Capital Model & Compounding Path

- **Packaging & Pricing Anchor:** Can be deployed either internally as CBa Consult's own cross-pillar membership tier (monthly/annual subscription for living templates + monthly Q&A) or offered as a €1,000–€4,000 advisory setup sprint for partner/client community programs.
- **Zero Working-Capital Mechanics:** Uses transaction-funded or free-tier/low-cost off-the-shelf community + Stripe billing tools (or client-funded accounts when delivered as advisory), preserving the \$0 upfront working-capital rule.
- **Unit-Economics Guardrail:** Cap ongoing Director commitment at 2–4 hours/month for one live group Q&A and batched quarterly template refreshes recycled from active engagements so recurring revenue scales with near-zero marginal delivery cost.
- **Compounding Path:** Every Tier A/B/C sprint (`OP-RSK-004`, `OP-FIN-008`, `OP-MKT-009`, etc.) feeds graduates into the membership ecosystem, building predictable MRR and surfacing expansion triggers for flagship advisory engagements.

## 5. Risk Register & Compensating Controls

| Risk ID | Risk Description | Likelihood / Impact | Compensating Control |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `R-X10-01` | Member churn if ongoing template/Q&A value isn't consistently delivered | High / High | Batch quarterly template refreshes from active client work rather than inventing bespoke content |
| `R-X10-02` | Launching too early with too few alumni to sustain group Q&A energy | Medium / Medium | Gate launch behind achieving 5–10 completed Q1–Q2 client engagements or founding commitments |
| `R-X10-03` | Scope creep from members expecting 1-on-1 consulting inside base fee | Medium / Medium | Restrict membership to group Q&A + shared assets; route 1-on-1 work to paid `OP-*` sprints |
| `R-X10-04` | Platform subscription fees exceed early founding-cohort revenue | Low / Medium | Launch on free-tier/usage-based tooling until recurring MRR covers software overhead 5x |
