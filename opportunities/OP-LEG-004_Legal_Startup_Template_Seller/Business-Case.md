## 2. Business Need & Cost of Inaction

Micro-businesses and early-stage founders frequently operate without consistent NDAs, advisor/contractor onboarding schedules, or baseline data-privacy SOPs due to prohibitive hourly legal fees. Unstructured ad-hoc documents stall partner onboarding and create avoidable operational friction before formal legal counsel is engaged.

For CBa Consult, curating a low-effort digital template pack monetizes existing lifecycle-spec IP with near-zero marginal delivery cost. *Illustrative Pre-Pilot ADPA Source Modeling — Not Realized Client Actuals*: the source ADPA `.docx` sets an illustrative target of 50+ templates and 5% micro-business penetration in 12 months; these are treated strictly as pre-pilot conceptual ambitions rather than validated commercial forecasts.

## 4. Commercial Packaging, $0 Working-Capital Model & Compounding Path

`OP-LEG-004` requires zero paid infrastructure by using CBa Consult's existing document IP and standard digital distribution channels:

- **Starter Pack Offer:** Curated bundle of operational NDAs, advisor/contractor schedules, and data-privacy/BI governance checklists with usage guides.
- **Compounding Path:** Generates evergreen passive/low-touch sales while qualifying buyers who need structured contract/compliance review workflows (`OP-LEG-001`) or HR foundations (`OP-HR-001`).
