## 2. Business Need & Cost of Inaction

Organizations adopting AI workflows face two simultaneous workforce costs: accelerating skills decay in legacy roles and expensive external hiring caused by invisible internal mobility paths. Without a structured capability baseline, CHROs either delay AI transformation or buy costly enterprise "skills cloud" SaaS (€50k–€250k+/yr) on top of broken job architectures.

**Cost of Inaction & ADPA Source Baseline:**
- **For Buyers:** Unmeasured skills decay leads to failed AI rollouts, single-point-of-failure operational risk, and 15–25% recruiting premiums on external hires who could have been reskilled internally.
- **For CBa Consult:** Capturing CHRO inbound pull converts existing HR Transformation IP into a high-margin diagnostic that cross-sells directly into `OP-ITG-014` ontology governance.
- **ADPA Modeling Disclaimer:** As confirmed in [Source-Documents.md](Source-Documents.md), **no ADPA `.docx` ROI or NPV model is assigned** to `OP-HR-010`. Any illustrative benchmark modeling (e.g., avoided recruiting fees or internal mobility savings) must be treated strictly as *Illustrative Pre-Pilot ADPA Source Modeling — Not Realized Client Actuals* until Pilot #1 establishes empirical client baselines.
