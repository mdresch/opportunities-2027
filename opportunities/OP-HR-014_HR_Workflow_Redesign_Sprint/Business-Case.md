## 2. Business Need & Cost of Inaction

CHROs face a credibility gap in 2027: enterprises have funded AI copilots and workforce training, yet HR cycle times (time-to-hire, onboarding readiness, employee ticket resolution) remain flat because legacy processes were never re-engineered around human+AI handoffs:

- **Sunk License Drag:** Inaction leaves organizations absorbing Copilot and HRIS fees without measurable capacity recovery.
- **Compliance Exposure:** Fragmented shadow-AI usage in recruiting and performance reviews creates EU AI Act Annex III governance risk.
- **Decision Paralysis:** A bounded 90-day-aligned sprint gives CHROs auditable baseline-to-pilot evidence to justify scaling what works or halting low-yield automation.

*Illustrative Pre-Pilot Sizing Estimates — Not Realized Client Actuals:* Per [Source-Documents.md](Source-Documents.md), zero ADPA `.docx` ROI/NPV models are assigned to `OP-HR-014`. The €2,000–€8,000 capital/effort envelope in [inventory-of-ideas.md](../../inventory-of-ideas.md) reflects pre-pilot solo-principal sizing only; concrete ROI baselines must be captured from the client's Week 1 workflow telemetry during the pilot.

## 3. Strategic Options Analysis (BABOK v3 Aligned)

| Option | Description | Effort & Cash Outlay | Commercial & Risk Profile | Verdict |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Option A: Single-Workflow 90-Day Sprint** | Fixed-scope baseline -> 5-mode redesign -> live pilot -> scale/stop gate on 1 HR workflow | 4–8 wks (8–12 hrs/wk) / \$0 upfront | Low buyer friction; proves measurable ROI and naturally unlocks `OP-HR-002` | **Recommended** |
| **Option B: Full HR Operating Model Redesign** | Multi-workflow HR transformation spanning TA, L&D, Comp, and Ops simultaneously | Multi-quarter / High | High stall risk; too expensive as an initial wedge against Big Four incumbents | **Deferred (Phase 2 via `OP-HR-002`)** |
| **Option C: Generic Copilot Training** | Half-day HR prompting workshops without process baseline or redesign | 1–2 wks / Low fee | Reinforces the "transformation paradox"; weak differentiation and no compounding path | **Rejected** |

**Options Conclusion:** Option A maximizes buyer conversion by isolating one measurable HR bottleneck while keeping delivery within 8–12 hours per week.

## 4. Commercial Packaging, $0 Working-Capital Model & Compounding Path

`OP-HR-014` operates on a **\$0 initial working-capital** model using CBa Consult's existing HR toolkit, Microsoft *Frontier HR Leader* checklist (01–06), and the client's existing HRIS/M365 environment. The engagement is structured as a fixed-fee sprint anchored around two pre-selected, fast-to-baseline wedge templates—**New-Hire Onboarding** and **Structured Interview Scorecards**—reducing pre-sales scoping friction:

- **Phase 0 Wedge Economics:** Fixed-scope pricing removes open-ended consulting risk for CHROs and protects solo-principal hourly yield.
- **Zero Software Outlay:** Pilot workflows run entirely on client-licensed M365/Copilot, Teams, and existing ATS/HRIS tools.
- **Compounding Path:** Week 7–8 executive gate evidence feeds directly into **`OP-HR-002`** (HR AI Transformation), **`OP-HR-009`** (Human+Agent RACI), **`OP-HR-011`** / **`OP-CHG-001`** (change adoption), and **`OP-X-004`** anonymized case studies.
