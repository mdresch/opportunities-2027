## 2. Business Need & Cost of Inaction

Solo practitioners and micro-consultants lose billable hours and margin when bookkeeping remains manual:

- **Cost of Inaction:** Manual data entry consumes 4–8+ hours monthly, late invoice follow-ups strain working capital, and misclassified receipts trigger avoidable accountant cleanup fees or tax-deadline penalties.
- **Commercial Value Proposition:** Configuring off-the-shelf bank feeds, intelligent expense tagging, and automated invoice reminders saves time, surfaces real-time cash flow, and prepares clean ledgers for tax filing.
- **Model Provenance Notice:** Benchmark targets in `5 Automated Bookkeeping for Freelancers.docx` (such as 80% reduction in manual entry, 95% categorization accuracy, and 50% time savings) represent *Illustrative Pre-Pilot ADPA Source Modeling — Not Realized Client Actuals* and require validation against actual pilot client baselines.

## 4. Commercial Packaging, $0 Working-Capital Model & Compounding Path

- **Package Structure:** Fixed-scope setup sprint (€500–€2,000) using client-licensed accounting software and no-code connectors, paired with a reusable expense-tagging rulebook and cash-flow dashboard template.
- **Working-Capital Discipline:** `\$0` CBa Consult software/API licensing outlay—all underlying accounting subscriptions and bank-feed tools sit directly on the client's own accounts.
- **Compounding & Scale Path:**
  1. **Bundle with `OP-FIN-001`:** Combine automated bookkeeping setup with the Finance starter kit to lift average order value and eliminate fragmented Small-tier sales cycles.
  2. **Template Replication:** Standardize setup checklists per accounting platform so delivery hours drop below 10 hours per client.
