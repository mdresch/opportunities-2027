## 2. Business Need & Cost of Inaction

Small founders frequently delay structured cash-flow tracking until a tax surprise or liquidity crunch forces reactive borrowing. Free household spreadsheets break on business cash timing, while enterprise FP&A software introduces recurring SaaS cost and onboarding drag.

- **Cost of Inaction for Buyer:** Persistent blind spots in 13-week cash runway, missed tax-deductible expense categorization, and 3–5 hours/month of manual spreadsheet reconciliation.
- **Source Modeling Caveat:** *Illustrative Pre-Pilot ADPA Source Modeling — Not Realized Client Actuals:* `Budget & Finance Tracker Templates.docx` projects a `70%` manual data-entry reduction, `95%` reporting accuracy, `500` pilot users, and `80%` retention across 10 API-integrated templates. Those figures are unverified 2026 draft targets tied to fintech integrations and are **not** adopted as CBa Consult commercial commitments.
