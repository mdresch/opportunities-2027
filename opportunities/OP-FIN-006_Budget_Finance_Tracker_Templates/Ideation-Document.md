## 1. Problem Context & 2027 Market Trigger

Founders, solo operators, and Small-tier finance leads struggle with fragmented spreadsheets, manual transaction categorization, and disconnected cash-flow forecasting. While generic personal budgeting spreadsheets exist for free, SMB buyers in 2027 still face three practical triggers:

1. **Cost-Conscious Tool Discipline:** Small businesses avoid premature multi-thousand-euro FP&A subscriptions when cash-flow visibility and tax-deduction readiness can be solved with structured templates.
2. **Resolution-Aligned Workflow Gaps:** Generic templates track backward-looking expenses rather than linking cash flow directly to goal-driven workflows (debt payoff, tax reserve, runway forecasting).
3. **Low-Friction Advisory Entry:** Founders seeking quick financial hygiene prefer a fixed-scope `€300–€1,500` starter kit or setup sprint before committing to fractional CFO retainers (`OP-FIN-001`).
