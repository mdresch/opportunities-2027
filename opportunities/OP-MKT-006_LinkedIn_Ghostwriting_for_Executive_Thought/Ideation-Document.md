## 1. Problem Context & 2027 Market Trigger

B2B founders, CMOs, and consulting leaders recognize LinkedIn as a primary organic demand channel, yet consistently fail to publish beyond sporadic bursts because executive calendars leave zero time for structured drafting, narrative refinement, and audience analytics. Generic AI-generated posts damage executive credibility by sounding synthetic and disconnected from real operator insight.

- **Target Buyers:** Founders, CEOs, CMOs, and B2B Marketing Leads at Small & Mid-tier firms.
- **2027 Market Trigger:** Rising paid-acquisition costs push B2B firms toward founder-led organic authority, while audience fatigue with generic AI content rewards authentic, interview-extracted executive viewpoints.
- **Core Pain (The Wedge):** Executives possess deep domain insights but lack a low-friction system to convert 30-minute conversations into consistent, high-engagement LinkedIn thought leadership.

## 2. Solution Architecture & Delivery Workflow

Built on CBa Consult's **Sales, Marketing & Communication Strategy** Signature Toolkit, `OP-MKT-006` delivers an **8–12 week** fixed-scope executive content sprint:

1. **Phase 1 — Positioning & Voice Calibration (Weeks 1–2):** Conduct a baseline narrative audit, extract the executive's tone-of-voice profile, define 3–4 core authority pillars, and establish target KPIs (reach, engagement rate, inbound connections).
2. **Phase 2 — Structured Executive Extraction Cadence (Weeks 3–10):** Run bi-weekly 30-minute recorded founder interviews using domain prompts to capture authentic stories, client lessons, and market theses.
3. **Phase 3 — Drafting, Review & Publishing Rhythm (Weeks 3–11):** Produce 8–12 tailored LinkedIn posts (and optional newsletter editions) per month, moving each draft through a lightweight single-pass executive approval loop.
4. **Phase 4 — Performance Telemetry & Playbook Handover (Week 12):** Deliver a 90-day engagement scorecard tracking impressions, engagement-rate trajectory, follower growth, and inbound pipeline signals.

### Core Deliverables & Cadence Artifacts

- **Executive Voice & Content Pillar Charter:** Documented editorial guardrails, banned clichés, and 3–4 strategic themes tied to commercial pipeline.
- **Monthly Post & Newsletter Library:** 8–12 interview-derived LinkedIn posts per month ready for native publishing.
- **90-Day Reach & Pipeline Scorecard:** Tracks engagement rate, follower growth, and qualified inbound DM/profile inquiries.

## 5. Explicit Negative Scope Boundaries & Stage 1b Gate

To avoid low-margin agency sprawl under a `$0` working-capital rule, `OP-MKT-006` enforces strict exclusions:

- **Out of Scope:** Paid LinkedIn ad buying/media management, graphic-design/video-production studio retainers, cold outbound SDR dialing, and multi-channel website SEO overhauls.
- **Client Prerequisites:** Guaranteed 30-minute bi-weekly interview slot with the named executive and 48-hour draft approval turnaround.
- **Accountable Ownership:** CBa Consult conducts structured interviews, drafts posts, and tracks engagement KPIs; the executive retains final editorial approval and native account publishing.
- **Stage 1b Review Outcome:** Framed as a Tier C supporting engine; prioritized for CBa internal BD authority building prior to external client pilots.
