## 2. Business Need & Cost of Inaction

B2B founders and executives lose organic pipeline leverage when their LinkedIn presence stalls:

- **Cost of Inaction:** Over-reliance on expensive paid ads or cold outbound, invisible market positioning against vocal competitors, and wasted executive hours spent staring at blank drafts.
- **Commercial Value Proposition:** A structured interview-to-post sprint captures authentic operator expertise in 60 minutes per month of executive time while building measurable reach, engagement, and warm inbound conversations.
- **Model Provenance Notice:** Performance targets referenced in `Opportunities to Publish/LinkedIn Ghost Writing.docx` (such as 8–12 posts/month, >=5% average engagement rate within 3 months, and 70% executive time savings) represent *Illustrative Pre-Pilot ADPA Source Modeling — Not Realized Client Actuals* and must be validated against real account analytics during any Pilot.

## 4. Commercial Packaging, $0 Working-Capital Model & Compounding Path

- **Package Structure:** 8–12 week pilot sprint (€1,000–€3,500 depending on monthly post volume and newsletter inclusion) covering voice calibration, bi-weekly 30-minute interviews, draft revisions, and monthly KPI reporting.
- **Working-Capital Discipline:** `\$0` software or contractor outlay; uses standard recording/transcription workflows and the executive's native LinkedIn account.
- **Compounding & Scale Path:**
  1. **Internal Authority Multiplier:** CBa Consult can reuse the exact interview-to-post workflow to publish flagship whitepapers and posts for `OP-ITG-018` (MSP -> MSSP -> MIP).
  2. **Retainer Conversion:** Successful 12-week client sprints convert into light ongoing monthly retainers using standardized voice libraries.
