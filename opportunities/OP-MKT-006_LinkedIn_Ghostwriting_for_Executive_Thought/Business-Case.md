1. Executive Summary

1.1 Project Overview

The LinkedIn Ghostwriting initiative is a strategic digital content service designed to elevate executive thought leadership through authentic, high-impact LinkedIn newsletters and posts. This project aligns with the growing demand for personalized, value-driven content that establishes credibility, fosters engagement, and drives professional influence in a competitive digital landscape. By leveraging ghostwriting expertise, executives can consistently share insights without the time burden of content creation, while maintaining their unique voice and perspective.

This project operates under the PMBOK 7 framework, emphasizing value delivery, stakeholder engagement, and adaptive planning. The initiative will be structured as a medium-priority project, with a focus on scalability, quality, and measurable outcomes. Key deliverables include a content strategy framework, a library of ghostwritten posts and newsletters, and a performance tracking system to monitor engagement and growth.

1.2 Objectives and Benefits

The primary objectives of this project are to:

Enhance executive visibility by producing 8-12 high-quality LinkedIn posts per month, tailored to the executive’s industry and audience.

Increase engagement by achieving a minimum 5% average engagement rate (likes, comments, shares) within the first three months.

Establish thought leadership by positioning the executive as a go-to resource in their field, measured by follower growth and inbound connection requests.

Streamline content creation by reducing the executive’s time investment in content development by 70%, allowing them to focus on core responsibilities.

The benefits of this initiative extend beyond individual executives. Organizations will see improved brand perception, stronger talent attraction, and enhanced business development opportunities. For executives, the project offers a scalable way to build personal brand equity, expand professional networks, and drive career growth.

1.3 Strategic Alignment

This project aligns with broader organizational goals, including:

Digital transformation: Leveraging digital platforms to amplify leadership presence.

Talent development: Empowering executives to develop and communicate their expertise.

Brand differentiation: Creating unique, authentic content that stands out in a crowded market.

Revenue growth: Indirectly supporting business development by increasing visibility and credibility.

The change control process for this project will follow a 7-step approach to ensure that all changes are evaluated, approved, and implemented effectively:

1. Identify Change: The need for a change is identified by a stakeholder or project team member.

4. Approve or Reject: The Change Control Board (CCB) reviews the change request and decides whether to approve or reject it.

5. Implement Change: If approved, the change is implemented by the project team.

6. Update Documentation: Project documentation (e.g., scope, schedule, budget) is updated to reflect the change.

Business Case: LinkedIn Ghostwriting

1. Executive Summary

1.1 Project Overview

Project Name: LinkedIn Ghostwriting

Business Sponsor: Project Sponsor

Prepared By: Menno Drescher, Senior Project Management Consultant

Date: 2025-12-22

The LinkedIn Ghostwriting initiative is a strategic digital content service designed to elevate executive thought leadership through authentic, high-impact LinkedIn newsletters and posts. This project aligns with the growing demand for personalized, value-driven content that establishes credibility, fosters engagement, and drives professional influence in a competitive digital landscape. By leveraging ghostwriting expertise, executives can consistently share insights without the time burden of content creation, while maintaining their unique voice and perspective.

Operating under the PMBOK® Guide (7th Edition), this project emphasizes value delivery, stakeholder engagement, and adaptive planning. The initiative is structured as a medium-priority project, with a focus on scalability, quality, and measurable outcomes. Key deliverables include a content strategy framework, a library of ghostwritten posts and newsletters, and a performance tracking system to monitor engagement and growth.

1.2 Business Need and Value Proposition

The core business problem addressed by this project is the lack of consistent, high-quality thought leadership content from executives due to time constraints and limited content creation expertise. This gap results in missed opportunities to build professional influence, engage with key audiences, and drive business growth through digital channels. Currently, executives either produce sporadic, low-impact content or forgo content creation entirely, limiting their visibility and credibility in their industries.

The LinkedIn Ghostwriting initiative directly addresses this challenge by providing a structured, scalable solution for producing 8-12 high-quality LinkedIn posts per month, tailored to the executive’s industry and audience. The value proposition includes:

Enhanced executive visibility through consistent, authentic content that reinforces thought leadership.

Increased audience engagement, with a target of 5% average engagement rate (likes, comments, shares) within the first three months.

Time savings for executives, enabling them to focus on core responsibilities while maintaining a strong digital presence.

Strategic alignment with organizational goals, including brand reputation, talent attraction, and business development.

Financially, the project is projected to deliver a Net Present Value (NPV) of $120,000 over three years, with a Return on Investment (ROI) of 150% and a payback period of 18 months. These metrics are based on conservative estimates of engagement-driven opportunities, including speaking invitations, partnership inquiries, and client leads.

1.3 Recommendation

Based on the Cost-Benefit Analysis and Strategic Alignment detailed in this business case, we recommend proceeding with Option 3: Full-Service Ghostwriting Solution. This option delivers the highest Net Value ($120,000 NPV) and aligns most closely with the strategic objective of establishing executives as industry thought leaders. The full-service model includes dedicated ghostwriting, editorial support, analytics, and compliance review, ensuring a seamless, high-quality content creation process. While this option requires the highest upfront investment ($60,000), it offers the most scalable and sustainable solution for long-term value delivery.

2. Problem Statement

2.1 Current State and Enterprise Limitations

The current state of executive thought leadership content creation is characterized by inconsistency, inefficiency, and missed opportunities. Executives face significant challenges in maintaining a regular cadence of high-quality LinkedIn content due to competing priorities, limited time, and a lack of specialized content creation skills. Key limitations include:

Sporadic Content Production: Executives often publish content irregularly, leading to gaps in visibility and reduced audience engagement. Without a structured content calendar, posts are reactive rather than strategic, failing to capitalize on trending topics or industry events.

Inconsistent Quality and Tone: Content produced by executives varies widely in quality, tone, and relevance. Some posts may lack depth or fail to resonate with the target audience, while others may deviate from the executive’s authentic voice, diluting their personal brand.

Limited Audience Engagement: Current engagement metrics (e.g., likes, comments, shares) are suboptimal, with an average engagement rate of 2-3%, below the industry benchmark of 5-7% for thought leadership content. This indicates a disconnect between the content and the audience’s interests or needs.

Resource Constraints: Executives lack dedicated support for content creation, relying on ad-hoc assistance from marketing or administrative teams. This approach is unsustainable and diverts resources from other critical business functions.

Compliance and Legal Risks: Content is often published without thorough review, increasing the risk of compliance violations, miscommunication, or reputational damage. The absence of a structured approval process exacerbates this risk.

These limitations collectively hinder the organization’s ability to leverage LinkedIn as a strategic platform for thought leadership, business development, and brand building.

2.2 Business Impact (Cost of Inaction)

The cost of inaction—failing to address the current limitations in executive thought leadership content creation—is substantial and multifaceted. Quantitatively, the annual impact includes:

Lost Engagement Opportunities: With an average engagement rate of 2-3%, the current approach underperforms the industry benchmark by 2-4%. For an executive with 10,000 followers, this translates to 200-400 fewer engagements per post. Over a year, this results in 24,000-48,000 missed interactions, including likes, comments, and shares, which are critical for expanding reach and influence.

Reduced Lead Generation: Thought leadership content is a proven driver of business development opportunities. Industry data suggests that 30-50% of B2B buyers engage with thought leadership content before making purchasing decisions. The current approach is estimated to miss 10-15 qualified leads annually, equating to $500,000-$750,000 in lost revenue (assuming an average deal size of $50,000).

Diminished Brand Reputation: Inconsistent or low-quality content erodes the executive’s credibility and the organization’s brand reputation. This can lead to reduced speaking invitations, media opportunities, and partnership inquiries, further limiting growth potential.

Increased Compliance Risks: The lack of a structured review process exposes the organization to regulatory fines, legal disputes, or reputational damage. For example, a single compliance violation could result in fines of $10,000-$50,000, in addition to the cost of remediation and brand recovery.

Opportunity Cost of Executive Time: Executives spend an average of 4-6 hours per month on content creation, including drafting, editing, and publishing. This time could be redirected to high-value activities, such as strategic planning, client engagement, or business development, with an estimated opportunity cost of $20,000-$30,000 annually (based on an executive’s hourly rate of $200-$250).

In total, the annual cost of inaction is estimated at $580,000-$830,000, encompassing lost revenue, missed opportunities, and compliance risks. Addressing these challenges through the LinkedIn Ghostwriting initiative is not only a strategic imperative but also a financially sound investment.

3. Solution Options (Strategy Analysis)

Description:

Maintain the current approach to executive content creation, where executives are responsible for drafting, editing, and publishing their own LinkedIn posts and newsletters. This option requires no upfront investment or changes to existing processes but perpetuates the limitations outlined in Section 2.1. Content production remains ad-hoc, with executives relying on internal marketing or administrative teams for occasional support. No dedicated resources, tools, or processes are allocated to improve content quality, consistency, or engagement.

Pros/Cons:

Pros:

No upfront investment or resource allocation required.

Minimal disruption to existing workflows.

Maintains full control over content with the executive.

Cons:

Continued low engagement rates (2-3%), below industry benchmarks.

High opportunity cost of executive time, estimated at $20,000-$30,000 annually.

Increased compliance and reputational risks due to lack of structured review processes.

Missed revenue opportunities, estimated at $500,000-$750,000 annually from lost leads and partnerships.

Inconsistent content quality and tone, diluting the executive’s personal brand and thought leadership.

Estimated Cost:

Annual Cost of Inaction: $580,000-$830,000 (as detailed in Section 2.2).

No upfront or operational costs, but the financial and strategic impact of inaction far outweighs any short-term savings.

3.2 Option 2: Hybrid In-House Solution

Description:

Implement a hybrid in-house solution that combines internal resources with limited external support. This option involves hiring a part-time ghostwriter (20 hours/week) to assist executives with content creation, while leveraging existing marketing and compliance teams for editing and approvals. A basic content calendar and performance tracking system (e.g., LinkedIn Analytics) would be introduced to improve consistency and engagement. The ghostwriter would work closely with the executive to develop content in their voice, while the marketing team ensures alignment with brand guidelines.

Pros/Cons:

We recommend proceeding with Option 3: Full-Service Ghostwriting Solution as the optimal approach for the LinkedIn Ghostwriting initiative. This recommendation is based on the following key justifications:

1. Highest Net Value: Option 3 delivers the highest Net Value ($540,000 over 5 years) and a positive NPV of $120,000, demonstrating strong financial viability. While the upfront investment is higher than other options, the quantified benefits ($1,200,000 over 5 years) far outweigh the costs, resulting in a 150% ROI and an 18-month payback period.

2. Strategic Alignment: The full-service solution aligns most closely with the organization’s strategic goals, including enhancing executive thought leadership, driving audience engagement, and generating business development opportunities. By achieving a 5-7% average engagement rate, this option maximizes the impact of the executive’s digital presence, positioning them as industry leaders and attracting high-value opportunities.

3. Scalability and Sustainability: Option 3 provides a structured, end-to-end content creation process, with dedicated resources for ghostwriting, editing, analytics, and compliance. This ensures consistency, quality, and scalability, enabling the executive to maintain a strong digital presence without diverting time from core responsibilities.

4. Risk Mitigation: The full-service model includes comprehensive compliance reviews, performance tracking, and risk management processes, reducing the likelihood of legal, reputational, or operational issues. The structured approach also minimizes the risk of misalignment with the executive’s voice or audience interests.

5. Executive Time Savings: By reducing the executive’s content creation burden by 90%, Option 3 frees up 4-6 hours per month for high-value activities, such as strategic planning, client engagement, and business development. This time savings is estimated at $50,000 annually, further enhancing the project’s financial and strategic value.

While Option 2 (Hybrid In-House) offers a lower-cost alternative, it lacks the scalability, engagement potential, and long-term sustainability of the full-service solution. Option 1 (Status Quo) is not viable due to its negative Net Value (-$2,900,000) and high cost of inaction ($580,000 annually).
