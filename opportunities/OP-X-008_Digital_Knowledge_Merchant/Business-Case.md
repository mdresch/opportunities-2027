## 2. Business Need & Cost of Inaction

Every Wave 1–2 engagement produces repeatable questionnaires, trace matrices, and governance checklists. Leaving those artifacts un-monetized inside local folders caps CBa Consult's earnings strictly to billable hours (`10–15 hrs/wk` ceiling) and misses buyers who prefer self-serve toolkits before hiring an advisor.

- **Source Modeling Caveat:** *Illustrative Pre-Pilot ADPA Source Modeling — Not Realized Client Actuals:* `Digital Knowledge Merchant.docx` models a `$15,000` initial investment, break-even at Month 10, `$3,500/month` by Month 12, `$5,000/month` recurring revenue by Month 18, `15–20` products in Year 1, `60–70%` gross margins, and a `$335B` Statista global market figure. Those projections assume capital outlay and full-time publishing capacity—they are **not** CBa Consult's `$0`-capital budget or validated sales forecast.
