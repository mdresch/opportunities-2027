## 1. Problem Context & 2027 Market Trigger

Consulting practices routinely leave reusable frameworks, assessment questionnaires, and implementation checklists locked inside one-off client folders. In 2027, three shifts make packaging that IP into standalone digital products viable without upfront capital:

1. **Zero-CAC Digital Shelf Leverage:** Buyers who are not yet ready for a `€3,500–€12,000` advisory engagement willingly purchase `€49–€500` playbooks, checklists, and compendiums to test methodology rigor first.
2. **Cross-Pillar Asset Accumulation:** Across CBa Consult's 17 pillars and 18 Signature Toolkits (plus `OP-X-004` sanitized case briefs and GEO benchmarks), raw templates already exist and only require systematic curation.
3. **Zero-Inventory Economics:** Modern digital distribution (Gumroad / Lemon Squeezy / direct portfolio checkout) eliminates physical fulfillment while creating an evergreen discovery funnel into Tier A advisory.
