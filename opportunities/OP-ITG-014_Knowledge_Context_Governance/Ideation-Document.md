# Ideation Document — `OP-ITG-014`: Knowledge Intelligence & Context Governance Assessment

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Governance & SSOT Callout:** This Stage 1b Ideation Document consolidates CBa Consult's Tier A flagship framing under [Tailored Hybrid SIDPA Governance](../../docs/governance/Tailored-Hybrid-SIDPA-Governance.md), reconciled against [Tier_A_Executive_Summary_2027.md](../../Tier_A_Executive_Summary_2027.md), [inventory-of-ideas.md](../../inventory-of-ideas.md), and [Tier-2-Market-Research.md](Tier-2-Market-Research.md). Client actuals remain unrecorded pending Stage 4 pilot execution.

## 1. Problem Context & 2027 Market Trigger

Mid-to-large enterprises deploying Microsoft 365 Copilot, internal RAG pipelines, and enterprise agents consistently discover that unstructured content sprawl across SharePoint, Teams, and Confluence degrades answer quality and amplifies oversharing risk. Conflicting SOP versions, stale wikis, and absent ownership metadata poison AI context before models ever run.

- **Target Buyer:** CIOs, Chief Data Officers (CDOs), and Chief AI Officers (CAIOs) in **Medium & Large** enterprises.
- **2027 Market Trigger:** Microsoft's **Work IQ / IQ Platform** (Ignite 2026) and Gartner's **AI Context Platforms** category elevate context governance from an informal KM cleanup task into a funded enterprise architecture priority alongside Copilot licensing.
- **Strategic Wedge:** Pair seamlessly with `OP-ITG-013` via [Combined-ITG-013-014-Phase-0-Wedge.md](Combined-ITG-013-014-Phase-0-Wedge.md) to give buyers both AI governance guardrails and an agent-ready organizational intelligence layer.

## 2. Solution Architecture & Delivery Workflow

`OP-ITG-014` is an **Audit / Diagnostic** built on the **Data Analytics Strategy** toolkit and CBa Consult's proprietary **ADPA**, **ECS (Evidence Chain & Context Scoring)**, and **Knowledge Graph / Ontology** IP, delivered over **6–10 weeks** (8–12 hrs/wk) with **$0 initial working capital**.

1. **Discovery & Inventory (Weeks 1–2):** Execute [Knowledge-Context-Governance-Questionnaire.md](Knowledge-Context-Governance-Questionnaire.md) and populate [Context-Source-Inventory-Register.md](Context-Source-Inventory-Register.md) across M365, SharePoint, Teams, and Confluence.
2. **Diagnostic Workshop & Scoring (Weeks 3–5):** Facilitate [Workshop-Agenda.md](Workshop-Agenda.md) and score repositories on RAG readiness, taxonomy maturity, ontology maturity, permission hygiene, and lifecycle staleness.
3. **Architecture Blueprint & Remediation Plan (Weeks 6–8):** Synthesize findings using [Delivery-Playbook-and-SOW.md](Delivery-Playbook-and-SOW.md) into a prioritized context-governance charter and metadata/lineage roadmap.
4. **Executive Readout (Weeks 9–10):** Present scored maturity profile and Phase 1 remediation business case via [Executive-Readout-Template.md](Executive-Readout-Template.md).

## 5. Explicit Negative Scope Boundaries & Stage 1b Gate

To keep the diagnostic wedge fast and capital-free, `OP-ITG-014` excludes downstream engineering execution unless separately contracted:

- **No Bulk Content Migration or Manual Rewriting:** CBa Consult diagnoses, scores, and designs governance rules; mass SharePoint file cleanup or manual SOP rewriting by client teams is out of scope.
- **No Production LLM / RAG Software Build:** Excludes custom Azure AI Search coding, Copilot Studio bot deployment, or M365 tenant administration changes.
- **No Unbounded Tenant Crawling:** Operates via structured sampling, inventory registers, and read-only metadata exports rather than intrusive production ingestion.
