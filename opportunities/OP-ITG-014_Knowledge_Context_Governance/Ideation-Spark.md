## Positioning

> For **CIOs, CDOs, and Chief AI Officers** at medium-and-large enterprises who struggle with **unstructured SharePoint/Teams/Confluence content that isn't actually usable as AI context**, **Knowledge Intelligence & Context Governance Assessment** is an **Audit / Diagnostic** backed by **your own ADPA, ECS, Ontologies & Knowledge Graphs IP** that delivers **a scored RAG-readiness, taxonomy, and ontology maturity profile** with $0 working capital.

## What it is

Assess SharePoint, Teams, M365, and Confluence for RAG readiness, taxonomy maturity, ontology maturity, and knowledge lifecycle — turning organizational intelligence into an agent-ready context layer.

## 7 Pivotal Questions

1. **Buyer & 2027 Trigger** — CIO / CDO / Chief AI Officer. Microsoft Ignite 2026's Work IQ / IQ Platform push and Gartner's AI Context Platforms category make "is our content actually usable by agents" a live 2027 budget line.
2. **The Wedge (Pain)** — Years of unstructured content sprawl across legacy SharePoint/Teams/Confluence tenants means most enterprises have no real idea whether their own knowledge is RAG-ready or just noise.
3. **Deliverable & Existing IP** — This is flagged in the inventory as the **strongest personal IP match** in the whole portfolio: it directly leverages your own ADPA (asset/document processing), ECS (Evidence & Context/Chain-of-Something Scoring — confirm exact expansion before client-facing use), Ontologies & Knowledge Graphs work, not third-party frameworks.
4. **Sweat-Equity Feasibility** — 6–10 weeks at 8–12 hrs/wk, High effort — same band as `OP-ITG-013`, and the two make a natural combined Phase 0 wedge (see the shared "IT Technical & Information Quality Baseline" entry wedge in the Tier A Executive Summary).
5. **Outcome & Proof** — A scored taxonomy/ontology/RAG-readiness report per content source, identifying exactly where an agent would currently retrieve garbage vs. trustworthy context.
6. **Boundaries & Risk** — Legacy tenant sprawl is the main execution risk, not the methodology — some clients will have years of ungoverned content that no single assessment cycle can fully untangle.
7. **Compounding / Scale Path** — Pairs with `OP-DTX-005` (Microsoft IQ & Copilot Studio FinOps Rollout) and feeds `OP-FIN-011` (Intelligence Capital Measurement Framework, Tier D thought leadership).
