## Positioning

> For **CIOs, CDOs, and Chief AI Officers** who struggle with **SharePoint/Teams/Confluence content that isn't trustworthy as AI context**, **Knowledge Intelligence & Context Governance Assessment** is an **Audit / Diagnostic** backed by **ADPA, ECS (Evidence Chain & Context Scoring), and Knowledge Graph** IP that delivers a **scored RAG-readiness, taxonomy, and ontology profile** at **\$0** working capital.

## What it is

Assess M365, SharePoint, Teams, and Confluence for **RAG readiness, taxonomy maturity, ontology maturity, and knowledge lifecycle** — an agent-ready context layer.

6. **Boundaries & Risk** — Legacy sprawl may exceed one cycle; assessment defines backlog, not full cleanup.
