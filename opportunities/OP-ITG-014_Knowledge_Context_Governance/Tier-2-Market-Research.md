# Tier-2 Market Research & Competitive Validation — `OP-ITG-014`: Knowledge & Context Governance

## 1. Market Demand & 2027 Enterprise AI Context Bottleneck

While enterprises race to adopt Microsoft 365 Copilot, *Work IQ / IQ Platform* (MS Ignite 2026), and autonomous agents, **~50% of enterprise AI initiatives stall on data and context readiness** — not model quality:
1. **Unstructured Content Sprawl:** SharePoint, Teams, OneDrive, and Confluence hold years of duplicated, superseded, conflicting, and unlabelled documents that poison RAG retrieval.
2. **Oversharing & Broken Permissions:** Deploying Copilot/RAG over flat SharePoint permissions exposes confidential HR, payroll, legal, and M&A folders to unauthorized employees and agents.
3. **Absence of Semantic Backbone:** Most mid-market and large enterprises lack canonical entity definitions, domain taxonomies, or ontologies connecting documents, processes, and operational systems.
