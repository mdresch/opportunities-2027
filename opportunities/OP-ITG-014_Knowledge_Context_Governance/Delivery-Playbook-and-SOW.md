## 1. Two-Tier Engagement Structure

- **Tier 1 — Phase 0 Information Quality & Oversharing Baseline (`3 weeks · €2,000–€5,000`):** Rapid diagnostic across top 5–10 SharePoint/Teams/Confluence spaces, P0 oversharing red-flag report, and executive readiness readout.
- **Tier 2 — Full Knowledge Intelligence & Context Governance Blueprint (`6–10 weeks · €3,500–€10,000`):** Complete 4-workstream delivery below.

## 2. 6–10 Week Delivery Sprint Plan

| Weeks | Workstream | Key Activities | Deliverable Signed Off |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Weeks 1–2** | **Content & RAG Readiness Audit** | Run 25-control [Knowledge-Context-Governance-Questionnaire.md](Knowledge-Context-Governance-Questionnaire.md); scan M365/SharePoint/Teams/Confluence for duplication, stale SOPs, and broken permissions; define golden RAG test set. | **RAG & *Work IQ* Readiness Scorecard** |
| **Weeks 3–5** | **Taxonomy & Ontology Blueprint (ADPA/ECS)** | Define canonical business entities (Customer, Contract, Policy, Control, System); design Term Store taxonomy & cross-system Knowledge Graph relationships linking structured ERP/CRM to unstructured docs. | **Enterprise Context & Ontology Blueprint** |
| **Weeks 6–7** | **Knowledge Lifecycle & Split-Clock Retention** | Establish authoritative Source-of-Truth designation, automated draft/archive quarantine rules, and Split-Clock GDPR erasure vs. WORM audit retention controls. | **Knowledge Lifecycle & Provenance Policy** |
| **Weeks 8–10** | **Zero-Oversharing & 30/60/90-Day Roadmap** | Map Purview sensitivity labels, Role-Based Context Scoping for human vs. agent queries, and prioritized remediation backlog unblocking Copilot / `OP-ITG-013` agents. | **Zero-Oversharing & 30/60/90-Day Context Roadmap** |

## 3. Fixed-Scope SOW Shell
- **Fee & Duration:** Tier 1 (`€2,000–€5,000`, 3 wks) or Tier 2 (`€3,500–€10,000`, 6–10 wks), billed 50% at kickoff / 50% at final roadmap presentation.
- **Explicit Out-of-Scope:** Bulk manual file migration of legacy file shares or bespoke LLM fine-tuning.
