## 2. Business Need & Cost of Inaction

Enterprises investing heavily in Microsoft 365 Copilot and internal RAG assistants face stalling adoption when SharePoint, Teams, and Confluence repositories suffer from duplicate SOPs, broken taxonomy, and permission oversharing. Doing nothing leaves CIOs exposed to hallucinated agent outputs, compliance leaks, and wasted AI license spend just as Microsoft Work IQ / IQ Platform and Gartner AI Context Platforms reshape 2027 buyer expectations. In the underlying ADPA `.docx` digest (`Build a Trusted AI Ready Data Supply`), an objective of achieving a 95% accuracy rate in data used for AI training is cited (*Illustrative Pre-Pilot ADPA Source Modeling — Not Realized Client Actuals*); realized client metrics will be established only during live pilot scoring.
