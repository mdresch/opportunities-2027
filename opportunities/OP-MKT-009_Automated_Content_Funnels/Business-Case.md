## 2. Business Need & Cost of Inaction

Small and mid-sized B2B firms routinely publish insights or run webinars without an automated follow-up mechanism, causing warm prospects to leak out of the pipeline within 48 hours. Maintaining manual outreach consumes founder and marketing hours that could go toward closing qualified conversations.

Without a standardized repurposing and nurture loop, marketing teams stay trapped in linear content production where every campaign starts from zero and conversion drop-off points remain invisible.

> *Illustrative Pre-Pilot ADPA Source Modeling — Not Realized Client Actuals:* The legacy ADPA source (`Automated Content Funnels.docx`) projects 80% sales automation, 5,000 leads/month, and USD 50,000/year passive revenue per digital product (USD 150,000/year across three B2C products) alongside a custom `SocalEngage` platform concept. All such legacy ADPA figures are strictly *Illustrative Pre-Pilot ADPA Source Modeling — Not Realized Client Actuals* and do not reflect CBa Consult's B2B client-service economics, which are anchored to fixed €600–€2,500 client delivery engagements.

## 4. Commercial Packaging, $0 Working-Capital Model & Compounding Path

- **Packaging & Pricing Anchor:** Fixed-scope 4–6 week Automated Content Funnel Sprint inside the €600–€2,500 Small-tier band (50% deposit / 50% live-funnel handover), pairing implementation with an editable template pack.
- **Zero Working-Capital Mechanics:** Built entirely on the client's existing ESP/CRM, CMS, and low-cost automation subscriptions (e.g., Make/Zapier/n8n or native ESP workflows), keeping CBa Consult's upfront working capital at \$0.
- **Unit-Economics Guardrail:** Restrict build scope to 1 lead magnet, 1 opt-in flow, 1 7-day sequence, and 1 repurposing pipeline so Low–Med delivery effort yields strong effective hourly margin inside the €600–€2,500 fee band.
- **Compounding Path:** Generates fast early cash flow and quantifiable opt-in/conversion proof (`OP-X-004`), while each engagement refines reusable B2B funnel blueprints and creates an upsell bridge into `OP-X-010` recurring membership/community programs or broader GTM strategy.
