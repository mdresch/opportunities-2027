## 2. Business Need & Cost of Inaction

Small founders and lean operations teams adopt Notion or Obsidian to centralize SOPs, projects, and knowledge, yet without operating-model discipline their workspaces quickly fracture into unsearchable silos. Inaction wastes team onboarding hours and encourages redundant SaaS subscriptions. While the ADPA source `.docx` (`Notion Obsidian Consultant.docx`) frames a digital productivity dashboard venture aiming at passive template monetization alongside custom setups, no quantitative conversion or passive-income projections are claimed as realized actuals (*Illustrative Pre-Pilot ADPA Source Framing — Not Realized Client Actuals*).

## 4. Commercial Packaging, $0 Working-Capital Model & Compounding Path

- **Tiered Packaging (€600–€3,000):** Entry self-serve/guided template pack at the lower band; full 4–6 week "done-with-you" team operating dashboard installation and onboarding workshop up to €3,000.
- **$0 Working-Capital Model:** Built directly inside the client's existing Notion or Obsidian environment using CBa Consult's internal operating-model templates, with zero software hosting or ad spend required.
- **Compounding Path:** Every client setup refines downloadable workspace templates for repeat resale and creates a practical Small-tier bridge toward higher-value knowledge governance (`OP-ITG-014`).
