## 1. Problem Context & 2027 Market Trigger

Small-tier founders, solo operators, and lean RevOps/Ops teams adopt Notion or Obsidian hoping for an all-in-one operating hub, only to end up with fragmented pages, broken relational databases, and unused trackers. In 2027, cost-conscious small teams want lightweight, modular productivity dashboards and knowledge bases installed quickly without paying for enterprise ERP software or full-time operations coordinators.

- **Target Buyer:** Founders, CROs, and Sales/RevOps Leads (**Small** tier).
- **Core Wedge Pain:** Unstructured Notion/Obsidian workspaces that slow onboarding, obscure project status, and fail to standardize lightweight ops tracking.
- **2027 Market Trigger:** Demand for fixed-scope, low-risk workspace templates paired with "done-with-you" setup (€600–€3,000) that eliminate tool sprawl immediately.

## 2. Solution Architecture & Delivery Workflow

`OP-OPS-006` combines a **Template / Tool Service** with **Operating Model & Organization Design** principles over **4–6 weeks** (Low–Med effort, **$0 initial working capital**):

1. **Week 1 — Workflow & Tool Alignment:** Audit current notes, task trackers, and team rituals; lock tool choice (Notion for collaborative team ops vs. Obsidian for local-first knowledge graphs).
2. **Weeks 2–3 — Modular Workspace Build:** Configure pre-built CBa Consult dashboard modules covering project tracking, SOP knowledge base, goal cadence, and lightweight CRM/ops queues.
3. **Week 4 — Team Onboarding & Training:** Run a live handover workshop teaching database hygiene, tagging conventions, and weekly review rituals.
4. **Weeks 5–6 — Calibration & Template Packaging:** Refine friction points from live usage and extract anonymized template patterns for repeatable digital distribution.

## 5. Explicit Negative Scope Boundaries & Stage 1b Gate

To avoid endless tinkering and low-margin customization traps, `OP-OPS-006` enforces clear negative boundaries:

- **No Custom Software / Plugin Coding:** Excludes writing custom Obsidian TypeScript plugins, Notion API middleware backends, or third-party SaaS engineering.
- **No Unbounded Page Migration:** Bulk legacy file/email migration is excluded beyond structured starter datasets and SOP templates.
- **Single-Platform Commitment per Engagement:** Client selects either Notion or Obsidian at kickoff; dual-platform mirroring is out of scope.
- **Stage 1b Gate Readiness:** Ideation framing is complete; over-customization controls and dual-track (template + setup) packaging are evaluated in [Business-Case.md](Business-Case.md).
