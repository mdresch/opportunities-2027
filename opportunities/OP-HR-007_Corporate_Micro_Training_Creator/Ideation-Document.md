## 1. Problem Context & 2027 Market Trigger

Small and mid-sized organizations face continuous upskilling pressure—onboarding new hires onto internal workflows, rolling out AI/software tools, and enforcing compliance policies—yet traditional multi-hour LMS courses suffer from low completion rates, high production costs, and rapid obsolescence. HR and People Ops teams lack internal instructional design capacity to turn dense policy documents or tool SOPs into crisp, consumable learning.

In 2027, tighter L&D budgets and mandatory compliance training cycles (EU AI Act literacy, data privacy, security hygiene, and workplace process updates) push CHROs and HR Transformation Leads toward just-in-time **5–10 minute micro-training modules** that minimize employee downtime while demonstrating verifiable completion.

- **Target Buyer Segment:** CHROs, People Ops, and HR Transformation Leads in Small & Mid organizations.
- **Core Wedge Pain:** Low engagement and high employee time-off-task from traditional long-form compliance and tool training.
- **Positioning Anchor:** Fixed-scope Track A micro-training starter kit backed by the **Human Resources & Talent Management** toolkit with `$0` initial working capital.

## 2. Solution Architecture & Delivery Workflow

While the legacy ADPA draft envisioned a 50-module library at venture scale, CBa Consult scopes `OP-HR-007` down into a lean **4–6 week** Track A **Digital Product / Starter Kit**:

| Phase | Timing | Core Activities | Concrete Client Deliverables |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Skill-Gap & Compliance Scoping** | Week 1 | Lock exact target audience and 3–5 priority 5–10 minute training topics (strictly capped to prevent module bloat). | Micro-Training Scope Brief & Learning Objectives Matrix |
| **2. Modular Script & Visual Authoring** | Weeks 2–3 | Convert client SOPs/policies into structured micro-scripts, slide/PDF job aids, and concise AI-assisted video/walkthrough segments. | Script Pack & Draft 5–10 Minute Modules (Pilot Batch) |
| **3. Pilot Cohort Review & Quiz Gate** | Weeks 4–5 | Test modules with a representative employee cohort; add 3-question comprehension checks and satisfaction feedback loop. | Comprehension Quiz Pack & Pilot Feedback Summary |
| **4. Handover & Reusable Authoring Kit** | Week 6 | Deliver final SCORM/PDF/video assets plus a repeatable CBa micro-module template so internal HR can update future modules. | Final Micro-Training Pack & Internal Authoring Playbook |

### Design Principles from ADPA Source

- **Outcome Over Output:** Each 5–10 minute unit targets one observable workplace behavior or compliance check rather than passive slide narration.
- **Workflow-Embedded Format:** Portable PDF job aids, short video clips, and lightweight comprehension checks deployed inside the client's existing Teams/SharePoint/LMS environment.
- **Anti-Bloat Gate:** Strict upfront cap on module count (3–5) and maximum runtime (10 minutes) protects both client learner attention and CBa delivery margin.

## 5. Explicit Negative Scope Boundaries & Stage 1b Gate

- **In Scope:** Scoping and authoring a capped pilot batch of 5–10 minute micro-training modules (video/PDF/quiz job aids) plus a reusable internal template kit.
- **Explicitly Out of Scope:** Building a custom LMS software platform, managing enterprise-wide L&D operations, or committing to an unpriced 50-module catalog upfront.
- **Required Delivery Skills:** Instructional design, AI/video authoring, and corporate L&D facilitation (Director-led, zero external agency spend).
- **Client Responsibilities:** Provide source SOPs/policies, designate one SME reviewer, and host final modules on internal Teams/SharePoint/LMS.
- **Target Outcome & Proof (`OP-X-004`):** Portable 5–10 minute micro-training pack + verified completion/quiz lift over long-form baselines.
- **Compounding / Scale Path:** Gradually productize reusable non-client-specific core modules into a subscription micro-learning library.
- **Primary Boundary Risk:** Requires crisp topic and revision scoping so starter packs do not bloat beyond Low–Med effort.
- **Lifecycle & Gate Status:** `Status = Idea` | `Active Pilots = 0`. Promotion to Stage 2 (`Hypothesis-Register.md`) and Stage 4 (`G4`) requires locking a capped module count (e.g., 3–5 modules) and validating buyer willingness to pay inside the €800–€3,000 envelope.
