## 2. Business Need & Cost of Inaction

Small and mid-sized employers lose productive staff hours when onboarding or compliance training relies on bloated multi-hour workshops or unreadable policy PDFs.

- **Cost of Inaction:** Low completion rates, slow tool/SOP onboarding, repeated HR administrative follow-ups, and unverified EU AI Act / compliance literacy across staff.
- **Time-to-Value Advantage:** Replacing 60-minute passive sessions with focused 5–10 minute modules preserves billable/operational employee hours while creating auditable quiz completion records.

*Note on ADPA Source Evidence:* The legacy source document `4 Corporate Micro Training Creator.docx` models an aspirational scale-up trajectory (50 modules, 100 corporate clients, \$2M revenue by Q2 2028, 40% training-time reduction, 90% user satisfaction). All such figures are **Illustrative Pre-Pilot ADPA Source Modeling — Not Realized Client Actuals** and are intentionally down-scoped under CBa Consult's `$0`-capital, fixed-scope 2027 governance model.

## 4. Commercial Packaging, $0 Working-Capital Model & Compounding Path

- **Working-Capital Discipline:** `$0` upfront cash outlay. Modules are authored on demand for paying pilot clients using existing instructional design templates and client-provided SOPs/tools.
- **Commercial Packaging & Milestones:** Priced within **€800–€3,000** for a capped pilot starter pack (3–5 micro-modules + comprehension checks + internal authoring template), billed 50% kickoff / 50% final asset delivery.
- **Success Proof Metric (`OP-X-004`):** Module completion rate, quiz pass rate, and post-training satisfaction score captured across the pilot cohort.
- **Compounding Path:** Retain non-client-specific structural templates and compliance core modules to gradually assemble a repeatable, productized **subscription micro-learning library** without speculative upfront inventory.
