## 2. Business Need & Cost of Inaction

Every new CBa Consult assessment, starter kit, or Track B wedge faces the same early-stage hurdle: prospects hesitate to pay full commercial rates without peer proof, yet delivering unconditioned free pilots drains Director capacity without yielding structured metrics or testimonials.

- **Cost of Inaction:** Slow Q1–Q2 portfolio launch velocity, unvalidated template UX friction, and delayed Case Study proof (`OP-X-004`).
- **Compounding Ecosystem Engine:** Standardizing early-adopter co-design creates a repeatable launch ramp across every pillar without external research spend.

## 4. Commercial Packaging, $0 Working-Capital Model & Compounding Path

- **Working-Capital Discipline:** `$0` upfront cash outlay. Uses existing CBa Consult contracts, survey forms, and Google/M365 collaboration tools; pilot clients pay a committed early-adopter fee rather than receiving free work.
- **Commercial Packaging & Terms:** Structured at **€300–€1,500 / launch** (or a time-boxed founding-cohort credit off standard list pricing) in exchange for signed co-design terms: (1) Week 1 baseline metric, (2) Week 5 feedback interview, and (3) Week 6 Case Study / testimonial sign-off.
- **Success Proof Metric (`OP-X-004`):** Completed structured feedback rate (100% of pilot cohort) and ≥1 publishable Case Study/testimonial per launch cycle.
- **Compounding Path:** Designed to become CBa Consult's **standard launch path** for every new template, diagnostic, or starter kit across Pillars 1–15.

## 5. Risk Register & Compensating Controls

| Risk ID | Risk Description | Likelihood / Impact | Compensating Control |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **R-01** | Pilot clients take the discount but fail to provide structured feedback or testimonials. | High / Medium | Tie final handover deliverable or post-pilot bonus review explicitly to completion of the Week 5–6 feedback/metric step. |
| **R-02** | Expectation mismatch—early-adopter clients treat a co-design pilot like bespoke consulting. | Medium / Medium | Lock capped scope, fixed revision limits, and explicit "co-design v1.0" expectations in the Pilot Charter. |
| **R-03** | Excessive discounting anchors future full-price buyers too low. | Medium / Medium | State standard commercial anchor price visibly on every invoice/SOW with an explicit one-time "Founding Co-Design Credit". |
