# Ideation Document — `OP-HR-012`: Workforce Listening & Qualitative Theme Analytics

> **Governance SSOT:** Governed by [Tailored-Hybrid-SIDPA-Governance.md](../../docs/governance/Tailored-Hybrid-SIDPA-Governance.md), tracked in [inventory-of-ideas.md](../../inventory-of-ideas.md), and evaluated alongside [Tier_A_Executive_Summary_2027.md](../../Tier_A_Executive_Summary_2027.md). ## 1. Problem Context & 2027 Market Trigger

CHROs and People Analytics teams at medium and large enterprises collect thousands of open-text comments across annual engagement surveys, pulse checks, and exit interviews. Most teams lack the governance rules and structured synthesis workflows to convert raw qualitative text into executive-ready action priorities without violating employee privacy, works-council agreements, or AI transparency rules.

- **Target Buyer:** CHROs and People Analytics Leads at Medium & Large organizations (typically 250–5,000+ employees).
- **The Wedge (Pain):** Open-text feedback sits unread in spreadsheets or triggers ad-hoc manager forwarding that breaks anonymity promises in small departments.
- **2027 Market Trigger:** Sourced from Microsoft *Frontier HR Leader* eBook (Use Case 06) and reinforced by **EU Pay Transparency / GDPR / EU AI Act** workplace scrutiny, boards expect structured listening programs that demonstrate responsiveness while strictly prohibiting individual surveillance or re-identification.

## 2. Solution Architecture & Delivery Workflow

Delivered as a 6–8 week fixed-scope diagnostic (`6–10 hrs/wk`, `Medium` effort) using the **Human Resources & Talent Management** toolkit:

1. **Weeks 1–2 — Governance & Threshold Design:**
   - Establish minimum-cohort reporting thresholds (e.g., minimum `n` per department/region) and quote-redaction standards.
   - Define works-council consultation gates and Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA) review triggers.
2. **Weeks 3–4 — Taxonomy & Cohort-Safe Synthesis:**
   - Structure an executive theme taxonomy (retention drivers, manager capability, tooling friction, workload, culture).
   - Process de-identified qualitative survey and exit-interview exports through controlled thematic coding.
3. **Weeks 5–6 — Executive Action Pack & Bias Check:**
   - Produce cohort-level theme frequency matrices and severity-weighted executive action briefs.
   - Apply human-in-the-loop verification (pairing with `OP-RSK-002` wherever AI/NLP assists text clustering).
4. **Weeks 7–8 — Listening Cadence Handover:**
   - Deliver reusable governance playbook, anonymization checklist, and quarterly listening review template for HRBP teams.

## 5. Explicit Negative Scope Boundaries & Stage 1b Gate

- **No Individual Employee Surveillance or Scoring:** Output is strictly aggregated at or above minimum-cohort `n` thresholds; zero individual profiling, attrition scoring, or sentiment tracking of named employees.
- **No Unreviewed AI Auto-Decisions:** Any AI-assisted text clustering requires human governance review and cannot trigger automated HR actions (`OP-RSK-002` / EU AI Act workplace high-risk boundary).
- **No Legal Sign-Off:** Client Legal/DPO and Works Council retain formal approval of DPA and employee-notice language.
