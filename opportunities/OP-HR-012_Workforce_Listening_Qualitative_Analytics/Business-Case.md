## 2. Business Need & Cost of Inaction

Organizations invest heavily in employee engagement and exit surveys yet routinely underutilize open-text responses—or worse, allow managers to read raw comments that risk re-identifying team members in small units. Without cohort-governed synthesis, leadership misses early retention and operational friction signals while exposing HR to GDPR and works-council pushback.

- **Cost of Inaction:** Persistent blind spots behind quantitative engagement scores, avoidable regrettable attrition, and non-compliant handling of sensitive workforce feedback.
- **Expected Pre-Pilot Value (Unvalidated Hypothesis):** Faster synthesis of qualitative feedback into prioritized executive action themes with documented minimum-cohort privacy safeguards, enabling credible "you said, we acted" leadership follow-through.

## 4. Commercial Packaging, $0 Working-Capital Model & Compounding Path

- **Packaging:** Fixed-scope 6–8 week advisory sprint producing four core artifacts: (1) Minimum-Cohort & Redaction Standard, (2) Qualitative Theme Taxonomy, (3) Executive Priority Brief, and (4) Quarterly Listening Runbook.
- **Engagement Cadence:** Solo-Director advisory (`6–10 hrs/wk`) structured across HRBP, DPO, and People Analytics stakeholder checkpoints.
- **$0 Working-Capital Model:** Sweat-equity advisory using exports from the client's existing survey/HRIS tools; zero software procurement or subcontractor float required.
- **Compounding Path:** Extends `OP-HR-002` (HR Transformation / analytics dimension) and creates direct pull-through for `OP-RSK-002` (AI bias/fairness review whenever LLMs/NLP read employee text) and `OP-HR-003` (transparency).
