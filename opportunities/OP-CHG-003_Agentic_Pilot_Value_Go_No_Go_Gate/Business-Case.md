## 2. Business Need & Cost of Inaction

Enterprise buyers face an asymmetry in 2027: technology vendors and SIs capture revenue on implementation volume, while sponsors carry full balance-sheet risk when agentic pilots stall (~23% scaling rate vs. 62% experimentation). Without an independent gate:

- **Sunk-Cost Escalation:** Weak pilots consume €50k–€250k+ in internal engineering and external SI fees before missing unit economics or compliance blockers surface.
- **Compliance & Audit Exposure:** Unregistered autonomous workflows bypass EU AI Act risk tiering, audit logging, and human-approval accountability (`OP-RSK-004` / `OP-ITG-013` triggers).
- **Sponsor Credibility Erosion:** Boards increasingly cancel unmeasured AI programmes mid-flight, stalling legitimate automation initiatives alongside failing ones.

*Note: `OP-CHG-003` has no assigned ADPA `.docx` financial model; commercial pricing bands (`€2,000–€5,000`) reflect portfolio benchmark sizing and remain unvalidated pre-pilot anchors.*
