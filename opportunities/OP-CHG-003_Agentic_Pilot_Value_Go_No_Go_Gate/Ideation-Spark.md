## Positioning

> For **COOs, CIOs, and programme sponsors** about to fund **six-figure agentic or SI programmes** while **only ~23% of enterprises scale agents successfully**, **Agentic Pilot Value & Go/No-Go Gate** is a **3-week fixed decision sprint** that sets **KPIs, risk tier, governance minimum, and kill/scale criteria**—so CBa Consult **de-risks transformation spend** without building the agent factory.

## The Problem (Before Solution)

- **Accenture / McKinsey / PwC** sell multi-month reinvention; sponsors lack **go/no-go discipline** before signing.
- Gartner-style **agentic project cancellation** risk when governance lags pilots.
- Internal teams confuse **Copilot licenses** with **production agent governance**.

## What the Client Is Entitled To (Deliverables)

**Duration:** ~3 weeks · **Fee band:** €2,000–€5,000

1. **Value hypothesis** — KPIs tied to P&L or risk reduction (not vague productivity).
2. **Risk tiering** — EU AI Act / internal policy alignment for pilot use case(s).
3. **Go / no-go / scale criteria** — thresholds, owner, decision date.
4. **Governance minimum** — inventory row, logging, human approval path (feeds `OP-RSK-004`, `OP-ITG-013`).
5. **Decision memo** — sponsor sign-off for steering committee or board.

**If GO:** handoff checklist to SI partner, `OP-CHG-001`, or `OP-RSK-004`.  
**If NO-GO:** documented stop to avoid sunk cost.
