## 1. Problem Context & 2027 Market Trigger

Mid-market and enterprise COOs, CIOs, and transformation sponsors face heavy pressure in 2027 to fund six-figure agentic AI and systems-integration (SI) programmes, even though market benchmarks cited in [Ideation-Spark.md](Ideation-Spark.md) show that roughly **62% of organizations experiment with AI agents while only ~23% scale them successfully**. Without structured pre-commit discipline, sponsors absorb preventable sunk costs:

1. **Premature SI & Platform Commitments:** Large consultancies pitch 8–18 week reinvention tracks before value hypotheses, baseline costs, or kill thresholds are agreed.
2. **Copilot-vs-Agent Governance Confusion:** Internal teams equate M365 Copilot seat licenses with production autonomous-agent governance, exposing processes to silent compliance and operational risk.
3. **Missing Go/No-Go Accountability:** Pilot initiatives drift past budget with no pre-committed decision date, named sponsor, or EU AI Act risk tiering.

`OP-CHG-003` solves this upstream problem with a **3-week fixed decision gate** that establishes P&L-linked KPIs, risk tiering, governance minimums, and explicit kill/scale criteria before factory spend begins.

## 2. Solution Architecture & Delivery Workflow

Delivered within `5–8 hrs/wk` (`~15–24` solo hours total) with `$0` software procurement, `OP-CHG-003` runs across three crisp weekly sprints:

| Week | Sprint Focus | Core Activities | Client Entitlement (Deliverable) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Week 1** | **Value Hypothesis & Baseline** | Audit target workflow economics, map baseline cost/cycle time, and isolate P&L or risk-reduction levers rather than generic productivity claims | **Value Hypothesis Brief** (baseline metrics + target KPIs) |
| **Week 2** | **Risk Tiering & Governance Minimum** | Classify pilot use cases against EU AI Act and internal policy thresholds; define minimum inventory row, logging, and human-approval path | **Risk Tier Matrix & Governance Minimum Spec** (feeds `OP-RSK-004` / `OP-ITG-013`) |
| **Week 3** | **Kill/Scale Criteria & Sponsor Gate** | Lock quantitative go/no-go/scale thresholds, decision dates, and accountable owners; conduct steering readout and handoff check | **Executive Go/No-Go Decision Memo** + GO handoff checklist or documented NO-GO stop |
