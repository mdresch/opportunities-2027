## 1. Problem Context & 2027 Market Trigger

Mid-market finance teams routinely face repeat findings in annual statutory audits and accountant's reviews (*Accountantsverslag* / Management Letters). Because findings arrive in static PDFs without structured root-cause links, assigned owners, or evidence trails, the same internal-control, cutoff, payroll-to-GL, and segregation-of-duties (SoD) issues recur every audit cycle—driving mounting auditor overrun fees and rushed, under-evidenced management responses.

- **2027 Regulatory & Audit Catalyst:** Q1–Q2 2027 annual statutory audit season collides with **IFRS 18** (effective January 2027) presentation changes and heightened external auditor scrutiny over IT general controls and revenue/expense cutoff.
- **Independence White Space:** Under PCAOB/AICPA and EU Public Interest Entity (PIE) auditor-independence rules, the signing external auditor cannot design management's remediation architecture or draft management's internal control workflows. That creates conflict-free pre-assurance demand for CBa Consult.

## 2. Solution Architecture & Delivery Workflow

`OP-FIN-012` is a 3–6 week (4–8 hrs/wk) fixed-scope **Audit / Diagnostic & Advisory** engagement built on CBa Consult's Financial Truth and Evidence Governance methodology:

1. **Week 1 — Findings Ingestion & Taxonomy Mapping:** Parse prior-year and current-year Management Letters / *Accountantsverslag* PDFs into a structured Findings & Root-Cause Matrix tagged by severity, financial statement impact, and control domain.
2. **Week 2–3 — Evidence Pack & Root-Cause Verification:** Assemble underlying reconciliations, close checklists, and SoD logs to validate root causes and close documentation gaps before auditor follow-up.
3. **Week 4 — Management Responses & Side-Letter Drafting:** Draft structured, evidence-backed management responses and side-letter schedules (for client legal counsel review) with explicit compensating controls and target closure dates.
4. **Week 5–6 — Closed-Loop Remediation Register & Handoff:** Stand up a living remediation tracker with owner accountability, quarterly check-in gates, and direct escalation paths into continuous close (`OP-FIN-009`), rate-engine governance (`OP-FIN-010`), or IT retention/access (`OP-ITG-010`, `OP-ITG-018`).

## 5. Explicit Negative Scope Boundaries & Stage 1b Gate

- **No Statutory Audit Opinion or Assurance Attestation:** CBa Consult prepares management's reporting, evidence packs, and remediation architecture; the external auditor retains sole statutory opinion authority.
- **No Binding Legal Counsel:** Drafted side-letter schedules address operational/control commitments and must be reviewed and approved by the client's legal counsel before execution.
- **No Tax Return Filing or Statutory Bookkeeping Takeover:** Scope is restricted to findings structuring, evidence compilation, and remediation tracking.
