## Positioning

> For **CFOs, Corporate Controllers, and Audit Committees** at small-and-mid enterprises who struggle with **recurring annual accountant's review (*Accountantsverslag* / Management Letter) findings, rushed side-letter responses, and mounting external auditor overrun fees**, **Annual Accountant's Review Findings Reporting & Side Letters** is a **conflict-free Pre-Assurance & Findings-Remediation Advisory** backed by **CBa Consult's Financial Truth & Evidence Governance methodology** that delivers **structured findings reporting, evidence-backed side letters / management responses, and a closed-loop remediation tracker** with \$0 initial working capital.

## The Problem (Before Solution)

Every annual audit / accountant's review cycle exposes the same recurring operational breakdowns in mid-market finance teams:
- **Unstructured Findings & Repeat Management Letters:** External accountants flag the same internal-control, cutoff, payroll-to-GL, SoD, and documentation findings year after year because findings live in static PDFs with no owner, root-cause link, or evidence trail.
- **Rushed Side Letters & Management Responses:** Controllers scramble under statutory filing deadlines to draft management responses, side letters (disclosures, remediation commitments, carve-outs, subsequent-event notes, and technical accounting explanations), burning weeks of overtime and triggering avoidable external auditor overrun billings.
- **Auditor Independence Vacuum:** Statutory external auditors (Big Four and mid-tier audit firms under PCAOB/AICPA and EU PIE NAS rules) **cannot** design or remediate the client's internal controls or draft management's remediation architecture without violating auditor independence.

## What It Is

A fixed-scope, **100% conflict-free** finance & pre-assurance engagement structured around the annual accountant's review cycle:
1. **Findings Structuring & Executive Reporting:** Translate the external accountant's review findings / Management Letter (*Accountantsverslag*) into a prioritized **Findings & Root-Cause Report** for the CFO, Management Board, and Audit Committee (severity, financial-statement impact, SoD/ITGC linkage, and realistic remediation dates).
2. **Side-Letter & Management Response Package:** Draft clear, evidence-backed **side letters**, management responses, reconciliation memos, and representation support packs so the external accountant receives clean, audit-ready documentation on the first pass.
3. **Closed-Loop Remediation Register:** Track every finding through root-cause fix and verifiable closure evidence so prior-year findings close permanently before next year's interim audit.
