## 2. Business Need & Cost of Inaction

Every Q1–Q2 statutory audit cycle, mid-market finance teams absorb repeat Management Letter (*Accountantsverslag*) findings—unreconciled GL/subledger cutoffs, payroll-to-GL variances, and segregation-of-duties gaps—because prior-year findings remain trapped in static PDFs without root-cause remediation or pre-audit evidence packs. In 2027, **IFRS 18** transition complexity amplifies auditor testing scope and overrun risk.

- **Cost of Inaction:** Escalating out-of-scope external auditor overrun fees, strained Audit Committee governance, and repeat control deficiencies that delay statutory sign-off.
- **Evidence Basis:** *No ADPA `.docx` ROI modeling is assigned to `OP-FIN-012`.* Commercial economics rest strictly on conflict-free pre-assurance demand documented in [Tier-2-Market-Research.md](Tier-2-Market-Research.md) and zero fabricated client savings claims.

## 4. Commercial Packaging, $0 Working-Capital Model & Compounding Path

- **Packaging:** Tiered fixed-scope engagement: **€1,500–€2,500** (single-entity SME Management Letter triage & tracker) up to **€3,500–€5,000** (multi-entity side-letter schedules, evidence packs, and Audit Committee briefing).
- **$0 Working-Capital Execution:** Delivered via Director-led workshops and structured Excel/Markdown/M365 remediation registers using client-held financial records—zero new GRC software required.
- **Compounding Path:** Secures annual recurring Q1–Q2 audit-prep retention while uncovering structural root causes that convert into `OP-FIN-009` (Continuous Close), `OP-FIN-010` (Rate Engine), `OP-ITG-010` (Retention/Recovery), and `OP-ITG-018` (MIP).
