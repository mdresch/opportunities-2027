# Tier-2 Market Research & Competitive Validation — `OP-ITG-018`: Managed Intelligence Provider (MIP)

## 1. Market Demand & The 2027 MSP/MSSP Governance Vacuum

Mid-market and regulated enterprises (50–2,500 seats) already contract an **MSP** for infrastructure uptime and an **MSSP** for 24/7 security monitoring. Between 2026 and 2027, a structural third layer — **Enterprise Intelligence Operations** — has opened with no accountable owner:

1. **Agent Sprawl & FinOps Shock:** Organizations deploying Microsoft 365 Copilot, Copilot Studio, and custom/MCP agents discover uncontrolled agent proliferation without named human sponsors, identity boundaries, or token spend ceilings.
2. **Rapid Context & RAG Decay:** Knowledge bases (SharePoint, Teams, Confluence, ERP extracts) drift within 60–90 days of initial pilots, silently degrading answer accuracy and leaking stale or overshared content.
3. **Live Regulatory Enforcement:** IFRS 18 (Jan 2027), EC ESG Assurance (Jul 2027), EU AI Act Annex III (Dec 2, 2027), ISO/IEC 42001, and DORA/NIS2 require continuous operational evidence rather than point-in-time audit binders.
4. **Executive Talent & Independence Bottleneck:** Hiring a full-time Chief AI Officer + Governance team costs **€250k–€500k+/year**, while Big Four audit firms face strict PCAOB/AICPA and EU PIE non-audit service (NAS) independence restrictions.

## 2. Target Buyer Persona & Conversion Funnel

- **Primary Economic Buyers:** CEO, CIO, CFO, CISO, and Private Equity Operating Partners.
