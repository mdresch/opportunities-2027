## 2. Business Need & Cost of Inaction

Enterprises investing in AI copilots, autonomous workflows, and automated financial/ESG reporting face acute post-deployment governance decay:

- **Cost of Inaction:** Unmanaged agent sprawl drives runaway token bills, stale SharePoint/RAG context corrupts decisions within 90 days, missing EU AI Act Annex III / DORA / IFRS 18 logs create audit exposure, and hiring a full-time CAIO + governance team costs €250k+ annually in fixed payroll.
- **Commercial Value Proposition:** A fractional, conflict-free MIP retainer provides continuous **Intelligence Health & Governance**—spanning Agent FinOps, Context Hygiene, Continuous Compliance Evidence, Sovereign Runtime Boundaries, and Board Intelligence Capital Reporting—at 20–40% of internal executive headcount cost.
- **Model Provenance Notice:** Any internal maturity or efficiency projections referenced from `ADPA Playbook.docx` and `ADPA_Playbook_Lifecycle_Management.docx` constitute *Illustrative Pre-Pilot ADPA Source Modeling — Not Realized Client Actuals*. Commercial ARR and unit-economics viability must be proven through Tier A pilot graduation and `HYP-2027-004` buyer validation.

## 4. Commercial Packaging, $0 Working-Capital Model & Compounding Path

- **3-Tier Retainer Architecture:**
  - **MIP Essentials (€2,500/mo · ~4 hrs/wk):** Monthly Agent Inventory & FinOps ceiling review, RAG/context drift spot-check, and compliance evidence summary.
  - **MIP Core (€5,500/mo · ~7 hrs/wk):** Full 4-week 6-Stage Truth Production cadence, EU AI Act / ISO 42001 evidence pack upkeep, and quarterly executive readout.
  - **MIP Enterprise / Fractional CAIO (€10,000–€12,000+/mo · ~10–12 hrs/wk):** Full 5-pillar stewardship including MXC Hyper-V / cryptographic sovereignty oversight, Continuous Close alignment, and Board Intelligence Capital reporting.
- **Working-Capital Discipline:** `\$0` upfront infrastructure or platform CapEx; CBa Consult runs governance telemetry on client-owned M365/cloud environments using existing CBa frameworks (`ADPA`, `ECS`, `sidpa-template`, `ICT-Governance-Framework-Application`).
- **Compounding Path:** Every Q1–Q2 2027 Tier A pilot (`OP-ITG-013`, `OP-ITG-014`, `OP-ITG-015`, `OP-ITG-017`, `OP-RSK-004`, `OP-FIN-009`, `OP-STR-004`) carries a built-in "Graduate to MIP Retainer" clause, turning initial projects into multi-year ARR and a licensable **MIP Operating Playbook**.
