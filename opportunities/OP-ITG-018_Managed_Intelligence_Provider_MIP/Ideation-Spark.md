## What it is

Just as **MSPs** emerged to manage servers/endpoints and **MSSPs** emerged to manage SOC/security alerts, **2027–2030 creates a third mandatory category: the Managed Intelligence Provider / Professional (MIP)**.

An MIP takes ongoing operational and governance accountability for the five pillars of an enterprise's Intelligence Stack — powered by CBa Consult's **6-Stage Truth Production Governance** (`Observe → Measure → Govern → Trace → Certify → Improve`):

1. **Digital Labor & Agent Fleet Stewardship (`OP-ITG-013`, `OP-ITG-016`, `OP-HR-009`):** Continuous agent inventory, Entra Agent Identity reviews, Agent 365 observability, Agent FinOps token/budget guardrails, and human-escalation governance.
2. **Organizational Knowledge & Context Hygiene (`OP-ITG-014`, `OP-DTX-005`):** Ongoing RAG readiness, taxonomy/ontology stewardship, ADPA/ECS context-layer curation, and Work IQ / Fabric data-quality governance.
3. **Continuous AI Compliance & Evidence Assurance (`OP-RSK-004`, `OP-RSK-005`, `OP-ITG-010`):** Always-on EU AI Act Annex III, ISO 42001, NIST AI RMF, and Split-Clock WORM retention evidence — conflict-free pre-assurance ready for external auditors.
4. **Sovereign Execution & Hardware Containment (`OP-ITG-015`, `OP-ITG-017`):** Ongoing Cryptographic Sovereignty / Trust Topology monitoring and MXC Hyper-V `<500ms` JIT Scale-to-Zero sterile sandbox pool governance.
5. **Financial Truth & Intelligence Capital Reporting (`OP-FIN-009`, `OP-FIN-010`, `OP-FIN-011`):** Continuous Close reconciliation oversight and quarterly Board-level Intelligence Capital ROI reporting (SIDPA).
