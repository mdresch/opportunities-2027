## 2. Business Need & Cost of Inaction

- **Buyer Pain:** Small-business founders adopting generative AI or processing EU customer data cannot justify €2,500–€10,000+ legal retainers just to decode baseline EU AI Act and GDPR obligations.
- **Cost of Inaction:** Without an accessible operational starting point, founders either stall AI adoption out of uncertainty or deploy unvetted tools in violation of transparency and data-protection duties.
- **Pre-Pilot Modeling Note:** *Illustrative Pre-Pilot ADPA Source Modeling — Not Realized Client Actuals:* `OP-LEG-003` is adapted from 2026 `OP-IE-009` with no separate ADPA `.docx` model; no realized unit sales or client ROI figures are claimed pre-launch.

## 3. Strategic Options Analysis (BABOK v3 Aligned)

| Option | Description | Commercial & Risk Profile | Recommendation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Option A — Do Nothing** | Rely solely on bespoke advisory (`OP-LEG-001`/`002`) | Lacks a low-friction, scalable top-of-funnel digital product for Small buyers | Reject |
| **Option B — Interactive SaaS Compliance App** | Build custom web software for AI/GDPR assessments | Breaks \$0 working-capital rule; high engineering and maintenance drag | Reject |
| **Option C — Digital Cheat-Sheet PDF & Starter Pack** | Author modular PDFs (€500–€2,000 band/bundles) backed by Risk Management toolkit | Zero marginal cost per sale, fast Q1–Q2 2027 release, feeds `OP-LEG-001` | **Recommend** |

## 4. Commercial Packaging, $0 Working-Capital Model & Compounding Path

- **Zero Working-Capital Mechanics:** Authored purely via sweat equity (6 weeks, Medium effort) using existing Risk Management templates and standard document publishing tools—€0 inventory or software development cost.
- **Packaging & Distribution:** Direct digital PDF downloads for niche founder communities and bundled "compliance starter packs" paired with `OP-LEG-001` to lift average order value into the €500–€2,000 band.
- **Compounding Path:** Serves as a low-risk lead magnet generating early cash flow, `OP-X-004` proof, and warm upgrades into higher-ticket compliance or AI governance diagnostics.

## 5. Risk Register & Compensating Controls

| Risk ID | Risk Description | Impact / Likelihood | Compensating Control |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **R1** | Buyer treats plain-language PDF as binding legal advice | High / Medium | Place prominent non-legal advisory disclaimers on cover, footer, and checkout terms |
| **R2** | EU AI Act guidance or EDPB opinions shift post-publication | Medium / High | Timestamp every PDF release and commit to a lightweight quarterly changelog update |
| **R3** | Low standalone unit price (€) limits direct revenue impact | Medium / Medium | Prioritize B2B community licensing and mandatory `OP-LEG-001` starter-pack bundling |
