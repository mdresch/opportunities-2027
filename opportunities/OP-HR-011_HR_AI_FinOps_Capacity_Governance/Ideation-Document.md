## 1. Problem Context & 2027 Market Trigger

By 2027, medium and large enterprises have rolled out Microsoft 365 Copilot seats and HR self-service agents across recruiting, onboarding, L&D, and people operations—yet CHROs and CFOs cannot prove which AI workflows justify ongoing token or license spend. License renewals and consumption-based agent billing are climbing while Finance demands the same capital-allocation rigor for AI capacity as for headcount requisitions.

Grounded in Microsoft's *Frontier HR Leader* (`SRGCM17235`) FinOps-for-AI paradigm and CBa Consult's **Human Resources & Talent Management** Signature Toolkit, `OP-HR-011` establishes a joint CHRO + CFO governance model. The 2027 trigger is budget accountability: replacing blanket Copilot seat allocation and untracked HR agent consumption with explicit workflow owners, outcome baselines, risk thresholds, and quarterly scale-or-stop gates.

Three structural shifts make Q2 2027 the optimal entry window:

- **Seat-to-Consumption Shift:** Autonomous HR agents consume variable message/compute packs that break static annual HR SaaS budgeting.
- **CHRO–CFO Alignment Gap:** HR measures adoption qualitatively while CFOs demand hard capacity reallocation and headcount-equivalent business cases.
- **Regulatory Overlap:** Scaling high-volume HR AI workflows without risk tiers creates unmonitored EU AI Act Annex III exposure.

## 2. Solution Architecture & Delivery Workflow

Delivered over 4–6 weeks at 6–8 hours/week, the engagement institutes a repeatable HR AI capacity-governance cadence:

1. **Weeks 1–2 — HR AI Consumption & Workflow Inventory:** Baseline current HR Copilot seats, Copilot Studio / agent message consumption, and shadow-AI usage across core HR processes (talent acquisition, onboarding, policy Q&A, performance cycles), mapping each workflow to a named HR owner.
2. **Weeks 3–4 — Capacity Allocation Worksheet & Outcome Baselines:** Co-design the **HR AI Capacity Allocation Worksheet** (workflow x target workforce outcome x named owner x monthly consumption cap x EU AI Act risk tier) aligned with Finance cost-center rules.
3. **Weeks 5–6 — Scale/Stop Gate Governance & Cross-Lane Alignment:** Establish monthly CHRO-CFO review rituals, automated threshold alerts in existing Microsoft admin telemetry, and clear escalation rules linking HR workflow ROI (`OP-HR-014`) with enterprise platform FinOps (`OP-DTX-005`).

Core tangible deliverables produced across the sprint:

- **HR AI Capacity Allocation Worksheet:** Live governance ledger tying each HR workflow to owner, budget cap, and workforce KPI.
- **CHRO–CFO Scale/Stop Gate Playbook:** Quarterly decision rubric for expanding, redesigning, or retiring underperforming HR agents.
- **Cross-Lane Escalation Matrix:** Clean operational boundary handoffs to `OP-HR-014` (ROI proof) and `OP-DTX-005` (platform FinOps).
