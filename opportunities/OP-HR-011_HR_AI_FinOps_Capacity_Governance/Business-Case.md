## 2. Business Need & Cost of Inaction

Without capacity governance, HR departments either over-provision expensive AI seats with low daily utilization or deploy uncontrolled autonomous agents that consume pooled enterprise message packs without measurable HR SLA improvement. When Finance initiates mid-year cost cuts, unmeasured HR AI initiatives face blunt license clawbacks rather than evidence-based reallocation toward high-ROI workflows.

Installing a lightweight CHRO–CFO allocation rhythm protects high-value HR automation (such as candidate scheduling or policy Q&A) while reclaiming dormant or low-impact capacity before annual enterprise license true-ups lock in wasted spend.

All unit-economic assumptions—redirecting 15–30% of underutilized HR Copilot/agent capacity toward verified workflows within a €2,000–€6,000 advisory sprint—are studio-level pre-pilot estimates requiring Finance-partnered baseline validation before pilot promotion.

## 4. Commercial Packaging, $0 Working-Capital Model & Compounding Path

- **Packaging & Pricing Anchor:** Fixed-scope 4–6 week HR AI FinOps & Capacity Governance Sprint (€2,000–€6,000 band), delivered independently or as the HR governance workstream inside an `OP-DTX-005` / `OP-HR-002` enterprise bundle.
- **Zero Working-Capital Mechanics:** Delivered purely via Director/advisory workshops, Microsoft/HRIS usage exports, and CBa spreadsheet allocation templates—requiring \$0 third-party FinOps SaaS procurement.
- **Unit-Economics Guardrail:** Cap initial scope to the top 5–8 HR AI workflows and one primary M365/HRIS tenant so delivery remains inside 6–8 hrs/week over 4–6 weeks.
- **Compounding Path:** Once capacity caps and workflow baselines are established, clients naturally commission `OP-HR-014` to prove workflow-level ROI, `OP-DTX-005` for enterprise-wide Microsoft IQ/Copilot Studio FinOps, or `OP-RSK-004` where HR AI triggers EU AI Act Annex III obligations.
