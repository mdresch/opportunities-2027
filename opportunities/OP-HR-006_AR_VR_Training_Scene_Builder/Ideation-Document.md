## 1. Problem Context & 2027 Market Trigger

Medium and large enterprises in manufacturing, logistics, healthcare, and field operations face persistent onboarding bottlenecks and high safety-incident exposure when training staff on complex equipment or hazardous workflows. Custom AR/VR training simulations typically require €50,000+ in bespoke 3D engineering, specialized Unity/Unreal contractors, and 4–9 month production cycles, pricing out internal L&D teams and mid-market training providers.

In 2027, CHROs and HR Transformation Leads face stricter operational resilience mandates and tighter L&D budgets, creating demand for modular, reusable scenario templates rather than one-off spatial-computing builds. A no-code/low-code AR/VR Training Scene Builder toolkit bridges the gap between static PDFs/LMS slide decks and prohibitively expensive custom VR studios.

## 2. Solution Architecture & Delivery Workflow

Backed by CBa Consult's **Digital Transformation** Signature Toolkit and the ADPA source blueprint (`1 AR VR Training Scene Builder.docx`), `OP-HR-006` delivers a standardized library of customizable spatial training scene templates and an instructional design playbook.

1. **Modular Scene Template Library:** Pre-structured scenario blueprints for three high-value enterprise domains: facility/equipment safety walkthroughs, standard operating procedure (SOP) hazard spotting, and structured onboarding orientation.
2. **Instructional Design & Assessment Framework:** JSON/YAML or drag-and-drop scene configuration guide covering learning objectives, branching decision checkpoints, scoring rubrics, and LMS/xAPI completion export mapping.
3. **Cross-Platform Authoring Blueprint:** Reference workflow targeting browser-accessible WebXR and mainstream standalone headsets (e.g., Meta Quest / standard tablet AR) to minimize proprietary SDK lock-in.
4. **Delivery Cadence:** 8–12 week build of the core template pack and pilot scene blueprint, sold first as a Digital Product (PDF / Toolkit + starter scene assets) with optional advisory sprints for enterprise L&D customization.

## 5. Explicit Negative Scope Boundaries & Stage 1b Gate

To avoid capital-intensive custom game-studio engineering, `OP-HR-006` enforces strict boundaries:
- **No custom 3D asset modeling or proprietary headset hardware procurement:** CBa Consult provides scene templates, instructional blueprints, and configuration playbooks—not bespoke photogrammetry or hardware leasing.
- **No full-time software engineering headcount before validation:** Initial toolkit builds rely on WebXR/open-standard template structures and sweat equity only.
- **No regulated medical or aviation certification claims:** Templates support corporate onboarding and general ops/safety awareness, subject to client EHS sign-off.

**Stage 1b Gate Status:** Ideation framing complete; due to High effort (8–12 wks) and hardware/platform fragmentation risk, requires Stage 2 buyer discovery (3–5 L&D / EHS interviews) before committing build capacity.
