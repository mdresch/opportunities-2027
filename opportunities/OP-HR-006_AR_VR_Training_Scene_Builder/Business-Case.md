## 2. Business Need & Cost of Inaction

Medium and large enterprises face rising onboarding costs and workplace safety compliance pressure, yet bespoke AR/VR training studios quote €40,000–€150,000+ per custom scenario with multi-month delivery lags. Training providers and internal L&D teams want reusable, configurable scene blueprints that cut authoring cost and technical barriers.

The ADPA source (`1 AR VR Training Scene Builder.docx`) outlines qualitative adoption and cost-reduction drivers for reusable AR/VR training templates across healthcare, manufacturing, and corporate training (*Illustrative Pre-Pilot ADPA Source Modeling — Not Realized Client Actuals*). No verified financial ROI or client pilot metrics exist yet. Given the High effort (8–12 weeks) required to build credible spatial templates, jumping straight into full production without a paying anchor buyer carries significant opportunity cost against faster Q1–Q2 Tier A/B cash wedges.

## 4. Commercial Packaging, $0 Working-Capital Model & Compounding Path

- **Starter Offer (€2,000–€4,000 Toolkit & Pilot Blueprint):** Industry-specific AR/VR training scene blueprint pack (onboarding + safety/ops SOP scenarios), instructional design rubric, and 2-hour L&D enablement workshop.
- **Enterprise Licensing (€4,000–€8,000/yr):** Multi-scene industry library license with annual template updates and LMS/xAPI integration guidance.
- **$0 Working-Capital Discipline:** Zero headset inventory, zero paid 3D engine licenses, and zero contractor spend; pilot scenes built strictly on open WebXR / free-tier authoring stacks only after LOI or deposit commitment.
- **Compounding Path:** Anchor deployments generate `OP-X-004` proof assets and cross-sell into broader Digital Transformation and Human + Agent Workforce (`OP-HR-009`) programs.
