## 2. Business Need & Cost of Inaction

Small founders and solo operators lack affordable, structured operating playbooks, while CBa Consult holds substantial practitioner IP that currently earns revenue only when delivered synchronously via billable consulting hours. Relying purely on 1:1 consulting caps capacity and slows early 2027 cash-flow diversification.

The source ADPA digest (`AI-Powered Digital Products.docx`) frames the opportunity with the following *Illustrative Pre-Pilot ADPA Source Modeling — Not Realized Client Actuals*:

- **Macro Digital Products Market Signal:** Projects the broader digital products market reaching $146 billion by 2027 (12.3% CAGR). *(Illustrative Pre-Pilot ADPA Source Modeling — Not Realized Client Actuals.)*
- **Source Operational Targets:** Models a 70% reduction in manual asset design time via AI-assisted workflows, launching 10+ products in 12 months, targeting a 20% net profit margin within 18 months via Gumroad/Etsy/Notion distribution, and scaling toward 10,000+ users within 24 months. *(Illustrative Pre-Pilot ADPA Source Modeling — Not Realized Client Actuals — treated strictly as directional aspiration, not a committed CBa 2027 forecast.)*

## 4. Commercial Packaging, $0 Working-Capital Model & Compounding Path

- **Zero-Inventory Sweat-Equity Model (€500–€2,500 Initial Target):** Assets are authored using existing CBa Consult AI tools and sold via zero-upfront-fee marketplaces (e.g., Gumroad, Notion template gallery) where platform fees apply only upon sale.
- **Compounding Path:** Serves as the entry product engine feeding `OP-X-008` (Digital Knowledge Merchant) and generating inbound discovery leads for higher-ticket advisory sprints (`OP-MKT-001`, `OP-SLS-001`, `OP-X-001`) plus Case Study proof (`OP-X-004`).
