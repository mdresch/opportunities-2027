## 2. Business Need & Cost of Inaction

- **Buyer Pain:** Sales commitments made in CRM/CPQ frequently diverge from what Legal approves, Fulfillment can deliver, and Finance can cleanly invoice or recognize.
- **Cost of Inaction:** Broken Quote-to-Cash handoffs cause delayed billing, unbilled scope creep, credit-note rework, elevated Days Sales Outstanding (DSO), and recurring friction between Revenue and Finance leadership.
- **Pre-Pilot Modeling Note:** *Illustrative Pre-Pilot ADPA Source Modeling — Not Realized Client Actuals:* `OP-OPS-002` is Spark-derived (no assigned ADPA `.docx`); no realized client DSO or margin recovery figures are asserted prior to paid pilot delivery.

## 4. Commercial Packaging, $0 Working-Capital Model & Compounding Path

- **Zero Working-Capital Mechanics:** Executed via principal Lean 6 Sigma process mapping and client-provided CRM/ERP exports—requiring €0 software procurement or external staffing.
- **Engagement Tiers:** Core Q2C Handoff Diagnostic (€3,000–€5,500, 8 weeks) and Full Cross-Functional Control & SLA Redesign (€6,500–€10,000, 12 weeks), billed on milestone deliverables.
- **Compounding Path:** Generates quantifiable before/after handoff-cycle proof (`OP-X-004`) and feeds enterprise case studies directly into `OP-OPS-003` and Tier A/B finance-operations retainers.
