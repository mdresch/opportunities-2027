## 1. Problem Context & 2027 Market Trigger

Small and mid-market enterprises rapidly adopting SaaS automation and generative AI tools manage operational, third-party vendor, and AI risks through disconnected spreadsheets or reactive incident threads. Without a unified risk register, scoring matrix, and escalation cadence, leadership faces delayed risk detection, inconsistent issue triage, and growing exposure under 2027 compliance regimes.

- **Target Buyers:** CROs, CISOs, Internal Audit Leads, and Compliance Committees in the Small & Mid segment.
- **2027 Market Trigger:** Approaching EU AI Act obligations, NIS2/DORA vendor-risk accountability, ISO/IEC 23894 and NIST AI RMF alignment pressure, and demand for lightweight, ready-to-use governance kits rather than costly enterprise GRC software rollouts.
- **Core Pain (The Wedge):** Organizations lack a practical, unified register and quarterly review rhythm that covers operational, vendor, and AI risks in one place.

## 2. Solution Architecture & Delivery Workflow

Built on CBa Consult's **Risk Management** Signature Toolkit and extensive ADPA AI-Driven Risk Management templates, `OP-RSK-001` delivers a turnkey starter kit over a **4-week** low-effort sprint:

1. **Phase 1 — Risk Taxonomy & Baseline Intake (Week 1):** Inventory the client's top operational, third-party AI/SaaS vendor, and model-usage exposures; calibrate likelihood/impact scales and risk appetite thresholds.
2. **Phase 2 — Unified Register & Scoring Matrix Setup (Week 2):** Deploy a ready-to-use Excel/Notion/M365 risk & model register aligned with ISO/IEC 23894, NIST AI RMF, and EU AI Act risk tiers, including automated triage and escalation rules.
3. **Phase 3 — Mitigation Playbooks & Owner Assignment (Week 3):** Map top 10–15 active risks to named internal owners, early-warning triggers, and actionable resolution guidance.
4. **Phase 4 — Quarterly Governance Cadence Handover (Week 4):** Facilitate the inaugural risk-committee review session and hand over the quarterly maintenance playbook.

### Core Deliverables & Governance Artifacts

- **Operational & AI Risk Register:** Pre-built spreadsheet/Notion workbook integrating operational, vendor, and AI model risks.
- **Scoring Matrix & Escalation Thresholds:** Calibrated risk appetite bands mapped to ISO/IEC 23894, NIST AI RMF, and EU AI Act categories.
- **Quarterly Review Runbook:** Standing agenda, owner update protocol, and board summary template.

## 5. Explicit Negative Scope Boundaries & Stage 1b Gate

To protect margin at a €500–€2,000 entry price and respect `$0` working-capital limits, `OP-RSK-001` enforces strict boundaries:

- **Out of Scope:** Legal conformity determinations under the EU AI Act, statutory internal/external audit attestation, continuous 24/7 risk-monitoring SaaS engineering, and ongoing fractional CRO staffing.
- **Client Prerequisites:** Named internal risk owner committed to running the post-handover quarterly review cadence and timely access to existing vendor/AI tool lists.
- **Accountable Ownership:** CBa Consult configures the unified register, scoring matrix, and inaugural review cadence; the client risk owner maintains ongoing updates and escalation actions.
