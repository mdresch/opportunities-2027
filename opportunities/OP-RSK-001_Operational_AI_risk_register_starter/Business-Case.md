2. Problem Statement

2.1 Current State and Enterprise Limitations

ADPA’s current risk and issue management processes are highly manual, siloed, and reactive, leading to significant inefficiencies and operational risks. The current state is characterized by the following limitations:

Delayed Risk Identification:

Risks are typically identified after they have materialized, rather than proactively predicted. This delay results in last-minute firefighting, increased project disruptions, and higher mitigation costs.

Manual risk assessments rely on static spreadsheets and periodic reviews, which fail to capture real-time changes in project parameters (e.g., schedule slippage, resource bottlenecks, budget overruns).

Inefficient Issue Triage and Escalation:

Issues are often manually logged and triaged, leading to inconsistent prioritization and delayed escalation. This inefficiency results in prolonged resolution times and increased project disruptions.

Escalation paths are static and rigid, failing to account for dynamic project conditions (e.g., stakeholder availability, project phase, issue context). This rigidity leads to misrouted issues and further delays.

Reactive Resolution Strategies:

Resolution strategies are reactive rather than proactive, with project teams often scrambling to address issues after they have already impacted project timelines, budgets, or quality.

Lack of root cause analysis (RCA) automation means that recurring issues are not systematically addressed, leading to repeated disruptions and inefficiencies.

Siloed Systems and Data:

Risk and issue data are stored across disparate systems (e.g., project management tools, spreadsheets, email), leading to inconsistencies, duplication, and lack of visibility.

The absence of integrated dashboards hinders ADPA’s ability to monitor project health in real time and make data-driven decisions.

Compliance and Governance Risks:

Manual processes lack the transparency and auditability required by global governance frameworks, such as ISO/IEC 23894, NIST AI RMF, and the EU AI Act. This exposes ADPA to compliance risks, fines, and reputational damage.

The lack of explainable AI (XAI) in current processes makes it difficult for stakeholders to understand how decisions are made, eroding trust and adoption.

Resource Constraints:

Manual risk and issue management processes require significant time and effort from project teams, risk management teams, and SMEs. This diverts resources from value-added activities, such as strategic planning and innovation.

The lack of automation means that ADPA is unable to scale its risk and issue management capabilities to support its growing portfolio of projects.
