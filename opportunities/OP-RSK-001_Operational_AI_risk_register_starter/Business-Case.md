## 2. Business Need & Cost of Inaction

Small & Mid organizations deploying AI tools without structured risk visibility accumulate silent operational and regulatory exposure:

- **Cost of Inaction:** Unlogged shadow-AI and third-party vendor exposures, reactive crisis firefighting when incidents occur, and audit readiness gaps ahead of 2027 EU AI Act / NIS2 deadlines.
- **Commercial Value Proposition:** A low-friction, 4-week starter kit establishes a living risk register, scoring matrix, and quarterly governance cadence with zero enterprise GRC license spend.
- **Model Provenance Notice:** Financial figures in `ADPA AI Driven Risk Management Resolutions 2026.docx` and `Business_Case_Template_3b733b80.docx` (such as an estimated \$2.1M annual cost of inaction, \$840k annual savings from 40% disruption reduction, and 15–20% cost savings) represent *Illustrative Pre-Pilot ADPA Source Modeling — Not Realized Client Actuals* derived for enterprise-scale ADPA scenarios, not verified Small/Mid pilot outcomes.

## 4. Commercial Packaging, $0 Working-Capital Model & Compounding Path

- **Package Structure:** Fixed-fee starter kit (€500–€2,000 standalone; preferred €3,500–€6,000 when bundled with `OP-LEG-001` and `OP-ITG-001` as a Governance Starter Suite) delivered on client-owned Excel, SharePoint, or Notion workspaces.
- **Working-Capital Discipline:** `\$0` software, hosting, or contractor outlay; powered entirely by CBa Consult's pre-built ADPA Risk Register, Model Register, and scoring matrix IP.
- **Compounding & Upsell Path:**
  1. **Governance Suite Bundle:** Package `OP-RSK-001` + `OP-LEG-001` + `OP-ITG-001` to raise deal size without proportional sales effort.
  2. **Tier A Escalation:** High-risk AI systems identified inside the starter register immediately qualify the client for Tier A EU AI Act / ISO 42001 compliance programs (`OP-RSK-004`) and ongoing MIP retainers (`OP-ITG-018`).
