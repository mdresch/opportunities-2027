## 2. Business Need & Cost of Inaction

Multi-cloud enterprises lose speed and audit readiness when governance stays trapped in manual quarterly reviews:

- **Cost of Inaction:** 90+ day lag between cloud configuration drift and detection, 30%+ control gaps surfacing during annual audits, and 20–40% engineering productivity drag caused by ad-hoc compliance reconciliation.
- **Commercial Value Proposition:** Combining CBa Consult's existing CI-passing `ICT-Governance-Framework-Application` with COBIT 2019 / ITIL 4 / ISO 38500 / NIST CSF 2.0 rubrics delivers board-ready visibility and concrete remediation priorities at a fraction of Big Four fees.
- **Model Provenance Notice:** Quantitative target tables inside `Business_Case_32495a5f.docx` (such as <=500ms compliance latency, 100% continuous coverage, <5% engineering overhead, and 95%+ control effectiveness) represent *Illustrative Pre-Pilot ADPA Source Modeling — Not Realized Client Actuals* and cannot be cited as verified client benchmarks prior to an external pilot.
