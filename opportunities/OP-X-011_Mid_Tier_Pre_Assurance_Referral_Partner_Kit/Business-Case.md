## 2. Business Need & Cost of Inaction

For a solo Director operating at `10–15 hrs/wk`, cold direct sales of €3k–€8k pre-assurance wedges (`OP-RSK-004`, `OP-FIN-012`, `OP-SUS-001`, `OP-RSK-006`, `OP-RSK-008`) carries high customer acquisition cost. Meanwhile, mid-tier accounting and advisory networks already hold trusted relationships with hundreds of mid-market CFOs and boards—yet lack conflict-free, fixed-scope upstream prep capacity.

- **Cost of Inaction:** Over-reliance on slow direct outbound BD once core prep templates are proven, leaving high-intent mid-tier channel referrals uncaptured.
- **Expected Pre-Pilot Value (Unvalidated Hypothesis):** Turns mid-tier networks from perceived competitors into recurring distribution partners while reinforcing CBa's market position as an independent pre-assurance specialist.

## 4. Commercial Packaging, $0 Working-Capital Model & Compounding Path

- **Packaging:** One-time partner enablement kit (5 SKU scope cards, independence/disclaimer pack, intake/exit handoff checklists, optional co-branded one-pagers) paired with bilateral referral fee or white-label margin SOWs.
- **Delivery Integrity:** End clients receive standard CBa deliverables under partner-introduced SOWs ("PwC-ready binder", "assurance-ready ESG pack") without bespoke rework.
- **$0 Working-Capital Model:** Kit is assembled from already-proven CBa templates (`OP-X-005` + Tier A/B pre-assurance SKUs) with zero software spend, zero partner exclusivity retainers, and zero bench hiring.
- **Compounding Path:** Extends `OP-X-005` white-label operations and accelerates recurring deal flow across `OP-RSK-004`, `OP-FIN-012`, `OP-SUS-001`, `OP-RSK-006`, and `OP-RSK-008`.
