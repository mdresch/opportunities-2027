# Ideation Document — `OP-X-011`: Mid-Tier Pre-Assurance Referral & White-Label Partner Kit

> **Governance SSOT:** Governed by [Tailored-Hybrid-SIDPA-Governance.md](../../docs/governance/Tailored-Hybrid-SIDPA-Governance.md), tracked in [inventory-of-ideas.md](../../inventory-of-ideas.md), and gated behind **3 proven pre-assurance client cases** alongside [Tier_A_Executive_Summary_2027.md](../../Tier_A_Executive_Summary_2027.md).

## 1. Problem Context & 2027 Market Trigger

Benelux mid-tier accounting, audit, and advisory networks (e.g., BDO, Baker Tilly, Crowe, Grant Thornton local practices) are scaling fractional CFO, EU AI Act governance, and ESG assurance practices faster than Big Four teams. However, these networks face two structural bottlenecks: **auditor independence conflicts** prevent them from building pre-assurance readiness packs for audit clients, and their partner rate cards make €3,000–€8,000 fixed-scope preparation wedges uneconomic to staff internally.

- **Target Buyer / Partner:** Practice Leads and Advisory/Assurance Partners at Benelux mid-tier accounting & advisory networks.
- **The Wedge (Pain):** Partners need predictable, conflict-free upstream routing so clients arrive "assurance-ready" without forcing the partner firm to build an unprofitable prep desk.
- **2027 Trigger:** **EU AI Act, CSRD/VSME, DORA/NIS2, and IFRS 18** deadlines create surging demand for upstream readiness binders that mid-tier firms want to route cleanly while retaining the downstream audit/advisory relationship.

## 2. Solution Architecture & Delivery Workflow

Built once in 4–6 weeks (`Medium` effort) as a channel multiplier extending `OP-X-005`, activated only after **3 direct CBa client proofs**:

1. **Scope Cards & SKU Packaging:**
   - Standardize partner-facing fixed-scope cards, € bands, and exclusions for `OP-RSK-004` (*Assurance for AI Bridge*), `OP-FIN-012`, `OP-SUS-001` (*VS Reporting Starter*), `OP-RSK-006`, and `OP-RSK-008` (*AFM AI & DORA Prep Sprint*).
2. **Independence & Disclaimer Language:**
   - Codify clear pre-assurance vs. statutory-assurance boundaries so the referring audit/advisory partner maintains regulatory independence.
3. **Intake & Exit Handoff Checklists:**
   - Define standardized intake prerequisites and exit evidence packages ("PwC-ready binder", "assurance-ready ESG pack") for seamless handback to the partner.
4. **Referral Economics & Co-Branded One-Pagers:**
   - Establish confidential per-partner referral fee or white-label margin terms plus optional co-branded one-pagers aligned with `OP-X-005`.

## 5. Explicit Negative Scope Boundaries & Stage 1b Gate

- **No Audit Competition or Statutory Sign-Off:** CBa delivers upstream pre-assurance readiness and never competes on statutory financial audit or formal ESG/AI assurance sign-off.
- **No Network Exclusivity or Employment:** Partner MOUs are non-exclusive; CBa remains an independent specialist firm and never joins a partner's payroll or network membership.
- **Confidential Fee Card:** Partner referral/margin schedules are strictly bilateral and never published publicly.
