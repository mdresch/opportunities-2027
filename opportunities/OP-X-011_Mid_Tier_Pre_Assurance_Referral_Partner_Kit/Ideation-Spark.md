## Positioning

> For **Benelux mid-tier accounting and advisory networks** that scale **fractional CFO, AI governance, and ESG assurance** but need **independent, fixed-scope prep** their own teams cannot deliver without **independence conflicts**, **Mid-Tier Pre-Assurance Referral & White-Label Partner Kit** is a **partner enablement package** (scope cards, handoffs, economics) so **end clients receive standard CBa deliverables** while partners retain audit/advisory relationships.

## The Problem (Before Solution)

- Networks grow faster than Big Four but **lack cheap prep capacity** for €3k–€8k wedges.
- Partners need **predictable routing** for `OP-RSK-004`, `OP-FIN-012`, `OP-SUS-001`, `OP-RSK-006`.
- CBa should **multiply reach** without becoming a network member or hiring.

## What the Partner Is Entitled To (Deliverables)

**Commercial model:** one-time kit build + per-referral intro fee or margin (negotiated).

1. **Scope cards** — fixed offerings with boundaries and € bands (`OP-RSK-004`, `OP-FIN-012`, `OP-SUS-001`, `OP-RSK-006`, `OP-RSK-008`).
2. **Independence & disclaimer language** — who prepares what before partner assurance/advisory line.
3. **Handoff checklist** — intake, evidence deliverables, exit criteria (“PwC-ready binder”, “assurance-ready ESG pack”).
4. **Referral economics template** — intro fee / white-label margin per engagement type.
5. **Optional co-branded one-pagers** — aligned with `OP-X-005` white-label operating model.

**End clients** receive normal CBa work products under partner-introduced SOW.

## What CBa Consult Does — and Does Not

| CBa does | CBa does not |
| --- | --- |
| Enable partners to sell prep **without** building duplicate practice | Compete on full assurance or audit |
| Deliver standardised pre-assurance engagements | Guarantee partner network exclusivity |
| Maintain IP in templates and scope discipline | Become employee of partner firm |

## Why CBa Consult Should Offer This

- **Multiplier** within **10–15 hrs/wk** when prep products are standardised.
- Mid-tiers are **distribution**, not competitors on wedges (per market analysis).
- Reinforces brand: **CBa = pre-assurance specialist**.
- Gated until **3 reference cases** prove repeatability.
