## 2. Business Need & Cost of Inaction

From a portfolio capital-allocation standpoint, there is no business need for CBa Consult to enter consumer print-on-demand apparel or home decor in 2027; the cost of inaction is effectively zero, whereas the opportunity cost of diverting finite senior consulting hours away from €4,000–€15,000+ Tier A/B engagements is substantial. The legacy ADPA `Print-on-Demand (POD).docx` digest cites illustrative targets of 50+ designs, a "15% conversion rate on Redbubble listings within the first six months," and "$5,000 in passive income within the first 12 months" (*Illustrative Pre-Pilot ADPA Source Modeling — Not Realized Client Actuals*). Given intense marketplace saturation and low unit royalties, those legacy figures are non-credible for 2027 resource allocation.
