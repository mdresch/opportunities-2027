## 1. Problem Context & 2027 Market Trigger

The legacy ADPA `Print-on-Demand (POD).docx` artifact outlines an AI-generated merchandise store (apparel, accessories, home decor) listed on Redbubble with zero physical inventory. While POD platforms eliminate warehousing and fulfillment logistics, the 2027 e-commerce landscape for generic AI-generated graphics is heavily saturated, characterized by thin platform royalties (typically 10%–20%), algorithm dependency, trademark/IP scrutiny, and negligible synergy with CBa Consult's B2B advisory, governance, and transformation positioning. Accordingly, `OP-ING-006` is documented strictly for portfolio completeness and archive hygiene rather than active 2027 commercialization.

## 2. Solution Architecture & Delivery Workflow

If ever reactivated as a low-effort digital asset experiment or client workshop illustration, the workflow consists of four lean steps:

1. **Micro-Niche & Keyword Research:** Identify underserved enthusiast or professional niches with clear visual identity and low trademark collision risk.
2. **AI-Assisted Asset Generation & Curation:** Produce vector/high-DPI graphic sets with manual typography and print-bleed quality checks rather than raw unedited outputs.
3. **Zero-Inventory Marketplace Listing:** Publish SKUs to POD fulfillment platforms (Redbubble, Printful/Shopify) with standardized metadata, mockups, and margin floors.
4. **Conversion & Pruning Cadence:** Track organic impressions and unit margin over 4–6 weeks, archiving non-performing listings to avoid catalog bloat.
