## 2. Business Need & Cost of Inaction

- **Buyer Pain:** Medium and large organizations often lack visibility into single-point-of-failure (SPOF) suppliers, outdated continuity SLAs, and fragile lead-time buffers across critical inputs.
- **Cost of Inaction:** An unmapped tier-1 or tier-2 supplier failure forces emergency spot-purchasing at punitive margins, stalls production or service SLAs, and creates third-party resilience audit findings.
- **Pre-Pilot Modeling Note:** *Illustrative Pre-Pilot ADPA Source Modeling — Not Realized Client Actuals:* `OP-SCM-001` expands inventory coverage to Supply Chain based on portfolio design (no ADPA `.docx` assigned); no realized client cost-avoidance actuals are claimed pre-pilot.
