# Ideation Document — `OP-SCM-001`: Vendor & Supply-Chain Continuity Quick-Audit

## 1. Problem Context & 2027 Market Trigger

- **Target Buyer:** COOs, Chief Procurement Officers (CPOs), and Supply Chain / Procurement Directors in Medium & Large organizations.
- **2027 Market Trigger:** Geopolitical logistics volatility, third-party operational resilience rules (NIS2/DORA supplier-risk expectations), CSRD Scope 3 supply-chain transparency demands, and 2027 working-capital discipline expose hidden single-point-of-failure (SPOF) dependencies.
- **Core Wedge:** Mid-to-large enterprises often hold hundreds of vendor contracts with unmapped tier-1/tier-2 concentration, stale SLAs, and undocumented lead-time failure modes. `OP-SCM-001` delivers a fast, fixed-scope continuity quick-audit before a disruption forces emergency spot-buying.

## 2. Solution Architecture & Delivery Workflow

Built on CBa Consult's **Supply Chain Strategy** Signature Toolkit, the 6–8 week (Medium effort) diagnostic follows four steps:

1. **Weeks 1–2 — Critical Vendor & Spend Tiering:** Ingest procurement master lists, spend concentration, and operational dependency mappings to isolate critical tier-1 and tier-2 suppliers.
2. **Weeks 3–4 — Single-Point-of-Failure & SLA Gap Review:** Audit supplier SLAs, geographic/logistics concentration, lead-time variance, and contractual continuity/exit clauses.
3. **Weeks 5–6 — Risk Scoring & Mitigation Checklist:** Score continuity exposure (probability x operational impact) and build a prioritized mitigation checklist (dual-sourcing options, buffer policies, SLA amendments).
4. **Weeks 7–8 — Executive Handover & Cross-Portfolio Pull:** Deliver board-ready continuity register and seamlessly pair remediation actions with `OP-RSK-001` (Risk Management) and `OP-LEG-002` (Contract Lifecycle).

## 5. Explicit Negative Scope Boundaries & Stage 1b Gate

- **In Scope:** Critical supplier mapping, SPOF identification, SLA continuity gap review, lead-time risk scoring, and prioritized mitigation checklist.
- **Explicit Negative Scope (Out of Scope):** CBa Consult does **not** act as a commercial procurement broker, negotiate binding supplier pricing on the client's behalf, manage physical warehouse/freight logistics, or execute third-party financial solvency audits.
- **Stage 1b Gate Criteria:** Standardize procurement data-ingestion requirements (to mitigate vendor-data access delays) and finalize the joint cross-sell bundle with `OP-RSK-001` and `OP-LEG-002`.
