## 1. Problem Context & 2027 Market Trigger

Growing small businesses and founder-led B2B teams frequently run sales out of spreadsheets, personal calendars, and ad-hoc inbox threads—or purchase a CRM that quickly turns into "shelfware" because pipeline stages and qualification rules were never defined. Without stage discipline, revenue forecasting is guesswork and qualified opportunities stall silently.

The 2027 market trigger is disciplined, capital-light revenue execution:

- **Avoid Full-Time RevOps Overhead:** Small teams need reliable pipeline visibility and deal hygiene without hiring a full-time RevOps specialist or paying for overbuilt enterprise CRM implementations.
- **Fixed-Scope Cash-Flow & Proof Wedge:** Buyers demand pragmatic operational improvements that show cleaner pipeline conversion and faster follow-up within weeks rather than multi-quarter CRM re-platforming.

CBa Consult targets Founders, CROs, and Sales Leads in the Small tier with a fixed-scope **Advisory / Starter Kit** that pairs practical sales-process design with a lean CRM rollout.

## 2. Solution Architecture & Delivery Workflow

Delivered over 4–6 weeks (4–6 hrs/week) using CBa Consult's *Sales, Marketing & Communication Strategy* toolkit, the engagement follows a four-step implementation workflow:

1. **Week 1 — Sales Motion & Qualification Design:** Map the client's actual buyer journey and define 5–7 crisp pipeline stages with explicit exit criteria and simple qualification rules (e.g., BANT/MEDDIC-lite).
2. **Week 2–3 — Lean CRM Configuration:** Configure deal pipelines, mandatory stage fields, email/calendar sync, task reminders, and clean contact/company properties sized for reps to update in under 2 minutes per call.
3. **Week 4 — Forecasting & Activity Dashboards:** Build an executive pipeline view showing stage conversion, deal aging, weighted forecast, and weekly rep follow-up hygiene.
4. **Week 5–6 — Team Enablement & Handoff Playbook:** Run live rep coaching, document a concise CRM operating guide, and prepare optional handoff alignment into operations (`OP-OPS-001`).

## 5. Explicit Negative Scope Boundaries & Stage 1b Gate

To ensure rapid adoption and protect fixed-scope delivery economics, the following boundaries are enforced:

- **No Enterprise ERP/Billing Integrations:** `OP-SLS-001` covers core CRM pipeline, email/calendar sync, and reporting; complex CPQ, custom API middleware, or ERP invoicing integrations are excluded.
- **No Outsourced SDR / Lead Generation:** CBa Consult establishes the sales operating system and qualification framework; the client team executes outbound prospecting and closing.
- **No Custom Software Development:** Configuration uses native CRM features on client-owned subscriptions.
- **Stage 1b Gate Status:** Stage 1b capability framing is complete. Progression requires Director adjudication on the Stage 3 Business Case and optional bundling with `OP-OPS-001` / `OP-MKT-001`.
