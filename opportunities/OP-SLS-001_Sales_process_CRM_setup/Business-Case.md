## 2. Business Need & Cost of Inaction

Small B2B sales teams operating without clear pipeline stages or a right-sized CRM lose revenue silently through inconsistent qualification, forgotten follow-ups, and unreliable revenue forecasts. The cost of inaction includes:

- **Avoidable Pipeline Leakage:** Deals stall across inboxes and spreadsheets because exit criteria and next-action owners are never enforced.
- **Wasted CRM Subscriptions ("Shelfware"):** Teams pay for CRM seats that reps abandon within 60 days due to cluttered, over-engineered forms.
- **Premature RevOps Overhead:** Founders hesitate to fix sales hygiene because hiring a full-time RevOps specialist or enterprise SI is unaffordable in 2027.

## 4. Commercial Packaging, $0 Working-Capital Model & Compounding Path

- **Fixed-Scope Engagement (€500–€2,500):** Delivered over 4–6 weeks (4–6 hrs/week) on milestone/upfront billing, ensuring $0 working capital. Client holds all CRM licenses directly.
- **Compounding Path:** Pairs directly with `OP-OPS-001` (sales-to-operations handoff map) and `OP-MKT-001` (marketing-ops quick-start) as a modular Small-tier RevOps starter bundle, while generating early cash flow and Case Study proof (`OP-X-004`).

## 5. Risk Register & Compensating Controls

| Risk | Likelihood / Impact | Compensating Control |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **CRM adoption drop-off after handover** | High / High | Restrict pipeline to 5–7 stages, enforce <2-minute deal update hygiene, and run mandatory Week 5–6 rep coaching. |
| **Scope creep into custom API/ERP integrations** | Medium / Medium | Lock SOW to native CRM features and standard email/calendar sync; quote custom integrations separately. |
| **Messy legacy contact spreadsheets** | Medium / Medium | Cap data import to clean active accounts/open deals; archive stale leads outside core migration. |
| **Lower margin if client lacks decision speed** | Low / Medium | Gate Week 2 CRM build on client sign-off of Week 1 stage/qualification blueprint. |
