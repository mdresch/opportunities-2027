## 2. Business Need & Cost of Inaction

Mid-market and large enterprises without a unified ESG governance framework face escalating financial, commercial, and reputational costs:

- **Financing & Diligence Friction:** Fragmented E/S/G spreadsheets stall institutional investor, lender, and M&A diligence reviews.
- **Supply-Chain Questionnaire Losses:** Unstandardized metrics delay responses to Tier-1 enterprise procurement mandates.
- **Big Four Fee Fatigue:** Buyers need a conflict-free 8–12 week rollout establishing measurable governance clarity at a fraction of legacy consultancy rates.

## 4. Commercial Packaging, $0 Working-Capital Model & Compounding Path

`OP-SUS-002` preserves **\$0 initial working capital** by packaging existing CBa *Management Consulting* governance templates, RACI matrices, and materiality scorecards inside the client's existing ERP/BI/reporting infrastructure:

- **Milestone-Gated Fixed Fees:** Given the 8–12 week High-effort scope, commercial delivery enforces executive sign-off gates at Weeks 2, 5, and 9.
- **Zero Carbon-SaaS Dependency:** Runs on client-owned systems without software reselling or custom engineering.
- **Compounding Path:** Links directly to **`OP-SUS-001`** (upstream SME supplier ESG tier) and **`OP-SCM-001`** (supply-chain resilience & visibility), while anonymized governance benchmarks feed **`OP-X-004`** case studies.
