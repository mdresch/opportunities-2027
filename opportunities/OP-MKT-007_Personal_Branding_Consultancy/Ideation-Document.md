## 1. Problem Context & 2027 Market Trigger

Founders, technical directors, and B2B consultants in 2027 struggle to convert deep domain expertise into inbound advisory and speaking opportunities because most personal branding efforts fall into two traps:

1. **Tactical Posting Without Commercial Positioning:** Executives publish ad-hoc LinkedIn posts without a clear buyer thesis, proof anchor, or offer-to-pipeline funnel.
2. **All-or-Nothing Market Options:** Buyers face either generic `"post 3x/week"` creator checklists or `€10,000+/mo` bespoke agency retainers with opaque attribution.
3. **Proof-by-Building Advantage:** In 2027's AI-saturated content market, executive buyers discount generic thought leadership and respond to concrete **engineered/verified portfolio proof**—validated internally by CBa Consult's own `OP-ITG-017` (MXC) Personal Branding & Portfolio Pack (`mdresch/my-portfolio-menno`).
