## 1. Problem Context & 2027 Market Trigger

Aspiring solo founders, mid-career professionals, and independent specialists increasingly seek resilient supplemental income streams without risking personal savings or taking on debt. However, the creator/side-hustle education market is saturated with hype-driven "get rich quick" courses that demand paid ad budgets, inventory outlays, or complex software stacks—leaving learners stalled between inspiration and first revenue.

In 2027, economic uncertainty, AI-driven role displacement, and flexible-work adoption accelerate demand for practical, zero-capital, step-by-step execution playbooks. Solo operators want concrete, resolution-aligned business blueprints backed by structured management frameworks (offer validation, minimal tooling, first-client outreach) rather than generic motivational video playlists.

## 2. Solution Architecture & Delivery Workflow

Grounded in CBa Consult's **Business Plan & Entrepreneurship** Signature Toolkit and the ADPA source (`Side-Huste Blueprint Courses.docx`), `OP-ING-007` packages zero-capital venture playbooks into modular digital micro-courses.

1. **Core Curriculum Architecture:** 4–6 concise micro-courses covering specific zero-capital service/digital-product models (e.g., niche auditing, template packaging, AI-assisted ops setup, micro-consulting launch).
2. **Resolution-Aligned Execution Toolkit:** Each module pairs short instructional lessons with actionable templates—1-page lean canvas, $0 tool stack checklist, 14-day first-client sprint tracker, and pricing calculator.
3. **Proof-First Funnel Integration:** Course graduates who validate demand and want live feedback or bespoke venture architecture are routed directly into `OP-X-003` Design Clinics, turning low-ticket digital buyers into higher-ticket advisory leads.
4. **Delivery Cadence:** Built over 6–8 weeks (Medium effort, $0 working capital) using existing CBa Consult entrepreneurship templates and low/no-cost hosting platforms.

## 5. Explicit Negative Scope Boundaries & Stage 1b Gate

To protect brand credibility and prevent scope sprawl, `OP-ING-007` excludes:
- **No income-guarantee claims or speculative crypto/arbitrage schemes:** All blueprints focus on legitimate B2B/B2C service and digital-product execution.
- **No custom 1-on-1 coaching included in base course price:** Live feedback is strictly gated behind paid `OP-X-003` Design Clinics.
- **No paid customer-acquisition spend before case-study proof:** As flagged in risks, conversion depends on documented case-study proof (`OP-X-004`) from initial pilot cohorts.
