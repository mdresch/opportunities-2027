## 2. Business Need & Cost of Inaction

Solo operators and aspiring founders seek low-risk, zero-capital pathways to launch supplemental B2B or digital-product income streams, yet most online courses lack operational rigor and fail to bridge learners from theory to first paying client.

In the ADPA source (`Side-Huste Blueprint Courses.docx`), macro market framing cites a projected global e-learning market of $457.8B by 2026 at a 14.6% CAGR (*Illustrative Pre-Pilot ADPA Source Modeling — Not Realized Client Actuals*). No CBa Consult conversion rates, learner completion metrics, or realized course revenues exist yet. While skipping `OP-ING-007` has low short-term downside compared to direct B2B advisory, building it *after* initial Q1–Q2 CBa client wins allows the curriculum to be anchored in real, verifiable 2027 proof rather than abstract theory.

## 4. Commercial Packaging, $0 Working-Capital Model & Compounding Path

- **Micro-Course Offer (€79–€199 self-serve):** Step-by-step zero-capital playbook with lean canvas, $0 tech stack, and 14-day first-client sprint templates, targeting €500–€3,000 across early cohorts.
- **$0 Working-Capital Discipline:** Recorded and hosted using existing studio/screen-capture tools and zero-fixed-cost checkout platforms; zero paid acquisition spend.
- **Compounding Path:** Graduates seeking live review of their offer or operating model convert directly into paid `OP-X-003` Design Clinics, while standout student launches reinforce `OP-X-004` case-study proof.
