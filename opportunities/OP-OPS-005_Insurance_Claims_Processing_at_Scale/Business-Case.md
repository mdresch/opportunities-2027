## 2. Business Need & Cost of Inaction

High-volume claims processors face mounting operational drag from unstructured claims submissions, manual document triage, re-keying errors, and slow fraud/compliance verification. Inaction perpetuates elevated loss-adjustment expense (LAE), SLA breaches, and fragmented audit evidence under DORA and EU AI Act oversight.

The assigned ADPA source digest (`Opportunities Business/Insurance Claims Processing at Scale.docx`) references an enterprise-scale reference context (broker/TPA claims processing at scale) modeling a full internal platform budget of `$1,500,000` over a 9-month build window *(Illustrative Pre-Pilot ADPA Source Modeling — Not Realized Client Actuals)*. Crucially, CBa Consult's commercial wedge is **not** underwriting a $1.5M software build, but delivering the upstream €5,000–€15,000+ **Audit / Diagnostic** so buyers validate process readiness, compliance controls, and IDP ROI before committing capital.
