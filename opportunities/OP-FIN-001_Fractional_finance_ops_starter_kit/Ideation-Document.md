No client actuals or external commitments exist prior to Stage 4 Director sign-off.

## 1. Problem Context & 2027 Market Trigger

Early-stage and growth-stage founders, CFOs, and finance leads in the **Small** tier routinely outgrow ad-hoc spreadsheets before they can justify the fixed payroll cost of a full-time financial controller. In 2027's cost-conscious operating climate, buyers favor fixed-scope, low-risk engagements that cut overhead, eliminate month-end compliance friction, and improve cash visibility without adding permanent headcount.

- **Target Buyer:** Founders, CFOs, and Finance Leads (Small segment).
- **Core Wedge Pain:** Fragmented cash tracking, inconsistent month-end close habits, and lack of rolling 13-week cash-flow visibility when a full-time controller is unaffordable.
- **Trigger Event:** Cash runway compression, board/investor reporting pressure, or preparation for financing/tax deadlines where delayed close cycles create avoidable working-capital surprises.

## 2. Solution Architecture & Delivery Workflow

`OP-FIN-001` packages CBa Consult's **Financial Modeling, Planning & Analysis** signature toolkit into a fixed-scope **Advisory / Starter Kit** delivered over **4–6 weeks** (5–8 hrs/wk) with **$0 initial working capital**.

1. **Week 1 — Discovery & Baseline Mapping:** Review existing bank feeds, invoicing cadence, and accounting exports using [Source-Documents.md](Source-Documents.md) and [BD-Quick-Reference.md](BD-Quick-Reference.md) scoping checklists.
2. **Weeks 2–3 — Starter Kit Configuration:** Deploy a lightweight rolling cash-flow forecasting model alongside a standardized monthly close checklist tailored to the client's chart of accounts.
3. **Weeks 4–5 — Live Close Dry-Run & Handover:** Execute one guided month-end close cycle with the founder or finance lead, calibrating variance thresholds and cash alert triggers.
4. **Week 6 — Stabilization & Retainer Transition:** Deliver the final operating playbook, before/after cycle-time comparison, and optional monthly advisory cadence.

## 5. Explicit Negative Scope Boundaries & Stage 1b Gate

To protect sweat-equity capacity and avoid regulatory liability, `OP-FIN-001` enforces strict negative scope boundaries:

- **No Statutory Bookkeeping or Tax Filing:** CBa Consult does not act as the client's bookkeeper, statutory auditor, or tax preparer; scope creep into daily transaction coding is explicitly excluded.
- **No ERP Implementation:** Does not include migrating core accounting software (e.g., Exact, Xero, NetSuite) or custom API engineering.
- **No Regulated Investment Advice:** Cash-flow scenarios support operational management only and exclude regulated treasury or securities advice.
- **Stage 1b Gate Readiness:** Capability framing is complete; advancement to Stage 2/4 requires Director review of the [Business-Case.md](Business-Case.md) and confirmation of available sweat-equity bandwidth relative to Tier A priorities.
