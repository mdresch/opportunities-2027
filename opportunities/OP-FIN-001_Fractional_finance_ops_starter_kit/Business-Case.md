## 2. Business Need & Cost of Inaction

Small-tier founders often delay structured cash-flow forecasting and disciplined month-end closes until a liquidity squeeze or investor diligence request forces reactive firefighting. Hiring a full-time controller prematurely drains scarce operating cash, while relying on ad-hoc bank-balance checks creates blind spots around payables, VAT timing, and runway. Because `OP-FIN-001` has zero assigned ADPA `.docx` digests ([Source-Documents.md](Source-Documents.md)), no pre-pilot quantitative ROI or NPV projections are asserted here; any target savings must be baselined per client during discovery.
