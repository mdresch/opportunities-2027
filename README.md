# Opportunities 2027 — Growth Without Working Capital

Continuation of the 2026 "Opportunities with No Working Capital" initiative (Menno Drescher, CBa Consult), evolved into a **2027–2030 Consulting Portfolio Architecture**. The project identifies and executes consulting, assessment, and digital-product opportunities that need no initial monetary capital — using time, proprietary IP, and existing frameworks as the starting capital, then reinvesting proceeds into higher-yield offerings.

**2027–2030 portfolio architecture:**
- **17 Functional & Cross-Functional Pillars:** All **16 Management Consulting Functional Areas** (*Finance, Human Resources, Legal & Regulatory, Marketing, Sales, IT & Technology, Operations, Strategy, Digital Transformation, Innovation & Growth, Supply Chain, Risk Management, Customer Experience, Change Management, Organizational Design, Sustainability*), **Cross-Pillar 2026 Ecosystem Categories**, and Pillar **17. Intelligence & Autonomous Systems Governance**.
- **18 of 18 Signature Toolkits & 2026 Database Schema:** Every opportunity in [inventory-of-ideas.md](inventory-of-ideas.md) (**122 projects**) is mapped to a Signature Toolkit, 2026 Category, Skills, Time Horizon, Effort, Expected Capital, Scalability Path, Risks, and 2027 Market Validation.
- **5 Strategic Assets & Tier A–D Prioritization:** Organized around *Financial Truth* (`OP-FIN-009`–`011`), *Organizational Intelligence* (`OP-ITG-014`, `OP-HR-010`), *Digital Labor* (`OP-HR-009`, `OP-ITG-013`, `OP-ITG-016`, `OP-ITG-017`, `OP-ITG-020`), *Evidence & Governance* (`OP-RSK-004`–`005`, `OP-ITG-002`, `OP-ITG-010`, `OP-STR-004`/`005`), and *Sovereign Information* (`OP-ITG-015`, `OP-ITG-017`), unified under the `OP-ITG-018` Managed Intelligence Provider (MIP) umbrella retainer, with **Tier A Flagships** prioritized for Q1–Q3 2027 pilots. See [ideation-template.md](ideation-template.md) Section 2 for the full thesis.

## Governance (Tailored Hybrid SIDPA)

This repository operates as the lightweight **Stages 1–4 portfolio funnel** (full stage-by-stage specification & completion gates in [Tailored-Hybrid-SIDPA-Governance.md](docs/governance/Tailored-Hybrid-SIDPA-Governance.md); quick reference in [ideation-template.md §6c](ideation-template.md#6c-tailored-sidpa-governance--hybrid-graduation-map) and [.github/instructions/sidpa-portfolio-qa.instructions.md](.github/instructions/sidpa-portfolio-qa.instructions.md)):
- **Advisory / Assessment pilots (Track A)** stay lightweight inside `opportunities/<ID>/` (Charter → Delivery → Actual Outcome → Lessons Learned).
- **Platform-backed / build-heavy flagships (Track B)** graduate into standalone repositories initialized from `sidpa-template` (`Stages 5–18`).

## Origin

Adapted from `opportunities no working capital 2026.docx` (CBa Consult, v1.0, 2025-12-22 / v1.0.1, 2026-01-08). The 2026 source carried a full upfront PMBOK governance suite (Business Case, Project Charter, 9 separate subsidiary management plans, and persona research) before any opportunity was validated. For 2027, that heavy upfront layer is deliberately replaced by the **tailored hybrid SIDPA** approach above: keep the portfolio funnel lightweight (Stages 1–4, 3 registers, zero premature subsidiary plans) so effort stays on the idea and pilot pipeline, and graduate into full PMBOK 8 / SIDPA Stages 5–18 via `sidpa-template` only when a platform-backed flagship clears the Tier 3 Pre-Pilot Gate.

## Workflow

1. Add a candidate idea to the inventory (even half-formed).
2. Research it enough to fill in capital/time required and a feasibility rating.
3. Promote the strongest candidates to a pilot.
4. Track outcomes; reinvest proceeds from successful pilots into scaling.
5. Review the inventory quarterly and prune or double down.
