# Opportunities 2027 — Growth Without Working Capital

Continuation of the 2026 "Opportunities with No Working Capital" initiative (Menno Drescher, CBa Consult), evolved into a **2027–2030 Consulting Portfolio Architecture**. The project identifies and executes consulting, assessment, and digital-product opportunities that need no initial monetary capital — using time, proprietary IP, and existing frameworks as the starting capital, then reinvesting proceeds into higher-yield offerings.

**2027–2030 portfolio architecture:**
- **17 Functional & Cross-Functional Pillars:** All **16 Management Consulting Functional Areas** (*Finance, Human Resources, Legal & Regulatory, Marketing, Sales, IT & Technology, Operations, Strategy, Digital Transformation, Innovation & Growth, Supply Chain, Risk Management, Customer Experience, Change Management, Organizational Design, Sustainability*), **Cross-Pillar 2026 Ecosystem Categories**, and Pillar **17. Intelligence & Autonomous Systems Governance**.
- **18 of 18 Signature Toolkits & 2026 Database Schema:** Every opportunity in [inventory-of-ideas.md](inventory-of-ideas.md) (**115 projects**) is mapped to a Signature Toolkit, 2026 Category, Skills, Time Horizon, Effort, Expected Capital, Scalability Path, Risks, and 2027 Market Validation.
- **5 Strategic Assets & Tier A–D Prioritization:** Organized around *Financial Truth* (`OP-FIN-009`–`011`), *Organizational Intelligence* (`OP-ITG-014`, `OP-HR-010`), *Digital Labor* (`OP-HR-009`, `OP-ITG-013`, `OP-ITG-016`), *Evidence & Governance* (`OP-RSK-004`–`005`, `OP-ITG-002`, `OP-ITG-010`, `OP-STR-004`/`005`), and *Sovereign Information* (`OP-ITG-015`), with **Tier A Flagships** prioritized for Q1–Q3 2027 pilots. See [ideation-template.md](ideation-template.md) Section 2 for the full thesis.

## Origin

Adapted from `opportunities no working capital 2026.docx` (CBa Consult, v1.0, 2025-12-22 / v1.0.1, 2026-01-08). The 2026 source also included a full PMBOK-style governance suite — Business Case, Project Charter, and separate Risk, Cost, Schedule, Quality, Resource, Stakeholder, Communications, Integration, Scope, and Document-Management plans, plus persona research. That bureaucratic layer was deliberately left out of the 2027 version so effort goes into the actual idea pipeline instead of process documentation. Happy to recreate any of those plans for 2027 if they turn out to be useful.

## Workflow

1. Add a candidate idea to the inventory (even half-formed).
2. Research it enough to fill in capital/time required and a feasibility rating.
3. Promote the strongest candidates to a pilot.
4. Track outcomes; reinvest proceeds from successful pilots into scaling.
5. Review the inventory quarterly and prune or double down.
