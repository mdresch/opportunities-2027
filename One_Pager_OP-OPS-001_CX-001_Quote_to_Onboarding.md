## Stop Losing Margin and Retention Between "Signed Deal" and "Day 90"

Most B2B teams focus intensely on closing the deal—and then drop the ball the moment the contract is signed. Sales promises get lost in handoff, delivery teams improvise onboarding, invoicing stalls, and new clients experience early friction before they ever see value.

**`OP-OPS-001 → OP-CX-001`** fixes the entire **Quote → Contract → Handover → Delivery → Onboarding → Adoption** chain in one focused, fixed-scope sprint—cutting early churn and protecting Net Revenue Retention (NRR) without buying new CRM or CS software.

---

## The Problem We Eliminate
- **Broken Sales-to-Delivery Handoffs:** Unclear scope, missing assumptions, ad-hoc discounting, and missed billing triggers between Sales, Legal, Finance, and Delivery.
- **Slow Time-to-Value (First 90 Days):** New clients wait weeks for kickoff, provisioning, and clear milestones—driving buyer's remorse and early churn.
- **Over-Automated, Low-Trust Onboarding:** Replacing human checkpoints with generic automated checklists erodes client confidence at high-stakes moments.
