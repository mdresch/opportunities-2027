## Real-Time Finance Approach & Official KPI Candidates (`OP-FIN-009` Companion)

To close the gap between **real-time financial data processing** (including the **23rd payroll close**) and **scheduled monthly close reporting (after the 3rd)** without violating **US GAAP / IFRS**, **Need-to-Know**, or **Segregation of Duties (SoD)**:

### 1. Real-Time Data-Driven Operating Approach (3-Phase Continuous Close Loop)
- **Proprietary Methodology Provenance:** Built on a two-stage track record initiated by **Menno Drescher**: *(1)* **2005 → 1 Jan 2006:** moving the company car into payroll to secure social-security premium alignment toward a **unified payroll baseline**; and *(2)* **2007–2008 at Resources Global Professionals (RGP):** establishing the baseline for **P&L and Balance Sheet specification reporting cycles and payroll closure** (later productized through BlackLine).
- **3-Phase Exception-Based Cadence:**
  - *Days 1–22 (Phase 1 — Transaction Matching):* Daily bank, AP, and AR feeds auto-match; clean items clear continuously and unmatched items receive Reason Codes.
  - *23rd–Month-End (Phase 2 — Payroll Close & Pre-Validated JEs):* On the **23rd payroll close**, real-time payroll data triggers automated payroll-to-GL matching, 24th–25th master-data sync, and pre-validated 23rd–month-end delta accruals (**Preparer → Reviewer → Approver**).
  - *Days 1–3+ (Phase 3 — Variance Analysis & Close):* Accounts within risk thresholds auto-certify; Controller reviews *only* out-of-tolerance breaks/variances so company-wide reports publish cleanly **after the 3rd**.
- **Provisional vs. Attested Separation:** Data flowing between the 23rd and the 3rd is tagged **`Provisional / Pre-Close`**. Official company-wide P&L and balance-sheet statements publish only **after the 3rd** upon Controller sign-off (and under **IFRS 18** effective 1 Jan 2027, any Management-Defined Performance Measures must reconcile cleanly to IFRS subtotals).
- **Automated Accrual Controls (6 Guardrails):**
  1. *23rd–Month-End Delta Rule:* Accrue only post-23rd variable changes above materiality with locked cut-off timestamps.
  2. *Mandatory Day-1 Auto-Reversal:* Every automated accrual batch carries a reversal-date tag; verify `Prior Accrual + Actual` nets cleanly after the 3rd.
  3. *Exception-Only Variance Alerts:* Flag lines >5% (or >materiality) vs. 3-month rolling average or HRIS headcount delta.
  4. *24th–25th Master-Data Sync:* Automated HRIS-to-GL cost-center/GL-code validation before month-end.
  5. *Sequenced Close Gates:* `AP/Bank Cut-off → Accrual Calc → Exception Review → GL Lock`.
  6. *Tiered Approval & Audit Trail:* Non-interactive service accounts post routine immaterial items; Controller signs off above materiality with attached calculation snapshots.
- **Need-to-Know & SoD:**
  - *Tier 1 (Finance/Payroll only):* Raw 23rd payroll & unposted accruals (zero individual salary/PII in BI).
  - *Tier 2 (Company-wide real-time):* Operational unit drivers & approved real-time KPIs below.
  - *Tier 3 (Company-wide after the 3rd):* Attested management reports (`Internal — Unaudited`), gated during M&A/public blackout windows.
  - *SoD:* Rule authors cannot approve rules or bypass GL locks; Payroll locks the 23rd run, Finance posts GL entries; Finance owns BI semantic-layer metric definitions.

### 2. Limited Candidates for "Real-Time Approved & Final Official KPI"
Only metrics that are **bank-settled, system-locked, or deterministic** (requiring no month-end accrual/judgment) can be published company-wide in real time as **Approved & Final Official KPIs**:

| Official Real-Time KPI | Data Source & Lock Point | Why It Qualifies as Final (Before the 3rd) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1. Cleared Cash Balance & Liquidity Runway** | Daily bank feeds (reconciled) | Cash-basis, bank-settled; zero accrual judgment. |
| **2. Collected Cash Receipts (Cash-Basis AR)** | Bank-settled customer payments | Deterministic cash inflow (distinct from ASC 606 / IFRS 15 accrued revenue). |
| **3. Locked Payroll Run Cost & Approved FTE Count** | Payroll system (**locked on the 23rd**) | Official & final as of the 23rd close *(post-23rd variable accruals remain provisional until the 3rd+)*. |
| **4. Committed PO / Approved Spend vs. Budget** | ERP procurement / PO approvals | Deterministic from approved commitments; no accrual estimation needed. |
| **5. Billable Utilization & Delivered Hours / SLA** | Time-tracking & delivery systems | Operational unit counts locked daily/weekly; zero PII or margin leakage. |
| **6. Close Readiness (% Auto-Matched / Open Exceptions)** | Reconciliation engine (23rd–3rd window) | Process KPI tracking real-time progress toward the post-3rd close. |

> **Never "Real-Time Final" before the 3rd:** Accrued Revenue (ASC 606 / IFRS 15), Gross Margin, EBITDA, Net Income, and Tax/Provision balances — these remain **`Provisional / Flash`** until Controller sign-off after the 3rd.

### 3. Policy-to-Margin Reconciliation Waterfall (`OP-FIN-010`)
To reconcile real-time hourly rates, all-in cost rates, annual billable hours, sales rates, **Revenue**, and **Cost of Sales (CoS)** across borders and entities, each rate/cost layer is effective-dated and inherited top-down:

1. **Global Policies:** Cross-border mobility, OECD/transfer-pricing & global baseline rules.
2. **Country Boundaries (Tax & Social Security):** Employer social security, statutory holiday/pension, wage-tax brackets, shadow-payroll/A1 rules.
3. **Sector-Specific Policies:** Industry levies, sector training/safety funds, statutory allowances.
4. **CLA / CAO Policies:** Collective labor agreement pay scales, overtime/shift multipliers, indexation, leave entitlements.
5. **Company Entity Policies:** Entity-level benefits, bonus/car/pension schemes, indirect overhead loading.
6. **Division / Department Variances:** Cost-center allocations, billable-hour targets, bench/non-billable ratios.
7. **Individual Employment Contract:** Base salary, FTE, contracted hours/week, role allowances, variable comp.

- **Cost of Sales (CoS) Itemization & Schema Exceptions:**
  - **Laptop / Hardware:** Monthly depreciation (above CapEx threshold) or direct allocation per FTE; flagged as an exception when client-billable.
  - **Employee Software License Subscriptions:** Amortize annual SaaS/seat prepayments monthly (`1/12`) into **Direct CoS / All-In Cost Rate** (billable seats) or **OpEx** (admin seats); reconcile active seat rosters against the **23rd HRIS headcount** to reclaim leaver/unused seats, and route non-exempt/mixed-use personal subscriptions to **WKR / taxable BIK** on the 23rd payroll.
  - **Statutory Retention, International Arbitration & Cross-Border Legal Hold:** Retain GL, SaaS/vendor invoices, payroll runs, and *loonaangifte*/RTI records per country minimums (**NL: 7 yrs** [10 yrs immovable/OSS], **UK: 6 yrs** corporate / 3+ yrs PAYE, **US: 4 yrs** payroll tax / **7 yrs** SOX/audit), then anonymize/purge employee PII under **GDPR** once expired—**unless** overridden by a longer governing-law limitation period (e.g., **10–12 yrs** for cross-border commercial contracts/deeds/SPAs) or an **Active Legal Hold** for **international arbitration / dispute settlement** (ICC, LCIA, NAI, UNCITRAL). Cross-border discovery/production of EU HR/payroll evidence must pass **GDPR Chapter V** redaction/pseudonymization & transfer gates, while disputed M&A/escrow claims are assessed under **IAS 37 / ASC 450** in the post-3rd close. **12-Year Retention Licensing Rule:** Never pay 12 years of active per-seat SaaS/ERP licenses to satisfy retention—decouple data at close/offboarding into **vendor-agnostic, immutable WORM storage** (signed PDF/A + CSV/Parquet + audit hashes) with **perpetual archive rights** in vendor contracts so active seats can be reclaimed on the 23rd without losing 12-year auditability. **Beyond 12 Years (No Arbitrary Legal Holds):** Under **GDPR Art. 5(1)(e) (Storage Limitation)**, employee payroll PII (payslips, BSN/SSN, bank/household details) cannot be kept indefinitely under blanket holds; any hold >12 years requires a **named Matter/Case ID, employee-scoped minimization, DPO/Legal sign-off, and mandatory annual review** (auto-purging within 30 days of settlement). Only **anonymized/aggregated GL & cost-rate history** (zero PII) and **long-tail pension entitlement/escrow records** (required until retirement/payout) may persist beyond 12 years.
  - **Study / Training & Certifications:** Monthly accrued/amortized allowance, tracked against country tax-exemption limits and early-exit clawback rules.
  - **Company Car / Mobility Budget & Lease/Fuel Reconciliation:** Reconcile standard **mobility/lease budgets + fuel/EV estimates** (used in the All-In Cost Rate and 23rd BIK payroll) against **actual invoiced lease & fuel-card statements** (which often arrive in arrears): (1) auto-match fixed base lease vs. budget, flagging only surcharges/excess-km/damage exceptions; (2) post a **fuel/EV estimate accrual** with Day-1 auto-reversal until the card invoice lands; (3) route **over-budget personal choices** to a 23rd payroll employee contribution and **under-budget balances** to flex/mobility allowance; and (4) isolate macro fuel-price/fleet deltas in a **Fleet Variance** line (re-baselining estimates quarterly if variance >5%).
  - **Flex Benefits Schema (Cafeteria / Choice Budget):** Effective-dated elections (buying/selling leave days adjusts the *Annual Productive/Billable Hours* denominator; cash vs. tax-advantaged swaps adjust employer social security).
  - **Healthcare Benefits & Employee Children / Dependent Insurance Premiums:** Reconcile monthly **Insurer Collective Invoices ↔ HRIS Dependent Eligibility Roster ↔ 23rd Payroll** (handling country-specific employer contributions, taxable BIK/P11D, and **dependent/child age-out or life-event tier shifts**); keep employer-subsidized tiers in the **Schema Exceptions Layer** and pass *only* aggregated premium/deduction codes to payroll/GL—isolating children's & health-tier PII (**GDPR Art. 8/9**) from BI/FP&A and purging dependent details promptly after coverage ends.
  - **Company Credit Card & Employee Receipt Declarations (e.g., SAP Concur):** Daily card-feed matching (Phase 1) splits transactions into **Client-Billable Pass-Through** (WIP/Revenue), **Direct Project CoS**, or **Internal OpEx** with VAT/GST validation; out-of-pocket employee declarations route to the **23rd payroll run** (or AP), while missing receipts or personal spend on company cards trigger an exception (employee receivable / 23rd payroll deduction or taxable BIK). **Concur Retention & Licensing:** Approved Concur reports, digital receipt images, VAT splits, and approval logs are **Tax/Accounting Statutory Records** (**7 yrs NL / 6 yrs UK / 4–7 yrs US**, or **10–12 yrs** if client-billable under a long-tail contract/Legal Hold), exported into **immutable WORM storage** so departed-employee Concur seats can be reclaimed on the 23rd; unsubmitted drafts/rejected/personal items purge on a short **GDPR cycle (≤90–180 days / ≤2 yrs post-exit)**.
  - **Pension Accruals, Profit-Sharing & M&A Deal-Success Pension Awards (with Administered / Escrow Account Reconciliation):** Monthly DC/DB pension accruals flow into the base All-In Cost Rate using an effective-dated **Witteveen franchise vs. actual household composition** rule (`Pensioengrondslag = min(Salary, Statutory Ceiling) − Effective Franchise` based on current household substance, actualizing the **taxable levy** on any excess/ceiling breach at the **23rd payroll close** with retroactive true-ups on mid-year household changes). **One-off profit-sharing** and **M&A deal-success employee pension awards** are treated as **non-recurring schema exceptions**—booked to a dedicated **Profit-Sharing / M&A Transaction Bonus** line (*excluded* from recurring All-In Hourly Cost Rates and project CoS so margins aren't distorted) and checked against country-specific annual fiscal pension caps (splitting any excess to taxable cash on the 23rd payroll). Funds are ring-fenced via a **3-way reconciliation** (**23rd Payroll / SPA Allocation ↔ Administered / Escrow Account [Restricted Cash, €0.00 bank variance, 4-eyes release] ↔ Pension Provider Confirmation**), surfacing unallocated leavers, pending SPA milestones, or escrow interest as post-3rd close exceptions. *(Connects with `OP-FIN-008`.)*
  - **Pension Reforms, Fund Consolidation & Prior-Year Personal Transfers:** During **2027 pension-reform transitions** (e.g., Dutch Wtp shift to flat DC premiums + *compensatieregeling* and **company pension fund consolidation / *invaren***), isolate any DB→DC settlement/curtailment (**IAS 19 / ASC 715**) and legacy-to-consolidated fund cut-overs from operational CoS. Route **additional voluntary/employer contributions** inside the company pension fund through the 23rd payroll + fiscal-cap check. Distinguish two prior-year mechanisms: (1) **Personal Value Transfer (*Waardeoverdracht*)** of prior-employer pots is strictly **administrator-to-administrator (off-company-P&L, zero CoS impact)**; (2) **Prior-Year Fiscal Carryforward (*Reserveringsruimte*)** used to shelter M&A/profit-sharing awards requires verified prior-year UPO/headroom proof before tax-exempt 23rd-payroll processing.
  - **Cross-Border Pension Reforms & State/Country-Line Transfers:** Map cross-border accruals to **Country Boundaries (Tax & Social Security)**—tracking **A1 / Totalization Certificates**, EU **IORP II / PEPP** host-country labor rules, and **US ERISA vs. state-mandated auto-IRA / state-tax nexus** on the **23rd shadow/split payroll** (updating entity-specific All-In Cost Rates). Flag **cross-border fund transfers (*internationale waardeoverdracht* / QROPS / non-treaty rollovers)** for **exit-tax / protective-assessment (*conserverende aanslag*)** compliance; keep individual transfers off-P&L, while isolating any **M&A carve-out or cross-border plan-transfer settlement** (FX, **IAS 19 / ASC 715**, cross-border escrow) in the post-3rd close.
  - **One-Person Households & Pension-to-Pension Funding:** Account for the **single-person household (*eenpersoonshuishouden*)** reality—no partner-income pooling or spousal fiscal sharing, applying single-household AOW/Witteveen franchise rules and individual-only fiscal headroom (*jaarruimte/reserveringsruimte*). In **pension-to-pension funding** (active contributions → collective solidarity/mortality reserves → pensioner payouts or inter-fund transfers), separate **individual DC pot allocations** from **collective reserve levies** on the 23rd payroll, and reconcile gross fund payouts vs. net single-household retiree settlements (withholdings/healthcare levies) through ring-fenced administered/escrow clearing.
  - **Weekly Payment to Monthly Payroll & Tax-Month Reconciliations:** Prevent 4-week vs. 5-week calendar distortions and split-week cut-off breaks by: (1) clearing each weekly pay run (e.g., UK RTI **FPS** per payday) through a **Weekly Payroll Clearing** account matched to bank disbursements; (2) posting an automated **Split-Week Daily Accrual** (with **Day-1 auto-reversal**) for partial weeks straddling month-end; (3) rolling up `Σ(Weekly Runs) + Month-End Split-Week Accrual − Prior Reversal` into the post-3rd **Calendar-Month GL** alongside the **23rd monthly payroll**; and (4) bridging weekly runs into statutory filing cycles—**UK** (**6th–5th** tax month, **19th EPS / 22nd HMRC payment**) and **Netherlands 12-period *Loonaangifte*** (calendar-month filing/payment due 1 month after period-end based on payday/*genietingsmoment*, reconciling the timing delta between *loonaangifte* payday-basis and GAAP/IFRS split-week accruals, plus any *correctieberichten* from post-23rd or household-pension true-ups).
  - **Schema Exceptions Layer:** Applied per individual/contract on top of the base entity schema so non-uniform perks (e.g., car vs. mobility cash, specialized study/hardware) and card/expense/pension exceptions never distort project gross margins.
- **Real-Time Reconciliation Outputs:**
  - **All-In Hourly Cost Rate** = `(Contract Gross + Country Tax/Social Security + Sector/CLA/Entity Loadings + Laptop/Study/Car/Flex Exceptions) ÷ Annual Productive/Billable Hours`
  - **Revenue & WIP Bridge:** `Billable Hours × Sales Rate` (operational WIP real-time; reconciled to ASC 606 / IFRS 15 Revenue after the 3rd)
  - **Cost of Sales (CoS) Bridge:** `Direct Billable Hours × All-In Cost Rate` (CoS) vs. `Unbilled/Bench Hours × All-In Cost Rate` (OpEx), trued up against the **23rd payroll close** and finalized **after the 3rd**.

### 4. Board Approvals & Company Foundational Policies (Governance Anchor)
All automated close rules, policy waterfalls, and retention exceptions trace back to **Board-approved foundational policies** and a formal **Delegation of Authority (DoA)**:
- **Simplified 2-Bucket Retention Standard (Indefinite Core + GDPR Compliance):** To avoid dozens of complex micro-timers while staying 100% compliant with **GDPR Art. 5(1)(e) & Recital 26**, all records fall into two simple buckets:
  1. **Bucket A — Indefinite Retention (Outside GDPR):** Truly **anonymized/aggregated** payroll totals, GL/P&L/Balance Sheet specs, All-In Cost Rates, 5-year FP&A capacity models, plus **Articles, Foundational Policies, and Board Resolutions** → **retained indefinitely** with zero GDPR collision.

- **Sign-Off Register Retention (Audit-Trail Metadata):** Sign-off registers (*Preparer → Reviewer → Approver* logs, timestamps, batch hashes) follow the underlying record class: **7 years** for monthly close, accrual overrides, and 23rd payroll/tax locks (**6 yrs** UK minimum); **10–12 years** for 4-eyes escrow releases, M&A SPAs, and deeds; **Matter Duration + 3–7 years** for Legal Hold / arbitration logs; and **Permanent (Lifetime of Entity)** for Board & foundational-policy approvals. Authority history (name, role, DoA scope, effective dates) is retained **7–12 years** (or **Permanent** for statutory directors) to prove historical signing validity during audits or arbitrations, with private PII purged per GDPR.
- **Employee Data Classified as Company Statutory Retained Information (Split-Clock Model):** Never apply one blanket retention clock to an entire employee file—partition employee records into 4 statutory classes: *(1)* **Corporate/Statutory Officer & IP/Deed Records** (KvK/Companies House filings, Board resolutions, DoA history, IP assignments, M&A SPAs → **10–12 yrs or Permanent** as company records); *(2)* **Tax & Payroll Statutory Records** (payroll runs, *loonaangifte*/RTI, WKR/car *bijtelling* → **7 yrs NL / 6 yrs UK / 4–7 yrs US**; NL ID-copy & tax-credit forms **5 yrs post-exit year**); *(3)* **Long-Tail Pension & Escrow Records** (retained until **pension settlement + buffer**); and *(4)* **General HR/Discretionary PII** (reviews, recruitment, personal receipts → purged **≤2 yrs post-exit** under GDPR). **Breaking the Monolithic "Employee File":** Traditional HR folders freeze forever because a 40-year pension or 15-year 30%-ruling sits inside the same folder as a 4-week CV and 2-year review; route documents at ingestion into **4 separate, independently-clocked containers** (and run a 1-time `OP-ITG-010` split-and-extract pass on legacy mixed files) so expired HR PII purges automatically without touching statutory or pension records.
- **Payroll Provider Historical Data Review (When Selective Deletion Is Not Supported):** Because many third-party payroll databases lack row/field-level deletion due to immutable *loonaangifte*/tax chains, apply **4 compensating controls**: *(1)* **Annual Historical Data Review & Logical Quarantine** (lock >7-yr / expired-PII records behind break-glass DPO-only access, and overwrite/mask non-statutory fields where supported); *(2)* **ROPA / DPIA Exception Logging** (document the vendor's technical limitation + compensating controls for GDPR Art. 5(1)(e) audits); *(3)* **Off-Provider WORM Extract & Tenant Wipe** (export statutory-only records to decoupled WORM storage at contract end and enforce full tenant deletion at the provider); and *(4)* **RFP Gate** requiring selective anonymization/purge in future payroll renewals.
- **GitHub Code Commits & Employee PII in Source Code:** Distinguish **commit attribution** from **leaked PII**: *(1)* developer `Author/Committer` work-email metadata in Git history is normally justified under **GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)** for **IP provenance, copyright, and SOX change-auditability** (prefer GitHub `noreply` addresses over personal emails); *(2)* **real employee/payroll/customer PII committed into source code, `tests/fixtures/`, or seed data** has *zero* lawful basis—deleting from `HEAD` is insufficient because Git history is immutable, requiring **`git filter-repo` history rewrite**, cache/fork purge, and DPO incident logging (unless frozen by a Legal Hold); and *(3)* enforce **pre-commit / CI push-protection** (`gitleaks` / `trufflehog` + BSN/SSN/IBAN/salary-payload scanners) to block PII before commit.
- **Disputes on Payroll Anonymization / Deletion Requirements (GDPR Art. 17(3), Art. 18 & Tax-Office Mandates):** When an employee, DPO, or third party demands erasure or disputes retention: *(1)* **Tax-Office & Statutory Override (Art. 17(3)(b) & (e)):** formal **Tax Office retention advisories/statutes** (*Belastingdienst* 7/10 yrs, HMRC 6 yrs, IRS 4–7 yrs) and open **tax objections/audits (*bezwaar/beroep*)** strictly override deletion demands—cite the exact tax ruling/statute in a **30-day written DPO notice**, extend retention until **final non-appealable tax-dispute resolution**, and immediately purge only non-tax discretionary PII; *(2)* **Legal vs. *Belastingdienst* Definition Dispute (`Actualiteitswaarde` / 30%-Ruling):** resolve clashes between "7 yrs from creation" (narrow Legal/GDPR) and **15-yr effective retention** by anchoring the clock to **`Active Validity (Actualiteitswaarde) End Date + 7 Years`**—e.g., an **8-year 30%-ruling** (or multi-year lease/SPA) + **7-year post-expiry tax audit window = 15 years** minimum retention for the *beschikking*, 150km residency proof, and initial contract; *(3)* **Non-Deletable DB Restriction (Art. 18):** if a legacy payroll DB cannot selectively delete expired PII, place the record under **formal Restriction of Processing** (quarantine lock, zero active processing) with a scheduled tenant-wipe date; and *(4)* **Pre-Purge Legal-Hold Check:** freeze WORM snapshots before any scheduled anonymization if a wage, M&A, tax, or pension dispute is raised.
- **Disputing Blind "Retention Cleansing Sessions" vs. Original GDPR Intent (Recital 4 & Art. 5(1)(e)):** GDPR was designed around **proportionality, purpose limitation, and protecting the data subject**—not blind, time-based destruction of lawful records. Automated "cleansing sessions" that wipe records purely by calendar age without checking **statutory tax floors, 10–12 yr contract/deed limits, active Legal Holds, or 40-yr pension entitlements** violate **Art. 17(3)(b)/(e)** and harm employees by destroying their own pension/wage proof. True GDPR compliance is achieved through **Split-Clock classification, pseudonymization/aggregation of GL history, and strict access restriction**—never reckless cleansing.
- **Recruitment & Selection Committee CV Cleansing (Zero Inbox Shadow Archives):** Unlike statutory payroll/tax records, rejected recruitment data has no 7-year tax floor and *is* subject to strict GDPR cleansing: *(1)* **never email raw CV attachments** to selection/recruitment committee participants—use **expiring, view-only ATS/SharePoint links** (no download/forward); *(2)* purge rejected candidate CVs and committee notes within **4 weeks** of rejection (**≤1 yr** only with explicit talent-pool consent); and *(3)* for any legacy emailed CVs, enforce **M365/email retention auto-delete labels** + mandatory committee deletion sign-off upon requisition close.
- **Annualized YTD Reporting & Tax-Year PDF Storage Rule:** Even a **1-day payment** to Employee X in **Tax Year Y** keeps Employee X in **all cumulative YTD payroll reports, *Jaaropgave*/P60/W-2 filings, and annual reconciliation PDFs through the end of Tax Year Y** (Dec 31 NL/US; Apr 5 UK). Consequently: *(1)* **Retention clocks start at Tax Year Y-end**, not exit date; *(2)* active SaaS/Concur seats still reclaim on the **23rd** upon exit while YTD tax reporting continues to year-end; and *(3)* multi-employee **Tax Year Y WORM PDF bundles** age out as a coherent tax-year unit at retention expiry rather than attempting impossible mid-PDF deletions.
- **Project Documentation & Employee 5-Year Planning Projections / Estimates:** Separate **forward-looking planning models** from **actual payroll PII**: *(1)* build 5-year workforce, CLA/indexation, and All-In Cost Rate projections using **Role / Grade / Country-Band Standard Rates** (and pseudonymous resource IDs for live bids) rather than named employee salary/household PII; *(2)* clearly label projections as **`Forecast / Estimate`** (never mixed with post-3rd attested actuals or 23rd payroll locks); *(3)* tie **Forecast-vs.-Actual capacity & CoS reconciliations** to **employees available in HRIS Time & Attendance** (`Contracted Hours − Leave/Flex/Absence = Available Productive Hours` reconciled against Billable + Bench hours, zero payroll PII needed); and *(4)* retain **Project Documentation** (SOWs, SOP handovers, bids) for **7–12 years** (matching client contract/deed limits) and **5-Year FP&A / Impairment Models (IAS 36 / ASC 350)** for **3–7 years** without personal employee PII.

### 5. Real-Time Employee Dashboards: Payroll Cost vs. CoS vs. Sales per Employee
Connects the **23rd payroll close**, **HRIS Time & Attendance**, and **client billing** into a Finance-owned BI semantic layer under strict **RBAC / Need-to-Know** and **GDPR** controls:
- **3-Line Per-Employee / Per-Unit Margin Reconciliation:**
  1. **Payroll Cost:** `Locked 23rd Payroll + Statutory/CLA/Pension/Car/Laptop/Study/Health Loadings` *(excluding one-off M&A/profit-sharing awards)*.
  2. **Cost of Sales (CoS) vs. Bench/OpEx:** `Billable Hours × All-In Hourly Cost Rate` (**CoS**) + `Unbilled/Bench Hours × All-In Hourly Cost Rate` (**Bench/OpEx**) — reconciling 100% back to total Payroll Cost.
  3. **Sales (Revenue / WIP) & Margin:** `Billable Hours × Client Sales Rate` (**Sales per Employee**) − **CoS** = **Gross Margin per Employee** *(tagged `Provisional WIP` intra-month; `Attested` after post-3rd close)*.
- **3-Tier RBAC Dashboard Views (No Company-Wide Salary/PII Leakage):**
  - *Finance / Authorized Practice Leads (Row-Level Security):* Full **Payroll Cost vs. CoS vs. Sales** for their scoped unit only *(zero household/children/health PII)*.
  - *Employee Manager (Line / Engagement Manager — Scoped Team):* Sees direct reports' **HRIS Time & Attendance (billable vs. bench hours, leave, utilization %)**, **Sales/WIP**, and **Standard Role-Band Cost Rates** (plus P&L CoS totals only if formal budget owner); approves timesheets & Concur business-purpose/billable tags *before* 23rd/3rd cut-offs, with **zero access** to personal household/children/health-tier/tax-levy PII and **zero SoD rights** to alter payroll/rates or post GL entries.
  - *`Reporting To` Hierarchy & Matrix Routing:* RBAC dashboard scope and approval routing inherit dynamically from the **effective-dated `Reporting To` tree in HRIS** (never hardcoded in BI/Concur); **solid-line `Reporting To`** owns leave/bench/utilization, **dotted-line Project/Engagement Manager** owns client-billable hours & pass-through Concur charges, and **zero self-approval** is enforced (manager/executive claims route upward along `Reporting To` to Director/CFO/Audit Committee).
  - *Company-Wide / Department Aggregates:* **Role-Band & Team Averages** (*Sales/FTE, Utilization %, Margin %*) — safe for broad visibility.
  - *Employee Self-Service:* Own **billable hours, utilization, and target attainment** only.
