Executive Thesis: Pricing Architecture Is Now an Accounting, Audit & Retention Decision
  • No Model Is "Free" of Hidden Charges: Shifting from Product Build (CapEx) to Lease, Subscription, or Per-Usage AI Tokens alters P&L/EBITDA optics, ITGC/SOC 1 audit scope, unbilled-usage accruals, and multi-year retention liabilities.
  • The Subscription Retention Trap: A SaaS/AI subscription can be cancelled in Year 2, but tax (7–15 yrs), SOX (7 yrs), SEC 17a-4 (3–6 yrs), and EU AI Act / MiFID II (5–10 yrs) clocks keep running — creating "zombie archive license" OpEx and IAS 37 / ASC 420 onerous-contract risk unless decoupled into WORM storage.
  • Telecom/Cable/Satellite Precedent for AI: Just as telcos, cable, and satellite operators moved from $/GB and flat "unlimited" into speed tiers, CIR SLAs, and converged bundles, enterprise AI is rapidly moving past raw tokens and flat seats into four durable 2027–2030 pricing archetypes.

Design Rule: Pair a predictable governance/SLA floor with capped variable capacity or verified outcomes — with client-owned WORM audit trails decoupled from Day 1.

Section 03

Lessons from 30 Years of Telecom, Cable & Satellite Pricing (Applied to AI)

Raw Units Always Commoditize

As $/GB collapsed into flat ARPU (pushing Nokia to 5G SA slicing and SES to CIR SLAs), raw $/million tokens is deflating 80–90%/yr. Margin shifts to determinism, latency SLAs, and verified outcomes.

Flat Unlimited Breaks on GPU Cost

Heavy users saturated radio/orbital cells; continuous autonomous AI agents break $20–$30/seat subscriptions because inference carries real, non-zero marginal compute cost.

Pure Pay-Per-Use = CFO Bill Shock

Cable "bill roulette" drove churn until 5-Year All-In Price Locks emerged; pure token billing triggers runaway-agent FinOps shocks, forcing hybrid ceilings and caps.
