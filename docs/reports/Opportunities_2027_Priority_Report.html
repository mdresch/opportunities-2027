04 / Strategic approach

One portfolio, two market routes, five assets

Market route by organization size

Small organizations · package and prove

Offer lightweight starter kits, templates and short advisory sprints where teams lack budget or transaction volume for full frameworks. Use these engagements to generate early revenue, client evidence and reusable methods.

Medium and large organizations · assess and standardize

Assess fragmented practices, harvest what works across units, then standardize with methodology- or platform-backed governance. Keep scope fixed and use existing client systems where possible.

Capital-light execution loop

1 · Research

Check market signal, functional fit and existing intellectual property.

2 · Evaluate

Estimate time, cash need, feasibility, risk and measurable outcome.

3 · Pilot

Time-box a low-cost wedge; define scope and proof before starting.

4 · Reinvest

Reuse revenue, templates, playbooks and case evidence to build the next offer.

5 · Review

Quarterly promote, scale, park or retire based on evidence.

Pre-pilot gate: answer the seven opportunity questions—buyer and trigger; pain; deliverable and existing IP; delivery feasibility; measurable proof; boundaries and failure mode; and compounding path. Keep the stated 10–15-hour weekly cap visible in the sequencing decision.
