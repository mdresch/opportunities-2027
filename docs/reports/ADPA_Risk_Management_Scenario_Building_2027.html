01 / Methodological Architecture

Five-step scenario building integrated into ADPA Risk Management

Traditional enterprise risk registers assume a single linear future and miss converging disruptions. ADPA pairs continuous operational risk controls (Observe → Measure → Govern → Trace → Certify → Improve) with multi-future foresight across the Futures Cone (Probable → Plausible → Possible).

STEP 01
Scope & Horizon

Frame a focused strategic risk question, explicit horizon year (2027–2030), and executive alignment.

STEP 02
PESTLE & Signals

Scan internal & external directional drivers across Megatrends, Trends, Weak Signals & Wild Cards.

STEP 03
Vote & Rate

Score drivers with CFO, CIO, CISO & Legal by uncertainty and threat to isolate the top 2–10 drivers.

STEP 04
Build Narratives

Construct 4 distinct worlds via Axes of Uncertainty (2×2) or multi-driver Futures Table paths.

STEP 05
Stress-Test & Backcast

Stress-test controls, map 1–9 yr actions, and monitor backcast early-warning triggers quarterly.

Decision Integration & Backcasting Discipline

  • Strategy Stress-Testing: Score current ITGC, AI, and finance controls against all 4 scenarios to reveal single-point failure blind spots.
  • Time-Horizon Action Mapping: Separate immediate 2027 hardening (1–3 yrs) from architecture evolution (4–6 yrs) and sovereignty options (7–9+ yrs).
  • Backcasting Early-Warning Milestones: Work backward from each scenario to define observable triggers (e.g., statutory audit letters, token-spend spikes, DORA RoI findings) tracked inside ADPA.
  • Continuous Living Practice: Re-evaluate scenario drift every quarter inside Managed Intelligence (OP-ITG-018) rather than shelving a one-off report.

ADPA Persistent Data Model (OP-ITG-019)

  • scenario_sets: Focal risk question, target horizon year, regulatory scope, and executive sponsors per tenant.
  • change_drivers: PESTLE domain, signal class (Megatrend / Trend / Weak Signal / Wild Card), stakeholder uncertainty × threat ratings, and Future States JSON.
  • scenarios: Mutually exclusive narratives, quadrant/path links, quantified threats/opportunities, and 1–9 yr action plans.
  • scenario_indicators: Observable backcast triggers linked directly to OP-RSK-001/004/006/007 risk controls and OP-ITG-013 P0 red flags.
