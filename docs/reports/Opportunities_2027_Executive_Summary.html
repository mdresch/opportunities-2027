01 / Executive overview

Build a focused portfolio from existing expertise

Opportunities 2027 continues the “no working capital” initiative as a 2027–2030 consulting portfolio. It organizes candidate offers around reusable methods, existing intellectual property and staged client engagements, with the aim of validating demand before investing in scale.

115candidate projects in the working inventory
11Tier A flagship opportunities
5strategic asset themes
10–15hours per week planning capacity cap

Strategic thesis

Position CBa Consult around Truth Production Governance: a repeatable cycle that helps organizations establish reliable operational evidence, assign accountability and improve decisions. The working model is Observe → Measure → Govern → Trace → Certify → Improve.

Portfolio intent
Start with bounded assessments and entry wedges, use each engagement to test the proposition and delivery method, then reinvest validated proceeds into reusable playbooks and higher-value follow-on work. The inventory remains a pipeline of ideas; it is not evidence that delivery has begun.

01   Financial Truth

Continuous close and statutory-to-contract rate reconciliation.

Knowledge readiness, context governance and information quality.

03   Digital Labor

AI agent governance, identity, oversight and operating architecture.

04   Evidence & Governance

AI governance readiness, operating maturity, M&A and process controls.

05   Sovereign Information

Cryptographic sovereignty, trust architecture and resilient control.
