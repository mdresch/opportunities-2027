# Dimensions Report: Real 2014 vs. 2018 Comparison

This replaces `Dimension Reporting overtime comparison and the linegraphs over our focaated performance.docx` as the reference for how Menno's Dimensions Report actually changed between 2014 and 2018. That document's "2018" entry was a copy-paste of 2014, and its "2024" entry was an unflagged AI extrapolation — see `dimensions-report-source.md` in project memory. Everything below is drawn directly from the two real reports:

- `Dimensions Report 2014.pdf` (Talent Q, Dutch, completed 09–10/10/2014)
- `Dimensions Report 2018.pdf` (Talent Q / Korn Ferry HayGroup, English, completed 24/09/2018)

**Confidence key:** 🟢 explicit score printed in the report · 🟡 narrative text only, no reliable score recovered · 🔵 inferred by comparing two sections against each other.

## Headline: this was not steady, uniform improvement

The old synthesis claimed every trait improved by about a point, every four years, forever. The real reports show something more specific and more useful: **a shift toward independence and away from active ambition/influence, with the core analytical/structured way of working staying constant.** Four pieces of independent evidence agree on this:

1. **Behavioral Type flipped outright: ESTJ‑A (2014) → ISFJ‑B (2018).** 🟢 Two of five letters changed (Extravert→Introvert, Thinking→Feeling) plus the action-orientation axis (Achieving→Balanced/relaxed). This is the single clearest, most independent confirmation — it's a different part of the questionnaire from the trait scales below, and it moved the same direction they did.
2. **Networking-type traits declined or stayed low** across every section that measures them — Trait Profile, Sales Profile ("Building Rapport"), Potential Report ("Networking"), and the Leadership Derailer report ("Isolation" scored 7/10, trending toward the derailer end).
3. **Ambition softened**, not stayed maxed — explicit in the Potential Report (2018 "Ambition" = 7/10, with the narrative explicitly saying he "may benefit from setting more stretching goals," versus 2014's narrative of setting "very challenging goals").
4. **Willingness to lead/influence/sell stayed low in both years**, not "improving" — Leadership Derailer "Over-dependence" = 10/10 in 2018 (max toward "prefers following to leading"), and Potential Report "Positive Impact" narrative in 2018 explicitly says he "tends to avoid roles involving selling or convincing others."

Against that, the parts of him that stayed flat are just as real: **structured, conscientious, methodical work style; strong fact-finding and planning; below-average self-rated cognitive test percentiles; low creativity/change-readiness.** None of that moved in either direction.

### 2. Team Profile 🟢
Both years rank the same way, essentially unchanged: **Analyst, Completer, Teambuilder, Implementer** preferred; **Coordinator, Networker, Creator, Driver/Commander** least preferred. (2018 adds "Driver" as the explicit bottom-ranked role — "leads from the front... competitive" — consistent with the Behavioral Type and Leadership findings above.)

### 3. Behavioural Type Indicator 🟢
- **2014: ESTJ‑A** — Extravert, Sensing, Thinking, Judging, Achieving. "Enjoy organising, value efficiency... act quickly and decisively... may be forceful in ensuring task completion."
- **2018: ISFJ‑B** — Introvert, Sensing, Feeling, Judging, Balanced. "Reliable and loyal, thorough... quiet and diligent... conscientious in ensuring tasks are completed and colleagues' well-being is maintained." Type B = "likely to be resilient, stress tolerant and relaxed."
- Shared letters: **S** (Sensing — practical, grounded, fit-for-purpose) and **J** (Judging — structured, methodical, organised) held constant in both years. This is the clearest single piece of evidence in the whole comparison, because it's a categorical classification, not a 1–10 scale that could be read as "roughly the same."

### 6. Potential Report 🟢 (2018 explicit scores for most elements; 2014 narrative-only, no reliable scores recovered)

| Element | 2018 score | 2014 narrative (for direction, not magnitude) |
|---|---|---|
| Resilience | 4 | "handles pressure well... recovers quickly" — 2018 narrative ("copes fairly well... reasonable degree of stress") is a softer, more hedged version of the same thing — roughly flat |

| Learning & Improving | 3 | Flat — both years: prefers established approaches, somewhat conservative learner |
| Developing Strategies | 5 | Flat — both years: good at synthesizing multiple views, risk of getting lost in detail |
| Networking | 3 | 2014: "has a considerable network... could use more initiative with people outside his circle." 2018: explicit low score + "may take time to build trust with others outside his immediate group" — **declined**, fourth confirmation of the isolation/networking shift |
| Demonstrating Integrity | 9 | Flat (high) — both years: reliable, keeps promises |

**Nuance worth keeping, not flattening:** 2018's "Collaborating with Others" narrative is explicitly positive — "enjoys a high level of contact with colleagues... finds it easy to collaborate... quickly build close relationships" — while "Networking" (building *new*, *wide* relationships) is explicitly low. These aren't contradictory: the real pattern is "works well within an existing close circle, doesn't proactively expand it," not "became antisocial." The old synthesis would have flattened this into a single "Networking: 5→6, improving" line and lost the distinction entirely.

### 7. Cognitive ability (Elements tests) 🟢
Both years took Verbal, Numerical and Logical reasoning tests, scored against a graduate/professional/managerial norm group:

| Test | 2014 percentile | 2018 percentile | Direction |
|---|---|---|---|
| Verbal reasoning | better than 20% | bottom 10% | Declined |
| Numerical reasoning | better than 25% | better than 25% | Flat |
| Logical reasoning | better than 20% | better than 25% | Slightly improved |

All three stayed solidly below the norm group in both years — this part of the old synthesis's "below average" claim happened to still be roughly true in 2018, purely by coincidence of copy-pasting, not because it was actually re-checked. Both reports independently flag the same tension: Menno sees himself as strong analytically, but the timed cognitive tests don't support that self-view as strongly — "a somewhat of an anomaly between his... inclination towards theory... and his very low score on verbal reasoning" (2018); near-identical wording in 2014.

## What this is actually useful for

- **If a real 2026+ retake happens:** the two traits most worth watching, because they moved once already and the instrument has three independent ways of catching it, are (a) networking/relationship-breadth (Trait Profile + Sales "Building Rapport" + Potential "Networking" + Derailer "Isolation") and (b) ambition/stretch-goal-setting (Trait Profile + Potential "Ambition"). Everything structured/analytical/conscientious has been rock-solid across a real 4-year gap and is a reasonable baseline to assume still holds without re-testing.
- **For the "no working capital" / consulting positioning work done earlier in this project:** the real profile — high planning/fact-finding/post-sale service, low proactive rapport-building and low "positive impact"/selling comfort — matches the B2B framework/audit angle (Finance/HR/Legal/Marketing/Sales × company size) better than anything that leans on cold outreach or persuasion-heavy sales. That's a genuine, evidence-based fit, not a coincidence of wording.
- **If this gets shared with anyone else:** flag plainly which numbers are 🟢 explicit-in-report vs. 🟡 narrative-only — several of the 2014 numbers (Risk Profile, Potential Report) could not be reliably recovered from the PDF text and are not stated as scores here.
