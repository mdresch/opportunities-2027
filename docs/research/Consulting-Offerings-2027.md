## 1. Executive Summary — What “2027” Means for Buyers

Across firms, 2027 offerings cluster into **five sellable bundles** (often combined in multi-year deals):

| Bundle | What firms promise | Typical buyer | CBa white-space |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Agentic reinvention** | Redesign workflows around human–agent teams; scale through “factories” and hyperscaler alliances | CEO / COO / CIO | Too large — attach **governance + evidence** wedges only |
| **AI controls & assurance** | End-to-end governance, model validation, attestations, ICFR/SOX where AI touches finance | CRO / CAE / CFO | **`OP-RSK-004`**, **`OP-ITG-013`**, **`OP-FIN-009`** — *pre-assurance readiness*, conflict-free |
| **Regulatory AI readiness** | EU AI Act (incl. **Dec 2, 2027** Annex III), GDPR Art. 22 sweeps, NIST/ISO 42001 mapping | DPO / GC / CHRO | **`OP-HR-003`**, **`OP-LEG-001`**, **`OP-RSK-004`** |
| **Sovereignty & resilience** | Sovereign AI stacks, cloud exit, DORA/NIS2 operational resilience | CISO / CIO | **`OP-ITG-015`**, **`OP-ITG-017`**, **`OP-RSK-006`** |
| **ESG / CSRD assurance path** | DMA, controls, **limited assurance → reasonable** trajectory (FY27 wave) | CFO / CSO | **`OP-SUS-001`/`002`** — readiness before auditor sign-off |
| **24/7 security operations** | Follow-the-sun **MSSP/MxDR** with SLAs, not architecture-only | CISO | **`OP-ITG-024`** — mesh **requirements & RACI**, not operating the SOC |

**Macro pattern (🟢):** Firms compress **junior delivery hours** and expand **governed agent platforms** (Deloitte *Omnia* / TWAI, PwC *ChatPwC*, EY *EY.ai*, KPMG *Clara* / *KCi*, Accenture *AI Foundation*). Buyers still need **independent prep** where audit/advisory independence, NAS caps, or “policies on paper” enforcement bite — see regulatory gap doc.

## 2. Big Four — 2027 Service Lines (Public Positioning)

### Deloitte

- **Trustworthy AI™ / AI Controls & Assurance (Aug 2026 expansion):** Governance frameworks, AI risk assessments, **model validation**, controls redesign (incl. financial reporting), internal audit enablement, third-party assurance (SOC-style), regulatory readiness. Human-led, AI-powered delivery.
- **Differentiator vs CBa:** Full attestations and deep ICFR integration — **conflict** if same firm audits.
- **CBa counter-position:** First/second-line **design assessment + evidence pack** before Deloitte-scale assurance (`OP-ITG-013`, `OP-RSK-004`, `OP-ITG-018` MIP add-ons).

*Sources:* [Press release](https://www.deloitte.com/us/en/about/press-room/deloitte-expands-end-to-end-ai-controls-and-assurance-capabilities.html) · [TWAI governance PDF](https://www.deloitte.com/content/dam/assets-zone3/us/en/docs/services/consulting/2026/twai-driving-trust-in-ai-through-governance-and-controls.pdf)

### PwC

- **Agentic AI:** Target operating model, **workflow redesign** (not lift-and-shift), platform/MCP integration, KPI-tracked agent performance, governance/orchestration.
- **EU AI Act / Responsible AI:** Readiness gaps, high-risk classification (agents by **use case**), roadmaps; notes **Dec 2, 2027** Annex III for high-risk systems.
- **CBa counter-position:** Regulatory **inventory + classification workshop** and HR high-risk module (`OP-HR-003`) before enterprise agentic program.

*Sources:* [Agentic AI (DE)](https://www.pwc.de/en/data-and-ai/agentic-ai-the-next-level-of-ai.html) · [EU AI Act timeline](https://www.pwc.de/en/risk-regulatory/responsible-ai/european-ai-regulation-and-its-implementation.html)

### EY

- **Trusted AI / Responsible AI:** Operating model, policies, **GenAI governance framework**, readiness assessment (six domains), NIST/EU AI Act/ISO 42001 alignment.
- **EY.ai for Risk:** Cross-links cyber, data governance, compliance; **agent identities**, escalation, real-time visibility; *Value Blueprints* per use case.
- **Technical depth:** LLM controls assessment, prompt injection / data poisoning testing, lifecycle tollgates.
- **CBa counter-position:** Mid-market **fixed-scope readiness** and board pack — not full lifecycle outsourced governance.

*Sources:* [Trusted AI US](https://www.ey.com/en_us/services/ai/trusted-ai-and-ai-governance) · [Responsible AI readiness (UK)](https://www.ey.com/en_uk/services/ai/responsible-ai)

### KPMG

- **Clara / Clara intelligence (KCi):** **Agentic audit** at scale — planning, orchestration, full-population analytics; **Trusted AI** traceability; 2027 rollout ambition for private audits.
- **Controls assurance with AI:** Custom agents for SOC walkthroughs, control attribute breakdown, M365 Copilot for evidence — still **human judgment** on conclusions.
- **AI Assurance** (advisory): Structured responsible adoption narrative for management.
- **CBa counter-position:** Clients preparing for KCi-style audits need **control descriptions, evidence trails, and AI inventory** early — `OP-RSK-005`, `OP-FIN-012`, `OP-ITG-014`.

*Sources:* [KCi launch](https://kpmg.com/us/en/media/news/kpmg-unveils-next-era-of-ai-enabled-human-assured-audit-with-us-launch-of-kpmg-clara-intelligence.html) · [Controls assurance PDF](https://assets.kpmg.com/content/dam/kpmgsites/uk/pdf/2026/01/reimagining-controls-assurance-with-ai.pdf)

## 6. MSSP / SOC Market vs `OP-ITG-024`

**What buyers procure from MSSPs (2026 RFP pattern):**

- **24/7/365** follow-the-sun with **named sites**, shift handoffs, severity SLAs, SOC DR.
- Native SIEM/XDR (often **Microsoft Sentinel**), AI-driven detection, **remediation** within contract scope.
- Co-managed RACI: MSSP monitors/triages; client authorizes disruptive containment.

**Examples:** SecureOps follow-the-sun positioning; Cortex XSOAR war rooms for handoffs; IOM global SOC RFP requirements.

**What Big Four / SIs generally do *not* sell at mid-market:** Cheap, independent **SOC mesh architecture readiness** (alliance/defence **DERA** patterns, escalation topology, evidence for regulators) **without** taking MSSP P&L.

**CBa `OP-ITG-024` boundary (maintain):**

| They sell | We sell |
| --- | --- |
| Operate desks, SLAs, staffing | Requirements, RACI, handoff design, regulator-aligned evidence |
| Tooling + playbooks as black box | Client-owned architecture decision record |
| Multi-year MDR contracts | 3–6 week architecture wedge → `OP-RSK-007`, `OP-ITG-016`, `OP-ITG-002` |

*Sources:* [SecureOps FTS](https://www.secureops.com/blog/choosing-an-mssp-boutique-black-box-or-large) · [Co-managed SOC RACI](https://www.ipsip.vn/en/post/co-managed-soc-how-to-divide-roles-between-the-ciso-internal-it-and-mssp)

## 8. Positioning Rules (Competitive)

1. **Do not pitch “AI transformation”** against Accenture/McKinsey — pitch **evidence, independence, and regulatory calendar** (`market-analysis-2027.md` § repositioning).
2. **Lead with deadlines** firms use in their own marketing: Dec 2027 high-risk AI, Jul 2027 CSRD assurance wave, Aug 2026+ AI transparency.
3. **Bundle as pre-work** for Big Four assurance: “We prepare; they attest” — aligns with PIE NAS / independence trends in market analysis.
4. **Explicit negative scope:** No MSSP operations (`OP-ITG-024`), no Reg BI valuation (GAP 4), no billion-euro agent factories.
