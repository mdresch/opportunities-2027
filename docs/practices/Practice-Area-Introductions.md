# Management Consulting Practice Areas — Introductions & Segments

**Owner:** CBa Consult · **Version:** 1.0 · **Date:** 2026-11-26  
**Scope:** All **17 portfolio pillars** (16 Management Consulting functional areas + Pillar 17).  
**Methodology:** [ideation-template.md](../../ideation-template.md) §2 · **Inventory:** [inventory-of-ideas.md](../../inventory-of-ideas.md)

**How to read “segments”**  
Each practice is explained for **Small** vs **Medium & Large** buyers and typical **offer posture** (starter kit, diagnostic, assessment, retainer wedge)—not industry verticals. Cross-pillar GTM segments (`OP-X-*`) are listed at the end.

---

## 1. Finance

**Introduction**  
The Finance practice helps leaders answer whether numbers are **true, timely, and decision-ready**. In 2027 the pressure is agentic close, payroll-to-GL reconciliation, and audit-season evidence—not another spreadsheet project. CBa Consult focuses on **pre-assurance readiness and operating-model finance** (continuous close, rate engines, management-letter remediation) with $0 new software spend as a design constraint, then ladders into deeper enterprise work when pilots prove signal.

**Segment explanation**

| Segment | Buyer & posture | Typical offers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Small** | Founder, freelance CFO, micro-agency | Template packs, Notion/Sheets close kits, fractional finance starters—fast, productized, low touch |
| **Medium & Large** | CFO, controller, shared services | Fixed-scope diagnostics (continuous close, reconciliation bridge), accountants’ review findings packs—measurable gates, SoD, audit trail |
| **Wedge → scale** | After Q1 proof | Pull-through to `OP-FIN-010`, Intelligence Capital (`OP-FIN-011`), and **Financial Truth** under MIP (`OP-ITG-018`) |

## 2. Human Resources

**Introduction**  
The HR practice bridges **people strategy, skills, and governed AI in the workforce**. CHROs face the 2027 mix: skills decay, agent sprawl in HR processes, and EU employment-AI expectations—not “Copilot training” alone. CBa packages **maturity assessments, HR AI boundaries, and work redesign** (including Frontier HR Leader themes) using the HR Transformation Toolkit where it strengthens the wedge.

**Segment explanation**

| Segment | Buyer & posture | Typical offers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Small** | Founder, first HR hire | Starter kits, clinics, micro-L&D, onboarding templates |
| **Medium & Large** | CHRO, HRBP lead, talent | `OP-HR-002` maturity, `OP-HR-003` HR AI governance, skills/listening (`OP-HR-010`–`012`), human+agent operating model (`OP-HR-009`) |
| **Wedge → scale** | Post-assessment | ESS agent governance packs, HR AI FinOps, 90-day redesign sprints bundled with Organizational Intelligence |

---

## 3. Legal and Regulatory

**Introduction**  
Legal and Regulatory is **governance and compliance readiness without practicing law**. Founders and risk leaders need plain-language EU AI Act / GDPR clarity, contract lifecycle discipline, and purpose clauses that match how AI is actually used. CBa stays on the **management-consulting side**: checklists, rollout playbooks, and audit-prep artifacts that legal and privacy can bless.

**Segment explanation**

| Segment | Buyer & posture | Typical offers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Small** | Founder, solo generalist | Cheat-sheet PDFs, compliance starter packs, template sellers |
| **Medium & Large** | Legal ops, DPO delegate, CISO | Document lifecycle, regulatory navigator patterns, AI purpose clauses, contract packs tied to `OP-RSK-004` |
| **Wedge → scale** | Certification push | Pre-assurance for ISO 42001 / EU AI Act; never substitutes for counsel or auditor opinion |

## 4. Marketing

**Introduction**  
Marketing turns **positioning and demand into repeatable, low-capital content and funnel mechanics**. For 2027, buyers want credible niche B2B presence and AI-assisted production with human quality control—not generic “AI marketing.” CBa favors **sprint-based copy, SEO micro-offers, and vertical content** that can fund Tier A work via cash flow and case studies.

**Segment explanation**

| Segment | Buyer & posture | Typical offers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Small** | Niche B2B, creator-led firm | Landing-page sprints, SEO articles, short-form video repurposing, personal branding |
| **Medium & Large** | Marketing lead, RevOps partner | CRO/deliverability, influencer micro-PR, funnel audits—often pull-through from Sales/OPS |
| **Wedge → scale** | Productized Tier C | Templates and retainers; case studies feed `OP-X-004` and flagship GTM |

## 5. Sales

**Introduction**  
Sales connects **revenue motion to operations reality**—pipeline hygiene, quote-to-cash handoffs, and lightweight automation in CRM and email. 2027 validation treats RevOps as permanent infrastructure, not a one-off tool setup. CBa emphasizes **diagnostics and micro-services** that prove handoff fixes before enterprise RevOps programs.

**Segment explanation**

| Segment | Buyer & posture | Typical offers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Small** | Founder-led sales, small RevOps | CRM/email intake assistants, marketplace optimizers, crowdfunding pre-sales |
| **Medium & Large** | CRO, sales ops, RevOps | Q2C diagnostics (`OP-OPS-003`), GTM alignment—paired with Operations and CX |
| **Wedge → scale** | Wave 1 GTM | `OP-OPS-001` → `OP-CX-001` quote-to-onboarding chain as fastest G4 proof |

## 6. IT and Technology *(includes IT/ICT Governance)*

**Introduction**  
IT and Technology is the **largest technical practice**: cloud and ICT governance, M365, data platforms, agent governance, and platform-backed assessments. Enterprise buyers face Agent 365, Entra Agent Identity, Purview/Fabric, and multi-cloud drift. CBa leads with **assessments and reference architectures** backed by the ICT Governance Framework Application and ADPA—not greenfield builds in the portfolio repo.

**Segment explanation**

| Segment | Buyer & posture | Typical offers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Small** | IT generalist, MSP-alike | Cloud quick-start, migrations, Power Platform CoE lite, micro-services |
| **Medium & Large** | CIO, CISO, CDO, CAIO | Tier A: agent governance (`OP-ITG-013`), context/knowledge (`OP-ITG-014`), ICT maturity (`OP-ITG-002`), retention, MXC/runtime (`OP-ITG-017`) |
| **Wedge → scale** | Post-assessment | MIP retainer, multi-agent architecture (`OP-ITG-016`), TaskPolicy layer (`OP-ITG-020`) |

## 7. Operations *(Sales-to-Operations / Quote-to-Cash & Lean 6 Sigma)*

**Introduction**  
Operations **closes the gap between winning a deal and delivering value**—quote-to-delivery maps, SOP handovers, lean waste removal, and RevOps-adjacent process truth. This is where CBa’s **fastest pilots** live: tangible before/after in handoffs and cycle time, within the 10–15 hrs/week cap.

**Segment explanation**

| Segment | Buyer & posture | Typical offers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Small** | Ops-minded founder | Notion/Obsidian ops, virtual assistance, insurance-claims patterns at micro scale |
| **Medium & Large** | COO, RevOps, delivery lead | Q2C diagnostic, project-to-ops SOP framework, continuous improvement |
| **Wedge → scale** | Pilot #1 path | `OP-OPS-001` + `OP-CX-001`; feeds Case Study #1 and Tier A buyer conversations |

**Segment explanation**

| Segment | Buyer & posture | Typical offers |

| **Medium & Large** | CISO, CRO, internal audit | AI certification readiness (`OP-RSK-004`), evidence assurance (`OP-RSK-005`), DORA/NIS2 RoI (`OP-RSK-006`) |
| **Wedge → scale** | Pre-assurance only | Conflict-free positioning: readiness and architecture, not auditor/counsel replacement |

**Segment explanation**

| Segment | Buyer & posture | Typical offers |
| --- | --- | --- |

| **Medium & Large** | CCO, customer success | Onboarding/retention friction audit (`OP-CX-001`), COAS-scale systems (`OP-CX-002`) |
| **Wedge → scale** | Quote-to-onboard | `OP-OPS-001` → `OP-CX-001` as single narrative for Wave 1 G4 |

## 14. Change Management

**Introduction**  
Change Management ensures **frameworks and tools actually stick** after finance, HR, IT, or legal assessments. Without adoption kits, audits become shelfware. CBa packages **stakeholder rollout, comms plans, and town-hall orchestration** as bolt-ons to medium/large diagnostics.

**Segment explanation**

| Segment | Buyer & posture | Typical offers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Small** | Light touch | Communication templates, simple rollout checklists |
| **Medium & Large** | Program sponsor, change lead | Framework adoption playbook (`OP-CHG-001`), town-hall orchestration (`OP-CHG-002`) |
| **Wedge → scale** | Attach to every audit | Sold with `OP-FIN-002`, `OP-HR-002`, `OP-LEG-002`, `OP-OPS-002`—KPI = adoption, not slides |

**Introduction**  
Organizational Design clarifies **who decides what** as teams outgrow founder mode—RACI, operating model, and role boundaries for human and agent work. It connects HR strategy with ops handoffs and requires executive sponsorship when reporting lines are in play.

**Segment explanation**

| Segment | Buyer & posture | Typical offers |

| **Small** | Growing team | RACI lite, operating-model clinics (`OP-X-003`) |
| **Medium & Large** | CEO, COO, CHRO | Operating model & RACI redesign (`OP-ORG-001`); pairs with `OP-HR-009` for human+agent RACI |
| **Wedge → scale** | Post-pilot | Follows successful OPS/HR diagnostics; avoids line-change politics without sponsor |

## 16. Sustainability

**Introduction**  
Sustainability helps SMEs and mid-market firms **respond to CSRD/ESG data requests** without building a full ESG department—checklists, supplier questionnaires, and assurance-aligned data prep. Timing follows ISSA 5000 and EU limited-assurance waves into 2027–2028.

**Segment explanation**

| Segment | Buyer & posture | Typical offers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Small** | Supplier to enterprise | ESG/CSRD readiness starter kit (`OP-SUS-001`), circular economy lite tools |
| **Medium & Large** | Sustainability lead, CFO delegate | Carbon-accounting adjacencies, supplier diligence with SCM/Risk |
| **Wedge → scale** | Assurance corridor | Links to Financial Truth and `OP-RSK-004`/`005` evidence narratives—not greenwashing reports |

## 17. Intelligence & Autonomous Systems Governance *(2027–2030 portfolio architecture)*

**Introduction**  
Pillar 17 is CBa’s **cross-functional enterprise layer**: the five strategic assets—**Financial Truth, Organizational Intelligence, Digital Labor, Evidence & Governance, Sovereign Information**—unified under the **Managed Intelligence Provider (MIP)** retainer. It is not a traditional silo; it is how flagship assessments compose into **ongoing intelligence governance** for CIO/CFO/CISO buyers through 2028–2030.

**Segment explanation**

| Segment | Buyer & posture | Typical offers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Small** | Generally out of scope | Tier C/D thought leadership only; no auction-platform or heavy sovereignty builds in 2027 |
| **Medium & Large** | CAIO, CIO, CFO, CISO, PE ops | Tier A core: `OP-ITG-013`, `OP-ITG-014`, `OP-RSK-004`, `OP-FIN-009`, umbrella `OP-ITG-018` MIP |
| **Wedge → scale** | Assessment → retainer | One-off diagnostics convert to MIP; Track B platforms (`mxc`, `agent-learning`, ADPA) stay in graduated repos |
| **Tier D** | Boards, research | Intelligence Capital (`OP-FIN-011`), evidence assurance (`OP-RSK-005`)—scoring/thought leadership, not primary 2027 revenue |
