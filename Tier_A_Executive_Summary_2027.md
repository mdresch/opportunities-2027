## 1. Executive Positioning: From Advisory Reports to Truth Production Governance

Enterprise buyers in 2027–2030 are saturated with generic AI roadmaps and static strategy decks. What remains scarce—and mandated by regulation—is **authoritative operational truth, verifiable governance, and decision accountability**.

CBa Consult’s **Tier A Flagship Portfolio** shifts consulting from *Assess → Advise → Deliver Report* to a repeatable **6-stage Truth Production Governance** operating model across all **17 consulting pillars** and **18 Signature Toolkits**:

> **Observe → Measure → Govern → Trace → Certify → Improve**

Instead of competing on \$50M+ systems-integration headcount (Accenture, Capgemini, TCS) or facing auditor-independence conflicts (Big Four and PE-backed Alternative Practice Structures under PCAOB/AICPA and EU PIE NAS fee-cap rules), CBa Consult operates as an **independent, conflict-free pre-assurance and operating-system partner**—delivering Tier-1 architectural rigor in **2–4 week Phase 0 diagnostics (€500–€5,000)** and **6–10 week fixed-scope flagships (€1,500–€18,000+)** with **\$0 initial working capital**, governed by binding multiparty engagement approval ([Rules-of-Engagement.md](docs/governance/Rules-of-Engagement.md)).
