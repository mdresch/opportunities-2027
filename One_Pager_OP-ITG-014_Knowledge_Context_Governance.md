Every enterprise wants Copilot, *Work IQ*, and autonomous agents to answer questions and execute workflows accurately. Yet **~50% of enterprise AI initiatives stall on data and context readiness**—because SharePoint, Teams, M365, and Confluence are full of duplicated, outdated, unlabelled, and conflicting documents.

**`OP-ITG-014`** transforms unstructured corporate content into a **governed, agent-ready Context Layer**—assessing **RAG readiness, taxonomy maturity, ontology maturity, and knowledge lifecycle** using CBa Consult's proprietary **ADPA, ECS, and Knowledge Graph** frameworks.

## The Problem We Eliminate
- **Hallucination & Stale Context:** Agents retrieving superseded policies, draft contracts, or conflicting SOPs from ungoverned SharePoint/Teams folders.
- **Broken Permissions & Oversharing:** Copilot and RAG pipelines surfacing sensitive HR, payroll, M&A, or client data to unauthorized employees.
- **No Semantic Backbone:** Lack of canonical entity definitions, taxonomies, or ontologies connecting documents, processes, and systems.
